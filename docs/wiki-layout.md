# Wiki layout

How pages are arranged and structured in the Wiki, an Obsidian vault. Terms are defined in `CONTEXT.md`.

Every path starts at one of two roots:

- **Vault root**: `wiki/`, the folder containing `.obsidian/`. Every `cfcli` command reads it as `--vault` (default `<repo root>/wiki`), and `src/` calls it `vault.dir`. "The vault root" in `cfcli` output means this folder. Wikilinks, `--page` values and vault-relative paths start here.
- **Repo root**: the git checkout, `--root`. `raw/`, `archive/`, `.cspell/` and every `sources` path start here.

```
wiki/                          vault root
  .obsidian/                   Obsidian settings and plugins
  .cspell-words.txt            in-world words with no page, one list for the vault
  index.md                     generated: each Campaign overview, then any shared pages by kind
  dm-settings.md               the DM's defaults (Session length: 4 hours)
  templates/                   one Obsidian template per page kind
  <Kind>/  attachments/        pages no single Campaign holds (World-free or reused), each folder created with its first page
  <campaign>/                  one folder per Campaign, a lowercase-hyphenated slug with no spaces (shattered-sea)
    <campaign>.md              Campaign overview (type Campaign); may override DM Settings
    <world>.md                 World overview (type World): tone, Calendar
    campaign-config.md         tone, themes, Lines and Veils: instructions agents follow
    story-so-far.md            arc-level view, rewritten by story-arc after each Ingest
    hot.md                     current state of play
    index.md                   generated catalog of this Campaign folder's pages
    log.md                     append-only record of the Agent's work
    attachments/               Visuals, Handout images and map data, flat
    Locations/  NPCs/  Creatures/  Factions/  Deities/  Items/  Spells/  Vehicles/  Lore/  House Rules/
    PCs/  Threads/  Quests/
    Sessions/Session <N>/      Prep, a page per Scene, Recap, Previously On (recounts Session N−1, read aloud at the start of N), Handouts
raw/                           Raw: waiting to be ingested; flat, no folders (outside the vault)
archive/                       Archive: already ingested; flat, no folders (outside the vault); also each Transcript's Session Ledger and recording
.cspell/dnd-terms.txt          D&D rules terms for spelling (outside the vault)
```

## Pages

- **One vault, many Campaigns.** A Campaign folder appears once that Campaign exists and contains every page of that Campaign and its World. Pages that belong to no one Campaign are filed in vault-root kind folders as `<Kind>/<slug>.md` (`Creatures/`, `Items/`, `Spells/`), each folder created with its first page. That covers a page a second Campaign reuses and a World-free page (`CONTEXT.md` **World-free page**).
  - **World-free pages.** The reusable kinds can be World-free, namely Creature, Item, Spell, Vehicle, NPC, Faction, Deity, Lore, Location and House Rule. The strictly Campaign kinds never are, namely Prep, Scene, Recap, Previously On, Handout, PC, Thread, Quest, `hot`, `campaign-config`, `story-so-far` and the Campaign overview. A strictly Campaign kind lives only in its Campaign folder, and the gate rejects one in a vault-root folder. The World overview is a World itself, never World-free. It is in its Campaign folder, or at the vault root once Campaigns share it, and keeps `revealed` required in either place. For a reusable kind, the content skill decides from the DM's ask whether a page is World-free. A World-free page contains the minimum that is playable and placeable in any World: a Creature's statblock and essentials, an Item's rules and a bare description. Its kind's one template serves it, with the Campaign-bound properties and the optional sections left out (**Templates define pages**). No `kind` or property marks a page World-free.
  - **Campaign versions.** A Campaign that needs a World-free page's content gets its own version, made lazily, when the DM's goal calls for it. The agent copies the World-free page into the Campaign folder's kind folder and adapts its statblock or rules. It then fleshes the copy out there with Narration, lore, ties and Party tuning. A Spell's version takes no ties, which go on the pages of those who cast it or pass it on (`CONTEXT.md` **Spell**). The version is a complete, self-contained page that Campaign pages link. It never links, embeds or cites the World-free page, which stays as it is. Two pages can't share a slug anywhere in the vault. The version takes the World's own name for its subject when Canon or the design gives one. Otherwise it keeps the World-free title and adds its Campaign's name in parentheses, as **Page names** sets for a collision: `title: Mire Lurker (Shattered Sea)` in `mire-lurker-shattered-sea.md`.
- **Minimal properties.** Every page records `title`, `type`, `summary` (one line) and `sources` in its frontmatter, plus `kind` where its page kind has kinds (Location, Scene). A page in a Campaign folder always writes its `summary`. A World-free page may leave it blank when its source states none, as a converted published entry does. Its index entry is then the link alone. Every page in a Campaign folder also records `revealed` (`CONTEXT.md` **Revealed**). In a vault-root kind folder, `revealed` and a Location's `parent` are optional, and a World-free page leaves them out. `DM Settings` has no `revealed`. The `sources` values are repo-relative paths of the archived Raw the page was built from, such as `archive/session-11-transcript.md`, written as plain strings because `archive/` is outside the vault. Page-kind properties are added only where needed: `parent` on a Location, the sort keys on a Creature, an in-world `date` on Prep, Recap, Previously On and `hot.md`, `status` on a Thread or Quest, `dndbeyond_url` on a PC, and `session_length_hours` on `DM Settings` and on a Campaign that overrides it. Links in properties are quoted wikilinks: `parent: "[[the-shattered-sea|The Shattered Sea]]"`.
- **Nesting is a link.** A Location records its containing Location in `parent`. Each kind has one flat folder.
- **Page names.** A page's `title` property names it, and its filename is that title's slug: lowercase, hyphenated, no spaces (`title: Nona Black-Jaw` in `nona-black-jaw.md`, per [ADR 0028](adr/0028-page-names-are-slug-filenames.md)). `title` is never blank. On a real title collision anywhere in the vault, add a parenthetical to the title: `title: Otar the Foul (Creature)` in `otar-the-foul-creature.md`. Fixed files keep their established filenames under fixed titles: `campaign-config.md` (`Campaign config`), `story-so-far.md` (`Story so far`) and `hot.md` (`Hot`). The generated `index.md` and `log.md` have no title. A Session's pages, Handouts aside, are titled `Session <N> - <kind or Scene title>`: `Session 3 - Prep` in `session-3-prep.md`, `Session 3 - Recap`, `Session 3 - The Drowned Bell`. Move or retitle a page with `bun run cfcli -- rename` (its `--help` gives the forms), which rewrites every link to it.
- **Every page is reachable.** Each page has a link in from another page, other than the roots (`index.md`, `DM Settings`, World and Campaign overviews, `log.md`, `hot.md`). A link in a property counts both ways, since the target's Base lists the page.
- **Links are wikilinks.** Each link targets the page's slug and shows its title: `[[nona-black-jaw|Nona Black-Jaw]]`, `[[nona-black-jaw#Roleplaying|Nona Black-Jaw]]`, `![[otar-the-foul-creature#Statblock|Otar the Foul (Creature)]]`. Other shown text stays on the slug: `[[nona-black-jaw|the Black-Jaw]]`.
- **Visuals.** `skill://visuals` decides, makes, places and checks each Visual (`CONTEXT.md` **Visual**), in the style of `user-config.md` § Art style.
  - **Files.** Every image sits flat in the `attachments/` beside its embedding page's home: the vault root's for a World-free page, the Campaign folder's for a Campaign page. It is named `<subject-slug>-<kind>.<ext>` in the page-name slug style: `nona-black-jaw-portrait.webp`. The subject slug is its page's slug. A second file of one kind adds a variant after the kind, `wolfrabbit-portrait-2.webp` or `session-11-angry-birds-battle-map-descent.png`. Art is `.webp`. A battle map or reference sheet keeps the format it was made in, and a new one is lossless `.png`. `bun run cfcli -- convert` brings older files to this form.
  - **Kinds.** `portrait`, `token` and `reference-sheet` show a character. `scene` is a scene-setter or recap illustration, `render` an Item, `banner` a Location or Vehicle establishing strip, `sigil` a Faction's mark, `symbol` a Deity's, `map` a Region or Settlement map, `battle-map` a fight's tactical map, and `handout-art` a Handout's image.
  - **Embeds.** Each embed is bare, `![[nona-black-jaw-portrait.webp]]`, alone on its line. A depiction goes in the page's lede above its first `##` section, where its template's optional slot puts it. A battle map opens its Scene's `### Battlefield` or `## Encounter`, or its Site's `## Areas`. A Handout's image goes under its callout.
  - **Diagrams.** Structure on a page is a mermaid block or a Markdown table, in the section whose structure it shows: a Faction's `## Structure`, the `## Timeline` of `story-so-far.md`.
  - **Battle maps.** A battle map is 64 px per 5-foot square, so its size in pixels gives its Foundry grid. Its walls, doors and lights come from a Universal VTT file beside it with the same name (`<subject-slug>-battle-map.uvtt` or `.dd2vtt`). Push builds a Foundry scene for each Scene whose page, or a Site it links, embeds a battle map.
- **The entry is the page.** A page opens on its entry, the frontmatter and the template's required parts. The entry is written as the generic, minimal mechanical entry a published book would print for its kind. `raw/alter-self.md` sets the standard. Its Spell page is the level line and casting block followed by the rules text and the Classes line, and that is a complete page. A 5etools monster is a complete Creature page too: its frontmatter and its `## Statblock` alone ([ADR 0044](adr/0044-creatures-are-their-statblock.md)). Everything else is optional and follows in template order. Visuals come first in the lede, then any `[!narration]` callout and each optional section, then `## Links` when the page has it.
- **Sections need content.** By default a page has only its template's required sections. A section or sub-section goes on a page only when it suits the page kind, is useful to the DM at the table and has content of its own, and each optional part's guidance comment states the condition that brings it in. Inside a section the page has, sub-sections are added freely as their content calls for them. A section never collects facts that lack a better home.
- **Titles say what they contain.** A section or sub-section title states its content in `CONTEXT.md` glossary terms (`## Roleplaying`, `## Hidden truths`, `## Rulings`). A wrapper titled for a reading mode or a stage of play, such as At a glance, Play or Depth, says nothing about its content and is never a section.
- **Templates define pages.** A page kind's template in `templates/` states that kind's required properties and sections. The gate reads them from the template, so editing a template changes how every page of that kind is checked. There are no separate schema files. Templates are minimal ([ADR 0040](adr/0040-templates-are-minimal-by-default.md)): a part is required only when every page of the kind needs it every time, and every other part is marked `Optional.` with the condition that brings it in. The reading rules:
  - A page's template is the file in `templates/` whose frontmatter `type` and `kind` match the page's. Each file is named for them in the page-name slug style: `templates/npc.md`, or `templates/location-site.md` for a page kind with kinds. `type` values are the glossary terms, plus `hot` and `DM Settings`.
  - Every frontmatter key in a template is required on the page, except a key whose line ends in a `# Campaign-bound.` YAML comment (`revealed: "" # Campaign-bound. …`). That key is required on a page in a Campaign folder and optional on a page in a vault-root kind folder. The reusable kinds' templates mark `revealed` this way, and the Location templates mark `parent` too. The comment is template guidance. A page's frontmatter doesn't hold a `#` comment. `type` and `kind` state their literal values. `title` is never blank, and `summary` is blank only where **Minimal properties** allows it. Any other key may be blank where it doesn't apply (`parent` on a top-level Region). An optional property stays out of the template's frontmatter and is named in its lede's `%% Optional. … %%` comment, as `inspired_by` is (root `AGENTS.md` **Sourcing**).
  - Every `##` heading in a template is a required section, in template order, unless its guidance comment opens with `Optional.` (`%% Optional. Include it when … %%`). A page leaves an optional section out or keeps it in template order, and `cfcli check --fix` deletes an empty one. `###` headings are optional structure: a page keeps the ones it has content for. Every callout type in a template is required on the page, where the template puts it, whenever the page has the section holding it, unless the callout's own guidance comment opens with `Optional.` (`> %% Optional. Include it when … %%`). The required Narration callouts are each Scene's Opening or Closing image, the Previously On and the Handout text. A bullet whose guidance reads `%% Optional. Add it when … %%` goes on a page only when its condition holds, and the gate doesn't check bullets.
  - The lede, between the frontmatter and the first `##` heading, holds the page's Visuals. Each one appears in a template as a default embed under an `%% Optional. … %%` comment, such as `![[<slug>-portrait.webp]]`. Every embed a page fills takes the page's own slug, and an unused one is deleted. The gate reports an embed left at `<slug>` as a missing attachment. A battle-map embed inside a template section works the same way. A template's mermaid diagram is the default layout, which a page redraws with its own members and Sessions.
  - Authoring guidance is written in `%% %%` comments, which a finished page removes. Each section's comment says what goes in it, and each optional part's comment opens `Optional.` and names the condition that brings it in. A skill writes an optional part when its condition holds and leaves it out otherwise, never to fill the template. Each comment is written to pass `bun run cfcli -- check` as page prose.
- **Retired shapes.** A layout the DM retires joins the deprecated-pattern registry in the gate's `template` layer (`src/check/layers/template.ts`). The registry holds retired `##` headings and retired frontmatter properties, the first being the NPC `creature` property ([ADR 0044](adr/0044-creatures-are-their-statblock.md)). Headings can be retired for one page type only: `## Tradition` and `## Who knows it` are retired on Spell pages, not Lore pages. Move each fact to the page of the caster, source or target, which links the Spell ([ADR 0051](adr/0051-a-spell-page-contains-only-the-spell.md), `spell-design` steps 4 and 7). It also holds retired layouts inside a section. The first is the PC's bulleted sheet under `## Sheet`, which the PC's Statblock fence replaced ([ADR 0048](adr/0048-combat-sim-engine.md)). From that moment no template contains it, and every skill for the affected kinds states only the current layout. `bun run cfcli -- check` then reports the pattern as a `template` finding on each page that still has it, naming the current template and the skill that writes the kind, and a retired heading or section layout opens its message with `Deprecated`. Pages migrate as agents touch them, and untouched pages wait for their own touch (root `AGENTS.md` **Fix on touch**).
- **New page kinds.** A page kind joins the Wiki in four places, each beside its nearest sibling kind's entry:
  - Its template, `templates/<type-slug>.md`, copying the sibling's frontmatter keys with their `# Campaign-bound.` marks and its optional `## Links` Base, and following **The entry is the page**. Only the parts every page of the kind needs are required. Each other section, callout and bullet is `Optional.` with its condition (**Templates define pages**). A strictly Campaign kind also gets a Campaign-only entry in `src/check/placement-table.ts` (**One vault, many Campaigns**).
  - Its glossary term in `CONTEXT.md` (`**<type>**:`, a one-line definition, then an `_Avoid_:` line), which Ingest's judge reads as the kind's rubric.
  - Its folder in the tree at the top.
  - Its entry in each `src/` list or table of page `type`s that references the sibling (the `grep` tool, pattern `\b<sibling type>\b`, path `src`, finds each).
- **PC sides.** The PC page is canon for both sides. `bun run cfcli -- pull` replaces the `Sheet`, `Spells` and `Inventory` sections whole from the PC's public `dndbeyond_url`, and when the character is private or unlinked, `statblock-design` keeps the Sheet's fence by hand. The pull never touches `Backstory`, `Goals and bonds` or `Plans`. It fills `summary` only when blank, and logs a `pull` only when a page changed.
- **Played Sessions are records.** Once a Session is played and ingested, its Prep, Scenes, Recap and Previously On record what was planned and what happened, and their `revealed` line records when each came up at the table. Later work changes the pages they link to and that `revealed` line, never the rest of these. Narration the Players have heard at the table belongs to that record too. Later work adds to it (a new tell) and rewrites it only when the DM asks or to fix a `bun run cfcli -- check` finding. The rewrite keeps every fact.
- **Callouts.** A callout contains only the words the DM speaks or shows to the Players at one moment of play, word for word.
  - `[!narration]` is the only callout type in a template. Its title states the moment (`Opening`, `Closing image`, `Handout text`, a place's first look), and its body is that moment's Narration, which the DM reads aloud or shows. Everything else on the page, Cues aside, is for the DM's eyes.
  - A part gets a callout when the DM will speak or show it at one point in play. A part the DM consults, such as a truth, a tactic, a ruling, a reminder or a list of facts, is an ordinary section, bullet or table. Two moments get two callouts.
  - A callout is never a bucket. Loose facts, DM notes, warnings, secrets and asides stay out of callouts and go to the section whose job they are (**Sections need content**).
  - The one other type is `[!visual-aid]`, the placeholder `visuals` leaves where an image is still missing, and a later run replaces it with the image.
- **Handouts.** Push shows Players only a Handout's `[!narration]` callout and the image embedded under it. The rest of the page stays with the DM.
- **Stat blocks.** A Creature or NPC that fights, or whose rules matter at the table, carries its Statblock on its own page: one Fantasy Statblocks block (`layout: Basic 5e Layout`) under its page's `## Statblock`, which `statblock-design` writes. A PC carries its Statblock under its `## Sheet` (**PC sides**). Other pages link that page. Only an Encounter where the being is fought embeds the block, as `![[<page-slug>#Statblock|<page title>]]`. No page retypes it.

## index, log and hot

Each Campaign folder has its own `index.md`, `log.md` and `hot.md`. The vault root has only the generated `index.md`.

- **`index.md`** is generated by `index` (`skill://lint` § Commands) from every page's `summary` and is never edited by hand. The root `index.md` lists each Campaign overview, then any shared pages by kind. Each Campaign folder's `index.md` lists every page in it, Sessions included, grouped by page kind, each entry a `[[slug|Title]]` link, then an em dash, then the page's summary. The gate fails a missing or stale index, and `--fix` regenerates it.
- **`log.md`** is append-only, one entry per Agent operation, written by `log` (`skill://lint` § Commands) once the operation's edits pass the gate: `## [YYYY-MM-DD] <op> | Title` (`create` is worldbuilding outside Ingest and Prep), then one `- [[slug|Title]]` bullet per page touched. It uses real-world dates and rotates to `log-YYYY.md` each year.
- **`hot.md`** is rewritten, never appended, after every Ingest and Prep. Only Ingest and Prep rewrite it, and an audit restates each of its lines a fix overturns. It is for orientation, not evidence, and stays under about 500 words (the gate fails over 550). It records the Party's in-world date and Location, the active Threads, the last Session's changes and what comes next.
