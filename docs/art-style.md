# Art style

The full guide behind `user-config.md` § Art style. `skill://visuals` reads it before each generation and applies all of it. The DM's own words, quoted in that section, win over every line here, and a Campaign's `campaign-config.md` `## Art style` section replaces this guide for that Campaign.

## Categories

| Category | Covers | Rendering | Frame |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Characters | NPC, PC and Creature portraits, tokens and reference sheets | Archer | 3:4 portrait, chest up. A neutral or characteristic expression, never mid-action. A muted background hinting at their surroundings, under dramatic side light or the light of that place. |
| Scenes | scene-setters, combat art, recap illustrations | Archer | 16:9. Setting and figures share the frame equally. Light matches the hour and the mood, and each figure stands in its place. Combat art catches the action mid-beat, with positions and distances easy to read. |
| Items | Item renders, Faction sigils, Deity symbols | photorealistic | The object alone, framed close around it. A sigil or symbol is drawn as a made object, such as a wax seal or a carved amulet. |
| Locations | Location and Vehicle banners | photorealistic | 3:1 or wider, an establishing strip that heads the page, more atmosphere than detail. A Vehicle is shown whole. |
| Maps | Region and Settlement maps, battle maps | cartographic | Top-down or a slight isometric view, as aged parchment or clean cartography. Battle-map scale and grid follow `docs/wiki-layout.md`. |

Handout art follows its subject. A person or a moment is drawn as Archer. A letter or an object is drawn as an Item, and a map as a Map.

## Archer

Adult animated adventure art in the look of the Archer TV show: polished television animation staged as a dramatic tabletop still.

- Thick-to-medium black outlines with confident contours, and finer interior lines for faces, folds, hair and kit.
- Cel shading with hard shadow edges, little soft blending, and clear highlights on skin, cloth, metal and wet surfaces.
- Expressive adult faces with clear eyes, arched brows and distinct noses, at grounded adult proportions.
- Poster composition, with a strong foreground subject and readable silhouettes. Diagonal lines of action and supporting figures explain the moment.
- A saturated, controlled palette of teal seas and warm sunsets, crisp whites and deep blacks, rich reds and a few bright accents.
- Backgrounds as stylised sets, detailed enough to place the era, weather and layout while the figures keep the focus.
- An adventure-serial mood, competent, wry and dangerous. Comedy shows only when the moment itself is funny.

## Photorealistic

A photograph of a real object or place in the World. Materials show their true texture and wear. Light falls from a source inside the scene, and the craftsmanship belongs to the period.

## Era and costume

The Shattered Sea is a Renaissance and Age of Sail world, about the 16th to 18th centuries in dress and technology. People wear frock coats, doublets, brocade bodices, full-sleeved linen chemises, waistcoats, tricornes and bicornes, buckled boots, cravats and lace. Naval and Crown officers wear long naval frock coats with gold facings and high collars under a tricorne or bicorne. Antheri brass-and-clockwork artifice, such as Catarina's gauntlets and owl, is the World's one flourish, and it is always ornate gearwork.

## Negatives

- **Every Visual** leaves out lettering of any kind (text, labels, captions, signatures), watermarks, logos and gore. Words a Player must read go in the page's text or the Handout's callout.
- **Every era** leaves out modern and industrial-era dress, such as peaked service caps, world-war greatcoats and epaulette-and-necktie uniforms. It also leaves out zips, modern eyewear, contemporary tailoring and electronics.
- **Archer categories** leave out photorealism and photographic faces, painterly, oil and watercolour rendering, 3D-render lighting and plastic skin, stock-photo composition, anime, chibi and manga speed lines, pixel art, superhero muscle, children's-cartoon softness, and any palette so dark it hides the linework.
- **Photorealistic categories** leave out outlines and cel shading, painterly illustration, a glossy computer-render finish and modern materials.
- **Maps** are drawn as maps, in neither the Archer nor the photographic style. A battle map has no grid lines, because Push lays the grid.
