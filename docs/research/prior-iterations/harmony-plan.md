### 3. prep-session — journeys, caps, signs, bench

Files allowed: `.agents/skills/prep-session/SKILL.md`, new `.agents/skills/prep-session/references/journey.md`, `.agents/skills/prep-session/evals/cases.yaml`.

- Create `references/journey.md` ("Composing a journey"), retold as follows. When the Session's middle is travel between Locations, compose it from the Region's routes as chart rows, one leg at a time. Every leg names five slots: a departure cost (what leaving now leaves behind, spends or owes); at least one named landmark the Party can approach, avoid or exploit that survives being ignored; the smallest set of events, each derived from a named PC's thread times a fact of the leg and stating that derivation in one clause — if no live material reaches the leg, ask the DM which pressure to pull rather than inventing filler; at least one concrete toll (time against a clock, supplies, exposure, a lost secret); and one line on how the Party arrives changed — a leg that ends where it started emotionally was a transition. Scale by travel time, not distance: a leg of hours carries about one event, a multi-day leg two, a route that is the Session's subject three to four plus a centerpiece. With two or more events, include a non-combat beat and never repeat a register back-to-back. Give the PC longest without a meaningful moment the leg's connection event and first pick of travel roles (scout, lookout, quartermaster, or a station at sea); a failed role check becomes the next complication, never a flat penalty. Hand each event to its Scene kind skill as a chart row; write nothing on the Region page. Measure routes in direction and travel time, never in distance bands.
