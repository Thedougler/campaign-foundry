# Prior iterations: findings

One file per earlier iteration of this project (GitHub search for `Shattered Sea`, newest first), listed under [Files](#files) with its repo, mined on 2026-10-02 for what would make Campaign Foundry's content, or its agents' writing, better. Each file ranks what to salvage, lists lore to retell and records what to skip. Findings are leads, and adopting one is its own later change. Mined lore is retold before it enters the Wiki. To go past a findings file, read a few files through GitHub, or search the local clones at `~/campaign-foundry-prior/<repo-name>/`, outside the repo. QMD indexes `shattered-sea-wiki` and `agentic-co-dm` there as collections of the same names.

## Ideas several iterations converged on

Ranked by how often they recur and how directly they improve the writing.

1. **PC Gravity**: opposed dials per PC, with an unreachable desire and a friction point. Prep that pulls on no PC is cut. Lands in `plan-session`, `prep-session`. Sources: shattered-sea, shattered-sea-site, shattered-sea-wiki, dnd-wiki, campaign-os, agent-skills.
2. **Mashup roleplay**: a one-line pop-culture mashup as an NPC's operating system, plus lines the DM can say aloud. Lands in `npc-design`, `creature-design`. Sources: shattered-sea, shattered-sea-site, shattered-sea-wiki, dnd-wiki.
3. **Off-screen world tick**: after play, a die roll advances touched Threads and faction clocks with a cited cause and stops for the DM at a filled clock. Lands after `ingest`, with `faction-design`. Sources: dnd-wiki, campaign-os, agent-skills.
4. **Default outcomes**: every situation states what visibly changes if the Party ignores it, plus a Three Clue gate for mysteries. Lands in `prep-session` and the Scene skills. Sources: shattered-sea, shattered-sea-site, shattered-sea-wiki, agent-skills.
5. **Shattered Sea tone equation**: "high competence trapped inside low emotional maturity", with NPC and scene formulas. Lands in `campaign-config.md`, `theatre-of-the-mind`, `npc-design`. Sources: dnd-wiki, shattered-sea-site.
6. **Named prose anti-patterns** such as the Deletion Test and the Spotlight Accident. Lands in `theatre-of-the-mind`, `humanizer`, Vale. Sources: agent-skills, shattered-sea-wiki, shattered-sea-campaign-os.
7. **Transcript discourse labels**: separate what happened from intentions, hypotheticals and table talk before Canon changes. Lands in `ingest`. Sources: agentic-co-dm, shattered-sea-campaign-os.
8. **Campaign horizons**: anchors set against possibilities, with a detailed current season and only the silhouette of the endgame. Lands in `new-campaign`, `plan-session`. Sources: agentic-co-dm, shattered-sea-campaign-os.

Single-source ideas to read next: presence pass before prose (shattered-sea-campaign-os), cold opens and writers-room compete mode (campaign-os), strong-start taxonomy (shattered-sea), empirical combat calibration (agent-skills), investigation Items and in-world ship manual (dnd-site).
