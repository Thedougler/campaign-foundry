# Prior iterations: findings

One file per earlier iteration listed in `.omp/AGENTS.md`, mined on 2026-10-02 for what would make Campaign Foundry's content, or its agents' writing, better. Each file ranks what to salvage, lists lore to retell and names what to skip. Findings are leads, not Canon or instructions: adopting one is a separate change to a skill, template or the Wiki, and mined lore enters the Wiki retold.

## Ideas several iterations converged on

Ranked by how often they recur and how directly they improve the writing.

1. **PC Gravity**: per PC, opposed dials, an unreachable desire and a friction point; prep that pulls on no PC is cut. Lands in `plan-session`, `prep-session`. Sources: shattered-sea, shattered-sea-site, shattered-sea-wiki, dnd-wiki, campaign-os, agent-skills.
2. **Mashup roleplay**: a one-line pop-culture mashup as an NPC's operating system, plus lines the DM can say aloud. Lands in `npc-design`, `creature-design`. Sources: shattered-sea, shattered-sea-site, shattered-sea-wiki, dnd-wiki.
3. **Off-screen world tick**: after play, advance touched Threads and faction clocks with a die roll, citing cause, stopping for the DM at a filled clock. Lands after `ingest`, with `faction-design`. Sources: dnd-wiki, campaign-os, agent-skills.
4. **Default outcomes**: every situation names what visibly changes if the Party ignores it, plus a Three Clue gate for mysteries. Lands in `prep-session` and the Scene skills. Sources: shattered-sea, shattered-sea-site, shattered-sea-wiki, agent-skills.
5. **Shattered Sea tone equation**: "high competence trapped inside low emotional maturity", with NPC and scene formulas. Lands in `campaign-config.md`, `theatre-of-the-mind`, `npc-design`. Sources: dnd-wiki, shattered-sea-site.
6. **Named prose anti-patterns**: Deletion Test, Negation Limit, Conclusion Theft, Spotlight Accident and kin. Lands in `theatre-of-the-mind`, `humanizer`, Vale. Sources: agent-skills, shattered-sea-wiki, shattered-sea-campaign-os.
7. **Transcript discourse labels**: separate what happened from intentions, hypotheticals and table talk before Canon changes. Lands in `ingest`. Sources: agentic-co-dm, shattered-sea-campaign-os.
8. **Campaign horizons**: anchors versus possibilities, detailed current season, silhouette endgame. Lands in `new-campaign`, `plan-session`. Sources: agentic-co-dm, shattered-sea-campaign-os.

Single-source ideas worth a look: presence pass before prose (shattered-sea-campaign-os), cold opens and writers-room compete mode (campaign-os), strong-start taxonomy (shattered-sea), empirical combat calibration (agent-skills), investigation Items and in-world ship manual (dnd-site).
