# Flesh Out Content

An **elaboration** pass. The owning kind, schema, craft skill,
mechanics, and dramatic function stay put.

**Primary job:** stock the parent with DM-facing facts so
`writing-player-prose` can select what the table hears. This skill
runs first. It writes facts and cues. It never writes `[!narration]`.

Add **presence**: the subject exists before the PCs arrive, while they
are present, and after they leave. The pass succeeds when the richer
page is easier to run *and* a later depiction can be spoken without
inventing world state.

Every added detail earns a job in `CONTEXT.md` Content Quality
Doctrine, or **discovery**. Prefer:

- **evidence** over labels
- **specificity** over density
- **motion** over portrait
- **causality** over decoration
- **particularity** over archetype
- **implication** over explanation
- **interaction** over spectacle

**Signature** is the smallest image, behavior, relationship,
contradiction, physical law, or recurring pattern that carries
identity. **Anchors** support it. **Layers** are how the table meets
it.

Kinds with no dedicated presence owner — `npc`, `creature` lore,
`faction`, `deity`, `business`, `event` — use this pass as the craft.

## Workflow

### 1. Bound the truth

Identify the subject. Load its owning page and only the linked context
needed to understand it.

Determine: kind and semantic layer; `canon`; established appearance,
behavior, mechanics, relationships, and history; table role; stated
emotional register.

Apply `CONTEXT.md` Creative Authority Ladder. Preserve established
facts; invent only in the remaining space. Surface consequential new
canon as a proposal.

If the object itself is missing or unrunnable, invoke its owner first
and return here for presence:

| Need | Owner |
|---|---|
| Spatial structure (non-dungeon) | `writing-places` |
| Dungeon complex | `writing-dungeons` |
| Item identity and rules | `writing-items` |
| Vehicle identity and operation | `writing-vehicles` |
| Combat math | `writing-statblocks` |
| Situation topology | `writing-narrative-islands` |
| Beat function | `writing-session-beats` / `writing-*-beats` |
| Challenge mechanism | `writing-traps-trials` |

Spoken `[!narration]` is `writing-player-prose`. Finish this pass,
then hand the parent over. Do not start that skill from inside a
step.

**Complete when:** immutable facts, invention space, content owner,
and desired table effect are known.

### 2. Diagnose the flat

Spend detail only on the **2–4 weakest** dimensions:

1. **Image** — what can the players immediately picture?
2. **Identity** — what makes this unmistakably *this* rather than its
   category?
3. **Life** — what is it doing, changing, maintaining, wanting,
   suffering, or causing?
4. **Contact** — what happens when the PCs approach, touch, question,
   disturb, follow, threaten, help, or study it?
5. **Depth** — what becomes apparent only after closer attention?
6. **Connection** — what does it reveal about the wider world?
7. **Emotion** — what facts create the intended feeling without naming
   that feeling?

**Complete when:** the pass has a named deficiency instead of a
mandate to add more detail.

### 3. Find the signature

One dominant fingerprint. Then anchors that differ in function.

| Scale | Budget |
|---|---|
| Minor | 1 signature, 1–2 anchors |
| Supporting | 1 signature, 2–4 anchors, ≥1 interaction or reaction |
| Major | 1 signature, 3–5 anchors, layers, discoverables, ongoing motion |

A useful set mixes: one immediately perceptible fact; one behavior or
process; one physical or social consequence; one invitation to
interact; one implication of something larger.

Use a sense only when it sharpens the mental model. Sight is one
channel; temperature, texture, vibration, pressure, smell, acoustics,
weight, breath, taste, balance, and bodily discomfort may be stronger.

**Complete when:** the identity would survive aggressive trimming.

### 4. Make it lived-in

Ask what forces have been acting. Keep only the strongest answers.

**Traces** — wear, repairs, stains, shortcuts, missing pieces,
polished surfaces, scars, nesting, habitual arrangements, graffiti,
improvised fixes, offerings, bite marks, bureaucracy, erosion.

**Routines** — what happens when nobody important is watching? Who
sweeps? What does the monster do when it is not fighting? What does
the noble complain about at breakfast? Where do wet boots go? Who
replaces the candles? What does the guard actually watch for?

**Pressures** — hunger, weather, debt, fear, maintenance, status,
rivalry, scarcity, migration, illness, orders, secrecy, grief,
ambition. What makes the current state unstable?

**History made physical** — a repaired gate says there was an attack.
Three sizes of handprint say a family remained. New locks on old
doors say trust recently disappeared.

**Connections** — food → supply; fashion → aspiration; damage → an
offender; ritual → belief; neglect → priorities; architecture →
danger or labor; scars → rivals.

**Complete when:** the subject is embedded in causes, relationships,
and ongoing processes rather than placed on an empty stage.

### 5. Layer contact

Prepare **layers**, not an exposition dump.

| Layer | Question |
|---|---|
| **Surface** | What is obvious immediately? Enough for a first mental model. |
| **Attention** | What becomes clear when a player looks, listens, talks, follows, compares, smells, remembers, or asks a sensible question? |
| **Contact** | What happens when they manipulate it? Objects resist, ring, stain, unfold, jam, warm, leak, attract notice, expose seams. People deflect, bargain, remember, posture, misread, soften, retaliate. Creatures stalk, retreat, bluff, circle, protect, investigate, feed, signal, or use the terrain. |
| **Disturbance** | What changes because they interfered? A moved object leaves evidence. An insult changes later behavior. A broken ward changes the room. A frightened animal warns something else. Humiliation becomes gossip. |

Prepare responses. Leave the method to play.

**Complete when:** at least two plausible engagements produce a
concrete world response.

### 6. Tune the register

Emotion is an effect of facts. Build the causes.

If a register is in play, load that heading in
`references/registers.md` and apply it. Skip the file when no
intended feeling or kind-specific presence work remains.

**Complete when:** every required fact from the loaded register is on
the page, and no sentence tells the players what to feel. If no
register was loaded, this step is already complete.

### 7. Compress and stock the prose

Cut hard. The DM should recover the subject in seconds.

Prefer a one-line mental model, short concrete bullets, verbs,
distinctive nouns, observable behavior, interaction cues,
discoverable layers, consequential details.

Preserve good existing prose. Write into the owning page's existing
sections. No `Fleshing Out` heading and no second source of truth.

Leave **prose stock** — more than `writing-player-prose` will speak,
so it can select:

- one signature image
- Surface facts perceivable at a glance
- at least two non-visual sensory facts (prose picks one)
- one toy the table can touch
- Attention / Contact / Disturbance as later layers
- hidden truth only in existing secret or DM sections

Classify each added fact as perceivable, knowable, or hidden
(`writing-player-prose/references/information-boundary.md`). Write
observable evidence, not spoken sentences and not mood instructions.

If the originating request includes spoken prose, this pass ends
here. `writing-player-prose` continues from the stocked parent.

**Complete when:** the richer version is easier to run, and
`writing-player-prose` can draft an establish depiction from Surface
facts without inventing world state.

### 8. Presence test

Test every modified subject. Stop when every applicable test passes.

| Test | Fail → |
|---|---|
| **Distinct** — replace the name with “generic tavern / noble / forest / monster” and much is lost | sharpen the signature |
| **Concrete** — the DM can picture or perform the important details | replace abstractions with evidence |
| **Active** — something happens independent of the PCs | add behavior, process, pressure, or change |
| **Interactive** — players can investigate or interfere | add responses or affordances |
| **Layered** — closer engagement reveals more than the opening | add discoverable depth |
| **Causal** — details imply makers, users, history, ecology, relationships, or consequences | cut disconnected decoration |
| **Emotional** — the feeling arises from what is happening | strengthen causes; cut mood instructions |
| **Memorable** — one callback-ready fingerprint | strengthen the signature |
| **Runnable** — a glance says what to emphasize and how it reacts | compress until yes |
| **Authoritative** — canon, audience, mechanics ownership, and player agency intact | correct the overreach |
| **Speakable** — Surface facts can become a `[!narration]` without new invention | add the missing signature, sense, or toy |

More detail after that is sediment.

## Who may invoke

Fire before `writing-player-prose` drafts a first picture; when the
user asks to flesh out a page or sketch; or when an owner has a
structurally sound object that is still generic.

Expected callers: `writing-player-prose` (Establish, Transition,
session-open pictures, or a Continue whose subject has no presence);
`writing-places`, `writing-dungeons`, `writing-items`,
`writing-vehicles`, `writing-statblocks`, `writing-narrative-islands`,
`writing-session-beats`, `writing-traps-trials`, `writing-cold-opens`.

This skill does not fire `writing-player-prose`. That skill resumes
after this pass when spoken text was requested.

## Delegation

- Owner skills in the step-1 table — the object is missing or
  unrunnable
- `visual-aids` — a look or named illustration

Out of scope: `[!narration]` and any sentence the table hears;
minting a new kind owner; rewriting mechanics; prescribing player
feeling or method; a universal presence section.
