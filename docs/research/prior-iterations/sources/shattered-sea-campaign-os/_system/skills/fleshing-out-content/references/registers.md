# Presence registers

Load only the heading that matches the intended feeling or the
subject's kind. Structural and mechanical invention stays with the
owner skill named there. These facts stay on the parent for
`writing-player-prose` to select from; this file does not write
`[!narration]`.

## Horror

Build **vulnerability + uncertainty + violation**.

1. Establish what normal looks like.
2. Introduce one precise wrongness.
3. Give evidence without complete explanation.
4. Let implications grow worse than the first observation.
5. Allow action while keeping some uncertainty alive.
6. Reveal only enough to change what the players fear.

Useful facts: something acting out of sight; familiar things following
the wrong rules; a trace whose cause is worse than the trace; absence
where presence is expected, or presence where none should be possible;
violated bodily, social, spatial, religious, or natural boundaries;
credible harm to something the characters value; ordinary protections
already failed; a pattern the players recognize before they understand
it.

Threaten certainty, safety, control, relationships, identity, and
expectation — not only hit points. Diegetic helplessness can create
horror; removing player agency creates frustration. Describe the
world and NPC reactions. Players decide what their characters feel.

## Wonder

Build **novelty + coherence + invitation**.

Give the fantastic thing one extraordinary premise, consistent local
logic, scale the players can orient against, recognizable landmarks,
something visibly happening, something they can manipulate, something
curiosity can uncover, and consequences that prove it is more than
scenery.

The strange detail becomes convincing when it behaves consistently.

## Villainy

Build **evidence of character**, not an instruction to hate.

Show what they choose when cruelty would be avoidable; who pays for
their comfort, ambition, ideology, vanity, or cowardice; a specific
harm with a specific victim or aftermath; how they treat someone
beneath their notice; the value they claim versus the exception they
make for themselves; the small behavior that makes the larger evil
personal; what they genuinely love, admire, fear, or protect; why
another real person might willingly follow them.

Hatred lands when the players can understand the choice and judge it.

## NPC

Build **desire + contradiction + behavior + relationship**.

Give a significant person: a want independent of the PCs; a second
want, duty, fear, or loyalty that complicates the first; one
performable physical or conversational cue; one specific competence
or limitation; one opinion that exposes values; one relationship that
changes how they behave; a reaction to pressure their stereotype
would not predict.

Prefer a cue the DM can perform instantly over personality
adjectives. There is no NPC craft owner — this register is the craft.

## Creature

Make mechanics and ecology visible in the fiction, on the `creature`
(or other lore) page.

Use distinctive movement; body physics; feeding, nesting, hunting,
defending, or social behavior; environmental traces; sounds or smells
produced for a reason; visible tells of important abilities; reactions
to injury, fear, advantage, hunger, territory, offspring, or rivals.

The monster inhabits the world before initiative. Combat math stays
with `writing-statblocks`.

## Place

Strengthen silhouette; reusable landmarks; local physical conditions;
evidence of use and history; spatial relationships that matter; active
processes; affordances; inhabitant behavior; transitions between
meaningful spaces.

Establish what players need to orient, imagine, and act. Structural
place design stays with `writing-places`. Dungeon complexes stay with
`writing-dungeons`.

## Dungeon

Strengthen whole-hole silhouette; zone landmarks; motif and visibility;
typical inhabitant activity and spoor; route signals; manipulable
features; danger tells; how rooms affect one another.

Structural dungeon design stays with `writing-dungeons`. Challenge
mechanisms stay with `writing-traps-trials`. Tonight's roster is Work.

## Item

Strengthen handling and material; wear or preservation; evidence of
makers and previous owners; the experience of using it; rituals,
habits, or precautions around it; visible consequences of its nature;
reactions it provokes in knowledgeable people.

Mechanical item design stays with `writing-items`.

## Vehicle

Strengthen silhouette and motion; propulsion and control cues; boarding
and access; characteristic sound, vibration, smell, wake, or tracks;
visible wear, repairs, trophies, or modifications; crew activity and
spoor; what fails first under stress; the experience of riding or
working it.

Structural vehicle design stays with `writing-vehicles`. Combat math
stays with `writing-statblocks`. Playable interiors stay with
`writing-places` or `writing-dungeons`.

## Faction

Make abstractions visible through ordinary practices, material
culture, status signals, etiquette, labor, recurring disputes,
internal disagreements, what members take for granted, and how ideals
survive or fail in mundane life.

A group feels real when members share influences without sharing one
personality. Same register for cultures, religions, and institutions.

## Situation

Add presence through visible pressures; people already acting;
evidence of what just happened; change already underway; likely
reactions rather than predicted outcomes; physical or social
consequences the PCs can notice; signs connecting the moment to
larger forces.

Keep prepared possibility as reaction and tide. Situation topology
stays with `writing-narrative-islands`; beat function stays with
`writing-session-beats` and `writing-*-beats`.
