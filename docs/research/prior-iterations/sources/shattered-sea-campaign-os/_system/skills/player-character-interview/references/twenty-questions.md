# The 20 questions

Ask one at a time, in order, prefixed **"Question N of 20."**
Acknowledge each answer briefly, then move on. Skip is valid.

Start with: *"Let's get to know your character. I'll walk you
through some questions — there are no wrong answers, and you can
keep things as simple or as rich as you like. Ready?"* Then Q1.
Close with: *"That's all of them. Let me put that together."*

1. What's their name? Any aliases, nicknames, titles? Is there a
   name that only one specific person ever called them?
2. They walk through the door. What do I see?
3. What's their class? And *why* — a calling, a choice, or something
   life did not leave them much choice about?
4. Is there a flaw and a gift that are bound up together? If so,
   what is that pairing, and do *they* know it's there?
5. What do they want right now, more than anything? Are they honest
   with themselves about it, or do they dress it up?
6. By the end of all this, who do they want to *be*? Not what they
   want to accomplish. Who. Do they even know yet?
7. What keeps them up at night? Something real, not monsters. Is
   there something specific they're afraid of losing?
8. What do they believe that most people around them would push
   back on? Have they ever said it out loud?
9. Is there a line they won't cross? Have they ever come close?
10. What did their life look like before this? Day to day — what
    were they actually doing?
11. What's the hardest thing they've had to survive — or do — to
    become the person we're meeting?
12. Do they carry something with a story? A weapon, a trinket,
    something worn down from use. What is it, and where from?
13. What's the worst thing they've ever done? Did they mean to?
14. Whose opinion of them matters most? Where is that person now?
15. Is there anyone who considers them an enemy? Who, and why?
16. Who would they trust with their life — not because that person
    is powerful, but because they *trust* them? What earned it?
17. How do they treat someone who can't do anything for them? A
    beggar, a servant, a child. Raised that way, or learned?
18. What's the one thing they want so badly they might compromise
    who they are to get it? Do they know that about themselves?
19. What are they carrying that would change how people see them
    if it ever came out?
20. Do they believe in the gods? Does it matter to them whether
    the gods believe in them?

Q4 is a bound flaw/gift. This vault names the pairing the **Mortis** —
the limitation is the Mark, the benefit is the Gift. Ask the pairing;
reference the Mortis, do not import a foreign mark.

After the 20: player name (required), class/level if they have a
sheet handy. Stated only.
