## Key Takeaways

- The Unsaid Macaw is the **privacy horror**: no combat budget, no lie detection — presence alone breeds self-censorship and suspicion.
- The Cave is Aruhe's **optional high-risk bypass**: faster route, almost no forage, fewer but worse encounters, uncertain exits — the party chooses between the Bear-Elk circuit they understand and the hole where nothing has a schedule. **The Flow** principle (down-tube = coastward, DC 14 Nature/Survival) and **junction decisions** (DC 13/15/18) make competence work — no arbitrary maze rolls.
- The Blackrail is the thing that makes the Cave's metaphor click: **an oncoming train encounter**. It gives ~30 seconds of warning, cannot turn sharply at full speed, cannot fit everywhere — the tiny holes the party hated become safety.
- Aruhe hazards cluster at DC 14–15; the Cave adds Dead Air (DC 14 Con), Drumstone (DC 15 Perception / DC 14 Dex), Flood Pulse (DC 15 Str), and the Squeeze (no roll — a route choice).
- The Cave is still Aruhe: **observation is free; taking living things fires the Taking Rule** — but cave seepage water is safe, making Root Wells vital when supplies run low.
