## Scene 1 — Cat's Curios *(flashback, fixed)*  ← example card (full skeleton)

Italic timing/rpurpose line: *"This is Catarina's table introduction — hand it to her player before anything else happens tonight. ~15–30 min."*

Image embed: `![[wiki/assets/...|establishing still, style description]]`

> [!dm] Frame this as a flashback: **"Two days ago."** History, not a live branch... Before reading anything aloud, ask Catarina's player to introduce herself: what she's working on at the bench, how she's standing, what she looks like today. Once she's done, the visit below fires.

> [!read-aloud] (dialogue block, Knighton's opening)

**What's true:** Knighton came in person — a Commodore doesn't visit a contractor's shop for something routine, and that fact alone is most of the message. ...play him exactly to type.

> [!read-aloud] (the offer, verbatim)

**Knighton's offer:** two days to prepare, Crown launch, no charge... He is not lying about danger existing underground — he is lying about what it is.

> [!check] Insight — Passive (automatic)
> Passive clears DC 13 without a roll: the urgency is genuine, but he's vague in a way a real Commission incident wouldn't be.

> [!check] Insight/Investigation — DC 15, if her player presses him actively
> Fail: reads as sincere concern... / Success: the story doesn't match procedure — ...

**If she asks why only her:** → read-aloud answer + **What's true**-style DM gloss ("Cold, bureaucratic, and true. The Crown protects its assets, not the city. This is the tell the scene exists to deliver.")

**If pushed on how he knows:** → one-sentence read-aloud + gloss.

> [!dm] **Do not confirm or deny the toxin connection.** ... seed for later, not a reveal.

**How she closes it out — she doesn't take the launch:** → final read-aloud + gloss ("He doesn't threaten — he replaces. The launch waiting empty *is* the message.")

**Then let the flashback breathe** — close on a fade read-aloud, cut to the present, pointer to where Scene 3 drops.
