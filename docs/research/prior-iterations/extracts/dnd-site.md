## Faction operational detail: The Passage
- source: `content/factions/Passage.md` (found)
- destination: `faction-design` (CF Passage has One Law, Holds, couriers; preserve only missing operational detail)
- load-bearing:
  - Three-layer structure: **the Run** (travelling families/crews, circuit knowledge passed generations, encoded as scratch-notation on bone and ceramic shards legible only to Kin); **the Hold** (caches inside port infrastructure — drainage conduits, dock substructure, wall cavities, cisterns; often predate the buildings they occupy; routes reseeded into new construction when cities rebuild); **the Tangle** (seven senior elders governing the Calveno Run; hold the most sensitive routing intelligence).
  - What a route IS: institutional knowledge, not a path — which harbour wall has a crawlspace, which dock beam carries a warning mark, which clerk is reliable, which tide opens a low tunnel, which names carry risk in a given port. Not shared with outsiders by default.
  - Kin vs Dry: a Dry can be a trusted partner, still not Kin; information flowing between Kin does not flow to Dry by default.
  - The One Law: the Passage does not move people in bondage — the rule most widely known outside Kin circles.
  - External powers: Dravosi records can list Rattkin as a bounty/fine category in Crown territory; the Tessarine Concordat has no formal dealings with the Passage (which is why Rattkin couriers carry off-ledger transactions); Waveservants collect from hidden sailors too; Grung treated with caution (raids, bondage).
  - Etiquette ("Advice"): knowing one shown route is not knowledge of the Passage; never mark a Hold on a map; never ask a courier to explain a message shard; never say "pest" where Kin are present.
