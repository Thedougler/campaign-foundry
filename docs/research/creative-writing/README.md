# Creative-writing research

Scout reports behind the creative process in `docs/creative-process.md`. They gather the research linked from these issues, plus supplementary reading on story craft and campaign craft:

- [#38 Character-agent simulation](https://github.com/Thedougler/campaign-foundry/issues/38)
- [#39 Multi-agent narrative planning](https://github.com/Thedougler/campaign-foundry/issues/39)
- [#40 Beat prompts and story-context assembly](https://github.com/Thedougler/campaign-foundry/issues/40)
- [#41 Evaluation methods and narrative diversity](https://github.com/Thedougler/campaign-foundry/issues/41)

Quoted prompts, rubric questions and principles are verbatim from their sources. Analysis lines are the scout's own. The issues supplied research inputs only. Their suggestions for how to build each change were left out.
