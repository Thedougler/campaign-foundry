# Story first, then adapt

`collab-with-me` riffed on the DM's raw ideas and filed them straight into the Wiki. Nothing in that process wrote a story: no premise, conflict, cast or ending was tested as fiction before it became Threads and Scenes, and one agent voiced every NPC at once, so each character could draw on the others' secrets. The research on story craft and campaign craft ([`story-vs-campaign.md`](../research/creative-writing/story-vs-campaign.md)) agrees on character, conflict and stakes and parts ways on authorship: a story fixes its events, while a campaign authors situations that the Party resolves by its own choices.

Decision:

- **Story first.** The creative process writes a Story with fiction craft: a Story Bible and an outline of Beats by default, with prose drafted one Beat at a time only when the DM asks.
- **Then adapt.** Adaptation turns the Story into situations, Threads, Clues and, through the Session stage, Scenes. Every PC decision in the Story becomes an open choice for the Party. Only adapted material reaches Raw and the Wiki.
- **One Persona per NPC.** A Simulation plays each NPC in its own isolated Persona agent under a Director. A Persona knows only its dossier and the events it witnesses, so voices and secrets stay with their owners.
- **Session-local drafts.** Story Bibles, outlines, prose drafts and Simulation ledgers live in `local://collab/`, outside the Wiki, until Adaptation files what the DM keeps.

`collab-with-me` is the single creative entrypoint and loads each stage's owner; [`docs/creative-process.md`](../creative-process.md) maps the stages, agents and owner files.
