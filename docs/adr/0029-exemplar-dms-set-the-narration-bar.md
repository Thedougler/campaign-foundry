# Exemplar DMs set the Narration bar

Extends [ADR 0025](0025-narration-craft-checks-and-voice-envelope.md).

The DM studied two full actual-play transcripts as the quality bar for everything Campaign Foundry writes for the DM's voice: Brennan Lee Mulligan, Critical Role Campaign 4 Episode 9 ("To the Hounds!", ASR transcript, 9001 lines) and Matt Mercer, Campaign 2 Episode 45 ("The Stowaway", 11140 lines). Only the DM side was studied. The DM's decision is binding: where any rule, lint check, skill line, template or eval disagrees with what Brennan or Mercer actually does at the table, the repo is wrong and changes.

Decision:

- **Rule removals.** `Narration.FilterVerbs`, `Narration.PerceptionHedges`, `Narration.NoCompass` and `Narration.NoFootMileCounts` are deleted, with their `.vale.ini` pins and hint entries. "You see / you hear / you notice" is the exemplars' spoken camera, not a fault; hedged perception ("what looks to be", "you could swear") calibrates certainty; bearings and measured space ("about 12 feet off the ground", "more than fifty miles") are playable detail the table needs. `Narration.PcInterior` is re-tokened to the decision and the emotion ("you decide", "you feel afraid", "your heart pounds"): sensations and knowledge ("You feel the cold of the stone", "You know that half the watch stood there") are legal narration.
- **Term pruning.** Words the exemplars use in DM narration leave the judgement lists. Dropped from `JudgementWords`: `ancient`, `strange`, `angry`, `sense of`. Dropped from the evaluative-adjective list in `src/narration/analyze.ts`: `ancient`, `beautiful`, `enormous`, `horrible`, `lovely`, `strange`, `terrible`, `tiny`, `vast`, `wonderful`. Evidence: DM-narration occurrences in the two transcripts ("an ancient orcish castle", "A strange stone slab in the cavern", "you see just this angry burning dragon", "Your sense of physicality is stretched", "this enormous, tawny gold mastiff", "faces twisted in a horrible expression", "a lovely little shop", "terrible, terrible costs", "a tiny ruby-like circle", "the vast rooftops", "this wonderful countryside"). `StockTells` keeps its list: no body tell appeared in either DM's narration.
- **Anaphora is legal.** `listOpener` loses its You/Your branch: "You arrive… You arrive… You see…" repetition is the exemplars' camera rhythm, not a list.
- **Cues.** A Cue is a short spoken line (one to three sentences) between `==` marks on a DM-side line right after its trigger (a check result, a question, a gambit, a turn); its trigger, rules terms and DCs stay outside the marks. `src/vault/parse.ts` collects them, the `style` layer reads them through the `Narration.*` token rules, the `narration` layer runs the full callout analysis on each (`narration/cue-length` guards the length), and Obsidian highlights them through `wiki/.obsidian/snippets/callouts.css`. A Cue is Narration (CONTEXT.md).
- **Style blend by moment.** Brennan's register for social, political, arrival, NPC and lore-on-ask moments (Hooks, Developments, NPC First look and lines, Settlement and Region Arrivals, Factions, Lore); Mercer's for spatial and tactical moments (Site Entering, Creature First sight, Cliffhangers, Climaxes, Encounters, combat Cues, Vehicles). Endings choose Brennan's mid-effect cliff or Mercer's decompression coda by what the Session's last Scene needs.
- **Corpus.** The voice envelope is rebuilt from Matthew Mercer and Brennan Lee Mulligan transcripts only: 90 passages per GM, both studied episodes always included. ADR 0025's three-GM, 500-passage floor becomes 180 passages over 2 GMs.
