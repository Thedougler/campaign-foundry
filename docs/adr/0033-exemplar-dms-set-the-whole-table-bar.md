# Exemplar DMs set the whole-table bar

Extends [ADR 0029](0029-exemplar-dms-set-the-narration-bar.md).

ADR 0029 studied the DM side of two actual-play episodes at sentence level. Issue #51 reread both transcripts in full ("To the Hounds!", Critical Role Campaign 4 Episode 9, and "The Stowaway", Campaign 2 Episode 45), Player turns as well as GM turns, with sponsor reads and announcements left out. The evidence is `docs/research/creative-writing/expert-dm-actual-play.md`.

Decision:

- **Scope.** The exemplar bar now covers the whole table across the authoring chain: Session pacing, Scene construction and transitions, revelation and tension, consequences, agency handovers, NPC motive and voice, locations, action clarity, humour, contrast and recurring details, improvisation, and resolution and continuity.
- **One owner per rule.** Each dimension's rule lives once, in the owner file the analysis's owner map names. Every other file points there instead of restating it.
- **Live craft stays live.** Techniques only the room can carry (reading the mood, timing a cut on a laugh, yes-anding a strange plan) are never written into Prep as obligations. Prep supplies what they draw on: handles, bench lines, Cues and planted details.
- **ADR 0029 stands.** Its Narration rules, Exemplar and Slots lines are unchanged.
