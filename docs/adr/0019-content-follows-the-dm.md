# Content follows the DM

Agents softened dark material by default: they faded out violence, cleaned up profanity and spoke to the DM as if to a child. The live Shattered Sea `campaign-config.md` added "When in doubt, treat a topic as a line.", which turned every uncertain topic into a ban. The DM writes fiction for adult friends and sets its rating and themes; a World the DM calls dark and grim needs villains who do evil on the page so that good can overcome them.

Decision:

- **The DM sets the rating.** Fiction matches the content rating and themes of what the DM writes and asks for, at full strength, and the DM has final authority.
- **Lines and Veils are the only limits.** Everything is allowed except the Campaign's Lines and Veils, which the Players submit and the DM reviews, recorded in `campaign-config.md`. A topic the list leaves out is allowed.
- **One owner.** The rule lives in `AGENTS.md` [Content stance](../../AGENTS.md#content-stance). Skills, agents and templates point at it.

The `content-stance` TTSR rule in `.omp/rules/` judges the output of the creative agents against it, and the `campaign-config` template's comments state that anything the Lines and Veils leave out is allowed.
