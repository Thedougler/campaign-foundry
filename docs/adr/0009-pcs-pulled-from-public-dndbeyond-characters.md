# PC sheets are pulled from public D&D Beyond characters

Players keep their characters on D&D Beyond. Each PC page stores its D&D Beyond character link, and when the DM asks, Ingest refreshes the sheet side of every PC page (level, stats, spells, inventory) while the story side (backstory, hooks, the DM's plans) stays as written. Characters must be set to public on D&D Beyond; we chose that over signing in with the DM's account because it needs no credentials and doesn't break when a session expires.
