# No approval gates: Canon is decided by precedence, not by review

The Agent never asks the DM to approve drafts, confirm edits the DM already made, or bless generated content. Canon follows a fixed precedence: what the DM says, then what the Wiki says, then Raw material being ingested, where it doesn't conflict with the Wiki. The DM's edits to the Wiki are taken as meant and built on. We rejected a Draft/Canon review workflow because a DM prepping a home game won't sit through approval queues, and an assistant that keeps asking costs more attention than it saves.
