# No approval gates: Canon is decided by precedence, not by review

The Agent never asks the DM to approve drafts, confirm edits the DM already made, or bless generated content. Canon follows a fixed precedence: what the DM says (to the Agent, or at the table as a Transcript records it), then what the Wiki says, then Raw material being ingested. Raw that contradicts the Wiki loses, and the Agent notes the dropped contradiction in the Ingest log instead of asking. Events are not contradictions: a Transcript in which the party kills an NPC moves the Wiki forward. We rejected a Draft/Canon review workflow because a DM prepping a home game won't sit through approval queues, and an assistant that keeps asking costs more attention than it saves.
