# Collab is a 1:1 workshop

Amends [ADR 0018](0018-story-first-then-adapt.md), whose closing line made `collab-with-me` load a stage owner for each idea.

On 2026-10-12 the DM reviewed `collab-with-me` and found it wrong in five ways. Its machinery (per-idea stages, a ledger and the filing chain) took attention away from the conversation. Its strict yes-and never stress-tested an idea. It drew none of his own ideas out, its pitches read as generic, and it had no clear rhythm of going wide and then narrowing. The Seeds that `docs/agents/co-writing.md` sent to parallel `creative-writer` dispatches, one per Stance, showed how machinery replaced the conversation. Five past workshopping sessions, mined in [`workshopping.md`](../research/creative-writing/workshopping.md), back the verdict. He brainstorms "loosely and collaboratively turn by turn", in "not an intensive grilling interview". The agent "should add to the conversation, not simply mirror back". His best ideas came off specific, well-connected pitches, and he twice stopped a fan-out of narrow agents mid-session.

Decision:

- **Conversation over machinery.** The conversation is the product. Replies are short, shaped like conversation and sized to the live idea, and the DM steers. Notes, lookups and filing run silently or in the background. `collab-with-me` keeps the same name and entrypoint, still invoked by the DM alone.
- **Co-writer and honest critic.** The agent pitches concrete, Canon-grounded material, with two to four options that differ in kind when going wide, and stress-tests an idea when narrowing. It draws the DM out by building, with at most one question per reply woven into a pitch. It points out a weak spot, its cost and a fix unprompted, in a line, whenever the weakness would show at the table, and gives full notes when he asks. It holds a craft judgement under pushback until he gives a reason, and then builds his version when he prefers it (Kim & Khashabi 2025 on sycophantic reversals).
- **Seeds and Stances are dropped.** The agent pitches contrasting options inline. `creative-writer` writes prose only.
- **Subagents serve the conversation from the background.** The agent dispatches each subagent, keeps talking and folds the result in when it reports. A fresh critic subagent is especially useful: it reads an idea cold, without the goodwill built up in the conversation. The agent dispatches one when the DM asks for a critique or a harder read, or when an idea needs stress-testing before lock-in. The agent then relays the critic's notes to the DM as talk and adds its own take on each. Lore sweeps, prose drafts and the filing chain run the same way.
- **Handoffs, loaded lazily.** Filing on "lock it in" (raw, Ingest, audit, Lint), the Story Bible with its Story, Adaptation and critique references, `simulate-npcs`, `story-arc` and `plan-session` load only when the DM's words call for them.
- **One shared method.** `docs/agents/co-writing.md` is the workshop method that `collab-with-me`, `plan-session`, `new-campaign` and `story-arc` use.

ADR 0018's Story-first, Adaptation, Persona and session-local decisions stand. Its closing line, "loads each stage's owner", gives way to the lazy handoffs above. The skill states each practice as the behaviour to follow, per ADR 0036, and [`docs/creative-process.md`](../creative-process.md) maps the handoffs.
