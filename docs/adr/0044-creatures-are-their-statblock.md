# Creatures are their statblock

Amends [ADR 0040](0040-templates-are-minimal-by-default.md) and [ADR 0041](0041-items-are-their-properties.md).

ADR 0040 made templates minimal, and ADR 0041 made an Item its `## Properties`. The Creature template still asked for more than the statblock. A World-free Creature kept `## Links` beside it, and its frontmatter gave agents nothing to sort a bestiary by. The DM wants Creatures handled the way Items and Spells now are. A 5etools monster is a complete generic Creature, and 1,575 of them arrive as one bestiary JSON (`raw/bestiary-sublist-data.json`).

Decision:

- **The statblock is the page.** A published monster's Creature page is its frontmatter plus `## Statblock`, which contains one Fantasy Statblocks fence (`layout: Basic 5e Layout`), as a complete 5etools monster is. Every other section is optional and added when its condition holds.
- **Statblocks are optional, and each page carries its own.** A Creature is a creature, monster or other sentient thing that isn't an NPC, and an NPC is a specific person the DM plays. Either kind has a `## Statblock` only when it fights or its rules matter at the table. The statblock of an NPC goes under the NPC page's own `## Statblock`. The NPC template's `creature` property, which pointed at a Creature page for the person's statistics, is retired, and a Creature is no longer the rules behind a person.
- **One skill writes the mechanics.** `statblock-design` writes every statblock, on a Creature page, an NPC page or in a reply, and absorbs `fantasy-statblock`. `creature-design` and `npc-design` write the being and hand off to it only when their subject needs combat statistics.
- **Sort keys.** Creature frontmatter carries `cr`, `creature_type`, `size`, `habitat` and `sourcebooks`, so agents and Obsidian sort and filter Creatures. `cr` is a number, `0.25` for 1/4, and `""` when the statblock has none. `creature_type` is the statblock `type` capitalised, and `size` the statblock `size`. `habitat` lists 2024 habitat names (`Underdark`, `Planar (Abyss)`). `sourcebooks` lists each published book's full name, and stays `[]` for homebrew.
- **Published fields.** The fence takes the `Basic 5e Layout` fields plus `modifier` (the initiative bonus), `mythic_description`, `mythic_actions`, `lair_actions`, `regional_effects` and `gear` when the Creature has them. A statblock with no `cr`, such as a summoned spirit's, is legal.
- **`## Links` is optional in every template.** The gate reads a section as optional when its guidance comment opens `Optional.`, and every template's `## Links` comment now does. No template, skill or gate keeps an exception for it.

Consequences: `cfcli convert-creatures` turns a 5etools bestiary JSON into one World-free page per monster at `wiki/Creatures/<slug>.md`, frontmatter and `## Statblock` alone, the same output for the same input. The batch itself is never gated. The run that later touches a converted page sends its findings to a `linter` ([ADR 0045](0045-linters-make-every-lint-repair.md)), as ADR 0040 migrates pages on touch. Existing pages keep any optional parts they already have, `## Links` included, and existing Creature pages gain the sort keys when an agent touches them. Converters stop copying `## Links`, so `cfcli convert-items` ends an Item page after its Properties. A World-free Item is its frontmatter and `## Properties`, which replaces ADR 0041's "and `## Links`".

An NPC page that still has a `creature` property uses a retired layout (`docs/wiki-layout.md` **Retired shapes**). The gate reports it, and the agent that touches the page copies the linked Creature's statblock, or the person's own version of it, under the NPC's `## Statblock`, then deletes the property. The linked Creature page stays, and played records keep their embeds of it. New Encounters embed the statblock of whichever page contains it, Creature or NPC, and push builds a Foundry actor from any page with a statblock.
