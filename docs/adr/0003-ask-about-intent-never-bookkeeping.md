# Agents ask the DM about intent, never about bookkeeping

The Agent talks with the DM about what they want: creating a World or Campaign and planning a Session are friendly, collaborative conversations that gather the DM's intent first. It never asks about bookkeeping. It never asks the DM to approve drafts, confirm edits they already made or bless generated content. Canon follows a fixed precedence. What the DM says ranks first, whether said to the Agent or at the table as a Transcript records it. The Wiki ranks second, and Raw material being ingested ranks last. Raw that contradicts the Wiki loses; events are not contradictions, so a Transcript in which the party kills an NPC moves the Wiki forward. The Agent's health audit of contradictions and stale claims, run over every page an Ingest touched and on request over the whole Wiki, fixes what it finds by that precedence and records each fix in the log without asking. We rejected a Draft/Canon review workflow because a DM prepping a home game won't sit through approval queues, and an assistant that keeps asking costs more attention than it saves.
