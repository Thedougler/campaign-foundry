## At a glance

- **The turn.** The Party reads the toll ledger and weighs two accounts of the same road.
- **Changes.** The Party chooses between Oswyn Arlott's quiet skim and Sela Marren's survey, and both want the Party's backing before dusk.
- **Where.** [[Stonewatch Tollhouse]]
- **Who.** [[Oswyn Arlott|Oswyn]] and [[Sela Marren|Sela]]

> [!narration] Opening
> You arrive at the gate. You arrive, and it's shut. You see the north road behind you, and you see that it runs north for more than fifty miles. The gatehouse wall stands twenty feet high, and a brass rod hangs about twelve feet up across its face, holding a lamp cage with one cold flame in it. You feel the cold of the stone through your gloves when you lean your hand against the bars. What looks to be a crystal hangs on a thong beside the bell rope, and through the bars you could swear the stars over the valley have shifted since you made camp. Cold iron. Wet hinge rust. A bell with its clapper tied back with cord. You know that half the watch stood in this yard at this hour, and not one of them stands here now. You wait at the bars, feeling the wind come off the kilns. It dusts your sleeves with ash.

## Play

### Situation

An hour before dusk, a bar goes across the gate from the inside. The passage beyond it runs twenty feet and ends in the yard. Its ceiling is a twenty-foot span, and the lamp rod sits twelve feet up, twice your own height. The ledger is chained open to that bench. The surveyor's barge is at the toll bank two hours after dusk, and both of them know it.

### Handles

- [[Oswyn Arlott|Oswyn]] wants his winter pay before the surveyor's visit. He offers the warm bench, the gate opened at dusk and his silence about where the Party slept. He holds back the second key to the coin box, and his tell is the thumb he rests over his belt when the box is named.
- [[Sela Marren|Sela]] wants the surveyor to read the ledger from the first page. She offers the ferry barge free for a season and a bed above the tollhouse. She holds back a signed page of her own from spring, and her tell is the chain she winds around her fingers when spring is named.

### Checks

- **Read the toll ledger.** Intelligence (History) DC 13. Failure: ==The toll has always been double. You remember a neat clerk's hand filling the winter page, and the memory is as good as a signature.== Pass: ==The columns run true until the winter page, where a different hand takes over and the sums go quiet.== Critical: ==Here is what you would know. The later entries are in a second clerk's hand, and that hand is Oswyn's own, entering no coin on the nights wagons come down from the north.==
- **Move the lamp cage aside.** Dexterity (Acrobatics) DC 12. Failure: ==The stool tips and the cage rings against the rod, and both faces at the bench turn to you.== Pass: ==The cage comes free of the rod, and a second key waits on a nail behind it.==

## Depth

The turn hides a theft. Oswyn fears the workhouse list, and Sela wants the whole book read.

### Hidden truths

- Oswyn copied the winter columns himself and entered nothing for the northbound night wagons.
- Sela's own spring page took a bribe from the kilns, so she wants the whole book read.
