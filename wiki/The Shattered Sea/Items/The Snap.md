## At a glance

- **Kind.** Armour (shield), uncommon.
- **Rarity.** Uncommon.
- **Attunement.** Required.
- **Changes.** Unfurls as a +2 AC shield without occupying a hand.
- **Held by.** [[Perrin Black-Jaw]].

> [!narration] First look
> Folded flat, bronze veins bloom across pleats no needle made. It wraps your wrist as a bracer. A flick of the wrist snaps it into a hard circle, then a closed hand folds it soft again.

## Play

### Properties

As a Bonus Action, unfurl the bracer into a shield granting the standard +2 AC without occupying the wearer's hand. That hand can hold an instrument, spellcasting focus, components or tool, but not a second weapon or two-handed weapon. Fold it back as a Bonus Action. While unfurled, shield-keyed effects treat the wearer as holding a shield; it does not stack with another shield.

### In use

The Snap grants no bonus beyond a mundane shield and never unfurls as a reaction. Folded, it works as a hand drum only as flavour.

## Depth

### History

[[Prospero Morsani]] sold it from [[The Cabinet of Morsani]] for 150 gold in Session 8. Perrin, [[Catarina Da'Virelli|Catarina]] and [[Crissdalynn Khinriss|Crissdalynn]] named it for its sound. Morsani claimed an inventor source; Catarina read that he found it by means not exactly legal.

### Hidden truths

The originator is unknown, and the grave-robbery suspicion may identify a prior owner if investigated. Its hand-free shield design was chosen for Perrin's bodhran tactics.
