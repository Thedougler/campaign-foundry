## At a glance

- **Kind.** Plot item, instrument of Umberlee.
- **Rarity.** Artifact.
- **Attunement.** - **Changes.** Collects souls from anyone who drowns near it without activation.
- **Held by.** [[Umberlee]], though it lies in the [[Drowned Maw]].

> [!narration] First look
> A fist-sized black pearl shifts with an iridescent sheen and never stays still. Anyone who senses death feels it before seeing it.

## Play

### Properties

The Pearl works continuously and collects souls from anyone who drowns near it. A soul inside cannot escape by normal means. It is one of seven instruments of Umberlee. The others are missing. No charges, saves or attunement rules are established.

### In use

The Pearl's signal crosses the planar boundary to the Elemental Plane of Water, drawing the [[Leviathan]] and waking [[Auralis]]. Recovering it stops the signal. Leaving it in place draws something larger to the fissure. Who accesses its souls remains an unresolved choice.

## Depth

### History

[[Delmar Fisk]] stole it from a Waveservant shrine under Chain Council direction. Umberlee sank his five ships in anger. The wreck and Pearl lie at the Red Lady site in the eastern Shelfworks. The five captains remain bound to Delmar while their crews are trapped inside.

### Hidden truths

The [[Tessarine Concordat]] claimed it wanted to protect the Pearl but sought control and to keep it from Aldric Drave. Khlysty / the Flock sees it as a route to transcendence. These motives emerge from mail trails and faction records.
