## At a glance

- **Kind.** Consumable.
- **Rarity.** Rare.
- **Attunement.** - **Changes.** Eating it grants Resistance to all damage for 1 minute.
- **Held by.** [[Delmar Fisk]] held four before he ate one in the last fight. [[Perrin Black-Jaw|Perrin]] refused the vine's fruit.

> [!narration] First look
> A stonepear hangs like a heavy pear with fitted grey and moss-green stone scales. Its chipped plates scrape like pumice against your palm, and it weighs twice what its size promises.

## Play

### Properties

As a Bonus Action, eat the fruit to gain Resistance to all damage for 1 minute. The DM counted it among the fruits that take a Bonus Action to eat, with the [[Rotheart]] and the [[Redheart Berry]].

### In use

Under [[Taking on Aruhe|Aruhe's law]], fallen fruit is safe to take, but fruit picked from its stem is a claim and the fallen fruit keeps until eaten. Delmar ate his as a Bonus Action in the last fight against [[Talon Skarn]], and the fight's damage halved against him for its minute. He rose out of the blood-spray that had marked his position as he went.

## Depth

### History

The stonepear grows in [[The Quiet]], [[Old Gardens]] and [[The Pantry]]. The Party first saw one at River Slack Basin in Session 11 and did not eat it. Delmar turned one up fallen at the river camp after Skarn fled and pocketed it, and he held four when the Party found the survivors' skylight. At the road camp a living stone pear hung on the vine beside the guava of Perrin's vision; the Party had passed the living fruit by as unsafe to take, and Perrin refused it with the guava.

### Hidden truths

Auralis's Session 12 “CONSUME” command offered Perrin the living stone pear beside the guava, meant to arm him for Talon Skarn, but Auralis did not know that picking living fruit creates an Aruhe claim. Perrin refused both fruits, and whether a picked pear draws the island's claim remains untried.
