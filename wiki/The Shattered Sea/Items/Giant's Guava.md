## At a glance

- **Kind.** Consumable.
- **Rarity.** Rare.
- **Attunement.** - **Changes.** Sets the eater's primary ability score to 25 for 1 hour.
- **Held by.** Fallen fruit is safe. [[Auralis]] urges [[Perrin Black-Jaw]] to eat one.

> [!narration] First look
> A giant's guava is a ribbed fruit the size of a man's head, gold deepening to pink along each rib. Its thick stem bows the branch, and its sweet scent carries a musky edge.

## Play

### Properties

Eating it takes a Bonus Action. For 1 hour the eater's primary ability score becomes 25: the first ability named by its class, or Charisma for a creature with no class. Rolls, save DCs and attack bonuses using that score change with it. For Perrin, Charisma becomes 25, spell save DC 18 and spell attack +10.

### In use

Picking a guava from its stem is a claim of [[Taking on Aruhe]]. Fallen fruit can be taken freely and keeps until eaten.

## Depth

### History

Giant's guavas grow in [[Old Gardens]], [[Memorial Grove]] and the great vine at [[The Pantry]]. Auralis's Session 12 “CONSUME” command directs Perrin to one.

### Hidden truths

Auralis wants Perrin at full strength for Skarn's approach but does not understand Aruhe's law. The Party discovers the cost when the fruit is picked or when responders react.
