## At a glance

- **Kind.** Wondrous item, artifact.
- **Rarity.** Artifact.
- **Attunement.** Required by a creature that knowingly risked its life to save another.
- **Changes.** Lets the holder watch one known creature and lend it Advantage.
- **Held by.** [[Crissdalynn Khinriss]].

> [!narration] First look
> A four-sided quartz top no bigger than a thumb stays cool in a warm hand. Spun in sunlight, it throws amber, blue and violet pools across the surface and ticks like glass on glass long after it should stop.

## Play

### Properties

Once per day, a Magic action spins the top and chooses one personally known creature on the same plane. For 30 minutes the attuned holder sees and hears through that creature without Concentration. While the vision lasts, the holder rolls a d20 whenever the target makes an ability check or saving throw; the target uses it as a second die and has Advantage.

### In use

The Spinner cannot speak through the vision, aid a fight the holder is in or affect the holder's own rolls. One vision only; another cannot open until dawn. A target leaving the plane ends it. Hiding it from a search is Sleight of Hand DC 15. Two Spinners spun face to face shatter; ordinary force cannot damage one.

## Depth

### History

[[Master Kyzil]] gave this Spinner to Crissdalynn before her pilgrimage. It bonded when she risked her life to save [[Delmar Fisk]] in the *Red Lady* wreck. Several Spinners exist; [[Talon Vantyrus]] carries one.

### Hidden truths

The Spinner descends from Sentinel practices older than non-intervention and is tied to Soul Incarnate documents. Countless hunts its carriers for Vantyrus's transformation technique. The Party can learn this at [[High Eyrie]] or from Kyzil.
