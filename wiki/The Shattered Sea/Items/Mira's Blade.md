## Depth

### History

[[Barnaby Rook]] kept the blade in his cabin aboard the HCS Surety ([[Uncertainty]]), beside a pendant marked for Mira, the [[Letters of Marque]] and a crate of flintlocks with twenty vials of Grung tincture. When the Party took the cutter in [[Session 2 - Recap|Session 2]] and Rook fell into the water without resurfacing, [[Perrin Black-Jaw]] took the blade.

### Hidden truths

Who Mira was, and why a blade with her name and a pendant marked for her rode in a Crown captain's cabin, is nowhere recorded.
