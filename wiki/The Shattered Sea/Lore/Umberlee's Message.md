## At a glance

- **The truth.** [[Umberlee]]'s summons to Delmar Fisk, delivered through [[Umberlee - Branca|Branca]] at the [[Waveservant Shrine]] in Calveno: bring the [[Pearl of Souls]] up from the Red Lady wreck and back to a shrine of hers, and he keeps his life. His counter-offer waits on her word.
- **Who knows it.** Delmar, the Party members present at the night communion, Branca, and the clergy her report reached. Harbour gossip says someone who stole from Umberlee is in the city, without a name.
- **Limits.** Her words set the Red Lady wreck as the destination and left the route, the date and the commissioners' fate open.
- **Reaches play through.** Shrine appointments in Calveno, the deep water's reach for Delmar, and [[Bring the Pearl of Souls to Umberlee]].

> [!narration] As it is told
> Somebody robbed the Bitch Queen, the harbour gossip says, and she means to have it back. What she took offence at stays her business. The water has been calling for a man, and the shrine keeps an appointment open that will not close.

## Play

- **Players notice.** Branca keeping an appointment open across weeks, harbour talk that agrees on the theft and stops at the name, and the way deep water keeps finding Delmar.
- **Clues.** A Waveservant who died at the shrine and walked again the same night tells the demonstration's size without a word of theology.
- **Accounts.** The clergy tell it as a communion and a grace. The harbour tells it as a theft and a chase. Delmar tells as little of it as he can.

## Depth

### The terms

At the night communion, Umberlee possessed Branca over the kneeling pool and gave the demand: retrieve the Pearl of Souls from the Red Lady wreck in the [[Drowned Maw]] and return it to a shrine of hers. The first price was a swift death for Delmar and the surrender of the souls he carries. He tried to lie his way through. She read him freely, and the revised terms left him his life while her claim on the souls stood. Delmar countered with the people who commissioned the theft. Her answer deferred the rest: "Bring me the pearl. We will talk then."

### What ignoring costs

The water calls to Delmar harder each time he touches deep seawater, and the appointment gets rescheduled. Umberlee does not chase a debtor. The shrine reschedules, and Branca waits without sleeping until he comes.
