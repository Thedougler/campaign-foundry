## Story

> [!narration] Portrait
> Jean-Claude is a small Grung with electric-blue skin marked by black patches and old scars, large gold eyes, and a long dark moustache called Corto di Velo. He wears a red beret over a dark hood and scarf, a ragged cloak, and a harness of pouches, with a bow always close.

### Backstory

Born blue caste among the Grung of Botukuri, Jean-Claude fled after Pell died in reprisal. His red beret marks him as censured, and his family still hunts him through Simone's elite unit. He stowed aboard the Saltwright one island ahead of them.

## Goals and bonds

- **Goal.** Stay ahead of Simone's hunters and keep other people from being chained or abused.
- **Bond.** The crew that gave him passage is now his chosen family. Crissdalynn is both an ally and an uncomfortable reminder of his fear of birds.
- **Fear.** Birds, exposure under the Mortis curse, and being returned to the Grung hierarchy.

## Plans

- [[Simone's Hunters]] advances whenever Simone gains his trail or a safe port becomes compromised.
- Use darkness, scouting, and his knowledge of Grung authority marks to protect fugitives and expose the captive pipeline.
