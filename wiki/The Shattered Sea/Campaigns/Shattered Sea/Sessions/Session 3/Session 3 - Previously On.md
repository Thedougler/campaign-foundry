> [!narration] Previously on
> The Crown cutter came under your control after its boarding crew went down. Perrin was named a Friend of the Passage, and word came that Nona Black-Jaw was hunting her missing grandson. One of you answered a Truth Stone and admitted that former kin were hunting him. Below deck, you found Ket, a Moucheron kept thin in a brass cage. You fed him and opened the cage, and he flew for Murrat. The ship's cook offered freedom at the next port and told you what he knew before the prize crew took shape. Six new hands came aboard, and Perrin told them he was a Black-Jaw. Then a tropical storm caught the cutter. Something struck the hull from below, once, twice, and a third time, and the ship lurched hard to port.
