> [!narration] Previously on
> You returned beneath Calveno with the artificer from the canal workshop. Her cannon and owl took positions around the powder stores. In one chamber, a defender laid down his arms and you passed through. At the still, Bazzoth used his finest remaining drink in a bomb and touched it off. Water swallowed him, two workers, his still, and the stores, leaving another powder cache gone. That left the primary chamber. A red figure knelt inside a chalk circle, chanting while four amphibious guards held the approach. One guard already lay against a wall. The air smelt of powder and river water, and every step brought the chant nearer. By the time you reached them, the circle still shone. Three guards remained between you and the ongoing ritual.
