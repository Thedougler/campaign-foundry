> [!narration] Previously on
> Barnaby Rook faced you without the sailors who had followed him. At Beaumont Sel's signal, Bisou entered through one gun opening and spoiled the powder. Its charge failed when Delmar Fisk fired through the other opening, and Ensign Wouters fell. Crissdalynn Khinriss bent Rook's first shot away and caught the next on her bracers. A chair from Delmar's hands knocked him from the rigging, and the black water closed over him as something eel-like rose nearby. Mira's Blade became Perrin Black-Jaw's prize. Beaumont told Perrin that the Passage counted him as a Friend. Jean-Claude Tabarnack held the Truth Stone and answered about the kin hunting him. Ket waited below in a barred brass cage. You fed him the ensign and unlatched it, sending him towards Murrat. In the galley, Grigori offered terms, and you chose a prize crew. Rain then came sideways. Three blows rose from beneath the hull. The Surety leaned hard to port.
