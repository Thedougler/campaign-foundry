## At a glance

- **Party at.** Aboard *Uncertainty* off Aruhe, kept moving rather than anchoring near the hungry island.
- **Big moments.** Grigori named Talon Vantyrus as the sender of the follower ship, the ship was crippled, and two survivors were rescued.
- **Left open.** More survivors remained inland on Aruhe, while the crown squid stayed near the treeline. [[Session 10 - Previously On]] records the lead-in.

## What happened

During the night crossing, Delmar trained Geoffrey Draves on deck. Shepherd Grigori appeared below deck, thanked the Party for warning Calveno, and said Talon Vantyrus had sent a ship one or two hours behind them to seize the Fate Spinner. He tied the hunt to the Sentinels of the Eyrie schism over fate and asked the Party to injure Corbin Knighton without killing or breaking him. The Party gave no firm answer. Grigori also raised Delmar's curse from Umberlee.

The follower ship caught up the next day. The Party slowed it with a broadside, set its sails on fire, and left it listing without boarding. At Aruhe, between one hundred and one hundred fifty people had come ashore from the raid ships. Most were dead, and the island had killed those who took its fruit. Fishing offshore appeared safer, while Grung survivors were hunted hardest. The Party rescued two people, then searched the terraces. Blood-marked fruit hung there beside at least six bodies, and wolfrabbits moved through the grass. Killing a spore plant drew up a crown squid, which struck Delmar and reached for the Party before illusion and flight broke contact.
