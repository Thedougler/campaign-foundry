## What happened

After Cobb's refit, *Uncertainty* left La Vasca for Sparhold, the first stop on the Grung fleet's trail. Nona's spell caster had taken Karath's destination from a captured purple Grung. A brief vision placed the Party inside a purple Grung garrison on Aruhe, where wolfrabbits hunted them and no rescue came.

Crissdalynn flew ahead over wreckage and found three sun-blasted Calveno captives on a shattered hull. An eastern hurricane had split the raid fleet in half. Two days out, HCS Ordinance intercepted *Uncertainty* under Dravosi Crown colours. Corbin Knighton crossed first, with Aleksander Malone aboard and hunting Shepherd Grigori. The Party played merchant, hid Perrin, Ruma Delacroix, and Jean-Claude below, and Delmar persuaded Knighton to sail for Calveno. The Party warned Nona by sending stone, then continued towards Sparhold with the captives aboard.
