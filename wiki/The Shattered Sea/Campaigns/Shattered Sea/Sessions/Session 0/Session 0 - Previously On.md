> The Shattered Sea had already taken its price. After a fleet went down, two days afloat left Crissdalynn Khinriss and Delmar Fisk needing a ship, and Beaumont Sel gave them berths on the Saltwright. From Keth-Naar, Perrin Black-Jaw joined them with a family obligation he had not explained. Jean-Claude Tabarnack joined at the last island, four months after fleeing the Grung who wanted him back. Five ships had vanished above the Drowned Maw while carrying the stolen Pearl, and Umberlee had answered. Something had crossed the seal. The Saltwright was on its final run towards Calveno when a Crown vessel closed in. You were all aboard as Barnaby Rook raised its inspection signal and began checking the ship's holds and papers.
