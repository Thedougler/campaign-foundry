## At a glance

- **Role at the table.** [[Catarina Da'Virelli]]'s clockwork owl. It scouts ahead and brings her word of what it sees.
- **Threat.** CR 0. It never fights a round. It helps one of the Party when a fight comes to it.
- **Tell.** None are recorded.
- **Weak to.** One hit ends it, and the Owl stat block gives it just 1 hit point.
- **Used by.** [[Catarina Da'Virelli]].

> [!narration] First sight
> A clockwork owl comes down the tunnel ahead of you on quiet wings and hangs in the air, looking you over. Then it is gone through a grate the way it came.

## Play

It scouts ahead through the tunnels and brings Catarina word of what it sees. It helps one of the Party when a fight comes to it, and its talons are a last resort.

## Depth

### History

On the push into the [[Calveno Sewer Magazines]], Strix came from Catarina's canal workshop with [[Ragnito]] and joined the local defence.

### Hidden truths

The Session 6 run guide calls Strix a clockwork owl run on the Owl stat block. This page reuses the 2024 SRD Owl unchanged. The size, numbers and rules are the published ones, and the clockwork body is the World's dressing on them.
