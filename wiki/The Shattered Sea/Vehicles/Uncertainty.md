## At a glance

- **Kind.** Ship. Captured Crown cutter.
- **Size.** Gargantuan, roughly 80 feet.
- **Speed.** 70 miles a day since the Calveno refit.
- **Crew.** Named complement includes [[Geoffrey Draves]], [[Sem Holst]], [[Old Faas]], [[Thunk]], [[Noor]] and [[Ruma Delacroix]]. [[Alys Kuiper]] served as its surgeon until the Calveno shore leave. Minimum four.
- **Captain.** No captain is established.
- **Berth.** [[La Vasca]], Calveno during refit. Currently kept offshore near Aruhe.

> [!narration] First sight
> a short distance of cutter, low and lean, runs blue-black from cap rail to copper. Gun muzzles sit along the side above an iron-edged ram, while a hardwood woman walks forward at the bow. From half a mile she reads as somebody's coastal trader.

### Crew and stations

Current names are [[Geoffrey Draves]], [[Sem Holst]], [[Old Faas]], [[Thunk]], [[Noor]] and [[Ruma Delacroix]]. Geoffrey is the carpenter under [[Sem Holst]], with Crown-era training that can fill a marine posting when needed. Thunk is gunner and field smith. [[Noor]] cooked before the refit, and the refit gave the galley to [[Ruma Delacroix]], who works as the cook and keeps the books, freeing Perrin of the ledgers. Eating well aboard grants temporary hit points at each long rest under the [[Shipboard Travel]] rule, and time aboard counts as a long rest. [[Alys Kuiper]] served as surgeon until she left the ship at the Calveno shore leave. The complement runs on a minimum of four, and empty-station consequences are not established. Current passengers include two Aruhe survivors and three Calveno captives.

### Components and weapons

The captured Crown identity and refit scars are established. The Calveno refit gave her a workshop, cargo capacity of twenty-five tons and a shallower four-foot draft that hides her silhouette, plus four more guns, though the Party cannot run all of them at once. A long blade fixed to the prow, the icebreaker, strikes first with its modified figurehead. The old captain's quarters are a break room now, with a large chair, a coffee table, snacks and terrible paintings. [[Catarina Da'Virelli]] keeps a crafting workshop below, and her spider-like construct Ragnito stands refitted as a cannon mount on deck, a deterrent whose controls are hers alone. Helm, movement controls, full weapon entries and component consequences are not established. Thunk has placed a thousand gold pieces of cannon on the ship's credit.

### Underway

Keep the ship moving off Aruhe instead of anchoring close. Merchant cover matters during Crown approaches. The Party can expose, disguise, protect or abandon the old HCS identity. Boarding and interception are possible, but ramming, initiative and sinking procedures are not established.

## Depth

### History

The crew took the HCS Surety during the Saltwright boarding and later renamed it Uncertainty. In the boarding, [[Bisou]] fouled the powder through a gun port and [[Delmar Fisk]] shot [[Ensign Wouters]] through the other, while [[Barnaby Rook]] went into the water twice and did not return. The Party kept the cutter as a prize under her Crown marks and had not yet chosen her new name, and the prize crew was named at the day's end. Her cabins yielded 45 gold, the [[Letters of Marque]] and a blunderbuss, and another 110 gold with two garnets from under the floor. One crate contained a pendant marked for Mira and [[Mira's Blade]], sealed together, and a second crate contained flintlocks with [[Grung Toxin Vials|twenty vials of Grung tincture]]. Two hours out a tropical storm found her, and something below struck the hull three times, the third deliberate, before she lurched hard to port. In a gap in the squall the Crown Isle of [[Aldenmere]] stood out to the northwest, and then the weather closed. The crew killed the sixty-foot [[Whip Shark|whip-shark]] that had struck her. Its scales and [[Jean-Claude Tabarnack|Jean-Claude]]'s Mending patched the hull over five days at sea, and she took the name Uncertainty on the approach channel to [[Calven and Calveno|Calveno]]. She then went into the [[La Vasca]] cradle under the Black-Jaw colours, password "garden". The refit cradle is at [[Calven and Calveno|Calveno]]'s La Vasca. Earlier Crown ownership is certain. Current ownership follows the crew's capture.

#### Session 9: out of the cradle and out to sea

The refit ended and the [[Uncertainty]] put out from Calveno on the season's first southerly, her paint still curing. On the tour [[Geoffrey Draves]] led, the refit ruling set her numbers: seventy miles a day and cargo space of twenty-five tons. The work brought a shallower four-foot draft that hides her silhouette and a minimum crew of four. It added four more guns, though the Party cannot run all of them at once. The new prow blade showed itself as the icebreaker.

[[Ruma Delacroix]] joined the complement as bookkeeper and cook. Travel-role passes held the ship's course on both days of the run, and Delmar's ginger drinks gave everyone who drank them advantage on seasickness saves for the next three and a half days. The [[HCS Ordinance]] took the ship for an inspection on the second day and turned for Calveno afterward, the Party having let her go. [[Catarina Da'Virelli]] set up a crafting workshop below and refitted her construct Ragnito as a cannon mount on deck.
