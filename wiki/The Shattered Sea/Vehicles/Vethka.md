## At a glance

- **Kind.** Grung raiding proa, wrecked.
- **Size.** Not established beyond an outrigger hull.
- **Speed.** None. She will not put to sea again.
- **Crew.** Grung raiders and Calveno captives when she sailed. [[Sandro]] and [[Nino]] shelter in her hull now.
- **Captain.** The raid's command went unnamed.
- **Berth.** Broken above the tideline on [[Western Landing]]'s beach, [[Aruhe]].

> [!narration] First sight
> A dark proa hull lies on its side above the tideline, outrigger snapped and ribs hung with wet cloth. Smoke threads up from a signal fire at her bow, and water jars stand in what shade the hull still throws. Sandro and Nino keep to that shade, and fresh footprints run between the fire and the tideline.

### Crew and stations

The raid's Grung crew drowned with her or died ashore within hours of landfall, taken by the island's corrupted fauna. Her living complement is the two captives who came ashore, Sandro and Nino.

### Components and weapons

The snapped outrigger lies beside her. Water jars, a fire ring and whatever the raid left make up her fit.

### Underway

She sails no more. Her work now is shelter, and while she holds, the beach keeps its one defensible place.

## Depth

### History

#### Session 9: torn open two days out of Calveno

The storm tore her hull open on the reef two days out of [[Calven and Calveno|Calveno]], and she lay wedged against the coral. Her captives huddled beneath the broken hull, and two purple Grung guards came ashore with them and held the beach against the island's [[Wolfrabbit|wolfrabbits]]. The tide erased the drag marks across the sand.

The storm of the Calveno raid put the Vethka proas on Aruhe's reef and beach line. This hull came to rest above the tideline with her outrigger gone, and three captives rode a broken outrigger strut ashore. Sandro, Nino, and [[Tomo]] reached the beach, and the first night took Tomo. The other two have held her shade since. Their signal fire is visible from the reef gap.

### Hidden truths

- The hull is failing around her squatters. When she goes, the beach loses its last shelter and the castaways lose their signal fire.
- She marks the raid's far end for [[Nona Black-Jaw]], who is looking for a dark hull.
