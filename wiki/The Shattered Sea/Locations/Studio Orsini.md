## At a glance

- **Draws the Party because.** The studio pays coin for eggs, curiosities, and monster parts that other shops will not touch.
- **Entrance.** A studio door on the Le Paludi canal walk, a street over from [[Casa Lupo]].
- **Occupants.** [[Marta Orsini]].
- **Danger.** None recorded.
- **Prize.** Coin for the strange things a crew cannot price itself.

> [!narration] Entering
> The streets outside fill with festival preparations, and inside, the studio buys what the sea gives up. Bring in what you have carried from it, and hear a price.

## Play

### Occupants

[[Marta Orsini]] runs the studio, prices what is brought to her, and buys.

### Likely actions

Sell eggs or curiosities, ask what the studio is currently buying, or hear which other Le Paludi traders she does not deal with.

## Depth

### History

The [[Casa Lupo]] shopkeeper sent egg and curiosity inquiries here. [[Jean-Claude Tabarnack]] brought three fertilised [[Whip-Shark (Creature)|whip-shark]] eggs in during the festival preparations and told [[Marta Orsini]] he had fertilised the egg himself, "the infant will have multiple extra muscles". She bought all three at 75 gp each, 225 gp the set, and the proceeds were meant for the fine moustache Jean-Claude meant to buy.

### Hidden truths

What Marta does with the eggs once bought is not recorded. Pressing the question at the studio, or watching the studio's door on later days, is how the Party could learn it.
