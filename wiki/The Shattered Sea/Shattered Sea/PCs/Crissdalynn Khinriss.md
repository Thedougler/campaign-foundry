## Story

> [!narration] Portrait
> Crissdalynn is a tall aarakocra with layered cobalt-blue feathers, a black hooked beak, and bright blue eyes. She wears geometric leather armour over a light shirt. Rolled charts fill her satchel. Her dark talons catch the light, and she moves loosely until a fight starts.

### Backstory

Banished from High Eyrie at fourteen, Crissdalynn joined Delmar Fisk's crew aboard the Red Lady. When the fleet sank, she pulled Delmar from the crow's nest. That act bound the Fate Spinner to her. Master Kyzil is her mentor, and she remains a pilgrim of the Sentinels of the Eyrie. In the fight for the Surety she turned Rook's first shot aside with wind and caught the second on her bracers, and later stood between Ket and Perrin's blade and let the Moucheron feed from her arm. When the whip-shark struck, she caught Perrin and Jean-Claude both as they fell, then flew out and lifted the sixty-foot shark clear of the sea. Rook's chart archive showed her he had broken Imperial rules about the Drowned Maw twice and did not record what he found. In [[Le Paludi]] her master [[Master Kyzil]] found her, and she broke down in the street.

## Goals and bonds

- **Goal.** Complete her pilgrimage and map the sea beyond the established charts.
- **Bond.** Delmar is her burden and her found family. She will pull him out of danger again.
- **Fear.** Losing control of the sky, or failing to protect the crew when flight and speed are their only advantage.

## Plans

- The Fate Spinner and its connection to Talon Vantyrus draw the Party towards the Sentinel schism.
- Use aerial movement and cartographic knowledge to make the Drowned Maw and its routes legible.
