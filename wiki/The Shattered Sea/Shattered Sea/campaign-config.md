## Tone

Dark heroic fantasy. Heroism costs. Victories are real and they leave a mark.

## Themes

- Rebellion against authority.
- Grey morality. Every side has dirt on it.
- Freedom from slavery, whether chains, debt, papers, or a patron's claim.
- Power corrupts. Those who hold it, and those who seize it.

## Lines and Veils

A line never appears in any form: not shown, referenced, implied or carried as backstory or consequence. A veil may happen in the fiction, but the telling cuts away before the act. One sentence acknowledges it. When in doubt, treat a topic as a line.

- **Line: self-harm.** No depiction, implication or consequence of self-inflicted harm.
- **Veil: hanging.** An execution or death by hanging may be named as an outcome. Cut before the act and never describe it.
- **Veil: sex.** It may be implied or acknowledged as having happened. It is never depicted, described or played out in scene.
