## Depth

### Origin

The Crown's inspection is ordinary naval law made urgent by a ship carrying fugitives, survivors, and a captain whose history is not in the papers. The Party's presence turns a routine stop into the Campaign's first commitment. [[Aleksander Malone]] is the Crown's harsher answer when inspection becomes judgement.

### Session 9

- Three hours into the second day's run for Sparhold, [[HCS Ordinance]], a man-of-war about twice the [[Uncertainty]]'s length, used a shoal as cover and turned onto an intercept under Dravosi colours.
- [[Delmar Fisk|Delmar]] ran up friendly signals and reversed his coat to Dravosi red with gold trim, while the ship herself flew a plain merchant flag, the captured Dravosi colours having been torn up. The Party agreed to stay outside gun range and run if the signal failed, [[Jean-Claude Tabarnack|Jean-Claude]] first: "We should not engage. We should not fight it."
- The Ordinance came alongside and its young officer demanded the captain for an inspection. Delmar's cover story made her a merchantman in [[Iacopo Fieschi|Factor Fieschi]]'s employ, "our maiden voyage", staged with an invented handmaid, and his second lie held that the ship "was originally the Assured", her logs swapped and her ledgers sloppy.
- [[Aleksander Malone]] commanded Delmar to confess at DC 17, and Delmar failed the save. The personality trigger brought the cold admiral out, and the inquisitor believed the staged confession at Delmar's Persuasion 15.
- Malone slapped [[Crissdalynn Khinriss|Crissdalynn]]; she caught his hand with Deflect Attacks, and his "Confess now" failed against her natural 20, though he left believing it had worked. Her false confession impressed him.
- [[Catarina Da'Virelli]] set her umbrella to imitate Umberlee's laughter around the ship, and the inquisitor stepped back, unsettled, at her Persuasion 23. Delmar flipped the refused toll coin out over the edge.
- [[Corbin Knighton]] drew a flintlock on Malone: "You heard them. I believe them. Go. We're going to Calveno." The warship departed for Calveno.

### Hidden truths

- The inspection is a pretext for finding contraband and fugitives as much as a safety check. The Party can learn this from the boarding questions and the crew's search pattern.
- Geoffrey Draves is not committed to Rook's side. The Party can turn the moment by offering him a survivable choice.

### Possible endings

The Party took the ship, and Crown attention remains attached to the renamed prize.
