## At a glance

- **Changes.** The 2024 travel rules on group checks and travel roles, and the 2024 long rest rules.
- **Applies to.** Voyages aboard a ship, such as the Party's [[Uncertainty]].
- **In one line.** Eating well aboard grants an extra 1d6 temporary hit points on every long rest, each check takes one roll per PC, travel runs as a group check needing at least four checks, and three passes hold the course.

## Play

**The cook.** A PC on cook duty turns out meals that grant everyone who eats well an extra 1d6 temporary hit points, added on each long rest aboard. The die rolls daily. [[Ruma Delacroix]] works the galley, and her first day's roll gave six temporary hit points to all.

**One roll each.** For each check only one roll counts, so a PC cannot double up cook and quartermaster on the same check.

**The group check.** A day's travel runs as a group check needing a minimum of four checks. Five PCs give one to spare, and the cook stays on passive cook duty while the others roll.

**Roles.** The day's stations are bosun, lookout, helm and navigator. Navigation rewards intelligence, the same turn of mind that suits a bookkeeper or a chemist. The ship stays on course when three of the day's checks pass.

**Aboard is rest.** Time aboard counts as a long rest: spell slots come back and temporary hit points are gained.

## Depth

### Why

A crossing can vanish in a line of narration or drag through empty days. These rules give every PC a station, and the day a routine the table can play. Duty happens by daylight and the cook's die is rolled at the long rest.

### Edge cases

The advantage on seasickness saves that followed Delmar's ginger brew was a played consumable, not part of the standing rule. His brew mechanics stay off this page.
