> [!narration] Previously on
> The festival's second morning brought small business above and violence below. A cannon was charged to your vessel, and a tomato flew back across the square. In the chamber, the artificer's weapon failed. One of you heard the patron's refusal. Two defenders died buying the chanter time. The blind guard fell after a throat strike, and the spell caster's assault on your gunner's mind failed. A pistol tore away his arm before an arrow ended him. With his last breath, he urged the chanter to begin. She slipped free, crossed to the artificer, and spoke one word, “Agni”. Stone tore loose overhead. When the dust cleared, the kneeling figure had become a figure that filled the chamber, and open daylight showed through the chamber roof.
