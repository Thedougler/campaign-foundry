> [!narration] Previously on
> You escaped the great hunter in the sky and pressed inland through Aruhe's terraces, where the island rewarded what had already fallen. Taking fruit from a branch called its hunters. At the river slack, you pulled Matteo Scola away from playful otters and learned the rule keeping him alive. He swallowed a ghost plum, vanished, then came back, while a stonepear promised a minute against every kind of hurt. As a little black flower sent laughter across the water at camp, a voice from the trees invited you closer. Lantern light revealed something had been watching. Jean-Claude would not answer, and Delmar hauled him along on his back. During the night, Talon Skarn dived for the pack holding the Fate Spinner, but Crissdalynn stopped him for a moment. He shook off a spell that should have dropped him and stayed within striking distance when play stopped.
