> [!narration] Previously on
> You escaped the great hunter in the sky and pressed inland through Aruhe's terraces. The island rewarded what had already fallen, but taking fruit from a branch called its hunters. At the river slack you pulled Matteo Scola away from playful otters and heard the rule that kept him alive. He swallowed a ghost plum, vanished, and came back, while a stonepear promised a minute against every kind of hurt. At camp, a little black flower sent laughter over the water. A voice from the trees invited you closer, and lantern light revealed that something had been watching. Jean-Claude would not answer, so Delmar carried him. During the night, Talon Skarn dived for the pack holding the Fate Spinner. Crissdalynn stopped him for a moment. He used one of his legendary saves yet stayed within striking distance when the Session ended.
