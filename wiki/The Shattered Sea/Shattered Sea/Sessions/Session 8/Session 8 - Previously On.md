> Something enormous erupted into Mercatura's market from below, followed by five pale spawn. Feeding on a trapped townsman gave one of them a human cast. Then a warning from the Warren sent neighbours fleeing and fighters forward. Kyzil descended and killed the spawn, but every wound in the brute closed again. Delmar forced a chair from the fleet commander's cabin down its throat and shot its eye. As the body rotted under the silent crowd's gaze, the Party recovered a golden seal of authority, a small fragment of unstable matter and an unopened note. Then a factor recorded your names on Tessarine paper, granting you the title Calveno's Defenders. An evacuation order in his room had been written before the disaster. When Perrin's stone warmed, a family voice summoned you home. More than three hundred people were missing, with dust still hanging over the market.
