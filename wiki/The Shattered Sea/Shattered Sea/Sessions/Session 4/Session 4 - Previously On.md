> [!narration] Previously on
> Perrin kept watch when the thing below returned. A huge form filled his vision and delivered one command, Grow. A whip-shark broke the water. Crissdalynn held it aloft while the crew ended it, Delmar kept the barb, and Jean-Claude claimed three eggs. Five days later, the cutter entered Calveno. Its Imperial prefix came off before La Vasca settled *Uncertainty* into a cradle. The harbour water spoke Delmar's name. Branca sent him towards the shrine of Umberlee, but he declined the summons. Nona learned that Vestra was gone. She called off her attacks, then traded Perrin a sending stone for a favour. Kyzil arrived from the sky before nightfall and said the wind had changed. The shrine remained open, and the crew had not yet paid its price.
