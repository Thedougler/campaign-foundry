> [!narration] Previously on
> The thing beneath Mercatura fell, and the crater became a place where families came to ask after the missing. In the dust, Iacopo Fieschi put your crew under Calveno's protection. Nona took charge of the search, counted more than three hundred fourteen absent men, and sent ships along the raiders' wake. You used Fieschi's payment on tools and a cloak, while a scrap and a falcon-featured stranger both carried one troubling name. The name belonged to a monk who had left the Sentinels during their split, but the falcon at the shop was unknown to Crissdalynn. A craft worker finished the last commission, and Cobb nearly finished the refit. Perrin accepted Nona's promised favour. By the time *Uncertainty* could sail, the raiders had distance, the writ was fresh, and your first course was open water.
