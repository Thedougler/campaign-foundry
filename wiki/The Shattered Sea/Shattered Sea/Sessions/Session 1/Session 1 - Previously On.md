> [!narration] Previously on
> You made the Saltwright's hold your ground when the Crown boarders came down. Perrin Black-Jaw gave the bulkhead a doorway that was not there, and Cap'n Gorgeous entered with two deckhands before the three vanished from the fight. Black powder robbed Crissdalynn Khinriss and Delmar Fisk of their hearing. Delmar hurled Crissdalynn onto the deck, where her wings swept sailors aside. Jean-Claude Tabarnack followed Perrin through broken planks and brought down the enforcers. Geoffrey Draves lowered his sword when Rook branded him a turncoat, then stood with you after Delmar offered him a choice. Jean-Claude washed Rook from the gangplank. Crissdalynn blew the alchemist's vapour towards the cutter. Rook reached his deck and fired at Beaumont Sel. The ball skipped from Beaumont's shell. The gap between the hulls remained.
