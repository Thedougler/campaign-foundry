> [!narration] Previously on
> When the Crown boarders descended, you held the Saltwright's hold. Perrin Black-Jaw put an illusory doorway in the bulkhead, drawing two deckhands and Cap'n Gorgeous through it and out of the fight. A black-powder blast deafened Crissdalynn Khinriss and Delmar Fisk. Delmar threw Crissdalynn onto the deck, and her wings swept the sailors aside. Jean-Claude Tabarnack followed Perrin through the broken decking and defeated the enforcers. Geoffrey Draves lowered his sword under Rook's accusation of being a turncoat, then joined you after Delmar offered a choice. Jean-Claude swept Rook off the gangplank with water. As Crissdalynn drove the alchemist's vapour towards the cutter, Rook regained his deck and shot at Beaumont Sel. Beaumont's shell deflected the ball. The ships remained separated.
