- **Big moments.** The crew killed the whip-shark and crossed five days of sea before reaching Calveno, where they reported Vestra's loss to [[Nona Black-Jaw]].
- **Left open.** Nona's favour, Umberlee's waiting shrine, and [[Master Kyzil]]'s arrival.

## What happened

The thing striking the cutter's hull returned during Perrin's watch. A vision placed him inside a sixty-foot body and left him the word “Grow”. The creature surfaced as a whip-shark. Crissdalynn held it clear of the water while the crew killed it. Delmar kept its barb, Jean-Claude took three fertilised eggs, and the scales patched the hull. [[Shepherd Grigori]] passed a red light from his wrist over Jean-Claude, which Perrin could not identify.

The crossing to Calveno took five days. Crissdalynn found two forbidden Drowned Maw crossings in [[Barnaby Rook]]'s chart archive, with no account of what he had seen. A pale, lit outline held the cutter's course. Delmar entered Admiral Fisk and remained there until Crissdalynn brought him back. Jean-Claude repaired the hull, with Sem Holst fixing what the spell missed.

The main approach was too busy for Crown colours. The crew removed the prefix and brought the prize in as *[[Uncertainty]]*. La Vasca placed her in a cradle in Le Paludi, with Cobb at the open gate. Water called Delmar's name once. Grigori left for a week or two. Branca stopped Delmar and Crissdalynn and demanded Delmar attend Umberlee's shrine, but he kept walking.

In Nona's kitchen, the crew reported Vestra's loss. Nona called off the attacks already underway, asked Perrin for an unspecified favour, and gave him a sending stone. That afternoon Kyzil found Crissdalynn in Calveno, knocked her back, held her, and left after saying the wind had turned foul.
