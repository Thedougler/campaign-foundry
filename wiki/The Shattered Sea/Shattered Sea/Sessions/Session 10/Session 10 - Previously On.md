> After the refit, *Uncertainty* aimed at Sparhold with three scorched Calveno survivors below. Nona's magic user had extracted Karath from a purple captive. The raiders' destination was known. The sea then showed you a few breaths through a purple Grung's eyes as Wolfrabbits rushed the garrison on Aruhe and no one came to help. Crissdalynn crossed the wreckage and returned with three more people clinging to a broken hull. A hurricane had broken the raid fleet apart. A Crown warship found you a couple of days from Calveno. Corbin Knighton came aboard to question the merchants, while Aleksander Malone pursued Shepherd Grigori from the other deck. Perrin, Ruma, and Jean-Claude stayed below as Delmar made the lie hold. With your story aboard, the warship headed back towards Calveno. You sent Nona a warning by stone, then kept the rescued people aboard and sailed towards Sparhold.
