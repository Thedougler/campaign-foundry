> Last time the sea tried you in another skin. You began as a purple Grung wrecked on Aruhe, and wolfrabbits ran the last swim down. Back aboard your own ship, Jean-Claude's first watch ran so clean that dolphins came with the dark. Crissdalynn flew down to drifting wreckage and hauled three drowning men out. She caught one over the waves when terror sent him jumping. Aleksander Malone came alongside, Tyr's own Hound, wanting a confession out of you. Delmar's cold admiral voice confessed a murder in full, and the inquisitor believed every word. Crissdalynn fed him a sham story of lifting that shark, and Catarina's umbrella filled the deck with Umberlee's laughter. Corbin Knighton raised a flintlock against his own inquisitor and steered the ninety-six guns towards Calveno. When Catarina priced Lamarae's Fang at one axolotl, Perrin toasted the sword with a groan of a joke about the price. Ahead, the broken wall of the Midchain climbed over the horizon.
