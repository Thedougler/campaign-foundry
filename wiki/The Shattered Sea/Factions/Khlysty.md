## At a glance

- **Goal.** Ascension beyond what a Hierarch can be, powered by heirs healed into unwitting blood-bonds.
- **Next move.** Grow the flock through cures nobody asks the price of, and keep [[Aleksander Malone]] a step behind.
- **Led by.** [[Shepherd Grigori]], Korabl of the Flock.
- **Base.** Nowhere and everywhere: threaded through Dravosi and Tessarine noble courts.
- **Strength.** Healing that leaves a dormant thread in every patient, and noble houses that owe it their children.

> [!narration] Public face
> Nobody meets the Flock outright. A warm man with cold hands saves a life nobody else could save, and the house he saved stops asking questions. His name arrives later in a heresy file, or in a whisper between factors, and by then the debt is yours. People who speak of the flock at all call it the Flock.

## Play

- **When met.** So far the Party has met the Flock only as [[Shepherd Grigori]]'s warmth, and as the Crown's confirmed-heresy file.
- **When opposed.** [[Aleksander Malone]] hunts its infiltration with scripture-cadenced certainty. The Flock's work hides behind the gratitude of healed houses.
- **Offers.** Cures for afflictions past medicine, asked of no one and paid to no one.
- **Costs.** A dormant blood-thread in the patient, and the question nobody in a saved house asks.
- **How to notice or interfere.** A DC 22 Arcana check detects a thread. Counterspell disrupts the healing and the binding together.

## Depth

### History

The Khlysty is a secret mystical flock threaded through Dravosi and Tessarine noble courts. Its shepherd heals dying heirs and binds them as unwitting phylactery threads, building toward an ascension beyond what a Hierarch can be. Confirmed heresy has [[Aleksander Malone]] hunting the flock with a port and a ship class in hand, though not yet the crew that carried the shepherd to Calveno. Faction records and mail trails place the Flock among those reading the [[Pearl of Souls]] as a route to transcendence.

### Hidden truths

- Grigori's healing anchors a patient's blood to him. A DC 22 Arcana check detects the thread. Counterspell disrupts healing and binding alike.
- The Flock treats the [[Pearl of Souls]] as a route to transcendence. Mail trails and faction records show the interest.

### Threads

The Flock drives the **Growing Flock** front. The hunts in [[The Hound of God]] and [[Grigori and the Crown hunt]] close on it, and [[Bring the Pearl of Souls to Umberlee]] holds what it waits for.
