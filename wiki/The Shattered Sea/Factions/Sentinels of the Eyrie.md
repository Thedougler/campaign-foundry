## At a glance

- **Goal.** Hold the watch and keep the Maw ledgers continuous.
- **Next move.** Continue recording conditions while restricting access to ledgers and founding documents.
- **Led by.** [[Master Kyzil]], senior master.
- **Base.** [[High Eyrie]].
- **Strength.** Two centuries of monastic training and continuous observation since 1295 DR.

> [!narration] Public face
> Aarakocra monks have held High Eyrie for two centuries. They record time, weather, position, water and occurrence at the Drowned The Maw matters, not the reason. Exact movement in peak wind is the training standard. Fairness is not part of it.

## Play

- **When met.** Apprentices train on exposed terraces while record-keepers write the latest Maw observations.
- **When opposed.** The Sentinels close access, preserve the records and refuse to act on interpretation.
- **Offers.** Disciplined observations when their rules permit access to the ledgers.
- **Costs.** Invitations, harnesses, magic or a watched climb. The order will not dive or intervene.
- **How to notice or interfere.** Seek the ledgers or founding documents. Exposing the schism can force a choice between observation and intervention.

## Depth

### History

The Sentinels began the continuous Maw ledger in 1295 DR and predate Dravosi rule. [[Talon Vantyrus]] argued that nonintervention was cowardice and left when Kyzil refused to break with the order, creating the faction. Younger members such as [[Crissdalynn Khinriss]] were not told of the schism.

### Hidden truths

- Founding documents beneath the summit may connect the Maw seal to the Soul Incarnate. Access requires reaching the guarded vault.
- The order's nonintervention doctrine prevents it acting on what it observes. Reading the ledgers alongside a fresh danger makes the cost plain.
- The Sentinels' records do not explain why events occur, even when the cause seems obvious.

### Threads

- [[Rule of Two]] and the faction turn the order's schism inward.
- [[Drowned Maw Awakening]] tests whether observation remains enough.
- The [[Fate Spinner]] descends from practices older than the present rule.
