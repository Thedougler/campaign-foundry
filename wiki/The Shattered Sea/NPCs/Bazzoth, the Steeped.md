## At a glance

- **Role.** Red-caste Grung alchemist and magazine guardian.
- **Wants.** To guard the powder magazine and punish anyone reaching its barrels.
- **Voice.** Heavy with age and reagent spirits. A workbench is more sacred than a life.
- **Found at.** Room 5 of the Calveno Sewer Magazines. Deceased after Session 05.

> [!narration] First look
> An old red-caste Grung stands thick and heavyset behind a bench, bone-plate apron hanging over reagent vials. He grips a drinking gourd as if the next swallow might buy another decade.

## Play

- **Opens them up.** Respect for the bench and a precise question about a concoction.
- **Shuts them down.** Weapons near his stock or anyone using his powder barrels.
- **Will share.** The danger of his reagents and the cost of crossing his workspace.
- **Will not share.** The recipe for his age-burning rite.
- **If pressed.** He uses poison and teleporting reek, then the Sump-Reek Bomb. The barrels die with him.

## Depth

### History

Bazzoth worked Room 5 with two Grung labourers. His age-burning rite made him faster and harder to reach. He died in Session 05 after using his Sump-Reek Bomb, destroying the room's powder barrels.

### Hidden truths

- His reagent-spirit has pickled fear and doubt out of him. He is immune to his own concoctions and never surprised while conscious.
- His age-burning rite is arcane transmutation and concentration. Breaking it ends the rite.

### Threads

He was an operative of **Simone's Hunters** and the Grung Clans' sewer magazine operation.
