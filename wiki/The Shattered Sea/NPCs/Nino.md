## At a glance

- **Role.** Calveno captive and castaway of the [[Vethka]] wreck, sheltering beside [[Sandro]].
- **Wants.** To leave Aruhe without ever crossing the garden line.
- **Found at.** In the [[Vethka]]'s shade on the [[Western Landing]] beach, well back from the tideline.

> [!narration] First look
> A second man keeps to the hull's shade, and his eyes move between your boat and the treeline. When the first man looks to the fruit shade, this one shakes his head and points instead at berries lying loose among the stones.

## Play

- **Opens them up.** A patient guest. He answers watching better than questioning.
- **Shuts them down.** The garden line. Talk of crossing it ends his help.
- **Will share.** Gesture by gesture, the survivors' rule: fallen fruit yes, living branches never.

## Depth

### History

The same raid and the same wreck took him captive toward [[Karath]], and the same outrigger strut carried him ashore. He has stayed at the beach's edge since the island took [[Tomo]], and his caution has kept both castaways unmarked.

### Hidden truths

- His caution marks the island's rule before he can put words on it. Watch him refuse living fruit and the survivors' law shows itself the quiet way.

### Threads

He sits at the start of [[Take on Aruhe]] beside Sandro, under the same rescue half of [[Perrin and Nona]].
