## At a glance

- **Role.** Nona Black-Jaw's personal bodyguard and enforcer.
- **Wants.** To catch every threat before it reaches Nona.
- **Voice.** Always polite and quiet. “Nona asked you a question” can be threat or favour.
- **Found at.** Nona's kitchen and safe houses in Le Paludi and the Warren, Calven and Calveno.

> [!narration] First look
> Behind Nona's right shoulder stands a compact black tabaxi, one hand free while the other passes bread and dried fish. He wears a tailored dark suit, immaculate, and a long pale scar cuts from his left cheekbone to his jaw. Filed claws gleam as his ears track every face in the room. “You should take some. Long day ahead.”

## Play

- **Opens them up.** Calm directness, an offered meal, and anyone who keeps Nona or a protected person safe.
- **Shuts them down.** A threat to Nona, a lie he catches, or a guest who ignores his warning.
- **Will share.** Practical information about Nona's security and safe passage when she authorises it.
- **Will not share.** Weaknesses in Nona's network or anything that makes her easier to reach.
- **If pressed.** He taps cigar ash to mark Nona's patience. When it burns too low, he draws one claw.
- **Combat profile.** AC 16, 78 HP, speed 30 ft. Climb speed 20 ft. CR 5. He uses a rapier and claws, Cunning Step, Cut Clean, Evasion, Uncanny Dodge, and Interpose to protect Nona.

## Depth

### History

Enzo has served as Nona's bodyguard from the Black-Jaw network in Le Paludi for years. He manages social reads while Ruk manages physical reads, and he arranged consolation for the Vestra's surviving crew when Nona ordered it.

### Hidden truths

- The cigar is not affectation. Enzo uses its burn as a visible timer for Nona's patience. The Party can learn this by watching a conversation he controls.
- His calm is trained rather than merciful. Once a guest crosses him twice, violence is easier for him than negotiation.
