## At a glance

- **Role.** [[Talon Vantyrus|Vantyrus]]'s apprentice and flying skirmisher, who took part in the [[Sentinels of the Eyrie]]'s Schism twenty-two years ago.
- **Wants.** [[Crissdalynn Khinriss|Crissdalynn]]'s [[Fate Spinner]] for his master, then his master's death when he is ready.
- **Voice.** Level, clipped, and patient. He names people by what they carry.
- **Found at.** Fighting the Party at their inland camp on [[Aruhe]], invisible but outlined by falling water.

> [!narration] First look
> A peregrine aarakocra drops from the glare, brown wings half open and chains ticking over a black robe. His amber stare fixes on the carrier's straps before he says, “Set the toy on the stone.”

## Play

- **Opens them up.** A clean defeat or talk that treats Vantyrus as beatable.
- **Shuts them down.** Questions about his master's name, threats, or pity.
- **Will share.** He names Vantyrus when he calls for help or offers a meeting in exchange for the Spinner. He says his orders forbid killing Crissdalynn, but leave her companions unprotected.
- **Will not share.** His routes or the reason for the Spinner.
- **If pressed.** He threatens companions to force Crissdalynn to surrender the Spinner. He escaped the river-camp fight severely wounded, ate stolen fruit to vanish, and returned to attack their next camp.

## Depth

### History

Skarn serves Vantyrus and studies every encounter as practice for killing him. Vantyrus once opened Skarn's throat and stopped. Skarn later watched Matteo vanish after eating a ghost plum, then attacked Crissdalynn at the River Slack Basin for the Fate Spinner. The ambush opened at a watch change, his approach quiet enough that both watchers' perception missed it, and his first grab at her pack came despite her grapple. Her staff found him once, and the Stunning Strike he failed cost him one Legendary Resistance to shake off. He was still in her face, empty-handed, when the session's recording stopped.

In [[Session 12 - Recap]], Skarn continued the river-camp attack before dawn, pressing his katana against Crissdalynn's staff as he searched for her necklace. He said he had come only for the toy. Remote watchers helped him through a device that let him counter the disadvantage on his attacks. He pinned [[Matteo Scola]] beneath a talon and stole Matteo's fruit pack, then threatened to kill him unless Crissdalynn surrendered the Spinner. As the Party wounded him, Skarn called out, “Vantyrus, help!” He stunned Crissdalynn and flew away, taking Matteo's hat as he rose. During the pursuit he ate nearly all the stolen fruit and disappeared from sight. He escaped inland without the Spinner. The DM reported that he had 12 of his 314 hit points left.

Skarn returned invisible during the Party's rest at their next camp. His dive broke Crissdalynn's arm, knocked her down and stunned her, but the falling spray from [[Delmar Fisk|Delmar]]'s decanter outlined him. His grab for her necklace failed because the Spinner had been moved into Delmar's bag of holding. He lifted her by the throat and demanded its location. Delmar tried to convince him that [[Jean-Claude Tabarnack|Jean-Claude]] had it, but failed to deceive him. Skarn released Crissdalynn and turned his attention to Delmar. He repeated that his orders were to take the Spinner without killing her. He threatened her companions and offered them a meeting with Vantyrus if they complied. The fight remained unresolved when the recording stopped. Crissdalynn's final strike failed to stun him, but reduced his speed and gave advantage on the next attack against him.

### Hidden truths

- The Rule of Two makes Skarn Vantyrus's eventual executioner. The contest ends only when one kills the other.
- Skarn does not know the Spinner's purpose. If he learns it reaches the Soul Incarnate technique, he will keep it from Vantyrus and come for it himself.

### Threads

He drives the **Rule of Two**, **Drowned Maw Awakening**, and the hunt for Crissdalynn's Fate Spinner.
