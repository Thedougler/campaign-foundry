## At a glance

- **Role.** [[Beaumont Sel]]'s capuchin monkey and delivery hand.
- **Service.** Carries a potion of healing or an alchemical item to a point of his choosing, or soaks an enemy's black powder until it will not fire.
- **Found at.** [[Beaumont Sel]]'s shoulder aboard the [[Saltwright]].

> [!narration] First look
> A small brown capuchin monkey crosses the deck toward you on all fours, bright amber eyes fixed on the stoppered vial hugged to her chest. She swarms up the wide tortle captain and wedges herself against his neck. He says "Bisou" once and she pours away along the rail, up into the rigging and out of sight before your eye can pick the rope she took.

## Play

- **Will share.** Whatever [[Beaumont Sel]] hands her, a potion of healing or an alchemical item, carried where he chooses.
- **If pressed.** He says her name and sends her; she delivers, or she soaks an enemy's exposed black powder so the charge fails.
- **Habits.** She steals with absolute confidence, the pipe from the corner of his mouth most often, and he corrects her with the patience of a man who has lost the argument many times. She has opinions about coin tricks, and the results depend on how good the trick is.

## Depth

### History

She has ridden his left shoulder for the eleven years he has run the Midchain route, her tail looped once around his neck when she settles. When Beaumont needs her still, the shift is immediate and complete.

In Session 2, Beaumont sent her through a gun port of the captured Crown cutter, now the [[Uncertainty]], where she fouled the powder and its charge failed. Before he called her back she had stripped 15 gp of shinies from the cutter's stores and never once looked sorry. He treated it as fair business, and the Party did not dispute it.
