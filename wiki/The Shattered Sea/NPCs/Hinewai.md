## At a glance

- **Role.** Undead guardian bound to Aruhe and the Death Bloom.
- **Wants.** Aruhe untaken, her graves unharmed, and her Calveno kept.
- **Voice.** Low, calm, and old, with long silences between short sentences.
- **Found at.** Memorial Grove. Elsewhere she appears only as the woman in the woods.

> [!narration] First look
> Between a pair of trunks the dark is deeper than it should be, and two orange eyes open at the height of a tall woman's face. Silence stretches until a low voice says, "You ate what my island gave you. You are already mine."

## Play

- **Opens them up.** A Grung without a gold seal who stands before her without the pull toward the graves.
- **Shuts them down.** Taking from the island or threatening fire.
- **Will share.** The truth that the Grung want her graves, and the law of what may be taken.
- **Will not share.** Her history or any reason to trust the Grung.
- **If pressed.** She claims fruit-eaters as hers. Damage to the Death Bloom brings her Blight form.

## Depth

### History

Karath captured Hinewai, an elf arch druid, and worked her in its hatcheries. She escaped with a companion who drowned before reaching Aruhe. She buried him beneath a fruit tree. Then she fused her soul and body to the land and died into it. Her grief became the island's law.

### Hidden truths

- The Death Bloom is her body and place-bound phylactery. Destroying it before killing her prevents her return.
- Every Calveno who ate fallen fruit belongs to her garden, and Aruhe's responders spare them. The Grung curse comes from the old capture and torture.

### Threads

She drives **Taking on Aruhe** and the island's conflict with the Grung.
