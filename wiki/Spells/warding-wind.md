# Warding Wind

*Level 2 Evocation.*

___

- **Casting Time:** Action
- **Range:** Self
- **Components:** V
- **Duration:** Concentration, up to 10 minutes

___

A strong wind (20 miles per hour) blows around you in a 10-foot radius and moves with you, remaining centred on you. The wind lasts for the spell's duration.

The wind has the following effects:

- It deafens you and other creatures in its area.
- It extinguishes unprotected flames in its area that are torch-sized or smaller.
- It hedges out vapour, gas, and fog that can be dispersed by strong wind.
- The area is difficult terrain for creatures other than you.
- The attack rolls of ranged weapon attacks have disadvantage if the attacks pass in or out of the wind.
