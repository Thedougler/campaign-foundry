# Silence

*Level 2 Illusion.*

___

- **Casting Time:** Action or Ritual
- **Range:** 120 feet
- **Components:** V, S
- **Duration:** Concentration, up to 10 minutes

___

For the duration, no sound can be created within or pass through a 20-foot-radius Sphere centred on a point you choose within range. Any creature or object entirely inside the Sphere has Immunity to Thunder damage, and creatures have the Deafened condition while entirely inside it. Casting a spell that includes a Verbal component is impossible there.

**Classes:** Bard, Cleric, Ranger
