# Color Spray

*Level 1 Illusion.*

___

- **Casting Time:** Action
- **Range:** Self
- **Components:** V, S, M (a pinch of colourful sand)
- **Duration:** Instantaneous

___

You launch a dazzling array of flashing, colourful light. Each creature in a 15-foot Cone originating from you must succeed on a Constitution saving throw or have the Blinded condition until the end of your next turn.

**Classes:** Bard, Sorcerer, Wizard
