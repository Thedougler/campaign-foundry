# Lesser Restoration

*Level 2 Abjuration.*

___

- **Casting Time:** Bonus action
- **Range:** Touch
- **Components:** V, S
- **Duration:** Instantaneous

___

You touch a creature and end one condition on it: Blinded, Deafened, Paralysed, or Poisoned.

**Classes:** Artificer, Bard, Cleric, Druid, Paladin, Ranger
