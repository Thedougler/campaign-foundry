# Fog Cloud

*Level 1 Conjuration.*

___

- **Casting Time:** Action
- **Range:** 120 feet
- **Components:** V, S
- **Duration:** Concentration, up to 1 hour

___

You create a 20-foot-radius Sphere of fog centred on a point within range. The Sphere is Heavily Obscured. It lasts for the duration or until a strong wind (such as one created by *Gust of Wind*) disperses it.
***Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot.*** The fog's radius increases by 20 feet for each spell slot level above 1.

**Classes:** Druid, Ranger, Sorcerer, Wizard
