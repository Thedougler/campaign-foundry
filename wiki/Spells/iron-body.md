# Iron Body

*Level 8 Transmutation.*

___

- **Casting Time:** Action
- **Range:** Touch
- **Components:** V, S, M (diamond dust worth 250+ GP, which the spell consumes)
- **Duration:** Concentration, up to 1 hour

___

One willing creature you touch transforms into living metal. Until the spell ends, the target's Exhaustion level can't increase; the target has Resistance to Bludgeoning, Fire, Piercing, and Slashing damage; and it has Immunity to Poison damage and the Paralysed, Petrified, and Poisoned conditions. If the target is Paralysed, Petrified, or Poisoned when you cast the spell, those conditions immediately end for the target.

**Classes:** Sorcerer, Warlock, Wizard
