# Purify Food and Drink

*Level 1 Transmutation.*

___

- **Casting Time:** Action or Ritual
- **Range:** 10 feet
- **Components:** V, S
- **Duration:** Instantaneous

___

You remove poison and rot from nonmagical food and drink in a 5-foot-radius Sphere centred on a point within range.

**Classes:** Artificer, Cleric, Druid, Paladin
