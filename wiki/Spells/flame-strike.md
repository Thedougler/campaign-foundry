# Flame Strike

*Level 5 Evocation.*

___

- **Casting Time:** Action
- **Range:** 60 feet
- **Components:** V, S, M (a pinch of sulphur)
- **Duration:** Instantaneous

___

A vertical column of brilliant fire roars down from above. Each creature in a 10-foot-radius, 40-foot-high Cylinder centred on a point within range makes a Dexterity saving throw, taking 5d6 Fire damage and 5d6 Radiant damage on a failed save or half as much damage on a successful one.
***Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot.*** The Fire damage and the Radiant damage increase by 1d6 for each spell slot level above 5.

**Classes:** Cleric
