# Hold Monster

*Level 5 Enchantment.*

___

- **Casting Time:** Action
- **Range:** 90 feet
- **Components:** V, S, M (a straight piece of iron)
- **Duration:** Concentration, up to 1 minute

___

Choose a creature that you can see within range. The target must succeed on a Wisdom saving throw or have the Paralysed condition for the duration. At the end of each of its turns, the target repeats the save, ending the spell on itself on a success.
***Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot.*** You can target one additional creature for each spell slot level above 5.

**Classes:** Bard, Sorcerer, Warlock, Wizard
