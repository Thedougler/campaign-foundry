## Properties

*Wand, Rare (Requires Attunement By a Spellcaster), 1 lb.*

Evolving Item

While holding this wand, you gain a +1 bonus to spell attack rolls. In addition, you ignore Half Cover when making a spell attack roll.

***Crusading.*** The Cleric's spellcasting skills help the characters defeat a group of fiendish cultists. The DM decides the wand evolves to become Rare, gaining the Crusading property.

***Evolving Item.*** *This item is an Evolving Item, see *Evolving Magic Items* for more information.*
