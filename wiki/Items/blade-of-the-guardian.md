## Play

### Properties

*Weapon (*Greatsword*) (Requires Attunement), Martial Weapon, Melee Weapon, 6 lb.*

2d6 Slashing

Evolving Item, Heavy, Two‑Handed

Mastery:  Graze

In this example, a Paladin finds a treasured *Weapon of Warning* (*Greatsword*), an Uncommon magic item, early in her adventuring career. Each time the weapon evolves, the shield patterns etched into its blade become more intricate.

***Heavy.*** You have Disadvantage on attack rolls with a Heavy weapon if it's a Melee weapon and your Strength score isn't at least 13 or if it's a Ranged weapon and your Dexterity score isn't at least 13.

***Two-Handed.*** A Two-Handed weapon requires two hands when you attack with it.

***Evolving Item.*** *This item is an Evolving Item, see *Evolving Magic Items* for more information.*

***Mastery: Graze.*** If your attack roll with this weapon misses a creature, you can deal damage to that creature equal to the ability modifier you used to make the attack roll. This damage is the same type dealt by the weapon, and the damage can be increased only by increasing the ability modifier.

Multiple variations of this item exist, as listed below:

- *Studious Blade of the Guardian*
- *Resistant Blade of the Guardian*
- *All-Seeing Blade of the Guardian*
