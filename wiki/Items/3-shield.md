## Play

### Properties

*Generic Variant, Very Rare.*

** This generic variant has the same name and source as the item *+3 Shield**.

While holding this Shield, you have a +3 bonus to Armour Class, in addition to the Shield's normal bonus to AC.

***Base items.*** This item variant can be applied to the following base items:

- *Shield* (*+3 Shield*)
