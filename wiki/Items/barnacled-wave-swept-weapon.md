## Play

### Properties

*Generic Variant, Uncommon (Requires Attunement).*

Evolving Item

This weapon is covered in oceanic motifs, from wavelike etchings on its blade to stylized sea monsters embroidered onto its leather hilt.

***Barnacled.*** Barnacles dot this blade's guard and the flat sides of its etched blade.

You have a +1 bonus to attack rolls and damage rolls made with this magic weapon. While holding this weapon, you can take a Magic action to touch up to a gallon of liquid and infuse it with brine, turning fresh water into saltwater, for instance.

***Evolving Item.*** *This item is an Evolving Item, see *Evolving Magic Items* for more information.*

***Base items.*** This item variant can be applied to the following base items:

- *Greatsword* (*Barnacled Wave-Swept Greatsword*)
- *Longsword* (*Barnacled Wave-Swept Longsword*)
- *Rapier* (*Barnacled Wave-Swept Rapier*)
- *Scimitar* (*Barnacled Wave-Swept Scimitar*)
- *Shortsword* (*Barnacled Wave-Swept Shortsword*)
