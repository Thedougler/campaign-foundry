## Play

### Properties

*Wondrous Item, Legendary (Requires Attunement).*

Vestige

A Stormgirdle is a wide belt made of thick leather branded with the symbol of Kord. The girdle's clasps are made from dragon ivory.

***Vestige of Divergence.*** *This item is a Vestige of Divergence, see *Vestiges of Divergence* for more information.*

Multiple variations of this item exist, as listed below:

- *Stormgirdle (Dormant)*
- *Stormgirdle (Awakened)*
- *Stormgirdle (Exalted)*
