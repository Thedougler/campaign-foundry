## Play

### Properties

*Generic Variant, Legendary (Requires Attunement).*

Bound into this armour is a level 6 spell. The spell is determined when the armour is created and must belong to the Abjuration or Illusion school of magic. The armour has 6 charges and regains 1d6 expended charges daily at dawn. While wearing the armour, you can expend 1 charge to cast its spell.

The spell's saving throw DC is 17, and its attack bonus is +9.

***Base items.*** This item variant can be applied to the following base items:

- *Breastplate* (*Enspelled (Level 6) Breastplate*)
- *Chain Mail* (*Enspelled (Level 6) Chain Mail*)
