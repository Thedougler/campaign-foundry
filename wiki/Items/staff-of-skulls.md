## Play

### Properties

*Staff, Weapon (Requires Attunement By a Spellcaster), Simple Weapon, Melee Weapon, 4 lb.*

1d6 Bludgeoning

Evolving Item, Versatile (1d8)

Mastery:  Topple

This *staff* is made of polished, interlocking bones. Runes are carved along its length.

***Versatile.*** A Versatile weapon can be used with one or two hands. A damage value in parentheses appears with the property. The weapon deals that damage when used with two hands to make a melee attack.

***Evolving Item.*** *This item is an Evolving Item, see *Evolving Magic Items* for more information.*

***Mastery: Topple.*** If you hit a creature with this weapon, you can force the creature to make a Constitution saving throw (DC 8 plus the ability modifier used to make the attack roll and your Proficiency Bonus). On a failed save, the creature has the Prone condition.

Multiple variations of this item exist, as listed below:

- *Ominous Staff of Skulls*
- *Chattering Staff of Skulls*
- *Pulverizing Staff of Skulls*
