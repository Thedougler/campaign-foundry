## Play

### Properties

*Rod, Very Rare (Requires Attunement By a Warlock), 2 lb.*

Evolving Item

While holding this rod, you gain a +1 bonus to spell attack rolls and to the saving throw DCs of your Warlock spells.

In addition, you can regain one spell slot as a Magic action while holding the rod. You can't use this property again until you finish a Long Rest.

***Vigilant.*** After discovering a strange tome full of symbols that match those on the rod, the Warlock feels the approval of the entity that fuels their magic. The DM decides the rod evolves and becomes Rare, gaining the Vigilant property.

***Psionic.*** While exploring clues the Warlock deciphered from the strange tome, the characters acquire psionically charged crystals from the Underdark. The crystals bear the same symbols the rod has. The DM decides the rod evolves to become Very Rare, gaining the Psionic property.

***Evolving Item.*** *This item is an Evolving Item, see *Evolving Magic Items* for more information.*
