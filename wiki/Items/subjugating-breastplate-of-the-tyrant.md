## Properties

*Generic Variant, Legendary (Requires Attunement).*

Evolving Item

You have Resistance to Psychic damage while you wear this armour.

***Regal.*** When the characters travel to an ancient battlefield, the DM decides the armour evolves, becoming Very Rare and gaining the Regal property. As the characters explore, they uncover iconography matching that on the armour.

***Subjugating.*** The characters' investigation leads them to an old castle, where they face an ancient, brutal tyrant wearing a tabard with iconography matching this armour. The characters defeat the tyrant thanks to the Fighter's heroics. The DM decides the armour evolves, becoming Legendary and gaining the Subjugating property.

***Evolving Item.*** *This item is an Evolving Item, see *Evolving Magic Items* for more information.*

***Base items.*** This item variant can be applied to the following base items:

- *Breastplate* (*Subjugating Breastplate of the Tyrant*)
