## Play

### Properties

*Generic Variant, Uncommon (Requires Attunement).*

This armour has 5 charges. While you wear it, you can use a bonus action to expend 1 or more charges to cast one of the following spells from the armour, targeting yourself: *Jump* (1 charge) or *Levitate* (2 charges).

This armour regains 1d4 + 1 expended charges daily at dawn.

***Base items.*** This item variant can be applied to the following base items:

- *Breastplate* (*Breastplate of Weightlessness*)
- *Chain Mail* (*Chain Mail of Weightlessness*)

- *Half Plate Armour* (*Half Plate Armour of Weightlessness*)
