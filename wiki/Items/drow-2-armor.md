## Play

### Properties

*Generic Variant.*

You have a +2 bonus to AC while wearing this armour. This armour loses its enchantment bonus permanently if it is exposed to sunlight for 1 hour or longer.

***Base items.*** This item variant can be applied to the following base items:

- *Breastplate* (*Drow +2 Breastplate*)
- *Chain Mail* (*Drow +2 Chain Mail*)
- *Chain Shirt* (*Drow +2 Chain Shirt*)
- *Half Plate Armour* (*Drow +2 Half Plate Armour*)
- *Hide Armour* (*Drow +2 Hide Armour*)
- *Leather Armour* (*Drow +2 Leather Armor*)
- *Padded Armor* (*Drow +2 Padded Armor*)
- *Plate Armor* (*Drow +2 Plate Armor*)
- *Ring Mail* (*Drow +2 Ring Mail*)
