## Properties

*Generic Variant, Legendary (Requires Attunement).*

Evolving Item

As long as this weapon is within your reach and you are attuned to it, you and allies within 30 feet of you gain the following benefits.

***Alarm.*** The weapon magically awakens each subject who is sleeping naturally when combat begins. This benefit doesn't wake a subject from magically induced sleep.

***Supernatural Readiness.*** Each subject has Advantage on its Initiative rolls.

***Studious.*** The DM decides the weapon evolves to a Rare item and gives it the Studious property, since the Paladin has improved her investigative skills to better understand potential threats.

***Resistant.*** When the Paladin defends her companions against infernal forces, the DM decides the weapon evolves again, becoming Very Rare and gaining the Resistant property. The DM chooses Fire as the damage type it grants Resistance to, since that's thematically appropriate.

***All-Seeing.*** The DM decides the weapon evolves one final time, becoming Legendary and gaining the All-Seeing property. The DM decides this represents the Paladin becoming a paragon of watchful protection.

***Evolving Item.*** *This item is an Evolving Item, see *Evolving Magic Items* for more information.*

***Base items.*** This item variant can be applied to the following base items:

- *Greatsword* (*All-Seeing Greatsword of the Guardian*)
