## Play

### Properties

*Wondrous Item, Very Rare.*

This smooth, rainbow-coloured, egg-shaped stone can be thrown up to 30 feet and explodes in a 10-foot-radius sphere of magical energy on impact, destroying the stone. Any active spell of 5th level or lower in the sphere ends.
