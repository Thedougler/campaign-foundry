## Properties

*Wondrous Item, Spellcasting Focus, Uncommon (Requires Attunement By a Druid or Ranger).*

This cloak shifts colour and texture to blend with the terrain surrounding you. While wearing the cloak, you can use it as a Spellcasting Focus for your Druid and Ranger spells.

While you are in an area that is Lightly Obscured, you can Hide as a Bonus Action even if you are being directly observed.
