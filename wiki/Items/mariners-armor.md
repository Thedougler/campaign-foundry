## Play

### Properties

*Generic Variant, Uncommon.*

While wearing this armour, you have a Swim Speed equal to your Speed. In addition, if you start your turn underwater with 0 Hit Points, you immediately regain 1d4 Hit Points. The armour can't heal anyone again until the next dawn.

The armour is decorated with fish and shell motifs.

***Base items.*** This item variant can be applied to the following base items:

- *Breastplate* (*Mariner's Breastplate*)
- *Chain Mail* (*Mariner's Chain Mail*)
- *Chain Shirt* (*Mariner's Chain Shirt*)
- *Half Plate Armour* (*Mariner's Half Plate Armour*)
- *Hide Armour* (*Mariner's Hide Armour*)
- *Leather Armour* (*Mariner's Leather Armor*)
- *Padded Armor* (*Mariner's Padded Armor*)
- *Plate Armor* (*Mariner's Plate Armor*)
- *Ring Mail* (*Mariner's Ring Mail*)
- *Scale Mail* (*Mariner's Scale Mail*)
- *Splint Armor* (*Mariner's Splint Armor*)
- *Studded Leather Armor* (*Mariner's Studded Leather Armor*)
