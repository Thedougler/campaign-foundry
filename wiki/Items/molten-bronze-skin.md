## Properties

*Generic Variant, Rare (Requires Attunement).*

This magical armour appears as a jug of molten bronze. When you attune to it, the bronze adheres and contours to your skin. The armour can be worn under normal clothes, but it doesn't impede bodily functions. Once you put it on, it can't be removed unless you choose to do so.

While wearing the armour, you have resistance to fire damage. The armour also doesn't impose disadvantage on Dexterity (Stealth) checks.
