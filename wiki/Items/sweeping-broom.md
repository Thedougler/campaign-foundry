## Play

### Properties

*Wondrous Item, Common.*

As a Magic action, you can use this unassuming broom's command word, choosing a point you can see on the ground within 60 feet of yourself. The broom animates and sweeps dust and detritus from the ground in a 20-foot square centred on the chosen point. It sweeps that area until you take another Magic action to deactivate it.
