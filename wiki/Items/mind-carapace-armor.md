## Properties

*Generic Variant, Uncommon (Requires Attunement By a Specific Individual).*

Any nonmagical suit of heavy armour can be turned by mind flayers into mind carapace armour. Only one creature can attune to it: either a specific mind flayer or one of its thralls. While worn by any other creature, the mind carapace armour functions as normal armour of its kind. To its intended wearer, the armour grants advantage on Intelligence, Wisdom, and Charisma saving throws and makes its wearer immune to the frightened condition.

***Base items.*** This item variant can be applied to the following base items:

- *Chain Mail* (*Mind Carapace Chain Mail*)
- *Plate Armour* (*Mind Carapace Plate Armour*)
- *Ring Mail* (*Mind Carapace Ring Mail*)
- *Splint Armour* (*Mind Carapace Splint Armour*)
