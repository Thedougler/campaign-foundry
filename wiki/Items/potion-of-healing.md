## Properties

*Potion, Common, 50 GP, ½ lb.*

This potion is a magic item. You can drink or administer the potion as a Bonus Action. Administering it to another creature requires that creature to be within 5 feet of you. The creature that drinks the magical red fluid in this vial regains 2d4 + 2 Hit Points. The potion's red liquid glimmers when agitated.

Rules reference: [SRD 5.2.1](https://media.dndbeyond.com/compendium-images/srd/5.2/SRD_CC_v5.2.1.pdf), “Potion of Healing”, “Potions” and “Potions of Healing”.
