## Properties

*Generic Variant, Rare (Requires Attunement).*

Bound into this armour is a level 2 spell. The spell is determined when the armour is created and must belong to the Abjuration or Illusion school of magic. The armour has 6 charges and regains 1d6 expended charges daily at dawn. While wearing the armour, you can expend 1 charge to cast its spell.

The spell's saving throw DC is 13, and its attack bonus is +5.

***Base items.*** This item variant can be applied to the following base items:

- *Breastplate* (*Enspelled (Level 2) Breastplate*)
- *Chain Mail* (*Enspelled (Level 2) Chain Mail*)
- *Chain Shirt* (*Enspelled (Level 2) Chain Shirt*)
- *Half Plate Armour* (*Enspelled (Level 2) Half Plate Armour*)
- *Hide Armour* (*Enspelled (Level 2) Hide Armour*)
- *Leather Armour* (*Enspelled (Level 2) Leather Armor*)
- *Padded Armor* (*Enspelled (Level 2) Padded Armor*)
- *Plate Armor* (*Enspelled (Level 2) Plate Armor*)
- *Ring Mail* (*Enspelled (Level 2) Ring Mail*)
- *Scale Mail* (*Enspelled (Level 2) Scale Mail*)
- *Splint Armor* (*Enspelled (Level 2) Splint Armor*)
- *Studded Leather Armor* (*Enspelled (Level 2) Studded Leather Armor*)
