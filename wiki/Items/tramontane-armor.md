## Play

### Properties

*Generic Variant, Very Rare (Requires Attunement).*

You gain a +1 bonus to Armour Class while wearing this armour. The armour is enchanted to look like a scholarly or priestly robe.

***Mountain Walker.*** While you're wearing this armour, Difficult Terrain composed of heavy snow, ice, rubble, or undergrowth doesn't cost you extra movement.

***Grasping Tendrils.*** As a Magic action, you can activate the armour, causing the hems of its sleeves and body to extend into ribbonlike tendrils.

As a Bonus Action while the armour is active, you can force each creature of your choice in a 20-foot Emanation originating from you to make a DC 15 Strength saving throw. On a failed save, a creature has the Grappled condition (escape DC 15) and is pulled up to 20 feet straight toward you.

The armor remains active for 1 minute or until you take another Magic action to deactivate it. Once you activate the armor, it can't be activated again until the next dawn.

***Base items.*** This item variant can be applied to the following base items:

- *Breastplate* (*Tramontane Breastplate*)
- *Chain Shirt* (*Tramontane Chain Shirt*)
- *Half Plate Armour* (*Tramontane Half Plate Armour*)
- *Hide Armour* (*Tramontane Hide Armour*)
- *Leather Armor* (*Tramontane Leather Armor*)
- *Padded Armor* (*Tramontane Padded Armor*)
- *Scale Mail* (*Tramontane Scale Mail*)
- *Studded Leather Armor* (*Tramontane Studded Leather Armor*)
