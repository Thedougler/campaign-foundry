## State and angles

- **Driven by.** [[delmar-fisk|Delmar Fisk]], through his own choices since the theft of the [[pearl-of-souls|Pearl of Souls]].
- **Stands at.** Tests, Allies, Enemies, as Sessions 10 to 12 show it.
- **Shows up as.** The crew he trains and shields, the Admiral Fisk persona, and a debt to [[umberlee|Umberlee]] that has sat still since Session 4.
- **Next development.** If nobody acts, Umberlee's claim keeps following the ship from harbour to harbour, as [[bring-the-pearl-of-souls-to-umberlee|Bring the Pearl of Souls to Umberlee]] sets out. Malone's search for [[shepherd-grigori|Shepherd Grigori]] runs on in Calveno, where Delmar sent him.
- **Angles.** Grigori's offer of help with the curse and the [[chain-council|Chain Council]]'s hand behind the Pearl job. The Calveno survivors aboard, and the axolotl he owes [[catarina-davirelli|Catarina Da'Virelli]].
- **Resolves when.** The Campaign ends, or Delmar leaves play.

## Hero's journey

| Stage | Sessions | What the PC did |
| ----- | -------- | --------------- |
| Ordinary World | Backstory, [[session-0-recap\|Session 0]] | He raised a privateer fleet of five ships and stole the Pearl from the shrine at Vel Orn. All five sank over the [[drowned-maw\|Drowned Maw]], and Crissdalynn pulled him from the Red Lady's crow's nest. |
| Call to Adventure | [[session-3-recap\|Session 3]], [[session-4-recap\|Session 4]] | Water called his name at La Vasca. On the way to the harbour he confessed the theft to the Party, and at the shrine Umberlee named the Pearl as her price. |
| Refusal of the Call | [[session-3-recap\|Session 3]] | Branca demanded that he attend Umberlee's shrine, and he kept walking. |
| Meeting the Mentor | [[session-10-recap\|Session 10]], Ledger L889 and L939 to L965 | He tested Grigori with an opposed Insight read and found him truthful. Grigori told him the Pearl job was cursed and contracted through the Chain Council, and Delmar took the scroll with the offer of help. |
| Tests, Allies, Enemies | [[session-1-recap\|Session 1]], [[session-7-recap\|Session 7]], [[session-9-recap\|Session 9]] (Ledger L4780, L6229 to L6245, L6605, L6941), [[session-10-recap\|Session 10]] (Ledger L85 to L93, L2013), [[session-11-recap\|Session 11]] (Ledger L1600 to L1670, L5632), [[session-12-recap\|Session 12]] (Ledger L10141 to L10146, L11736) | He won Geoffrey Draves over to the crew and killed Otar with the Fleet Commander's Chair. He sent the HCS Ordinance towards Calveno on a lie and promised Catarina an axolotl. He trained Geoffrey at the wheel, and he bore the catatonic Jean-Claude on his back across Aruhe. Before the island's keeper he pleaded for Jean-Claude as "a brother-in-arms". |

- **Now.** Tests, Allies, Enemies. In Sessions 10 to 12 he trained Geoffrey and shielded Jean-Claude, and he carried the [[fate-spinner|Fate Spinner]] for Crissdalynn. It tests his bond to the crew against his fear of Umberlee's judgement, since the debt has sat still since Session 4. In Session 9 a cold admiral confessed a planned murder under Malone's spell (Ledger L5195 to L5280). A failed save brought it out, so it marks a pressure on him and no stage. The Approach would show once his own choices move towards the Pearl, Umberlee or the Chain Council.
- **Possibilities.** Each is a possibility for the DM to offer, never a plan for the Player.
  - **A possibility from [[bring-the-pearl-of-souls-to-umberlee|Bring the Pearl of Souls to Umberlee]].** A Waveservant waits for Delmar at the next harbour and speaks the sea's claim where the crew can hear it. The Calveno survivors aboard already blame the hurricane on tribute unpaid to Umberlee (Session 9). Ignored, the claim keeps following the ship.
  - **A possibility from [[grigori-and-the-crown-hunt|Grigori and the Crown hunt]].** Grigori reaches for the Party again with his help for the curse and the unanswered errand against [[corbin-knighton|Corbin Knighton]]. Refused or ignored, Malone's hunt for Grigori runs on without the Party.
  - **A possibility from [[what-sunk-the-vestra|What Sunk the Vestra]].** The Chain Council's hand behind the Pearl job turns up in the [[red-lady|Red Lady]]'s records or the Council's sealed mail. It lies in the same water that took Perrin's family ship. Left alone, the two wrecks keep separate explanations.
- **Crosses.** [[bring-the-pearl-of-souls-to-umberlee|Bring the Pearl of Souls to Umberlee]], [[what-sunk-the-vestra|What Sunk the Vestra]], [[grigori-and-the-crown-hunt|Grigori and the Crown hunt]] and [[the-crown-inspection|The Crown Inspection]]. [[journey-of-perrin-black-jaw|Journey of Perrin Black-Jaw]] meets it in the Maw's water, which took both the Vestra and the fleet. [[journey-of-crissdalynn-khinriss|Journey of Crissdalynn Khinriss]] meets it in her rescue from the crow's nest and the Spinner in his bag. [[journey-of-jean-claude-tabarnack|Journey of Jean-Claude Tabarnack]] meets it in his word to the keeper.
