## State and angles

- **Driven by.** [[jean-claude-tabarnack|Jean-Claude Tabarnack]], through his own choices between a fugitive's silence and his fight against the Grung systems that chain people.
- **Stands at.** Tests, Allies, Enemies, bound since Session 12 to follow the garden's rules and to be brought before its keeper.
- **Shows up as.** His silence whenever Crown poison comes up, and his claims on Grung evidence. The red beret and his fear of birds.
- **Next development.** If nobody acts, the keeper's summons to [[memorial-grove|Memorial Grove]] stands over him, and Simone's hunters follow the captive route toward [[karath|Karath]]. His sister's toxin rides unexamined in the Party's own inventory.
- **Angles.** The seal samples from [[the-burnt-road|The Burnt Road]], the vials from the Surety and the sewers, Delmar's word to the keeper, and the hunters' trail.
- **Resolves when.** The Campaign ends, or Jean-Claude leaves play.

## Hero's journey

| Stage | Sessions | What the PC did |
| ----- | -------- | --------------- |
| Ordinary World | Backstory, [[session-0-recap\|Session 0]] | Born blue caste in [[botukuri\|Botukuri]], he fled after freeing slaves, and [[pell\|Pell]] died in the reprisal. He stowed aboard the Saltwright four months ahead of Simone's hunters. |
| Call to Adventure | [[session-1-recap\|Session 1]], [[session-8-recap\|Session 8]], [[session-9-recap\|Session 9]] (Ledger L5015 to L5050), [[session-10-recap\|Session 10]] (Ledger L2089 to L2113, L3053) | He recognised Simone's toxin in a Crown canister and called the taking of Calveno's men slaving. He asked whether the Ordinance had slaves aboard. He claimed any grung grenade as evidence against Simone and called the Aruhe rescue "our duty". |
| Refusal of the Call | [[session-1-recap\|Session 1]], [[session-2-recap\|Session 2]], [[session-10-recap\|Session 10]], [[session-12-recap\|Session 12]] | He told the Party nothing of the toxin at the gangplank or under the Truth Stone. Through Session 12 he kept his sister's secret to himself. |
| Tests, Allies, Enemies | [[session-1-recap\|Session 1]], [[session-6-recap\|Session 6]], [[session-9-recap\|Session 9]] (Ledger L6180), [[session-10-recap\|Session 10]] (Ledger L3285), [[session-12-recap\|Session 12]] (Ledger L9906 to L9938, L10221 to L10252, L11181) | He threw Rook overboard, and he helped Delmar hold his mind against Ozzeth before finishing him. He proposed flooding the Ordinance's lower decks and led the landing party inland on the survivors' tracks. On the burnt road he read the dead as Gold-caste conscripts and took the seals as evidence. He cursed the keeper to her face, and later watered the burnt ground at Delmar's asking. |

- **Now.** Tests, Allies, Enemies. His Player was absent from Session 11, and Jean-Claude travelled catatonic, so that Session doesn't show a stage for him (Session 11 Ledger, attendance line). It tests whether a fugitive who keeps one secret from his chosen family can expose the system that made him one. The Approach would show once his own choices act on the seal evidence or the canister, or move towards the [[gold-caste|Gold Caste]].
- **Possibilities.** What the world might put before Jean-Claude next, his Player free to take it up or leave it.
  - **A possibility from [[take-on-aruhe|Take on Aruhe]].** The keeper's summons to Memorial Grove stands, with her charge of kidnapper and murderer. Delmar won her word that Jean-Claude lives if he follows her rules, and that anyone may leave her garden willingly. If the Party reaches friendly terms with Hinewai, the Aruhe survivors could leave aboard Nona's ship. Hinewai may cast *Detect Magic* on Jean-Claude. It shows the wisps drifting east. She may share her reading or keep it to herself. Whatever he says there, or keeps back, her judgement goes ahead.
  - **A possibility from [[the-canister|The Canister]].** Twenty Surety vials and six sewer vials ride unexamined in the Party's inventory, and nobody searched the alchemist's bandolier. Any vial opened, counted or questioned puts his sister's work in front of the crew. If the Party brings up the canister in front of Hinewai, she may cast Legend Lore and share what she learns. Left alone, the secret is his alone.
  - **A possibility from [[simones-hunters|Simone's Hunters]].** His seal samples set down the Gold Caste's orders in writing. Grung witnesses rarely give that record, since Gold-caste grung blaze with a radiant light as painful to look at as the sun, and grung avert their eyes for protection as much as respect. Nona, Fieschi and anyone fighting the captive route would want those seals. Ignored, the hunters keep tracing the route toward Karath, half a mile across the channel from Aruhe.
- **Crosses.** [[take-on-aruhe|Take on Aruhe]], [[the-canister|The Canister]], [[simones-hunters|Simone's Hunters]] and the Gold Caste. [[journey-of-delmar-fisk|Journey of Delmar Fisk]] meets it in Delmar's word to the keeper. [[journey-of-crissdalynn-khinriss|Journey of Crissdalynn Khinriss]] meets it in the hunt for Skarn on her back. [[journey-of-perrin-black-jaw|Journey of Perrin Black-Jaw]] meets it in the captive route Nona's network chases.
