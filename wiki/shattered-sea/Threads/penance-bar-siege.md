## State and angles

- **Driven by.** [[corvin-ashe|Corvin Ashe]], Bishop of [[penance-bar|Penance Bar]], whose private incitement protects his public standing. Fear itself drives the faceless mob. Its members have no named rabble-rouser.
- **Stands at.** A frightened village, with ash-smeared doorsteps and tally-stones. A bell tolls at odd hours. Nobody has died. Ashe's lie draws and feeds a demon swarm while he blames the harmless [[sandy-grove-coven|Sandy Grove Coven]].
- **Shows up as.** Ashe contacts the Party directly, or a villager begs for help "before the witches finish it". The Party has not encountered these entry points yet.
- **Next development.** Investigation with [[sula-marn|Sula Marn]] yields evidence of a frame-up and enough reason to suspect Ashe, without the whole truth. The mob then organises and marches on [[sandy-grove|Sandy Grove]], starting the clock. Run the confrontation below before the mob departs, with the Party remaining at the shrine or doubling back on a hunch. They race to warn the village. Once the demon enters [[sorrowbell|Sorrowbell]], the Party sees both the shrine's failure and the danger to the island's last hallowed ground. The remaining task is to get the truth to the crowd before it reaches the grove.
- **Angles.** Compare the true ledger in the shrine's locked quarters with the public tally, hear the coven through [[halda-wren|Halda Wren]], and investigate the village with Marn. After the confrontation, take proof and testimony ahead of the march. If truth arrives late, physically delay the crowd without turning victims into a combat encounter.
- **Resolves when.** The crowd spares the grove or destroys it. Timely truth can avert the attack, and a late intervention can physically delay the mob. Getting the truth there in time is the victory. Do not run the crowd as a combat encounter.

### Locked confrontation

The Party witnesses this live. Preserve the DM's spoken words verbatim as the climax. The dialogue has not been spoken at the table.

Priest
> Are the witches dead? Get back out there, they must die before the sun goes down.

Demon
> The sun is already down.

Priest (surprised)
> You cannot enter the house of Tyr?!

Demon
> Tyr is not here. This is an empty box.

Priest
> Tyr is in all of his temples.

Demon
> Your God's love is not unconditional. He does not love us, and he does not love you.

Priest
> I have done Tyr's bidding, my life's work is in his name.

Demon
> Your life's work makes Tyr puke.

Priest
> I am the Bishop of Penance Bar!

Demon
> Your God knows we wouldn't be here without you, this is all your fault isn't it?

Priest (desperately)
> She was a witch!

Demon (mockingly)
> Lies? In your house of God? No wonder he's abandoned you. But *gestures to the other demons creeping into the temple* We love you.

Demon
> Let me kiss you.

Priest (afraid)
> NO! Get BACK!

The demon leans forward and kisses Ashe on the forehead. At once his body is violently reshaped into one of the devil-touched, his skin blackening where he committed his crimes. The demon's unopposed entry proves the shrine has lost Tyr's protection. The kiss publicly exposes a soul that Tyr no longer contests. [[sin-and-sanctuary|Sin and Sanctuary]] gives the law behind both acts.

## Hidden truths

The terror blamed on outsiders is created and weaponised by Ashe's own lies. Fear is a summoning ritual here: the bishop's sin attracts the demons, and he tries to use the swarm itself to settle his petty score. He cannot hear Tyr's warnings any more. The grove's unnamed archfey still protects it. Burning it would destroy the refuge the whole island needs.

## Possible endings

- **Truth arrives in time.** The mob stalls, breaks or turns on itself in shame. Ashe's fabrication is publicly exposed, the coven's people live, and the village must deal with a demon-touched thing where its priest used to be.
- **Truth arrives late.** The Party must physically slow or stop the crowd. This is hard and ugly. Run the mob as frightened victims, without giving it a statblock or making it a fight to win.
- **The Party fails.** The grove burns. The coven is killed or scattered, and the island loses its last hallowed ground when the swarm is strongest. This is a real loss.
