## At a glance

- **Driven by.** The [[leviathan|Leviathan]] took the [[vestra|Vestra]] on an ordinary fishing day, and [[umberlee|Umberlee]]'s storm took all five of [[delmar-fisk|Delmar Fisk]]'s ships at once. Both disasters share the Maw's water.
- **Stands at.** The theft behind the fleet was arranged through the [[chain-council|Chain Council]]. Whether that arrangement also placed the fleet over the fissure, or drew the Vestra into the Leviathan's path, is unproven.
- **If nobody acts.** The cipher name in the [[red-lady|Red Lady]]'s records is still unread, and the two disasters have separate explanations.
- **Levers.** The [[red-lady|Red Lady]]'s Fleet Intelligence Room, the Council's sealed mail, the [[vestra|Vestra]]'s wreck, and Auralis's account of the rescue.

## Play

- **Shows up as.** Wreck charts of both sites laid side by side, questions Delmar answers around, warm water over the Vestra's rim stretch, and the Leviathan's travelling ring holding to a wreck it has never robbed.
- **Next development.** The Party dives for the Fleet Intelligence Room records, or works out why the Vestra was near the Maw on her last day. Putting the rescue question to Auralis is another way in.
- **Levers.** Deciphering the commission records, the sealed mail between the [[chain-council|Chain Council]] and the [[tessarine-concordat|Tessarine Concordat]], [[perrin-black-jaw|Perrin Black-Jaw]]'s standing with his patron, and Delmar's unread answer.
- **Resolves when.** The design behind both disasters is named at the table, or the evidence settles into separate causes.

## Depth

### Origin

The Vestra sank on an ordinary fishing day. Her hull breached from below, and heat crossed her timbers before the strike. [[perrin-black-jaw|Perrin Black-Jaw]] lived because Auralis found him in the water afterward. The fleet's last voyage began with fog, and a rogue trench opened under the five ships before laughter carried across the water and Umberlee's rage followed it. The storm took all five at once. The sequence reads as a god who expected to reclaim the [[pearl-of-souls|Pearl of Souls]] from the wreck and found it already past her claim.

### Hidden truths

- The [[red-lady|Red Lady]]'s Fleet Intelligence Room holds encoded commission records bearing the contractor's cipher name. A recovery dive puts the room in Party hands, and deciphering names the arrangement.
- The [[leviathan|Leviathan]] knows the [[pearl-of-souls|Pearl of Souls]] lies in that wreck. Its ring crosses the site on every patrol, and a patient boat at the rim sees it leave the Pearl where it rests.
- Auralis's rescue of [[perrin-black-jaw|Perrin Black-Jaw]] was a selection. His reason is his to give, and he speaks one word at a time.
- The case for separate causes stands too. Umberlee had motive enough once the theft surfaced, and the Leviathan may have followed the Pearl's signal the way any hunter follows prey. Powers as great as these can share water without sharing a plan.

### Possible endings

- The records and the sealed mail agree on a design: the Pearl was meant to sink past Umberlee's reach, and the Vestra's loss served that end or escaped its author's notice.
- The sinking that looked like architecture comes apart into weather, appetite and a god's anger, and the thread closes there.
