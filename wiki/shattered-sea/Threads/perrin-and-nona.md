## State and angles

- **Driven by.** Nona Black-Jaw's family bond, the Passage, and Perrin's survival after the Vestra sank.
- **Stands at.** The Party paid Nona's favour with its rescue of the Calveno survivors. She counts it complete, though survivors remain on Aruhe and Karath. Nona sent a Passage ship to support the Party, with her employee [[ruk|Ruk]] as messenger.
- **Shows up as.** Safe houses, coded messages, people Nona has rescued, and questions about what happened to the Vestra.
- **Next development.** The Aruhe survivors may leave the island aboard Nona's ship if the Party makes friendly terms with Hinewai. The remaining survivors must be found on Karath, which may be harder.
- **Angles.** The sending stone, the Passage ship and Ruk's message, Nona's rescue network, and what Perrin shares about the Vestra.
- **Resolves when.** Perrin decides how far to bind the Party to Passage rescue work and what to reveal about the Vestra and Auralis.

## Origin

Perrin washed ashore after the Vestra went down. His grandmother Nona runs the Warren and its Passage rescue network. She sent word through [[cobb|Cobb]], while [[enzo|Enzo]] and [[ruk|Ruk]] protect the family, and expects her grandson to return with news. The Party's stolen or claimed ship gives Perrin a reason to seek her help.

Perrin reached the Saltwright without reporting to Nona. He later reported Vestra's loss, accepted a favour without hearing its terms, and carries Nona's sending stone. Nona protects Felix for one month under Ruk's guard. Felix remains alive under that arrangement.

### Session 9

- At the refit yard, [[perrin-black-jaw|Perrin]] confronted [[thunk|Thunk]] over the money spent on the refit, money the Black-Jaws paid, and sent word to Nona on her sending stone. Her answer: "I know about the money. I figured guns were useful for protecting yourself, but the break room is unnecessary."
- When the [[hcs-ordinance|HCS Ordinance]] broke off for Calveno, [[delmar-fisk|Delmar]] warned [[nona-black-jaw|Nona]] on the sending stone that the Dravosi were coming. She asked which ship it was and whether they meant to occupy the harbour. The Party told her the ship was the [[hcs-ordinance|HCS Ordinance]] and the hunt's target [[shepherd-grigori|Shepherd Grigori]]. [[perrin-black-jaw|Perrin]] added that the Hound of Tyr is named [[aleksander-malone|Aleksander Malone]].

## Hidden truths

- The Passage operates as an independent route and refuge beyond a family business. It formed after colonial pressure. The Party can learn this from Nona's safe houses and rescued witnesses.
- Perrin reported the Vestra's loss to Nona, but the full account remains private from the crew. The missing details surface when the Drowned Maw and the ship's fate intersect.

## Possible endings

Perrin may bind the Party to the Passage or preserve the network while making a clean break. If the family ship is lost, he inherits its obligation without it.
