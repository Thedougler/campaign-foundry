## Backstory

Perrin washed up on Keth-Naar after the Vestra went down and reached the Saltwright's hold. Auralis found him dying in the water and chose to hold him alive, and why he chose Perrin is a question his patron keeps. His grandmother Nona Black-Jaw runs a Passage network, and he has not told the whole crew that he is still chasing the lost family ship. His unknown patron is Auralis, a power tied to the Drowned Maw. After the capture of the Surety he named himself a Black-Jaw before the assembled crew, and Beaumont Sel delivered Nona's message and named him a Friend of the [[passage|Passage]]. During the whip-shark strikes his watch carried him into an abyss vision. He stood on a stone floor scored with channels under drifting light, then wore a sixty-foot barbed-tailed body whose hunting instincts rose in him. Something far larger below turned a curious attention his way, and one word, "Grow", burned into his mind. He met his grandmother in [[le-paludi|Le Paludi]], promised her an unstated favour, and carries her sending stone. His childhood contact [[cobb|Cobb]] runs the [[la-vasca|La Vasca]] dock, and a bioluminescent whale matched the ship's heading for a long while, a thing he had already seen in the vision.

At La Vasca's gate he shook [[cobb|Cobb]]'s hand, said it was all sorted, and bolted.

When [[hcs-ordinance|HCS Ordinance]] boarded the *Uncertainty*, Perrin hid below with the rescued captives, out of the bounty's reach. The Crown's standing [[rattkin-bounty|Rattkin Bounty]] pays 15 gp a head for a rattkin, dead or alive.

Shepherd Grigori's galley visit put Perrin at the prow, then ten feet up the rigging, and his tail stopped dead the moment the shepherd raised the ship's curse. "I'm paying his toll on the ship," he allowed once Delmar's look through the porthole had steadied him. The same visit confirmed his Bardic Inspiration as a d8. For the ambush he urged the Party to keep that inspiration for the saves that would matter and dealt a die to everyone before the fight. His own place in the dark held at stealth 23, and when the turn came he went into the rigging with the knack [[old-faas|Old Faas]] taught him and helped bring the sails in. "Nah, let's be gone, scene," he called across the deck, and the Party left the burning hulk astern.

At [[aruhe|Aruhe]] his perception 24 found the wrecked hulls and the campfire from the rail, with a second look fumbling in at 12. He put the island's rule into words for the Party. A calm forest was safe ground, and in a frenzy the creatures struck at whoever stood there, taking or no. He and Jean-Claude worked a Dexterity check of 26 together and brought the ship to a stop sixty feet off the sand. His counsel kept the anchor stowed. An anchor line would run from the seabed straight to the rail, a direct route for a skull crab. The ship circled the shallows instead. He noted that an injured rabbit triggered the creatures the same as a stolen fruit, and offered Jean-Claude invisibility against an island whose animals would die for the chance to kill a grung. He swam the landing party's crossing slung on Crissdalynn's back, and the landing explained his spare temporary hit point: every long rest aboard the ship granted him one.

The old gardens gave him a six on the travel roll and a large fruit for the picking. "Fresh from Aruhe, guys." His perception 19 caught Jean-Claude from above just before the trees converged, and he held on as Crissdalynn went vertical. When the [[crown-squid|Crown Squid]] rose he spent a spell slot on a *Silent Image* fifteen feet square, a flying humanoid tumbling out of the sky twenty feet to one side. The beast's check to see through it came far lower against his performance 23. On the flight out he offered the same trick a second time and spent both his inspirations on another throw of the performance check. It came up a natural 20, and the beast broke off to hunt the fake "little mouse" while the Party bolted for the ship. The DM gave the beast's contest roll advantage for a trick used twice, and it fell for the image anyway. He closed the day offering ginger for Jean-Claude's churning stomach.

At the road camp his drum slid him into the vision unasked: cold, a pressure on him, salt in the back of his throat, then the abyss, small and swimming, plankton and shrimp drifting past and jaws snapping beneath. "Consume. Consume," his patron said, and the vision set two living fruits in front of him, a gold-painted giant guava about his own size and a stone pear beside it, the fruits the Party had passed as unsafe to take. He rolled a die for the answer and disliked what it gave him. He turned his back on both, choosing the new crew over the old vendetta. He named it character development. The demand came back that night with his stun pinning him in place, "Or die, consume or die," and the fruit stayed on its vine, unpicked.

His work in the dawn fight was sabotage and triage. A *Hex* opened on Skarn's Dexterity checks, and *Tasha's Hideous Laughter* failed him twice at DC 16, the falcon saving both times. The taunt worked better: "Your, your tail feathers look like baby carrots, see?" spent a Cutting Words on the killing dive and turned it into a miss on Crissdalynn, and the stare the raptor fixed on him left him terrified behind a show of nerve. Skarn's wide arc knocked him prone and took 26 slashing. He laid a line of oats out for the birds while the forest burned. His inspiration never stopped moving, a die to Delmar for the rifle shot, one to Crissdalynn before she flew out of range, one to Jean-Claude as they took to the air.

On the road he worked the Party's kindnesses. His Medicine, a natural 20 with Delmar's help, pulled Matteo Scola back from the brink with "Snap out of it," and he spent the walk reassuring the rescued Calveno survivors, taking charge so the chaos stayed calm. Under the smoking skylight he stacked *Armour of Agathys* under *Mirror Image* and stood with the civilians while the Terror-Bird's steps shook the ceiling. At the dusk camp he ate his spare rations against the stress, tried a mouthful of grass, and called it terrible. In the night fight his *Tasha's Hideous Laughter* with a Cutting Words −6 behind it brought the falcon to the edge of its save and no further. His *Healing Word* put 13 into Crissdalynn. Skarn's unarmed strike ended his night at DC 18 Constitution, stunned while the patron's demand arrived.

> [!narration] Portrait
> Perrin is a small rattkin with a blackhead, white body, warm brown ears, large dark eyes, and a long pink tail. He wears an olive-green hooded cloak over an off-white tunic and dark trousers, with a sword, harness, and round shield. His whiskers never stop moving. The shortest member of the crew still fills a room with volume and nerve.

## Goals and bonds

- **Goal.** Survey the sea and find the Vestra while keeping the new crew alive.
- **Bond.** Nona, the Passage, and the Black-Jaw rescue network are family obligations he cannot simply abandon.
- **Fear.** Failing his obligations and losing the crew, then learning what his patron expects him to become.

Auralis's demands on Perrin now run to "Consume or die," and twice on Aruhe he has turned his back on the living fruit.
