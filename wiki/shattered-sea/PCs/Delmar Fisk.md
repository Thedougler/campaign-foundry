## Story

> [!narration] Portrait
> Delmar is a broad, athletic man with sandy-red hair in a messy topknot, a thick beard, and a curled moustache greying at the edges. He wears an open off-white shirt, navy sash, charcoal trousers, and tall boots. His scarlet admiral's coat stays folded away until the night needs Admiral Fisk.

### Backstory

Delmar assembled a five-ship fleet to steal the Pearl of Souls from Umberlee's [[Umberlee's Shrine|shrine]] on [[Vel-Orn]]. The fleet sank over the Drowned Maw, leaving him among the survivors. Crissdalynn pulled him from the [[Red Lady]]'s crow's nest, and he now sails with a new crew under the name Uncertainty. In the fight for the Surety his musket ball through a gun port killed [[Ensign Wouters]], and when the prize parted from the Saltwright he named Admiral Fisk to Beaumont Sel as a friend. Off Calveno he held the Admiral Fisk form too long, until his hair sat wrong, his cadence shifted, and he called Crissdalynn "Crystalline". A pull from her flask brought him back. At the [[La Vasca]] gangplank the water reached up toward him and he thought he heard his name, and then it was gone. He locked the admiral's coat in the captain's chest rather than wear Dravosi red ashore, and an older servant of the Bitch Queen left him her message only when his lie about Umberlee's own errand satisfied her.

The night watch before [[Aruhe]] ran to Delmar's pattern: another finesse session with [[Geoffrey Draves|Geoffrey]], and a quiet push for the man to stop hiding the ballet dream that put him on deck alone after lights out. [[Shepherd Grigori|Grigori]] found the galley that same night, and Delmar poured drinks for everyone from his flask of endless rum before asking, half careful, to see Crissdalynn's "pile of shinies". Grigori brought thanks and a warning: a ship sent by [[Talon Vantyrus]] lay an hour or two astern, and the Pearl job Delmar had taken as an independent contract had a hidden hand behind it. On the Schism question a Luck point turned a natural 1 into a 20, and his Insight, opposed by a 25, still read Grigori as truthful. The parting handshake felt corpse-cold, and his later recall of the touch, a 24 across history and investigation, gave him a dead man's hand. As he saw it, only the undead had hands that cold, and he kept that joke to himself. He took the spell scroll Grigori left, its charge to wound [[Corbin Knighton]] and send word rather than kill. His one question back was how many more Sentinels would come hunting, and he kept the answer open past taking the scroll.

The ambush off the stern ran to Delmar's calls: quiet hand signs woke the crew to the guns, with no whistle to betray the trap. He readied his rifle with explosive rounds, took the Heroic Inspiration Crissdalynn lent him, and put a Steady Aim shot through the pursuer's stern lantern for 31 damage, 17 of it Sneak Attack, calling the save at 16. The ship failed it, eight fire followed the round in, and fire goes through a hull in full, so the burning sail ended the chase. At [[Aruhe]]'s reef he had the sails weighed in and took the wheel himself. On a Dexterity save of 24 against 14, the [[Uncertainty]] threaded the one gap. "This is something you need to learn," he told Geoffrey over the shallows and the wrecked hulls. He spread the wings of his [[Flying Boots]] for the rescue, carried the smaller castaway on his back, and kept the crew's watch on the skull crabs below rather than let the tide nudge the ship ashore. To the parley he called [[Ruma Delacroix|Ruma]] and Mr. Tabarnack up from below, and he brought his own memory as evidence: in his own turn as a purple grung, a thrown spear meant to draw the frenzy had brought the swarm anyway. One note came right in the telling: the red-marked and yellow grenades in his old manifest came off the first ship's Dravosi alchemist, and no grung grenade ever rode in his kit.

In the [[Old Gardens|old gardens]] he flew up to share Crissdalynn's vantage and took the higher of their perception rolls at 22, enough for the wolfrabbit nests in the terrace caves and the wide-grinned watcher beyond them. When the trees closed on Jean-Claude he dropped, took the ranger on his back, and Dashed straight up with a pistol out and [[Lamarae's Fang]]'s hilt in his other fist. The [[Crown Squid]] found them anyway. His poured rum pooled on a bulk the eye kept sliding off, a Luck point bought disadvantage on its first grab, and the hooked tentacle gashed him for 18 slashing all the same. Heroic Inspiration and Perrin's Bardic Inspiration raised his Strength save past DC 19, and both fliers flew out of the tentacle's reach, while Uncanny Dodge halved the next hit's 18 to 9. He flew the Party up and away toward the ship, easy in the thought that the thing would meet cannon fire if it ever swam after them.

[[Talon Skarn]] struck the river camp before dawn, katana at Crissdalynn's chest and one demand in his beak: the Fate Spinner. Delmar came out of his bedroll quiet, a stealth 26 against the falcon's passive perception of 20, and opened the fight with [[Lamarae's Fang]] in place of a dagger, a natural 20 stacking Sneak Attack to 32 damage. A chain-hurled dagger answered through his shoulder for 16 piercing, and Uncanny Dodge cut it to 8. Once the falcon roosted he sheathed the fang, hid behind a tree, and worked the rifle, explosive rounds forcing DC 16 Dexterity saves and setting the roost tree alight, 29 damage on the first shot and 39 on the second. Steady Aim and a Cunning Strike trip burned the falcon's last Legendary Resistance. With Skarn fleeing beyond pistol range, Delmar tried the thrown-pistol trick, pitching a gun skyward to shoot the trigger as it turned. The DM priced the stunt at DC 30, and his rolls came to 23 and 29. Halfway into the pursuit he owned to forgetting his own [[Flying Boots|Winged Boots]]. A luck-boosted natural 20 lifted him off the ground, and his flying shot put 11 damage into Skarn. He landed to hold the camp. The fire his round had set sent the forest's creatures running toward the Party's own camp, and the chase broke off on Crissdalynn's shout.

The day ran to his rhythms. He fished the river for an hour and cooked the trout with slices of [[Giant's Guava]], passed the endless-rum flask around the rescued Calveno survivors, "A drink amongst friends. Here, take the edge off," and poured a measure onto the ground for the garden's dead. On the burnt road he stood between Jean-Claude and [[Hinewai|the woman in the woods]]. A persuasion of 25 brought her guarantee, the plea resting on "he is a brother-in-arms", and her word covered his safety and his life if he followed her garden's rules. His courtesies held through all of it, Mr. Tabarnack, Ms. Khinriss, Mr. Black-Jaw.

At the road camp he built the fire ringed with stones and put the evening's respect into words: "I'm not here to destroy the land." When Skarn returned invisible, the Fate Spinner already rode in his Bag of Holding, Crissdalynn having handed it over that morning before the pursuit. A luck point went into a lie that Jean-Claude held the Spinner, and the falcon's insight 25 made nothing of it. "Mr. Tabarnack, run!" followed before Delmar ate a [[Ghost Plum]] and turned invisible high above the fight. From thirty feet up he dropped the invisible [[Fleet Commanders Chair|Fleet Commander's Chair]] onto the shimmer, 100 pounds for 4d6 plus 5. The chair dealt 26 damage, and Skarn's reaction kept all but 8 of it away. A second luck point held the answer at disadvantage. Later, with blood spray marking his position, he ate the [[Stonepear]] and rose out of his own tell, every kind of damage against him halved for a minute. His explosive rounds stayed careless to the end, singeing Crissdalynn and Perrin for 5 fire each on the way to another 29 into Skarn.

## Goals and bonds

- **Goal.** Keep the crew alive and free while deciding whether he can give Umberlee what she asks for the Pearl.
- **Bond.** The people who sail with him are his found family. He will not tolerate them being called backstabbers or abandoned.
- **Fear.** [[Umberlee]]'s judgement, and the truth of what his ambition cost the five crews.

## Plans

- [[Bring the Pearl of Souls to Umberlee]] is the unresolved debt behind his hidden past.
- Follow Crown law, Passage obligations, and the Drowned Maw while keeping the Party together.
