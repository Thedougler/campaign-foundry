## Truth, Clues and accounts

- **The truth.** Wrongdoing has mass that attracts fiends and gravity that drags a soul down. Each god judges it by its own dogma. A patron's protection persists only while the patron actively maintains its claim. Few people know these laws or their reasons, and a lapse in sanctuary does not visibly alter a place.
- **Players notice.** Before a stronger fiend commits, a pack pushes its weakest member forward and watches what happens to it. Nobody explains this tell to the Party. Cruel places permit fiends to walk in daylight, while mostly clean places force them to skulk at the margins. Skin blackened where crimes occurred identifies someone changed by the kiss.
- **Clues.** Three for each conclusion the Party needs.
  - *Sanctuary can lapse without visible warning.* A demon's unopposed entrance through [[sorrowbell|Sorrowbell]] proves the shrine's failure. The old archfey's surviving claim protects [[sandy-grove|Sandy Grove]]. [[shepherd-grigori|Shepherd Grigori]] understands far more of these laws than most mortals.
  - *Ashe's sin attracted the swarm.* [[corvin-ashe|Corvin Ashe]]'s transformation publicly shows the nature of his soul. The true ledger in his locked quarters contradicts the public tally. The demon identifies Ashe as the cause in the [[penance-bar-siege|Penance Bar Siege]] confrontation.
- **Accounts.** Each holder's version, in the order of how little they know.
  - [[corvin-ashe|Corvin Ashe]] says "Tyr is in all of his temples." False. The god left Sorrowbell, and the demons walking in prove it.
  - The [[gold-caste|Gold Caste]] does not understand why fiends come. Without hallowed ground, its people answer attacks through power and skilled warriors.
  - [[aleksander-malone|Aleksander Malone]] names a place steeped in evil heresy and feels its weight. Partly true. What he feels is [[tyr|Tyr]]'s weighing, by Tyr's own dogma, and he has no formal grasp of it.
  - [[shepherd-grigori|Shepherd Grigori]] has a much more educated understanding, partly learned through bargains and tricks involving demons and devils. He worships no god and prepares to contend with evil without divine assistance.

## The full truth

### Sin has mass and gravity

The scale and density of wrongdoing in a place attracts fiends and determines how boldly they act. In places steeped in real cruelty they walk in daylight. Somewhere mostly clean forces them to skulk at its margins. Sin also exerts gravity on a soul: the further someone sinks into it, the harder climbing back out becomes.

### Every god weighs by its own dogma

No universal list of sins exists. Each god weighs every soul and every place by its own dogma, so one village can weigh light to one god and heavy to another. [[tyr|Tyr]]'s justice tolerates, and even blesses, brutal violence against the guilty, where another god would recoil from the same act.

Every fiend, celestial and god reads a mortal soul's true state at one glance. This perception is immediate and infallible. Mortals cannot lie to these beings, though they can deceive themselves and each other.

### Hallowed ground

A god, demigod or archfey can bless a site. The blessing is an active claim that the patron can revoke at any time. A fiend setting foot on protected ground invites immediate, violent reprisal. The patron destroys its form on this plane and casts its soul home: demons return to the Abyss and devils to the Nine Hells.

A claim lapses with no visible sign. The first fiend to walk in uncontested is the proof.

Fiends know this and test it with care. A pack pushes its weakest member forward first and watches before anyone stronger commits. The Party can learn to read that tell, and nobody will hand them a rule for it.

A patron warns a straying follower before it withdraws protection. The warnings stop only when the follower can no longer hear them. From outside that looks like abandonment, but the god did not go silent first.

### The kiss

A demon's or devil's kiss is a sanctioned public mark for a soul every side already agrees is gone. Even serious sinners remain within reach of their patron's intervention. Bandits can change their lives and robbers can return stolen goods. Murderers can repent and pay for their crimes, with forgiveness possible in time. The kiss is reserved for a soul whose quality makes all such redemption impossible. Its threshold is deliberately nebulous, sensed rather than measured.

A soul still within reach has a patron who steps in to stop the kiss. A kiss that lands unopposed is the god's own confirmation that it is deserved. A soul with no god has no patron to contest it at all.

The kiss immediately and visibly transforms its victim into a demonic version of themselves, one of the devil-touched. This former mortal becomes a demon subservient to its new master, representing its crimes for all eternity. Skin blackens where those crimes occurred. The mark is public by design. On the god's authority, the fiend strips a mortal fully allied with evil of the ability to hide among the innocent.

The fiend recognises its own rather than punishing the mortal. That soul was never going to reach its god's side of the ledger. Only the human body had yet to reflect its condition. "We love you" acknowledges that the soul already belongs to the fiends.
