## At a glance

- **The truth.** Any band may enter, entry is free, and there are no judges. Whichever band holds the densest crowd when the Grande Finale ends at midnight takes the Palio banner and a 300 gp purse.
- **Who knows it.** All of Calveno. A rotating committee of Mercatura merchant families runs the contest and keeps the winners' plaque at its office.
- **Limits.** The crowd votes with its feet all night, so a band that peaks early empties its own stage. Coin buys stages, crews and spectacle, never the crowd itself.
- **Reaches play through.** Registration, recruiting in the taverns, stage crews and crowd work, rival stunts and sabotage, and a public stage before the whole city.

> [!narration] As it is told
> Pick any bridge in Calveno and follow the noise. Across three nights, three stages fight for the city, and the crowd is the only judge the contest has ever needed. Where the crowd stands at midnight, that stage takes the banner.

## Play

- **Players notice.** Stages rise on the Mercatura plaza, on the Velo Quarter bridges and on the [[Le Paludi]] waterfront. Crowds stream between them all evening while crews behind each stage rig lights, powder and props.
- **Clues.** The committee office on the Mercatura takes entries until three days before La Prova and keeps the winners' plaque visible from the street. Whoever holds the crowd at midnight holds the Palio.
- **Accounts.** [[Il Gioco delle Beffe]] shares the festival season. Prospero Morsani has attended every Palio for as long as anyone can remember and usually appears at the winning stage just before midnight, which the crowd has started to read as an omen. Halfling musicians drink at [[The Ponte Bassa]] most evenings, and one fiddle player has spent the week watching the door, waiting to be asked if he is available.

## Depth

### The full truth

Any group that appears at the committee office on the Mercatura three days before La Prova needs only a band name and a home stage preference to register. Bands are assigned a home stage by draw but may busk and work the crowd between sets to pull listeners across the bridges. A band takes the banner through performers, riggers, pyrotechnic handlers, prop work and crowd work together, for music alone does not keep a crowd from dusk to midnight.

Each of the three stages keeps its character from year to year. **Il Palco della Mercatura** stands on the wide plaza at the main canal bridge, the largest capacity and the worst acoustics, drawing merchants, factors and officers who came because it was visible and stayed because they were seen. **Il Palco del Velo** stands across the low bridges where the city stops enforcing its rules, smaller, louder and unpredictable, with a crowd that came for it and has opinions. **Il Palco delle Acque** stands torch lit on the [[Le Paludi]] canal dock, where sound carries over the water, half the audience arrives and leaves by boat, and volume and visual drama win.

The Palio itself is a painted banner, commissioned fresh each year around something the organisers consider representative of the city's character. The purse is 300 gp, split among registered band members, and past winners are commemorated on a plaque outside the committee offices. A second-place set has come with a 100 gp split and brought a [[Tessarine Concordat|Tessarine]] booking scout around. Winning carries a standing in Calveno that money cannot directly buy.

### Chronology

- **La Prova, first night.** Short opening sets from every band and no eliminations. First impressions set here are hard to shift: the band that dominates does not always win, but the band that humiliates itself rarely recovers.
- **La Sfida, second night.** Full sets and open rivalries. Crowd movement accelerates and bands trade stunts, clash schedules and upstage one another while the organisers look the other way.
- **Il Grande Finale, third night.** Every band plays at once, at full effort, for three hours. The winner is the band the crowd is standing in front of at midnight.
- **This year.** Registration closes in three days. The committee office is on the Mercatura, two buildings from the Tessarine factor house.

### The current field

- **[[La Canzone Nera]].** A quartet of human musicians from the interior, technically flawless and completely boring. They win La Prova on execution and then watch their crowd erode across the two nights that follow.
- **[[Il Vento di Seta]].** A halfling trio with a [[Tessarine Concordat|Tessarine]] booking agent and a crowd that already knows all the words. They play the same set every year, perfectly, have placed second three times, and their following returns at exactly the same size.
- **[[Le Ossa del Toro]].** An orc and tortle two-piece on drums and a massive horn, loud, physical and without finesse. Their crowd surges and compresses and occasionally knocks people into the canal. They have never placed higher than third and have never cared.
