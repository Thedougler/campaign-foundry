## At a glance

- **The truth.** Lizardfolk are reptilian humanoids whose minds run on utility and survival before sentiment. In the Shattered Sea they settle the tidal margins and crew the Sea's hulls as salvage and repair hands.
- **Who knows it.** Warm-blooded crews know them as shipmates and read them as cold. The Party knows [[ruk|Ruk]], [[nona-black-jaw|Nona Black-Jaw]]'s lizardfolk guardian.
- **Limits.** Whether learned warmth is performance or feeling is a question lizardfolk cannot answer about themselves, and most stop asking.
- **Reaches play through.** [[ruk|Ruk]] at [[nona-black-jaw|Nona Black-Jaw]]'s side, [[bastian-crev|Bastian Crev]] of the lost [[loud-argument|Loud Argument]], and the careening bill that a lizardfolk hull-team cuts in half.

> [!narration] As it is told
> Watch a lizardfolk work a rescue. Say the wounded man is dying and they are moving before you finish, faces still as standing water. People call it cold, until they notice the help arrived faster than anyone else's would have.

## Play

- **Players notice.** Their villages sit low in the water and look temporary, and most have outlasted the grandfathers of the people who laughed at them. At high tide a village sleeps. At low tide it outworks a shipyard.
- **Clues.** A use-name is a tool, and [[bastian-crev|Bastian Crev]]'s name is exactly that. A lizardfolk-made object is ugly by warm-blooded taste and outlasts everything shelved beside it.
- **Accounts.** Warm-blooded crews tell the folk as cold. Crews that sailed years beside one tell of a shipmate who laughs now. Lizardfolk do not answer the question.

## Depth

### The full truth

A lizardfolk is a medium reptilian humanoid with a scaled hide, powerful jaws, and a swim speed to match its walk. The bite is a natural weapon that deals 1d6 slashing on a hit. It holds its breath for a quarter-hour, and its unarmoured hide turns a blow at AC 13 plus its Dexterity, with a shield in hand if the work calls for one. Once per long rest it can throw in a hungry jaws bite as a bonus action. On a hit it feeds for temporary hit points equal to its proficiency bonus. It reads animals, bodies, weather and ground the way a hunter reads them, without study.

Waste is the only sin that registers without translation. Bone becomes tools, hide becomes armour, shell becomes currency. Warm-bloods read the pragmatism as cold, and the word misses a lizardfolk parent who dies for a clutch without hesitation, because the calculation is instant. The flatness is efficiency: the help arrives at the same speed, and the face is still while it does.

A lizardfolk who spends years inside a warm-blooded crew develops what outsiders call emotional range. Whether it is learned performance or felt warmth, the lizardfolk cannot say. The laugh is real either way.

### Tide and settlement

Lizardfolk communities keep the tide's clock, not the sun's. Building, foraging and craft run at low tide. Rest, egg-tending and oral tradition ride the high water, so a visitor who arrives then finds a quiet village and one who arrives at low tide finds industry that puts shipyards to shame. Settlements sit on mangrove coasts and in river deltas, and on the brackish flats where salt and fresh water trade places. They build low and in the water, and when the tide pattern changes they relocate without grief. The structure served its purpose. The next one will too.

At sea they crew as hull-workers, salvagers and underwater repair hands. They work cold water without slowing, and the natural hide means fewer injuries from barnacle-crusted keels and broken planking. A ship that carries a lizardfolk hull-team puts in for careening half as often.

### Names

A lizardfolk true-name describes its bearer plainly, like *Holds-Still, Sees-Under-Water, Bites-Twice*. Among warm-bloods a lizardfolk takes a use-name from whatever culture it is embedded in, because a use-name is a tool and the folk do not separate a useful tool from a real one. Their place-names, rendered in Common, follow open syllables and nasal consonants on tidal roots, like *Tanjung, Teluk, Rawa, Pasang*.

### Known lizardfolk of the Sea

- [[ruk|Ruk]], [[nona-black-jaw|Nona Black-Jaw]]'s guardian of twenty years, who reads physical danger while [[enzo|Enzo]] reads the social kind.
- [[bastian-crev|Bastian Crev]], who came up through salvage before Fisk gave him the [[loud-argument|Loud Argument]] and a place in [[fisks-fleet|Fisk's Fleet]].
