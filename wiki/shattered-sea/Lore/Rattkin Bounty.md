## At a glance

- **The truth.** The [[Dravosi Crown]] pays 15 gp per rattkin, dead or alive, collected at any Crown fort. The bounty is standing policy and remains in force.
- **Who knows it.** Every port family on the Crown Islands can quote the rate, and the [[Rattkin|rattkin]] know it best of all.
- **Limits.** Only a Crown fort pays it. Calveno is neutral ground, and its rattkin are relatively safe.
- **Reaches play through.** [[Perrin Black-Jaw]]'s absence below during a Crown boarding, [[Nona Black-Jaw]]'s war against the Crown, and the persecution the [[Passage]] works against.

> [!narration] As it is told
> Fifteen gold a skull, and the fort paymaster takes the catch breathing or boxed. Harbours along the chain know the rate by heart, and rattkin families bar their doors on the nights shore-leave officers drink their way through Calveno.

## Play

- **Players notice.** Posting boards at fort gates, officers who count a small hooded crew member twice, and the tavern trouble that follows Calveno's naval visitors.
- **Clues.** The printed rate and the pay-fort list on a Crown posting, Perrin's habit of going below when grey hulls close, and the altercations that come in with resupply and shore leave.
- **Accounts.** The Crown files it as policy. The docks call it a price on a neighbour. Rattkin live under the number.

## Depth

### The full truth

The bounty stands in Crown law. The rate is 15 gp per rattkin, dead or alive, and any Dravosi fort's paymaster pays it out. It is still in force.

When the Crown implemented the bounty, [[Nona Black-Jaw]] had her political awakening and declared open warfare against the Crown personally. Her civil and illegal disobedience, the Warren's kitchens and the Black-Jaw mob's influence all answer the rate.

Crown authority ends at Calveno's quays, so its rattkin are relatively safe. The exceptions come off visiting ships: Dravosi naval officers resupplying or on shore leave occasionally start altercations with the rattkin they meet.

When [[HCS Ordinance]] boarded the [[Uncertainty]] on the crossing toward Sparhold, [[Perrin Black-Jaw]] hid below with the rescued captives rather than stand on a Crown deck with a price on him. He refused any parley and slipped below before the officers came aboard, sitting out the whole inspection in hiding. From a knot in the wall he showed [[Catarina Da'Virelli]] his tail and passed her his Bardic Inspiration.

### Chronology

- The Crown implemented the bounty, and Nona Black-Jaw declared open warfare against it personally.
- HCS Ordinance boarded the Uncertainty, and Perrin Black-Jaw hid below from a bounty that pays dead or alive.
