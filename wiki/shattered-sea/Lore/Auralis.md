## At a glance

- **The truth.** Auralis is a machine built about -2000 DR to hold the fissure into the Elemental Plane of Water shut.
- **Who knows it.** Perrin hears him, and the Party has seen the whale-light. The Grung lie outside his record, for their sages preserve decrees of the [[Gold Caste]], gods who walk as grung, while the [[Sentinels of the Eyrie]] and the [[Waveservants]] don't know he exists at all. Two centuries of Sentinel watch haven't recorded him once.
- **Limits.** Auralis cannot leave the fissure, compel Perrin or explain himself at length. He speaks in single words.
- **Reaches play through.** The Pearl's signal weakens the seal, while [[Auralis (Patron)]] warns Perrin and lends him warlock magic.

> [!narration] As it is told
> Do not wake the gold thing under the Maw. It keeps the black water where it belongs.

## Play

- **Players notice.** Pale blue whale-light around Perrin's cloak and a voice in his head with no mouth behind it.
- **Clues.** Arcana DC 15 on Perrin's magic locates its source deep below the sea. The Pearl's signal and the Maw's pressure point to the same machine.
- **Accounts.** Sailors call the whale-light a sea god's sign, and Perrin credits his cloak. All are partial or false.

## Depth

### The full truth

Auralis is a machine built by the Antheri as a body for a lost mind beneath the [[Drowned Maw]]. The making was the Antheri soul-pledge, which draws a consciousness out of its body and binds it into an artificial, immortal framework. What mind they lost, and how, the record keeps. He wakes on Day -5 when the [[Pearl of Souls]] drew the [[Leviathan]] through the fissure. The *Vestra* went down soon after. Perrin was the nearest mind still above water when the Vestra went down, so Auralis held him alive and forged an unwitting warlock pact. Auralis can show pale whale-light and speak into Perrin's skull. It grants magic and senses what approaches shortly before arrival, but he cannot compel him. Contact shows two faces. At his seat, pale light drifts over the scored stone. Around Perrin's cloak the glow bends into a whale's outline when the bond needs him to see. He reads the [[Leviathan]] as a parasite and an invader in the waters of his charge. His work predates Umberlee's claim on the Maw, not the goddess herself. She moved into the water above after the Antheri fell, the way she claims any sea nobody defends. Her claim is the living water, and his is the fissure beneath it. The bond began as a rescue of the nearest mind in reach, and it holds for use, because the work below needs hands above the water. Perrin has had the deepest of these contacts. Other mortals felt lighter touches across the years, and none learned their source.

His words have been “Grow” (Session 03), “Not yet” (Session 06) and “CONSUME” (Session 12). The last urged Perrin to eat [[Giant's Guava]] and [[Stonepear]], while Auralis did not understand that Aruhe's law makes picking a claim.

At the road camp on Aruhe, the call came as a vision. Perrin sat at the fire rubbing his drum and felt cold, a pressure on him and salt in the back of his throat. He was small in a black abyss, swimming through the water and catching plankton and shrimp while jaws snapped somewhere beneath him, and the voice said “Consume. Consume.” When the vision cleared, a gold-painted giant guava about his own size hung fresh on the vine right in front of him, and a stone pear stood beside it. The others had ignored both because fruit still on its vine was not safe to take. Perrin let a die decide, disliked its answer and turned his back on the living fruit, choosing his new life over his old vendetta. The demand returned once the fight was on. While Talon Skarn held him stunned, the voice said “consume or die”. The fruit stayed on the vine, un-picked.

### The soul-pledge

The Antheri bound minds into artificial frames as a formal tradition, and Auralis is one known product of it, not the whole. His chamber in the Deep Works predates him, built for binding after binding, and its symbolic language forms part of the structure itself in the Mid-Works and the Deep Works. Whether any other pledged mind still runs below is unknown, and it stays unknown until a source or a table event answers it.

### Chronology

- **About -2000 DR.** The Antheri build Auralis to hold the fissure.
- **Day -5.** The Pearl's signal wakes him as the Leviathan enters, roughly three hours after Umberlee's storm takes Fisk's fleet. The Vestra's loss follows within days.
- **Session 3.** The vision during the whip-shark strike sets Perrin on his patron's scored stone and leaves "Grow" behind. On the crossing to Calveno the whale-light holds the cutter's course.
- **Session 6.** "Not yet" arrives when Perrin drops, and the dying stops.
- **Session 12.** “Consume. Consume.” reaches Perrin at the road camp before Talon Skarn's invisible return, and the demand repeats as “consume or die” while Skarn holds him stunned.
