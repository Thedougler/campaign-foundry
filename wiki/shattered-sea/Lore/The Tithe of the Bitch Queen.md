## At a glance

- **The truth.** The Tithe is the mass of beautiful things [[Umberlee]] claimed from mortal ships across centuries, scattered across the [[Sunken Crown]] seafloor, and one piece lifted brings her spite down on the ship that carries it.
- **Who knows it.** Sailors hold the Tithe as legend, the [[Waveservants]] keep records that name the return of a piece as the remedy, and the clergy refuse to rule on whether the [[Pearl of Souls]] counts as Tithe.
- **Limits.** Salvage accounts of the hoard's extent stop at rumour, and the tidal caves that reach it open on the decade's lowest tides.
- **Reaches play through.** Sunken Crown salvage talk, a cursed ship in fair weather, and any chart that marks the tidal caves.

> [!narration] As it is told
> Lift one coin off her floor and the sea follows you home. Squall after squall stands on your mast until the piece goes back to saltwater. Her hoard keeps, and so does the bill.

## Play

- **Players notice.** Salvagers who agree too well about the curse, and a jewel on a fence's cloth that should have aged in saltwater.
- **Clues.** A ship that draws squalls in a fair-weather week has a piece stowed below. Shrine attendants go quiet when the Tithe is named beside the Pearl.
- **Accounts.** Pilots tell the curse as fact and the hoard as guess. The Waveservants treat the taking as settled business and the return as the whole of the remedy.

## Depth

### The hoard

The Tithe gathered without design. The finest things her storms took stayed kept, and the pieces show it. Jewels keep their polish, statues keep their edges, and gold bars keep a shine saltwater should have eaten. The collection lies scattered across the [[Sunken Crown]] seafloor, and the tidal caves that open above the waterline on the decade's lowest tides are the only way in.

### The curse

Taking a piece invites Umberlee's direct, personal spite. A ship that carries one draws localised squalls that follow her until the piece returns to saltwater, and passing the piece on passes the squalls with it.

### The Pearl question

The Tithe predates the theft of the [[Pearl of Souls]]. Hers is what the storms gathered unasked, the Pearl what was taken from her by hand, and her clergy refuse to rule on whether the Pearl belongs to the Tithe in any formal sense.
