## At a glance

- **The truth.** Several peoples share the Shattered Sea's ports and waters, and the [[rattkin|rattkin]] are the oldest continuous presence in every major port.
- **Who knows it.** Dockside families live the mix daily, and port books record it in customs categories.
- **Limits.** The catalogue is partial. The rattkin, the grung of the [[verdant-teeth|Verdant Teeth]], the minotaur pilots, the lizardfolk of the tidal margins and the [[human|humans]] spread through every port are set down here so far.
- **Reaches play through.** [[rattkin|Rattkin]] lore and every port the Party docks at. See also [[human|Human]], [[grung|Grung]], [[minotaur|Minotaur]] and [[lizardfolk|Lizardfolk]].

> [!narration] As it is told
> Ships come in flying different colours, and the quay speaks tongues the harbour office has no books for. Whoever your grandmother was, a rattkin family worked this quay before her.

## Play

- **Players notice.** Port districts sorted by flag and folk. [[calven-and-calveno|Calveno]] holds Concordat, Seven Houses Council, [[passage|Passage]] and rattkin communities side by side, and Crown sailors walk its streets only as visitors.
- **Clues.** In Calveno, the old drains answer to rattkin hands and were occupied by them longer than either colonial power.
- **Accounts.** Dock folk grant that the first hands on each quay were rattkin hands. The colonial powers' books begin much later.

## Depth

### The full truth

The Sea's ports are shared among the Dravosi Crown's and Tessarine Concordat's colonies and the folk who were there before and between them. The [[rattkin|Rattkin]] predate every colonial power's arrival, holding [[warren|the Warren]] beneath Le Paludi as their fixed origin point and threading the ports through [[passage|the Passage]]. Off Aruhe, Grung clans raid shipping routes on their own account. The grung keep the [[verdant-teeth|Verdant Teeth]] closed and meet outsiders through sanctioned beach trade, while minotaur navigators pilot the Sea's hardest channels, at the highest crew rates in the [[midchain|Midchain]]. The [[lizardfolk|lizardfolk]] live along the tidal margins, where they build low in the water and crew the Sea's hulls as salvagers and repair hands.
