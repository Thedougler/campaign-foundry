## At a glance

- **The truth.** Aarakocra are medium winged bird folk with talon-tipped legs and heads like parrots, eagles or crows, who think in altitude as ground-bound folk think in streets.
- **Who knows it.** Western ports see them rarely. The [[sentinels-of-the-eyrie|Sentinels of the Eyrie]] are aarakocra, and [[high-eyrie|High Eyrie]] has been theirs for two centuries.
- **Limits.** How many live is an open question. The folk are rare in [[dravosi-crown|Crown]] waters. They settle only with the [[sentinels-of-the-eyrie|Sentinels of the Eyrie]] at [[high-eyrie|High Eyrie]] and pass near [[the-tail|the Tail]]. See [[peoples-of-the-shattered-sea|Peoples of the Shattered Sea]] for the region's peoples.
- **Reaches play through.** [[crissdalynn-khinriss|Crissdalynn Khinriss]], the crew's cartographer, and the eyrie's exact ledgers of wind and water.

> [!narration] As it is told
> Sky-born. One of them steps off a yardarm into the wind's keeping, and the horizon takes them before the gulls decide anything. They don't queue at the ports and they don't stay bought to a harbour. Count the wind before you count them.

## Play

- **Players notice.** Crissdalynn steps off the rail into open air and the wind carries her. Her *gust of wind* arrives without material components. She picks out land or sail well before the lookout calls it.
- **Clues.** Wind with no weather behind it marks a Wind Caller, as at [[uncertainty|Uncertainty]]'s rail. A watcher in robes with every mark stripped away, mottled pale plumage and old eyes, is an aarakocra grown old, as with [[osset|Osset]]. Ledgers recording time, weather, position and occurrence to the exact minute belong to aarakocra record-keepers, the [[sentinels-of-the-eyrie|Sentinels of the Eyrie]].
- **Accounts.** Sailors tell the sky-born version, a folk that owes the sea nothing. The Sentinels tell it as duty: watch, record, do not interpret. Crissdalynn tells it as a trade: she charts from the air because the air is where she thinks. Each telling gives a piece of the truth about the winged folk.

## Depth

### The full truth

Aarakocra are medium humanoids, feathered from crown to heel, with large wings carried on talon-tipped legs and heads resembling parrots, eagles, crows and other birds. The feet are natural unarmed strikes dealing slashing damage. They fly as fast as they walk, at 30 ft., and flight stops when armour not built for wings replaces the feathers. Once per Long Rest an aarakocra can cast *gust of wind* without material components, choosing the casting ability when the character is made. Sight is keen, with advantage on Wisdom (Perception) checks that rely on it. A named Faction acts on the fact of them every day. For two centuries the Sentinels have watched and recorded the Drowned Maw without interpreting it, and Master Kyzil trains exactness into the next generation at High Eyrie.

### Known aarakocra

- [[master-kyzil|Master Kyzil]], a barn-owl aarakocra and master of the Sentinels at High Eyrie.
- [[osset|Osset]], a snowy-owl aarakocra grown old, the master who once trained the Sentinels.
- [[talon-vantyrus|Talon Vantyrus]], an aged snowy-owl aarakocra who watches without blinking, a thin blade at his hip.
- [[talon-skarn|Talon Skarn]], a peregrine aarakocra in a black robe with chains, who attacked the Party on Aruhe.
- [[crissdalynn-khinriss|Crissdalynn Khinriss]], the crew's aarakocra Kensei monk, level 5, a cartographer who works from above.
