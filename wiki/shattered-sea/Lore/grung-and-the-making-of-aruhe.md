> [!narration] As it is told
> An elder of the free boats gives the reason as practice rather than story. "We pass that island wide because it holds a debt of ours." A clan scribe gives the same raid as order and closes its ledger on what ran from it.

## Truth, Clues and accounts

- **The truth.** Two hundred years ago, Grung raiders took [[hinewai|Hinewai]], a druid of a far island in the [[midchain|Midchain]], from a beach meeting their kind for the first time, and tortured her at the [[karath|Karath]] hatcheries. She and [[the-unnamed-companion|a companion]] taken before her burned their way out. An arrow brought him down in the surf off [[aruhe|Aruhe]], and the grave she dug him stands at the root of the island's law. Only one witness remembers the whole escape, the clans kept their accounts uncollected, and the companion's name went with him.
- **Players notice.** Hinewai answers the first mention of the Grung with the island's law, and its responders attack grung without a claim against them. The crime is a debt she counts as current.
- **Clues.**
  - The fruit tree at [[memorial-grove|Memorial Grove]] shades two graves.
  - Expeditions came for those graves and lie in the soil along [[the-burnt-road|The Burnt Road]].
  - A clan account of the raid stops at the hatchery door. It is held by the [[grung-clans|Grung Clans]].
- **Accounts.**
  - Hinewai tells it as a wrong with a name and a grave. She holds the whole of it, counts the debt as still open, and so keeps the island's law, which [[taking-on-aruhe|Taking on Aruhe]] sets out. Her account is true. The Party knows that law, and the crime behind it stays with her.
  - The [[grung-clans|Grung Clans]] tell it as order and leave the druid out of the ledgers. Their account is distorted, and no record collects it.
  - Dockside crews hear only of raiders who move captives inland. That account is thin but not false.
  - The Gold caste has sent expeditions against the two graves ever since, and the party can follow their orders in [[two-grave-orders|Two-Grave Orders]].

## The full truth

The raid that began Aruhe's law was a slaving raid like the clans' others, aimed at a coast four islands east of [[karath|Karath]] that had never met grung. It took [[hinewai|Hinewai]] for the hatcheries, where a dawn toxin at the base of her skull kept her hands useful and her plans slow. [[the-unnamed-companion|A companion]] taken in an earlier raid worked at her side, and on the days the toxin ran weak the two whispered of fire and open water. The escape was his plan, and the lantern that burned the long hut was theirs, and the eastern path to the water was theirs. An arrow took him in the surf within sight of [[aruhe|Aruhe]], and three castings failed to wake his chest. [[hinewai|Hinewai]] cursed [[mystra|Mystra]] over his body before she carried him ashore. His grave went in by hand beneath a fruit tree in [[memorial-grove|Memorial Grove]]. In the third summer a grung sail traced the treeline, and she spoke the island's taking-law from inside a second grave dug for herself ([[taking-on-aruhe|Taking on Aruhe]]). Every grung expedition since has sailed for the same two graves ([[two-grave-orders|Two-Grave Orders]]).

## Chronology

- A night raid on a coast meeting grung for the first time took [[hinewai|Hinewai]] from a beach four islands east of [[karath|Karath]].
- Hatchery months: the morning toxin held her at the bench, and a fellow captive joined the planning on weak days.
- A lantern burned the long hut, and two captives took the eastern path for the water.
- An arrow killed the companion in the surf within sight of [[aruhe|Aruhe]], and three castings failed to wake him.
- [[hinewai|Hinewai]] cursed [[mystra|Mystra]] over him and carried him inland to a grave she dug by hand beneath a fruit tree in [[memorial-grove|Memorial Grove]].
- Her vow from inside a second grave, dug for herself in the third summer after a grung sail traced the treeline, made the island's law and fixed the debt the [[grung-clans|Grung Clans]] still carry.
- Gold-caste expeditions have come for the two graves ever since. The grung compelled to burn the forest ended in the soil along [[the-burnt-road|The Burnt Road]], well over a hundred of them.
