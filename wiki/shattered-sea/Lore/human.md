## At a glance

- **The truth.** Humans are the Shattered Sea's most demographically dominant and culturally varied people. Their oldest tells put their origin in Sigil, the City of Doors at the multiverse's hub, and their spread from there across every water.
- **Who knows it.** Every port lives among humans daily, on the crews, in the charters and behind the counting-house doors. What no port agrees on is which human way of life speaks for the rest.
- **Limits.** The Wiki does not record a Sea-wide human homeland, culture, religion or look. Variation by port is the rule.
- **Reaches play through.** [[delmar-fisk|Delmar Fisk]] and every colonial dock where the [[rattkin|rattkin]], the [[grung|grung]] and the other [[peoples-of-the-shattered-sea|peoples of the Sea]] share the quay.

> [!narration] As it is told
> Humans work every water, and no two quays keep the same gods. The old tellers walk them out of Sigil's doors, the city of Common's birth, and they never stopped walking. Ask one where his people began and he names the street of his birth.

## Play

- **Players notice.** Humans on every dock and in every crew, and no two communities alike in look, worship or custom.
- **Clues.** A human's origin shows in manner and background, never in the species alone. Crown charters, colonial streets and a found family can each carry a different human world.
- **Accounts.** Sailors name humans the way they name water, by the port. Other peoples read them as everywhere and varied, and leave it there.

## Depth

### The full truth

Humans are the multiverse's most varied people. Their oldest gathering is told in Sigil, the City of Doors at the multiverse's hub and the birthplace of Common, and from there they spread across every world the doors touch. They are diverse in appearance and worship many gods, and they live about eighty years.

The Wiki does not record a culture, religion, appearance or Sea homeland common to all humans, so a human's origin, worship and custom cannot be read from the species alone. In the Shattered Sea they are the most demographically dominant people, and the [[dravosi-crown|Dravosi Crown]] counts among the great human powers.

### Traits

- **Creature type.** Humanoid.
- **Size.** Medium (4 to 7 ft.) or Small (2 to 4 ft.), chosen at character creation.
- **Speed.** 30 ft.
- **Life span.** About 80 years.
- **Resourceful.** Heroic Inspiration whenever they finish a Long Rest.
- **Skillful.** Proficiency in one skill of their choice.
- **Versatile.** They take two Origin feats of their choice.
