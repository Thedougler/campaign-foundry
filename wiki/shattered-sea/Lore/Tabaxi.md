## At a glance

- **The truth.** Tabaxi are a feline folk whose city is [[Keth-Naar]], at the [[Blue Hole]]'s edge east of the [[Drowned Maw]].
- **Who knows it.** Western crews that have run the eastern road know the city. [[Perrin Black-Jaw]] washed up there after the Vestra went down.
- **Limits.** Tabaxi numbers, customs and range beyond Keth-Naar are beyond the record.
- **Reaches play through.** The last city on the eastern road, and the water and repairs its harbour sells before the unmarked water beyond.

> [!narration] As it is told
> Past the Maw, harbour means Keth-Naar, and Keth-Naar means tabaxi.

## Play

- **Players notice.** The far edge of the eastern road is a working city, and its people are tabaxi.
- **Clues.** [[Keth-Naar]] and its harbour are the place to see them.
- **Accounts.** Sailors speak of the city first and the folk after it, and of nothing else about them.

## Depth

### The full truth

Keth-Naar is a tabaxi city at the Blue Hole's edge, and it is the furthest reliable landmark eastward of the [[Drowned Maw]]. Nothing else about the tabaxi is recorded.
