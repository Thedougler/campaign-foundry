# Golden Presence

*Level 9 Transmutation.*

___

- **Casting Time:** 1 hour
- **Range:** Self
- **Components:** V, S, M (a gold bowl engraved with the laws of a people, worth 25,000+ GP, which the spell consumes)
- **Duration:** Until dispelled

___

You lead a circle of at least five other casters, and each of them expends a spell slot of level 3 or higher. As you cast, you name a people and a mark that sets a chosen few of them apart, such as a skin colour or a brand. You also name a belief: magic belongs to those who bear the mark and to nobody else. The spot where the bowl stood becomes the spell's **heart**.

***Believers.*** Every member of that people on the same plane who sincerely holds the belief is a Believer. Belief counts as consent, and the spell allows a Believer no saving throw. An unmarked Believer whose belief is whole loses their magic to the spell and can't cast spells or use innate spellcasting. A doubter keeps their magic but still adds to the reserve below. Once a creature gives up the belief entirely, the drain stops and any lost magic returns over 30 days.

***The Reserve.*** At each dawn, every Believer adds 1 point to the spell's shared reserve. An unmarked Believer whose belief is whole also adds the total levels of their spell slots and innate daily spells. The reserve's maximum is thirty times what it gained at the last dawn. A marked creature, or a Believer one of them authorises, can cast a spell by expending reserve points equal to its level instead of a spell slot. Cantrips are free.

***Endowments.*** A marked creature can bank reserve points in its own body as an **endowment**. Banked points are spent on nothing else. All endowments together can't exceed what the reserve gained at the last dawn. A small people can't save its way to a god. An endowment grants every tier at or below its size.

| Endowment | Light (bright/dim) | Gaze save | Gaze damage | Benefits |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1+ | 5 ft./5 ft. | DC 13 | 1d6 Fire | Light only |
| 100 | 10 ft./10 ft. | DC 15 | 2d6 Fire | Has every Wizard spell of levels 1–3 prepared |
| 1,000 | 30 ft./30 ft. | DC 19 | 6d6 Fire | Has Wizard spells through level 8 prepared and casts without components. Maintains up to three Concentration spells at once and automatically succeeds on saves to keep them |
| 10,000 | 60 ft./60 ft. | DC 27 | 12d6 Fire | Becomes a demigod, as below |

***The Burning Gaze.*** A creature that looks directly at an endowed creature makes a Constitution saving throw against its gaze, at most once per round. On a failed save, it takes the tier's Fire damage and has the Blinded condition until the start of its next turn. A creature can avert its eyes to avoid the save, treating the endowed creature as unseen. Against a demigod, a second failed save blinds a creature permanently until [[lesser-restoration|Lesser Restoration]] or stronger magic ends the Blinded condition.

***Demigod.*** At 10,000 points, the creature becomes a god in all but origin. Its Intelligence, Wisdom and Charisma scores become 28, 30 and 30. It gains a Fly Speed of 40 feet with hover and Truesight out to 120 feet. It has every Wizard spell prepared. Three times between dawns, it can cast [[wish|Wish]] for 1,000 reserve points and suffer none of Wish's usual stress. Spells of level 5 or lower fail against it. Its light hides it so completely that attacks, spells and other effects can't target it, and harmful areas exclude it. This ward can't be dispelled while the endowment stands. Each dawn, the form takes 1,000 reserve points to sustain, and an endowment left unpaid falls to 1,000.

***Ending the Spell.*** [[dispel-magic|Dispel Magic]] cast at the heart ends the spell as a level 9 effect, along with its reserve and every endowment. The demigod's ward doesn't protect the heart. As Believers lose their belief, the reserve and every endowment shrink with them.

***Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot.*** A higher slot changes neither the circle nor its harvest.

**Classes:** Sorcerer, Warlock, Wizard

> [!narration] Casting
> The circle hums around one kneeling figure, a low, steady sound you feel through the wet ground more than hear. The gold bowl in front of them folds in on itself like wax. Thin threads of light run from every humming throat into the kneeler's eyes. The humming stops, but those eyes keep shining.

## Rulings

- **Detect Magic on a Believer** shows faint Transmutation apart from any natural trait, with wisps of magic drifting towards the heart. A successful DC 19 Intelligence (Arcana) check identifies a continuing transfer.
- **Leaving isn't disbelieving.** Flight, rebellion or hatred of the marked rulers doesn't stop the drain while the creature still believes. Only giving up the belief stops it.
- **One ruler isn't the spell.** Killing or exposing one marked creature leaves the reserve intact. The spell weakens only as belief breaks across the whole people.
