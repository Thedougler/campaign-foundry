# Golden Presence

*Level 9 Transmutation.*

___

- **Casting Time:** 1 hour
- **Range:** Self
- **Components:** V, S, M (a gold bowl engraved with the caste's decrees, worth 25,000+ GP, which the spell consumes)
- **Duration:** Until dispelled

___

A gold Grung opens the circle with at least five willing secondary casters. They each contribute a spell slot of level 3 or higher. Their ceremony establishes an enduring link to the species. The place where the bowl stood becomes the circle's heart.

***Belief is consent.*** Across the same plane, every Grung who sincerely believes in the caste structure judges their own caste undeserving of the power reserved for gold. That conviction willingly surrenders their magical ability. A believer who thinks they have no magic still gives the primal affinity they cannot recognise. The spell does not manufacture faith. It exploits the believer's conviction that power belongs above their station.

The surrendered ability cannot be used independently while that belief holds. Known spells and learned rituals remain, and the body's poison, climbing and other species traits are unaffected. The gold lend some stolen power back to red sages as supposed divine favour. The sages receive that favour in honest devotion and preach the system that takes the gift from them.

***The common reserve.*** Each fully believing, untrained Grung supplies 1 point daily. Surrendered trained spellcasting adds the combined levels of its normal maximum spell slots. Innate spells add their levels multiplied by their normal daily uses, with an at-will spell counted once. Doubt reduces the gift in proportion to the belief that remains.

The caste shares one reserve. Its capacity is thirty times its current daily contribution. Casting expends points equal to a spell's level, whether the power serves a gold ruler or an authorised sage. Cantrips do not consume points. Extraordinary workings have the larger prices below. An empty reserve cannot fund another spell.

***Accumulation.*** Gold skin permanently fixed by [[ossketh|Ossketh]] admits a Grung to the receiving side. Power set aside in a gold body becomes an endowment, unavailable for other expenditure. The caste's combined endowments cannot exceed its current daily contribution. A small congregation cannot save indefinitely and produce a whole caste of gods.

***A hundred gifts.*** An endowment of 100 points supplies access to every Wizard spell through level 3. The body shines with Bright Light for 10 feet and Dim Light for another 10 feet. Its gaze hazard is DC 15 and 2d6 Fire damage.

***A thousand gifts.*** At 1,000 points, that repertoire extends through level 8 and components become unnecessary. Several believers' minds sustain up to three Concentration spells, with automatic success on saves to maintain them. Each additional simultaneous spell consumes 100 points. The light extends to 30 feet bright and another 30 feet dim. Its gaze hazard is DC 19 and 6d6 Fire damage.

***Ten thousand gifts.*** At 10,000 points, the accumulated power produces the [[gold-caste-serene|Gold Caste Serene]]. Its applied sheet records the complete result. The rite grants each part of that result:

- **Body.** Strength 20, Dexterity 22, Constitution 26, Intelligence 28, Wisdom 30 and Charisma 30. The body has AC 22 and maximum Hit Points 667, with Hover and a Fly Speed of 40 feet instead of walking. It has Truesight for 120 feet and all languages. Proficiency Bonus +9 applies to all ability saves and to Arcana, History, Insight, Perception, Persuasion and Religion.
- **Repertoire.** The beneficiary has the complete Wizard spell list prepared. The Serene's arcane slots and full innate repertoire are supplied by the endowment. Components are unnecessary. Spell save DC 27 and +19 to hit apply, including a +1 endowment bonus to Intelligence-based casting. Up to three Wishes between dawns avoid their normal stress and permanent-loss risk, consuming 1,000 points each.
- **Radiance.** Bright Light extends for 60 feet and Dim Light for another 60 feet. The gaze hazard becomes DC 27 and 12d6 Fire damage. The light hides the body so completely that attacks, spells and other effects cannot target it, and harmful areas exclude it. That protection cannot itself be dispelled or suppressed while the greatest endowment stands.
- **Immunities.** Poison, Psychic and Radiant damage cannot affect it. It is immune to the Charmed, Exhaustion, Frightened, Grappled, Paralysed, Petrified, Poisoned, Prone, Restrained and Stunned conditions. Spells of level 5 or lower fail against it.
- **Resistance.** Saves against spells and other magical effects have Advantage. Against higher-level spells, pooled conviction supplies automatic success for 100 points per save. A failed save against any effect can also become a success five times between dawns, for 100 points each.
- **Certainty.** The threefold Concentration of the thousand-gift tier persists. Once per turn, 1,000 points can force a cast spell's targets to fail their saves and its damage dice to take their maximum values.
- **Touch.** Contact with the skin requires a DC 27 Wisdom save. Failure imposes the control of [[dominate-person|Dominate Person]] for 1 minute, regardless of creature type and without Concentration. A successful repeated save at the end of the victim's turn ends it.
- **Commands.** Two spells can be cast with one Action. A counter within 300 feet automatically stops spells through level 8, using 3 points. Against level 9, a roll of 1d20 + 19 must reach 19. Shield supplies its +5 AC and Magic Missile protection as a Reaction for 1 point.
- **Intervention.** Three Legendary Action uses allow flight for one use, a spell through level 6 for two, or Wish for three. The Serene's sheet records their timing and casting limits. The reserve still funds each spell, and every Wish counts towards the same daily three.
- **Territory.** Lair Actions in the [[verdant-teeth|Verdant Teeth]] bind three creatures with roots within 300 feet, bar magical travel within 1 mile or blind creatures within 60 feet. The saves are DC 27. The root binding and directed flash consume 100 points each, and the travel prohibition consumes 1,000. Their durations and initiative timing appear on the applied sheet.

Sustaining this greatest body consumes another 1,000 points daily. Without that upkeep, its endowment falls to the thousand-gift tier. A loss of structural belief reduces the caste's available endowments proportionally and its reserve capacity with them. Ten thousand ordinary believers support one body at the ceiling, with daily upkeep and three extraordinary Wishes consuming 4,000 of their 10,000-point harvest. Every other act and beneficiary competes for what remains.

***The burning gaze.*** A newly receiving gold body already shines for 5 feet bright and another 5 feet dim, with a DC 13 gaze and 1d6 Fire damage. At every tier, a creature that looks directly at the gold makes a Constitution saving throw. Failure deals that tier's Fire damage and imposes the Blinded condition until the start of its next turn. Success causes neither effect. The glare demands at most one save per round, and averting one's eyes avoids it while treating the gold as unseen.

Only at the ceiling does a second failed gaze save against the same gold cause permanent blindness. The failures need not be consecutive. [[lesser-restoration|Lesser Restoration]], or a higher-level spell that ends the Blinded condition, cures even this lasting blindness. The Serene's directed flash is a separate effect and does not count towards it.

***Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot.*** A higher slot changes neither the circle nor its harvest.

**Classes:** Sorcerer, Warlock, Wizard

> Red grung hum around a kneeling gold one, and the sound vibrates through the wet ground. The gold bowl sinks into itself as narrow threads of light run from the ring into the kneeling body's eyes. The humming stops, but the eyes keep shining.

## Rulings

- **Belief outlasts allegiance.** [[jean-claude-tabarnack|Jean-Claude Tabarnack]] rejects the rulers and has fled their rule, but still believes in the caste structure and the golds' power. That remaining conviction partly forfeits his magic, and flight or censure alone does not break the tether. Once belief in the structure itself ends, the drain stops and the surrendered gift returns over 30 days.
- **The whole con must become visible.** Meaningfully weakening the current caste requires public understanding at the scale of the clans, with proof of claimed divinity failing. The fruit, Ossketh and the stolen gift explain why the failure matters. Opposition to an individual ruler leaves the engine intact.
- **The heart is separate.** [[dispel-magic|Dispel Magic]] at the heart ends the shared rite, its reserve and every endowment. It is a level 9 effect, with the normal DC 19 check for a lower slot. The body's ward does not protect that place. Ending this rite does not undo an [[grung-authority-seal|Authority Seal]]'s lifelong conviction.

## Tradition

The [[gold-caste|Gold Caste]] alone understands the theft. The red sages preserve and preach the religion in honest belief. Their rank makes them the highest fools in the order, and their authority gives the system its most trusted voices. Ceremony renews the conviction that every caste deserves its place and the gold alone deserve their power.

The [[grung|Grung]]'s primal affinity comes from their homeland's dense magic and its closeness to the Fey. The caste makes each believer deny any personal claim to that gift. Thousands of yielded minds then sustain a sovereign's spells and bear the strain of its wonders. What looks like personal divinity is a people's ability concentrated in one body.

The physical light intensifies as that body accumulates power. A fresh gold glows, while the greatest is as painful to study as the sun. Bowed heads protect the eyes as well as honour the caste. The [[gold-fruit|Gold Fruit]] farms control who can acquire a receiving body, and the religion supplies what fills it. [[auralis|Auralis]] is entirely separate from this working.

A sovereign can wish damaged eyes whole and collect the thanks of the people its light blinded. Their stolen gift supplies the miracle they kneel to receive.

## Who knows it

The true rite is held by the gold inside [[karath|Karath]]'s concealed works. Their private litany and the inscriptions at the heart explain its mechanism. Discovering them means entering territory where the caste's agents erase evidence and capture intruders. An Authority Seal is the price of being caught.

A sage's decree beside a spent seal shows the common script. [[detect-magic|Detect Magic]] on a believer shows Transmutation distinct from their nonmagical species traits, and a successful DC 19 Intelligence (Arcana) check identifies a continuing transfer. The sages call what they see a blessing. [[simone-tabarnack|Simone Tabarnack]]'s unfinished gold change shows how a receiving body can be made, while the failure of a supposedly divine act can expose what sustains it. The caste protects the rite because its explanation threatens both its rulers and the society that makes them powerful.
