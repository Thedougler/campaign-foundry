## At a glance

- **Kind.** Shortsword.
- **Rarity.** Uncommon.
- **Attunement.** Yes.
- **Changes.** +1 to attack and damage rolls while attuned.
- **Held by.** [[Perrin Black-Jaw]], who took it from [[Barnaby Rook]]'s cabin.

> [!narration] First look
> A plain blade slides from a scabbard gone stiff with salt. It sits light in the hand. Fingers have worn the grip's cord shiny, and sea air has pitted the fittings grey. Years of riding have worn a notch for a thumb into the pommel, its hollow rubbed smooth. The scabbard knocks against a palm like any other prize, and the name does the rest of the work.

## Play

### Properties

An attuned bearer gains a +1 bonus to attack and damage rolls made with the shortsword.

## Depth

### History

[[Barnaby Rook]] kept the blade in his cabin aboard the HCS Surety ([[Uncertainty]]), sealed in a confiscation crate with a locked jewellery box, set beside the [[Letters of Marque]] and a crate of flintlocks with [[Grung Toxin Vials|twenty vials of Grung tincture]]. His fee ledger logs the blade as "bladed goods, unlicensed", and Rook never registered the seizure with the Crown. It doesn't record who the blade was taken from, when the inspection happened, or where on the Strait it took place. The box held a small pendant engraved on the back, "For Mira, from the sea", so blade and pendant travelled together, whoever left them that way. When the Party took the cutter in [[Session 2 - Recap|Session 2]] and Rook fell into the water without resurfacing, [[Perrin Black-Jaw]] took the blade.

### Hidden truths

Who Mira was, and why a blade with her name and a pendant marked for her rode in a Crown captain's cabin, is nowhere recorded. Whoever first carried the blade kept it anonymous on purpose, and the steel is unmarked by engraving or maker's mark. Someone maintained the edge with care until they could no longer.
