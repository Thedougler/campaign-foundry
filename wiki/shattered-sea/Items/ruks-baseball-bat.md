> [!narration] First look
> Ruk rests a heavy, two-handed club across his palms, its head wrapped in battered iron around a worn wooden core. The bands sit tight. One flattens into a narrow guard across the head. He taps the iron against stone. A bright, hollow clang rings above the wood's dull knock. Deep grooves score the base of the head, worn smooth at their edges.

## Properties

*Weapon (*Greatclub*), Rare (Requires Attunement), Simple Weapon, Melee Weapon, 10 lb.*

1d8 Bludgeoning

Two-Handed

Mastery: Push

The bat is a greatclub of dark, salt-worn wood bound with iron. Its hollow head makes a bright metallic clang when it strikes a creature or object.

***Two-Handed.*** A Two-Handed weapon requires two hands when you attack with it.

***Mastery: Push.*** If you hit a Large or smaller creature with this weapon, you can push it up to 10 feet straight away from yourself.

***Guarding Reaction.*** While you are attuned to and holding the bat, when you or an ally you can see within 5 feet of you takes damage from an attack, you can take a Reaction to reduce that damage by 1d8 + your Strength modifier (minimum reduction 1).

***Pinning Stroke.*** Once per turn, when you hit a Large or smaller creature with the bat, you can forgo the weapon's Push mastery to force the target to make a Strength saving throw (DC 8 + your Strength modifier + your Proficiency Bonus). On a failed save, the target has the Grappled condition (escape DC equal to the saving throw DC) until the start of your next turn. This grapple ends early if you release the bat or if the target is more than 5 feet from you.

## History

Ruk calls the bat his, though no one has established who made it or when it came to him.
