## At a glance

- **Kind.** A fruit from [[Karath]]'s secret gold farms, and the base of the [[Gold Caste|Gold caste]]'s diet.
- **Rarity.** Mundane as fruit and rare as trade. The farms bar even the lower castes.
- **Attunement.** None.
- **Changes.** A grung's caste colour. Enough fruit shifts the pigmentation to gold by plain biology, and a casting of [[Ossketh]] then holds the colour for life.
- **Held by.** The Gold caste, on the farms at [[Karath]]. Small quantities reach outside buyers.

> [!narration] First look
> The fruit feels heavy in the hand for its size, gold-green skin pulled taut over the dense flesh within. The smell is thick and sweet, closer to a distillery than an orchard. Bite it and your lips stain saffron for a day. The stain is the tell. A grung fed on these wears the same colour on the skin, and it holds.

## Play

### Properties

The change is biological rather than magical. A grung who eats enough of the fruit takes on the gold pigmentation, and the spell only fixes what the food has made. Cast before the diet has done its work, the rite runs incomplete.

### In use

[[Simone Tabarnack]] bought a small quantity from outside Karath's control, ate her way partway to gold, and cast the rite before the colour had finished. The Gold caste could not deny her gold without exposing what the fruit does, so they acknowledge her as showing signs of divinity.

## Depth

### History

The gold fruit grows on Karath's secret gold farms, hidden even from the lower castes. The crop is the caste's own diet, and controlling the fruit means controlling which grung can turn gold.

### Hidden truths

The fruit's plain biology undoes the caste's divinity claim: any grung who reaches enough of the diet can wear gold, and [[Ossketh]] holds it there. The caste cannot deny a grung who manages it without preaching that same biology aloud.
