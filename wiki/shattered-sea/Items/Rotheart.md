## At a glance

- **Kind.** Consumable.
- **Rarity.** Very rare.
- **Attunement.** - **Changes.** Rolled healing takes its maximum for 24 hours.
- **Held by.** [[Crissdalynn Khinriss]] carries the two the Party did not eat.

> [!narration] First look
> A rotheart is a black, glossy fruit that sits dense in the hand. Where the skin has split, the flesh is dark as old blood, wet at every break. Warmth reaches your palm before your fingers close, and the fruit keeps that heat long after it leaves the tree.

## Play

### Properties

As a Bonus Action, eat one. For the next 24 hours, rolled healing takes its maximum, so Hit Dice pay their full value and a healing spell rolls no dice. Eating one also strips any Exhaustion and ends a poison or a disease. Aches and old tension loosen as it goes down, and one fruit is one use. A second bite only tastes good.

### In use

The Party gathered three fallen fruits at the road camp, and [[Crissdalynn Khinriss]] ate the first at that camp. The other two ride in her bag. Crissdalynn was still under it in the last fight against [[Talon Skarn]], and one [[Redheart Berry]] paid a flat 40 Hit Points instead of rolling. She rose fully healed, the arm Skarn broke knitting straight.

## Depth

### History

Fallen fruit lay at the road camp the Party reached at dusk, deep along Aruhe's burnt road, each rotheart warm and oddly heavy for its size. The fruit grows on the island's sickest trees, deep in Aruhe's interior, never in the groves a forager searches first.

### Hidden truths

The fruit reads as a cleanse for whoever eats it. The maximum rule sits underneath, and a healing roll taken in its 24 hours is where it shows. The Party met it in the last fight, when Crissdalynn's [[Redheart Berry]] jumped from dice to a flat 40.
