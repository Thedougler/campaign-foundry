> [!narration] First look
> The fruit feels heavy in the hand for its size, gold-green skin pulled taut over the dense flesh within. The smell is thick and sweet, closer to a distillery than an orchard. Bite it and your lips stain saffron for a day. The stain is the tell. A grung fed on these wears the same colour on the skin, and it holds.

## Properties

*Consumable (Fruit), Mundane (No Attunement).*

On [[aruhe|Aruhe]] a golden peach is a relatively ordinary fruit. It is delicious, and its distinct metallic shine is the only thing that sets it apart from a normal peach. Eating one works a minor magic that turns a portion of the eater's skin gold for a time. It is the base of the [[gold-caste|Gold caste]]'s diet.

***Gilding.*** Each peach eaten calls for a Constitution saving throw. The first peach in a day is DC 10, and each peach after it in that day raises the DC by 2, taking a determined eater from 10 to 12 to 14. On a success, one patch of skin about the breadth of the eater's palm turns a soft metallic gold, wherever the eater chooses, and holds for 8 hours. On a failure, the gold refuses that peach and only the day's saffron lip stain remains, and the next peach is harder still. The gold covers skin a patch at a time, and gilding a whole body takes a sackful of fruit and a steady constitution. Eating a peach in combat costs an action.

***Passing Disguise.*** The gold is colour and shine. Armour, saves and senses stay as they were, and under Detect Magic a faint transmutation clings to the gilded skin until it fades.

***Species.*** Any species gilds for the eight hours the magic lasts. Like every Aruhe fruit the Party has met so far, the peach roughly does what it looks like it does. A metallic gold peach reads at once as worth a bite, and the gold on the eater's skin shows within moments.

***Grung Colour.*** A grung's gold is plain biology. A grung who eats enough of the fruit takes the gold pigmentation into the skin, a change in the grung's caste colour, and a casting of [[ossketh|Ossketh]] then fixes the colour for life. Cast before the diet has done its work, the rite runs incomplete.

## History

The gold fruit grows on [[karath|Karath]]'s secret gold farms, hidden even from the lower castes, and on the tree at [[memorial-grove|Memorial Grove]], grown from seed [[hinewai|Hinewai]] stole off those farms on her escape. The crop is the caste's own diet, and controlling the farms means controlling which grung can turn gold, while the grove's tree stands outside their control. The fruit is mundane as fruit and rare as trade. The farms bar even the lower castes, the Gold caste holds them, and small quantities reach outside buyers.

[[simone-tabarnack|Simone Tabarnack]] came to [[aruhe|Aruhe]] herself and stole several golden peaches from the tree at [[memorial-grove|Memorial Grove]]. That stolen gold fruit took her partway to gold, and she cast the rite before the colour had finished. The Gold caste could not deny her gold without exposing what the fruit does, so they acknowledge her as showing signs of divinity.

## Hidden truths

The fruit's plain biology undoes the caste's divinity claim: any grung who reaches enough of the diet can wear gold, and [[ossketh|Ossketh]] holds it there. The caste cannot deny a grung who manages it without preaching that same biology aloud.

Only [[jean-claude-tabarnack|Jean-Claude]] knows what the gold on the eater's skin means. The tell is the gold itself, which shows within moments of the bite.
