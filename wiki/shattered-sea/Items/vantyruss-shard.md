> A grey shard lies flat against the harness before you, pierced by a rivet through one strap. You can trace the strap past the sliver and round the body, between the hanging weapons and the chain. The fastening goes through both. The shard comes away with that piece of harness.

## Properties

*Plot Object.*

A flat grey sliver riveted through [[talon-skarn|Talon Skarn]]'s harness strap carries [[talon-vantyrus|Talon Vantyrus]]'s gaze.

***Surviving the Hunter.*** If Skarn dies, the sliver remains riveted through the strap on his corpse. The Party can strip the harness or remove the shard and carry it away.

The shard's hidden connection is for the DM to run as described below. No power usable by its carrier has been established.

## History

Skarn carries the shard during his hunt for [[fate-spinner|Crissdalynn's Fate Spinner]]. Vantyrus watches his apprentice's fights through it and works fate in his favour from afar. Skarn still wears it at the road camp on [[aruhe|Aruhe]], where the fight remains unresolved.

## Hidden truths

***The Watcher.*** Skarn's death leaves Vantyrus's connection to the shard intact, and his gaze follows whoever takes it from the body. Passing it to another carrier gives him someone else to watch.

The Players are not meant to realise that the shard is looking back at them. Keep the watcher on the DM's side of the page. Neither a visible tell nor a means of discovering the watcher has been established.

***Fate through the Shard.*** Vantyrus works fate on Skarn's fights through this object. No power usable by its wearer has been established, nor has Vantyrus's ability to manipulate a new carrier's fate. Range, action costs, saves, interruption and destruction have not been settled. Do not grant further powers or impose new numerical mechanics from the shard alone.
