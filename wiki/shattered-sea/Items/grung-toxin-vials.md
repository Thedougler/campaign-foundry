## At a glance

- **Kind.** Stoppered vials of Grung tincture, Simone Tabarnack's preparation, made by Grung hands.
- **Rarity.** Mundane.
- **Attunement.** None.
- **Changes.** One honest look at a vial's contents ties the Crown's toxin to [[simone-tabarnack|Simone Tabarnack]] and puts [[jean-claude-tabarnack|Jean-Claude]]'s secret on the table.
- **Held by.** The Party. Twenty came from [[barnaby-rook|Rook]]'s crate aboard the [[uncertainty|HCS Surety]], six from the [[calven-and-calveno|Calveno]] sewer loot, and both lots are in Party hands.

> [!narration] First look
> Flintlocks fill the loot crate to the middle, and glass vials short as a thumb lie beneath them, each stoppered tight against spilling. Inside the glass a measure of Grung tincture rests still. Twenty came out of the Surety's crate, and six more came up from the Calveno sewers, and they all ride with the Party now. Lift one and its neighbours shift with a small glass click.

## Play

Each vial contains a dose of the same Grung-made tincture the [[dravosi-alchemist|Crown alchemist]] threw across the Saltwright's gangplank. A dose's delivery, potency and save stay unrecorded until someone puts one to work. The Surety lot and the sewer lot have waited unexamined since the day they came aboard.

### Properties

Grung-made poison in tincture form, carried as cargo rather than ordnance. The preparation matches the canisters of the [[alchemists-bandolier|alchemist's bandolier]], and [[ruk|Ruk]] sorted the sewer six as ordinary Grung poison by sight and smell.

### In use

The vials are still packed as they were on the day they were taken. Jean-Claude keeps what he recognised in them to himself, and the Party treats them as one more prize amongst many.

## Depth

### History

Rook kept a crate of flintlocks with twenty vials of Grung tincture in his cabin aboard the HCS Surety, and the Party took the lot with the prize. After the Warren raid, [[nona-black-jaw|Nona]]'s people catalogued the sewer loot, and six vials of the same tincture came up amongst it. Ruk handled those six with care and filed them as standard stock. Surety vials and sewer vials now share one inventory, and their comparison still waits for someone who knows what they are looking at.

### Hidden truths

- The tincture is Simone's work, supplied to the [[dravosi-crown|Dravosi Crown]] and deployed in the field. Twenty vials in one crate read as a standing contract, and the line from Simone to the Crown is real and running at scale. The Party can learn this by having a vial analysed or by pressing Jean-Claude on what he knew at the gangplank, and a comparison of the two lots would raise the same question (**[[the-canister|The Canister]]**).
