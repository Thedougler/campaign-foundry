## At a glance

- **Kind.** Bandolier of poison gas canisters, standard Dravosi Crown ordnance.
- **Rarity.** Mundane.
- **Attunement.** None.
- **Changes.** Whoever claims it holds live Grung toxin weapons and the first proof of the Crown's toxin supply trail.
- **Held by.** Nobody. It lies somewhere on the [[saltwright|Saltwright]]'s deck, unexamined since the boarding.

> [!narration] First look
> A canvas bandolier the length of a forearm, stiff where spilled paste has dried into the stitching. Iron canisters the size of a flask fill every loop, stencilled with a Crown mark and a grung handprint. Each pin comes free with a one-handed pull.

## Play

Each canister makes one cloud of the Grung toxin the Party saw cross the Saltwright's gangplank. Pull the pin and throw, and the cloud hangs where it breaks. How many canisters remain is not established. At least one is spent.

### Properties

Standard-issue Dravosi Crown ordnance, manufactured from Grung hunting toxin concentrate. The canisters match the one the Dravosi alchemist deployed on the gangplank.

### In use

The alchemist unhooked one canister and threw it across the gangplank to blanket the Saltwright's deck. The Party has not examined the bandolier or counted what remains.

## Depth

### History

The bandolier was field kit for the [[dravosi-alchemist|alchemist]] in [[barnaby-rook|Barnaby Rook]]'s boarding crew. When the gust caught the deployed cloud and threw it back, the alchemist went down on the gangplank and the bandolier went wherever the fight left it. It has not been found or inventoried since.

### Hidden truths

- The canisters contain the same preparation [[jean-claude-tabarnack|Jean-Claude Tabarnack]] recognised as his sister [[simone-tabarnack|Simone]]'s batch. He has said nothing (**[[the-canister|The Canister]]**). The Party can learn it by having a canister analysed or by pressing Jean-Claude on what he knew.
- A canister traced to a Crown chandler or magazine opens the supply line its officers keep off the books. The Party can learn this by following the stencil mark.
