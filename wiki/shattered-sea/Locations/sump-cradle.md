## Areas

**Hot mud.** ==The mud is hot without a flame.== Its healing works, but a cure leaves a permanent change. Visitors can seek its benefit or refuse that cost. The approach and access terms are unknown, as are the extent of the healing and the form of its side effects. No keeper or method for determining the change is established.
