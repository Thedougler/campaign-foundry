## Areas

Tor Vessane offers a chance to investigate a real curse without an agreed account of its effects. Pilots refuse to approach it deliberately.

- **Shingle shore.** ==Low green ridges rise beyond a strip of shingle, with no sheltered harbour breaking the coast.== Recorded visitors arrive through wreck, dare or desperation. Captains ordinarily steer a wide course past the island. No preferred landing or reliable chart of the interior exists.
- **Bramble ridges.** ==Grass and waist-high salt bramble cover gently rising ground.== Open patches give a view across the island between the taller thickets, but the vegetation itself says nothing about the curse.

## Hazards

A real curse grips the island. Its nature, effects and means of release remain unknown, and conflicting sailors' stories do not settle them. A captain's choice to avoid the island is established caution and confirms none of the versions.
