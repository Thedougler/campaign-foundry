## Areas

The island offers a lead into the origins of the [[khlysty|Khlysty]] and its older teaching. Wounds do not heal here. Its shore, access, settlements and safe places are not established.

## History

The tradition behind the Flock began here. [[shepherd-grigori|Shepherd Grigori]] misunderstands the older teaching from which his work descends.
