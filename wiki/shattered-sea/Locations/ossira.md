## Areas

**Nesting beach.** ==Feathers slide underfoot across the coral spit. Seabirds nest along the narrow shore.== Visitors cross a rookery whose cast feathers shift beneath their weight. The birds' species, season and response to disturbance remain unknown; no keeper, settlement or resource is established here.
