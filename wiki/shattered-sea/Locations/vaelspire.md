## Areas

A climber can obtain one truthful answer at the summit, once. Previous climbers refuse to disclose their questions.

- **Waterline.** ==Black basalt rises sheer from the sea, with old carvings near its foot. A low hum vibrates through the stone.== There is no beach, shelf or anchorage. A boat must hold position while climbers transfer directly onto the rock. The carvings mark the beginning of the climb.
- **Upper ledges.** ==Narrow ledges and cracks interrupt the column, with black lichen above the spray and plants rooted in pockets of soil.== There is no fixed climbing route or rope line. The ledges provide interruptions in the face, not a recorded shortcut to the summit.
- **Summit.** ==The column narrows above the sea.== Reaching the top allows the climber one true question and a truthful answer. The nature of the answering power and the previous climbers' questions remain unknown.

## Hazards

The ascent begins over open water without a shore landing. Climbers need their own means of securing the ascent and returning to the waiting boat. A vessel that leaves removes the established way off the spire.
