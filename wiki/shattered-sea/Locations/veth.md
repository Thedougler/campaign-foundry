## Travel

Veth is a country-scale rainforest island. Facing shores within the Teeth can be hours apart by water, while an interior journey can take weeks. The western reef approach leads by small boat to [[veth-west-beach|Veth West Beach]]. There is no pier. Trade permission ends on the sand.

A living root stair leads from that beach into the closed forest. Four or five canopy layers, high pools and vertical clan routes let the [[grung-clans|Grung Clans]] travel above ground. Outsiders who climb leave traces and expose themselves to patrols, with retreat harder from the upper routes. Water routes lead towards [[sorn|Sorn]] and [[orak|Orak]] without granting interior access. Exact crossings and journey times are not established.

## Places

- [[veth-west-beach|Veth West Beach]] is the sanctioned western exchange point, reached from the reef in a small boat.

## Powers

The Grung Clans control the canopy and beach access. Blue-caste intermediaries conduct exchanges while higher castes watch from the treeline. A visitor who lingers beyond the invitation or climbs inland turns a trading visit into a patrol matter.
