## Areas

Every ship entering [[kalowe|Kalowe]] through its navigable reef gap passes the shrine and owes payment. The shrine occupies a stone niche in the causeway wall, sheltered beneath a salvaged timber roof.

Beside the gap: ==Under a roof of reused timber, the basin is close enough to reach from a passing hull.== The shrine's position puts its collection point beside the town's only navigable entrance. Its attendant can identify ships by their outstanding debts.

## Occupants

An unnamed [[waveservants|Waveservant]] attendant keeps the basin and collects from each hull. The shrine shares control of arrival with the [[chain-council|Chain Council]] and local pilots.

## Likely actions

Pay the hull's tribute or question the attendant about what a ship owes. The amount due and the penalty for refusing payment are not established.

## Hidden truths

The Council has increased Kalowe's tribute to conceal the [[pearl-of-souls|Pearl of Souls]] theft. Those payments let the clergy identify others involved in the affair. The attendant's knowledge of ships' debts and the shrine's collection records offer a way to investigate the payments.
