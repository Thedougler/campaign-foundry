## Areas

Crews come to this council dock to learn a route, a warning or a debt before they sail on. It is the [[midchain|Midchain]]'s information gate. The port's name is not established, and neither are the council that keeps the dock, how that council is built or the fleet route any traveller learns here.

At the jetty: ==Boats queue along the jetty, each tied off beside a different sign. A council bell rings ashore, a pilot calls depths from a deck, and dock hands shout offers across the water that don't agree with one another.==

Several local signs compete for the queue, and no authority among them overrides the others. Which token a sign honours is not established. Proof or a relationship built here answers that inconsistency. The way around the dock is reef travel, and [[midchain-reef-cut|Midchain Reef Cut]] shows what that costs.

## Likely actions

- **Trade a token or question a pilot.** This is the dock's invitation. A pilot's answer comes through Influence, and the answer is a route, a warning or a debt. A pilot's charge is not established.
- **Study the wakes and compare ledgers.** The queue's wakes and the dock's ledgers are its two sources of information. What either holds is not established, and neither is who lets a visitor read a ledger.
- **Hide cargo.** The ship can keep cargo out of sight while it waits in the queue. What the dock does about hidden cargo is not established.
- **Take shelter.** The dock shelters the ship, and the shelter creates a record of the arrival or a local debt. Who keeps the record, who holds the debt and what it asks are not established.
- **Bypass the dock.** An unregistered pilot or a [[passage|Passage]] contact can take a ship round the main dock in exchange for a favour. The bypass is reef travel.

Information bought here comes with lost privacy, time or a favour, and a ship can lose its anonymity at the counter.
