## Areas

Visitors can compare the guild's instruments or negotiate access to its measurements. [[calders-verge|Calder's Verge]] lies north across the water.

- **Clock square.** ==Water drips between the ticking clocks. Sundials crowd the open paving, their shadows at different angles.== Robed timekeepers cross the square checking instruments. They answer practical questions about schedules and maintenance but refuse to identify what their count measures. The count's purpose remains unknown.
- **Cistern shore.** ==Samphire grows below the cisterns, above pools among the shore rocks.== The coastal greens can supplement salted rations. The clock towers remain visible from the interior and orient a visitor returning towards the square.

## Occupants

A guild of clock makers maintains the instruments and controls access to its measurements. No individual member has been named. The timekeepers' routine provides an opportunity to compare readings even when they refuse to explain them.
