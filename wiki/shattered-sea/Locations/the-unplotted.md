## Areas

Crews find shelter here when charts promise open water. Its position cannot be trusted on a return voyage.

- **Bay.** ==The water lies flat between the island's green slopes while the sea outside keeps moving.== Boats enter between rock arms. The sheltered water offers a place to stop and work on a hull. Compare the calm inside with conditions beyond the heads before committing the ship.
- **Beach and slope.** ==A stream runs down to the sand beneath trees heavy with untouched fruit.== Crews can seek water and fruit on the slope. The beach connects the stream mouth with the treeline. Old wrecks sit bow-first in the bay with their masts standing and no crews visible.

## Likely actions

Compare charts with the actual horizon. The island moves, so a chart's failure to show it does not establish how recently it arrived. A crew can inspect the wrecks from a boat before taking its own ship farther into the bay.
