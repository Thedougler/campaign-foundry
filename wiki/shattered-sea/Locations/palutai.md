## Districts

Fishing families divide Palutai through a long-running reef feud. A visitor can expect pressure to join one side within an hour of arriving. No family names, leaders, district boundaries or crossing times are established.

## Disputes

The families seek recruits among arrivals before newcomers know either claim. Taking a side joins the dispute. Refusing leaves the visitor facing conscription pressure whose penalty is unknown. The feud's origin, competing demands and prospects for mediation remain unspecified.
