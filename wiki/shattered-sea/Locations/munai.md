## Areas

Visitors seek medicinal fruit here, but cannot distinguish it from potent poisons by appearance.

- **Old tree rows.** ==Fruit trees run up the slope in broken lines, with brambles filling the spaces between them.== The surviving rows show the orchard's original planting and provide a way through the growth. Cutting across the brambles slows passage and exposes hands and legs to thorns.
- **Fallen-fruit margins.** ==Goats and rats feed beneath heavy branches. Some fallen fruit has rotted, while other pieces look freshly picked.== The animals tolerate the fruit without apparent illness. Their feeding is no assurance that the same harvest is safe for visitors.

## Hazards

Cures and poisons share the same outward appearance. Choosing an attractive fruit or copying the goats' diet does not identify its effect. The records establish neither recipes nor a safe test for distinguishing them.

## History

A shipwrecked botanist planted the orchard generations ago. It has since spread beyond its original rows, with no present keeper or governing power.
