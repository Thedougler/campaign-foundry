## Travel

The Mangroves form Aruhe's southern and southeastern coast, where freshwater forest turns tidal. Root walls trap boats before dry ground. From offshore, dark seams in the green wall narrow into root tunnels, shallow water or vegetation too dense for a boat to turn around. The reliable sea approach remains [[western-landing|Western Landing]].

High tide hides mud and opens temporary channels. Low tide exposes roots, pools, burrows and ground that cannot hold a person's weight. Salt and fresh water mix unpredictably. Water depth changes from inches to several feet between roots. A patch of firm ground may sit beside mud that swallows a leg. Dense roots make every route difficult terrain. The canopy hides anything more than a few trunks away. Surf sounds close without showing a way through.

Perception or Survival, DC 15, can show a traveller a narrow tidal passage offshore, though it does not lead to shore. A Dexterity (Water Vehicles) check, DC 15, can steer a small boat through a channel long enough to back out. Shallow water, roots or a changing tide may stop the boat before then. Survival, DC 15, can find a line through shallow water. Failure drops a traveller into sucking mud. Strength (Athletics), DC 13, can pull them free. Cutting living vegetation is slow, exhausting and loud. It counts as a claim under [[take-on-aruhe|Take on Aruhe]]. Wet growth resists fire. Smoke collects beneath the canopy, and nearby life notices the flames.

Swimming is difficult among the roots. Climbing above the water takes travellers into a predator-filled canopy. A few trunks can hide one companion from another. Insects, parasites and stinging colonies fill the air. Crabs and scavengers cover exposed mud at low tide, then vanish into holes as larger animals approach. Semi-aquatic predators hide under root shelves and in dark channels. Displaced water, bubbles or a wake moving against the current can warn of one.

## Powers

[[snapping-turtle-whale|Snapping Turtle Whale]] is being bred here for the channel between Aruhe and Karath. It must grow further before it can cross. [[hinewai|Hinewai]] needs a creature bred for the water to take her army across. No fleet could contain the feral beasts. After she opens the channel, they will attack Grung without distinction. Her aversion to water and distaste for leaving Aruhe also hold the army back.

## Hidden truths

Hinewai's pale glowing roots reach through Aruhe and connect her to the island's flora. Gold-caste scorched-earth raids have killed roots and wounded her. Her phylactery reaches as far as those roots do.
