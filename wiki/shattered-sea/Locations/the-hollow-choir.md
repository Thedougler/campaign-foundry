## Areas

**Tide caves.** ==A voice inside the caves returns as a choir, singing in harmony.== Speech and song both acquire the caves' harmony. Visitors can test the effect with their voices. The entrances and tidal access are unknown, and no source establishes a cause or further power behind the sound.
