> [!narration] Arrival
> You sail a bent chain of islands weeks beyond the last charted coast. Reef water, storm belts and mountain harbours divide the route, and every crossing leaves a record, a debt or a secret.

## Travel

Open water links the [[crown-islands|Crown Islands]], [[aruhe|Aruhe]], [[karath|Karath]], [[murrat|Murrat]], [[kalowe|Kalowe]] and [[sparhold|Sparhold]]. [[uncertainty|Uncertainty]]'s dawn run south raised [[sparhold|Sparhold]] to starboard first, its supplies and settled streets this close to the [[verdant-teeth|Verdant Teeth]]. Gizanmor and [[murrat|Murrat]] followed, with [[karath|Karath]] and Sorn beyond. [[aruhe|Aruhe]] lay half a mile off Karath across the channel to port, and Jean-Claude Tabarnack recognised Karath as lying close to his homeland. Dozens of smaller islands packed close together carry free ports, local councils and pilot families whose routes come down through generations rather than charts. The northern reef faces look across the [[central-strait|Central Strait]] toward the Crown Islands. A minotaur pilot is the most expensive crew hire in the Midchain, and the price buys passage through the hardest water. Minotaur communities sit where the water is hardest, in every major port.

The treeline often shows before land. Rainforest reaches the water on many coasts, while elsewhere pale limestone cliffs fall straight to reef. Turquoise shallows give way abruptly to blue-black water at the shelf edge.

The inhabited tropical shelf runs about 1,800 miles from [[kalowe|Kalowe]]'s approaches to [[the-tail|The Tail]]. Low green islands crowd the channels. Council bells compete with pilots calling depths and workers offering berths. Brine, fruit, pitch and cooking smoke drift across the water. Signal flags pass news between docks faster than distant officers can follow it.

Weather coasts are forest and cliff. Sheltered coasts give way to mangrove and reef flat. Deep-draught hulls find few easy landings outside Kalowe. Pilots divide the shelf into five bands for convenience, and their charts disagree about where each begins.

- **Reef channels.** Sail between neighbouring islands, usually half a day apart, paying a pilot or buying local waypoints. Fresh water and shelter are rarely far away, but charts disagree and reefs shift. Avoid unnamed blue holes, even when they offer shelter. Pilots associate some with Sawek lairs. [[knifes-wake|Knife's Wake]] can escape through channels a frigate captain refuses to enter.
- **Open lanes.** Sail between the established harbours without threading the inner reefs. The ship avoids confined passages but loses shelter and remains visible to patrols and raiders. Crown cutters intercept the predictable lanes.
- **Westbound back channels.** Near [[the-doldrums|The Doldrums]], winds turn more westerly and help ships avoid Strait inspection. The [[passage|Passage]] knows which channels are clean. Buy current route knowledge before departure, because the Doldrums' seasonal drift changes the southern approach.

Replenish water and seek sheltered anchorages among the islands before an exposed crossing. [[kalowe|Kalowe]] provides repairs, pilots and supplies, with a shrine payment at its reef gap. Rest in a harbour is sheltered from weather, but arrival can expose the ship to local authorities.

Treat reef routes as Difficult Terrain for vessels. Birds over a shoal, breaking foam and missing reef markers warn of bad water. A failed passage costs time or position, but does not erase the only clue to the route ahead. Chart and wake-reading use the Study action. Bargaining with pilots uses Influence, and handling signals and reef marks uses the Utilize action. A Passage contact or an unregistered pilot can bypass the main dock in exchange for a favour. The [[midchain-council-dock|Midchain Council Dock]] offers shelter at the cost of a record of the ship's arrival or a local debt, and the [[midchain-reef-cut|Midchain Reef Cut]] gives the choice between time and information.

### Pilot bands

- **West.** The largest islands stack into the haze around [[kalowe|Kalowe]], and the shelf drops into open sea beyond the last western island. Nearby lie [[penance-bar|Penance Bar]], [[ashkevet|Ashkevet]] and [[sabrique|Sabrique]].
- **North.** A defended strip two or three islands deep lies between the Central Strait and the Verdant Teeth. Permission matters as much as payment. It holds [[murrat|Murrat]], [[sparhold-isle|Sparhold Isle]] with [[sparhold|Sparhold]], [[yssenmoor|Yssenmoor]], [[the-unplotted|The Unplotted]] and [[huahei|Huahei]]. It also holds [[adwe|Adwe]], [[corrigans-rest|Corrigan's Rest]], [[barrow-ait|Barrow Ait]], [[drovannis|Drovannis]], [[calders-verge|Calder's Verge]], [[chronehall|Chronehall]], [[old-correspondence|Old Correspondence]], [[ilo-maren|Ilo Maren]] and [[nemwe|Nemwe]]. The rest are [[munai|Munai]], [[kwoss|Kwoss]], [[whitmoth-hollow|Whitmoth Hollow]], [[vetau|Vetau]], [[otanu|Otanu]], [[thessit|Thessit]], [[the-foundling-shoals|The Foundling Shoals]], [[quillane|Quillane]], [[tor-vessane|Tor Vessane]], [[ghal-idrissa|Ghal Idrissa]], [[the-long-wager|The Long Wager]] and [[vaelspire|Vaelspire]].
- **Verdant Teeth.** Five country-sized rainforest islands have facing shores hours apart and interiors that take weeks to cross: [[veth]], [[sorn]], [[karath]], [[dreth]] and [[orak]]. Sanctioned beach trade stops at the treeline.
- **South.** Steep volcanic islands have terraced slopes and short beaches. Crews negotiate their landing on the sand. [[halewick|Halewick]], [[blackrule|Blackrule]] and [[slackwater|Slackwater]] anchor the band, with [[ket-solane|Ket-Solane]], [[feyroa|Feyroa]], [[ossira|Ossira]], [[palutai|Palutai]], [[ravasol|Ravasol]], [[sable-roost|Sable Roost]], [[sanvashti|Sanvashti]], [[sump-cradle|Sump Cradle]], [[the-hollow-choir|The Hollow Choir]], [[twineye|Twineye]] and [[velk-ithera|Velk-Ithera]] among the smaller stops.
- **East.** Islands thin out beyond the Teeth except for [[aruhe|Aruhe]]. Farther east, charts and reef soundings become unreliable as the route approaches the Maw. The islands here include [[amberfall|Amberfall]], [[cambreys-fold|Cambrey's Fold]], [[coeur-bris|Coeur-Bris]], [[corvenna|Corvenna]], [[duskveil|Duskveil]], [[ferrymoor|Ferrymoor]], [[gullmoth-reach|Gullmoth Reach]], [[lethys|Lethys]], [[old-harrows-bones|Old Harrow's Bones]], [[otel-karn|Otel-Karn]] and [[ash-caldera|Ash Caldera]]. [[halythion|Halythion]] lies under Teikhinos Reef outside colonial surveys. Near the Maw, [[fathomrush|Fathomrush]] stages dives on the [[shelfworks|Shelfworks]], while the [[tail-staging-island|Tail's staging island]] serves the same last-supplies purpose.

## Powers

Island councils, free-port workers and pilot families govern most arrivals. Local access depends on harbour debts, council tokens and kinship more than distant law. The [[dravosi-crown|Dravosi Crown]] wants records and inspections where councils defend their autonomy. [[passage|Passage]] moves people through the same water beyond Crown inspection, while [[grung-clans|Grung Clans]] raid the routes. A ship can lose its anonymity while buying information at a council dock.

The southern islands have not sought the [[chain-council|Chain Council]]'s help against Grung pressure.

## History

The modern powers formed around the routes and the Maw. The raiding fleet has taken more than 314 people. Its trail led through the Midchain towards Sparhold before Karath was identified as the captives' destination.

[[simone-tabarnack|Simone Tabarnack]], Jean-Claude's younger sister, served the Sorn garrison. [[pell|Pell]], a gnome enslaved there, spoke to Jean-Claude with familiarity before his death in the reprisal that drove Jean-Claude to flee.

## Hidden truths

The charts omit islands and borders. Passage demands time, tribute and trust, and the tribute system may help hold the Maw fissure.
