## Areas

Bring a strange specimen to trade for tidal knowledge. The observatory has no deep anchorage, and its causeway disappears at high water.

- **Tide-pool shore.** ==Robed scholars call measurements over pools below a tilted dome.== Boats thread the shallow approach. A rock causeway connects the shore with the observatory at low tide and floods twice a day. Take a boat alongside or plan to wait if business runs beyond the low-water crossing.
- **Observatory.** ==Brass instruments hang crooked beneath a cracked dome. Water fills the collapsed nave below them.== The scholar-mages measure tides and currents from a building that sinks farther each year. Their instruments and records provide the knowledge they offer in barter.

## Hazards

High water floods the causeway and deepens the nave. Finish the crossing before the tide turns, retain a boat or wait for the next low tide. The rate and cause of the observatory's sinking remain unknown.

## Occupants

An independent colony of scholar-mages values truly new specimens and anomalies over impressive presentation. They exchange real knowledge for an unfamiliar find. Dried sea fennel is their ordinary coin export.
