## Areas

**Truce terrace.** ==Separate fires burn around the mossed stone. The groups watch one another without drawing weapons.== Worn approaches lead to a meeting ground shared by rival [[grung-clans|Grung Clans]]. Each group brings food and watchers to its campfire. Choosing a group makes a visitor's audience plain to the others. No permanent population is established at this terrace.

## Likely actions

Visitors can address rival clans together or approach a group under the scrutiny of its rivals. An agreement older than the current participants keeps weapons out of the meeting. Safety depends on that truce holding. Its penalties and enforcers are unknown.
