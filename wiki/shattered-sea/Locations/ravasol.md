## Districts

Ravasol stands on stilts above the water. The village's important business happens on the rooftops, controlled by children who pass gossip through their network. Anyone seeking local information needs to reach that network rather than assume the adults below can arrange a deal. The way from the water to the roofs, crossing times and the children's terms are unknown.
