## Areas

The islet gives access to [[sparhold|Sparhold]] and its harbour, the nearest port to the [[verdant-teeth|Verdant Teeth]].

- **Harbour slope.** ==Bare slopes rise from the harbour to timber walls facing the Teeth.== The half-mile islet lies at the southern lip of the northern island strip. Land through the settlement's harbour; berths, services and local authority are on Sparhold's page.
- **Cut-over ground.** ==The slopes above the walls lack their old spar trees.== The former timber stand fell for the fortifications. Logging crews now work the Teeth's fringe rather than an untouched forest on this islet.

## History

The fortress-market occupies part of the islet's harbour slope. Its walls consumed the island's local spar stand and pushed logging towards Grung-held shores.
