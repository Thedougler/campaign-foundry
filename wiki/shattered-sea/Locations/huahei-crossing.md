## Areas

People come to the crossing for Feywild loot, or for a bargain no one else will make.

### The spirals

Spiral and ring patterns are worn into the moss at the centre of [[huahei|Huahei]]. They are a soft spot between the Feywild and the mortal world, and nobody built them or guards them. Fey slip across onto the island regularly, and every desperate mortal who comes for the island's magic ends up here, where it lies thickest.

Stand at the centre long enough: ==The light turns the colour of sunset, and the air smells of cut grass.==

Feywild things sink into the bog on the far side of the island and turn up in Huahei's marsh like flotsam, magic items among them. Every item that comes off Huahei works, and each has a fey prank attached. Latent magic lies thick across the island.

## Hazards

### The Wild Hunt

On some nights the spirals wake fully, the crossing opens wide and a Wild Hunt comes through. No two witnesses describe it the same way.

- **Sign.** Every [[glutehorn|Glutehorn]] on the island stops tooting. Anyone who knows Huahei runs for the boats at that quiet. The Glutehorns scatter, the [[redcap|redcaps]] hide in the reeds and the hags bar their doors.
- **Effect.** The Hunt takes whoever is still standing in the open. Nobody knows what becomes of them. Now and then one comes back years later, no older than when they left, and will not speak of it.
- **Answers.** Leave on the boats at the quiet. Anyone caught ashore can stay out of the open, as the redcaps and the hags do.
- **Rumour.** Midchain sailors trade the story as a harbour rumour.

## Occupants

- [[huahei-coven|Huahei Coven]]. Three hags wait at the crossing, where every desperate mortal ends up. They do not hunt. They bargain, and the Party may deal with them someday.
- The fey that cross, from least to most dangerous. The [[glutehorn|Glutehorn]] is the least troublesome. A [[noggle-ransacker|noggle]] goes for the boats and unpicks them plank by plank. [[sprite|Sprites]] and [[pixie|pixies]] work charms, sleep and small transformations. The [[redcap|redcaps]] kill.

## Likely actions

- **Bargain with the hags.** The green hag trades help finding a way through the bog for an obligation on a later visit. Other bargains depend on the Party's request.
- **Search the marsh for loot.** Feywild items sink on the far side of the island and surface in the marsh. Each one works, and a fey prank comes with it.
