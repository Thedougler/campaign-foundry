## Areas

Travellers can wait here for passage through the fog, but the ferry doesn't follow a schedule. At the departure point: ==Fog hides the crossing.== Its landing arrangements, destination, route, fare and waiting time remain unknown.

## Occupants

The ferry's unnamed captain does not age. Their appearance, motives and presence between departures are not established. The crossing has no established affiliation.

## Likely actions

Waiting may bring a chance to board, but there is no reliable departure to plan around. Travellers can decline the crossing and stay with the Midchain's uncertain reef routes. Neither a direct route onward nor a travel time is established.
