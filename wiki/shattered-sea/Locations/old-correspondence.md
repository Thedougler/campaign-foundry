## Areas

The cottage and its drafts offer a chance to investigate a letter that has occupied one life for decades.

- **Landing path.** ==A narrow path climbs from the shore to a stone cottage, its single window bright with lamplight.== The low grass and scrub leave the approach visible from the cottage. The path provides the established route between the shore and the door.
- **Writing room.** ==An old scribe bends over a page beside stacks of crossed-out drafts.== He continues revising the same unfinished letter. His lamp burns day and night. The drafts establish the long labour but do not identify the addressee.

## Occupants

The scribe is the islet's sole resident. Interruptions meet dry patience and a claim that the letter is nearly finished. His name and the recipient's identity remain unknown.
