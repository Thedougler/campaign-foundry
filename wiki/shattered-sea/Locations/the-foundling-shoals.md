## Areas

The reef lies north of [[sparhold|Sparhold]]. Visitors can accompany the foundling watch or investigate an arrival before the tide covers its sandbar.

- **Sandbars.** ==Small waves wash over a pale reef. At dawn, a local fisher checks the exposed sand.== Unharmed infants have appeared here for generations. The local fishers watch for them and take each child into their care. Low-tide bars offer walking routes and shellfish gathering.
- **Reef channels.** ==Eelgrass bends below the water between bars coated with sea lettuce.== Incoming water separates the sandbars. A boat or a retreat before the tide rises avoids isolation on an exposed bar.

## Occupants

Local fishers treat raising the foundlings as a sacred duty. No named guardian or governing organisation is established.

## Hidden truths

The infants' origin, the cause of their appearances and the timing of the next arrival remain unknown.
