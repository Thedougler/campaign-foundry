## Areas

Crews come to trade on the sand without entering the farms. Arriving behind the reef: ==Pale sand runs up to a long-hut. Past it, crop lanes stand in water, and the nearest row is already going to vine.== A purple-caste garrison keeps the post, and blue-caste intermediaries handle brief exchanges. Raids do not launch from here. The farms beyond are closed to traders.

## Hazards

Floodwater turns a lane into a channel by afternoon, so an inland path at dawn can strand a trespasser. The ground behind the first rows is last season's field under vine. Extra patrols moving inland signal the hunt for [[jean-claude-tabarnack|Jean-Claude Tabarnack]].

## Occupants

[[simone-tabarnack|Simone Tabarnack]] has commanded the garrison here, and the [[botukuri|Botukuri]] farm the lanes behind it. Asking for either on the sand risks making the family hunt visible.

## Likely actions

Crews can wait offshore and trade through intermediaries. They can also ask for the Botukuri or Simone, or enter the lanes as trespassers. Leaving before the water rises is the safe retreat.
