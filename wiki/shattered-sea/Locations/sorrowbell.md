## Areas

The Party can investigate the shrine's records and witness the loss of sanctuary. Its headland position and causeway approach make it the village's visible religious authority.

- **Front door and temple.** Enter from the stone causeway. ==The village bell sounds from the shrine at the causeway's end.== [[corvin-ashe|Corvin Ashe]] preaches here. At the confrontation, a demon comes through the front door without reprisal. That entrance is the first proof that Tyr has withdrawn.
- **Locked quarters.** ==A lock bars the door to the bishop's quarters.== Inside is the true ledger, whose entries disagree with the public tally. Comparing the two gives evidence of Ashe's fabrication. A method of opening the door and the ledger's exact text are not established.

## Occupants

Ashe is Bishop of Penance Bar and the shrine's priest of [[tyr|Tyr]]. He uses its standing to send the town and the Party against the woods coven. The [[penance-bar-siege|Penance Bar Siege]] records the confrontation he cannot conceal.

## Hidden truths

The building's appearance gives away nothing about its lost protection. It is already unhallowed. Once the Party sees a demon enter freely, they can understand why [[sandy-grove|Sandy Grove]] is the island's last safe ground, just as a mob is preparing to burn it. The rule behind the entrance and Ashe's transformation is [[sin-and-sanctuary|Sin and Sanctuary]].

The Party can take the ledger into the village as material proof of the fabrication, and the demon's accusation provides testimony about its cause. Offer both routes to discovery without making one successful roll essential.
