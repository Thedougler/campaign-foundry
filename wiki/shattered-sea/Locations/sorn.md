## Travel

Sorn is a country-scale rainforest island whose facing shores can be hours from another Tooth, while interior travel can take weeks. The worked reef approach leads to [[sorn-guarded-beach|Sorn Guarded Beach]]. Sanctioned visitors trade on its sand through blue-caste intermediaries and cannot enter the farms.

Beyond the beach, seasonal floodwater changes crop lanes into channels between dawn and afternoon. The darker, vine-covered band beyond the first rows marks ground the forest has reclaimed. Freshly cut field edges and flood routes lead inland towards a closed ridge. Small-boat exchanges connect Sorn with [[veth|Veth]] and [[karath|Karath]], without granting permission to enter either interior. Exact crossings, safe rest places and journey times are not established.

## Places

- [[sorn-guarded-beach|Sorn Guarded Beach]] provides the island's controlled landing and garrison post.

## Powers

The [[grung-clans|Grung Clans]] control the farms and entry inland, and the [[botukuri|Botukuri]] work the flood-lane fields. A purple-caste garrison watches the landing. Extra inland patrols signal the hunt for [[jean-claude-tabarnack|Jean-Claude Tabarnack]].

## History

Jean-Claude's younger sister [[simone-tabarnack|Simone]] held a post in the island's garrison. [[pell|Pell]], a gnome enslaved here, greeted Jean-Claude as an old acquaintance. Pell died in the reprisal after Jean-Claude freed slaves, and Jean-Claude fled.
