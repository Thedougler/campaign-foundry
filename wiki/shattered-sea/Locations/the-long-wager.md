## Areas

The same contest has run for generations, begun by two captains that nobody now alive remembers.

- **Course clearing.** ==Rival teams argue beside a marked course worn bare by their feet.== Both crews continue their original rivalry. Dune grass borders the course, and tide pools below its western marker offer food between heats. What the contest measures and what counts as victory remain unknown.
- **Scoreboard and ledger.** ==Old tally marks cover the scoreboard. Beside it, a woman checks a ledger without joining the argument.== The neutral referee rules the contest. Her ledger gives visitors a way to check a disputed call rather than rely on either crew's account.

## Occupants

Two rival crews keep competing under one neutral, ageless judge. Neither the crews nor the referee has an established name. Her unchanged age is a fact, but its cause is unknown.
