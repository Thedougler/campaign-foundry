> [!narration] Entering
> The Flat Water stands beyond a low, salt-whitened wall in front of you, set back from the causeway under a roof of patched timber and layered palm. Its walls are stone. Pickled greens and rum scent the doorway, where the room opens towards a broad lagoon terrace and a staircase climbs to the upper floor. Behind the bar, a tortle woman sets down a mug. She works over a dark plank on two casks, below a board listing meals, drinks and beds.

## Areas

The Party can eat, find a berth and ask [[reweti-kahurangi|Reweti Kahurangi]] about local work, including contact with the [[five-blades|Five Blades]].

### Drinking room

==A dark plank spans two casks. Beyond it, the terrace opens onto the lagoon.== From the main causeway on [[kalowe|Kalowe]]'s second island, a short path leads past the low wall to the entrance. Meals are available from mid-morning until late at night. Reweti serves alone behind the bar, where visitors order food, settle tabs and arrange beds. Crews exchange rumours at the tables, though a story heard here still needs checking. The terrace opens off the room, and the stairs lead to the accommodation. Reweti will not serve anyone who starts trouble.

### Lagoon terrace and mooring

==Below the terrace, dinghies lie on the lagoon.== Crews take meals and drinks outside within sight of their boats. The lagoon-side landing connects the mooring to the terrace. Visitors can approach from the water instead of the causeway, then enter the drinking room to order at the bar or arrange a berth upstairs. The mooring fee covers a dinghy's stay, but does not include guarded storage.

### Upper floor

==Hammocks hang together upstairs. A private room has a window, and its door has no lock.== Reweti assigns berths downstairs before guests climb the stairs. Hammock guests share their space. The private room provides a door and privacy, but its lack of a lock makes it unsuitable for securing valuables. The stairs return to the drinking room.

Optional visit hook: an unnamed guest has paid cash for three nights in the captain's room, and nobody has seen them leave. The guest's identity and reason for staying remain unknown. Reweti can confirm the payment and lack of sightings, but does not know what has happened inside. Knocking at the door or looking through the window can begin an investigation. The hook leaves the occupant and outcome unknown. Outside this hook, the room is available at the listed price.

## Occupants

[[reweti-kahurangi|Reweti Kahurangi]], a tortle proprietor and former deckhand, works behind the bar from mid-morning until the last guest leaves. She wants orders paid for and conversations kept civil. Her page gives her responses to customers and the limits of her once-only useful observations.

### Ask for information or an introduction

Reweti listens to customers who settle their tabs and keep the peace. Payment does not oblige her to share everything she knows. She may volunteer one useful fact without prompting, then turns back to her work and never repeats it. Follow-up questions can seek a different fact, but pressure closes the conversation.

Reweti knows how to reach the [[five-blades|Five Blades]]. A prospective employer can give her a job enquiry to pass on. They decide whether to accept it and set their own price. Reweti cannot guarantee their availability.
