## Areas

Visit to hear the coven's account or reach the island's surviving sanctuary. Half a day's walk inland, past the last worked field, the old-growth stand begins. ==The furrows stop at the old trees. The plough has gone around them.== The [[sandy-grove-coven|Sandy Grove Coven]] lives here, with [[halda-wren|Halda Wren]] splitting kindling on a stump while visitors talk. She opens with "Ask what you came to ask."

## Occupants

About a dozen hedge-druids make their home here, including children. They have traded herbs and willow bark for salt fish since before the village shrine had a priest. The town never invited them in, so they stayed in the woods. They are harmless and have never sought a quarrel with it.

## Pressure

The [[penance-bar-siege|Penance Bar Siege]] starts its clock when the mob organises and marches. Get the truth to the crowd before it reaches the grove. Late arrivals must slow or stop people who are themselves victims of Ashe's lie. If the mob burns the stand, the coven's people die or scatter and the island loses its last hallowed ground at the swarm's strongest point.

## Hidden truths

An old archfey of the wood still maintains its claim here. The town never learned its name. A fiend entering the grove would face the patron's immediate reprisal under [[sin-and-sanctuary|Sin and Sanctuary]]. Unlike [[sorrowbell|Sorrowbell]], this site is still hallowed.
