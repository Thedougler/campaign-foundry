> [!narration] Arrival
> From the deck, the island fills the horizon end to end, a green volcano rising around a black crater lake. Gold-tan bands cross its lower slopes, and the rainforest shoulders down to limestone cliffs at the shore. White water foams along a reef that follows the whole coast. The green runs unbroken to the foam, uncut by road or field, unlit by any smoke. Then you see the way in. A dark lane of flat water runs through the reef toward the beach.

## Travel

Aruhe lies along the Midchain's inner edge near the [[verdant-teeth|Verdant Teeth]], with a half-mile channel between it and [[karath|Karath]]. The island is about 500 miles long and 150 miles across. Crossing straight over takes many days, while travelling lengthwise takes weeks. No roads, towns, fields or maintained passes cross it. The River is the nearest thing to a road, but current, shelves and otter families make it unsafe infrastructure.

[[western-landing|Western Landing]] is the reliable sea approach. The reef and shore are patrolled by Grung. Free Grung keep to their boats, and the sail that works the eastern reef when the water lies flat stays offshore. The sand has kept every free Grung that tried it. The Burnt Road records hundreds of compelled attempts on the island, and dead Grung and their spent seals line the route. Jean-Claude Tabarnack told the Party the Grung treat Aruhe as forbidden ground. The Grung keep this danger secret from outsiders: Aruhe lies too close to the [[verdant-teeth|Verdant Teeth]] for anyone else to come ashore safely.

## Rumors

- Fallen fruit is receiving. Fruit picked from living growth is a claim. Aruhe is many living systems made excessive, not one monster.
- Intent outweighs the act. Fruit the wind drops is safe. A tree felled on purpose for its fruit brings attacks from the island's native species. Some intelligence understands intent, and the island's life speaks in ways the Party cannot yet follow.

## History

Hinewai preserved her drowned [[the-unnamed-companion|companion]] at [[memorial-grove|Memorial Grove]]. The Death Bloom began as the tree, two graves, black flowers and bound soil together. Her pale roots have spread it beneath the whole island. Her vow, spoken from inside her own grave, made the island's taking-law and gave the Grung their name for it: the Hungry Isle. The raid and its aftermath are recorded in [[grung-and-the-making-of-aruhe|Grung and the Making of Aruhe]].

### Session 9: castaways before the Party's landing

A storm tore a Grung fleet vessel open on the reef two days out of [[calven-and-calveno|Calveno]]. Three purple Grung guards washed ashore with captives. The captives sheltered beneath the broken hull. An old bearded captive picked a peach-sized berry, and a grey blur dragged him screaming into the brush. [[wolfrabbit|Wolfrabbit]] packs then preyed on the beach's castaways, spreading in thirty-foot arcs across the channel mouth.

The tide erased the drag marks while the wreck remained wedged against the coral. A guard was killed in the surf. The cold open left the other two guards' fate uncertain.

### Session 10: landfall, rescue and the island's rule

The [[uncertainty|Uncertainty]] raised Aruhe at midday off [[karath|Karath]], with limestone cliffs and rainforest shouldering out of the sea. The crow's nest counted several vessel hulls on shore beneath a campfire-scale smoke still burning. Delmar Fisk threaded the reef gaps on a save of 24. [[crissdalynn-khinriss|Crissdalynn]] and Delmar flew two castaways from the beach to the ship.

Aboard, the castaways told of the raid that wrecked them. They counted between a hundred and a hundred and fifty captives on the island's sand, and thought few still lived. Fishing off the beach kept them fed. They said the island killed anyone who took from it. Those who tried the terraces came back calling them safe, then vanished. The DM called the isle cursed. Sandro added that it \"really hates the Grung\": its animals will choose death if the death buys a Grung's end. Perrin worked the law out loud, and the DM confirmed it. The frenzy punishes everyone on the island, whether they pick fruit or not.

### Session 12: the island's intent

A bite of a [[giants-guava|Giant's Guava]] at the river camp raised [[crissdalynn-khinriss|Crissdalynn]]'s Wisdom for an hour. She read the island's law past the fruit rule: fruit the wind drops is safe, while a tree felled on purpose for fruit draws attacks from native species. Some intelligence here understands intent. She passed the warning to the table, and the Party took it that the island's life speaks in ways they cannot yet follow.

The camp's fishing and cooking passed without the island stirring. During the falcon's attack, an explosive shot set a tree alight, and creatures rustled through every bush as they ran from the burn. Noise and blood later drew several things through the forest towards the camp where Perrin and the survivors sat.

The return from the pursuit added a second trail of smoke to the first on the horizon, one smaller and closer, the other half a day off. At the road camp, a Perception 25 left Crissdalynn sure she was being watched. Foraging turned up three fallen [[rotheart|Rotheart]] fruits among the blackened grass, their broken flesh dark as old blood.

## Hidden truths

Pale roots lead from Clear Lake through the Marshes into the graves, pulsing with a faint glow in the dark beneath the island. Over the centuries since the graves were dug, they have spread [[hinewai|Hinewai]] across Aruhe. The Gold caste is wrong that breaking the graves would destroy her. Its scorched-earth attacks have killed roots that connect her to the island's flora and wounded her. The roots mark the outer limit of her phylactery.

Removing [[hinewai|Hinewai]]'s presence realistically means burning down most of Aruhe, and no one who loves the island calls that a victory. She loves Aruhe back, and her presence stays unhealthy for its flora and fauna all the same.

The Party learned that fallen fruit is safe while living fruit is dangerous. Matteo Scola wants passage off the island, and an unseen watcher remains in the garden.
