## Areas

Passing crews use Vetau's anchorage between daily boils. There is no permanent settlement.

- **Lagoon anchorage.** ==Steam lifts from the shallows beyond a dry lip of stone.== The lagoon covers a geothermal vent. Fishers and visiting crews can shelter here outside the midday boil. The same water becomes impassable when the vent heats it.
- **Vent rocks.** ==Yellow mineral growth crusts the rock around the steaming throat.== Sulphur moss provides the material for a joint-ache remedy traded by passing fishers. Harvesting near the vent requires attention to the daily closure.

## Hazards

At noon, scalding water closes the only safe anchorage for an hour. Steam over the shallows warns of the heat. Leave before noon or wait ashore on dry ground until the boil ends instead of wading or launching through it. A crew caught inside cannot use the anchorage as an immediate escape route.
