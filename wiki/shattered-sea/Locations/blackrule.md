## Areas

**Boat stair.** ==A narrow stair descends the black rock to a place for one small boat. Bare rock meets the water on either side.== The stair is the only approach from the water and leads to the hall above. A second boat must wait offshore.

**Chapel and barracks.** ==A sleeping bench runs along one wall of the hall, and scales decorate the altar at its far end.== One room serves as chapel and barracks. The scales mark its dedication to [[tyr|Tyr]]. Blackrule has no garrison or staff, and no kitchen or stores are established.

## Occupants

[[aleksander-malone|Aleksander Malone]] chose this austere chapter house because he despised the comforts of civilisation. The [[dravosi-crown|Dravosi Crown]] formerly kept him here between deployments, controlling the reports and transport offered to him. He has since left [[calven-and-calveno|Calveno]] and is pursuing the Party through the Midchain. Do not place him waiting in Blackrule while that pursuit continues.
