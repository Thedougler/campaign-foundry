## Areas

Lethys offers a landfall for observing a wild griffon colony or investigating its origin. At the cliffs: ==Griffons occupy the high ground above the water.== The colony descends from an aristocrat's escaped menagerie and has lived wild for generations. A safe anchorage, route up the cliffs and any remaining menagerie structures are not established.

## Occupants

Feral [[griffon|Griffons]] inhabit the island. Their numbers, nests, territory and response to visitors are unknown, as are any current human inhabitants.

## History

The menagerie's aristocratic owner is dead. Neither their name nor the location of an estate or enclosure is recorded.
