## At a glance

- **Draws the Party because.** It is the first inland route and the terrace road to Grasslands.
- **Entrance.** Inland from [[Western Landing]].
- **Occupants.** Grinning Apes, Wolfrabbits, Vine-Lash, Young Snakewood, [[Grubnade]] and Black Lotuses.
- **Danger.** Collapsed steps, living hazards and tempting fruit.
- **Prize.** Fallen Redheart Berries, Giant's Guavas and routes to Grasslands or Quiet.

> [!narration] Entering
> Moss-dark terraces stack up the forest slope. Water runs through old ditches. Red berries hang at the lips and ribbed guavas bow over lower steps while split fruit sweetens the wet stone.

## Play

### Areas

Terrace route, green route, hanging vines, low-canopy lanes, irrigation ditches and collapsed steps.

### Hazards

Vine-Lash, Young Snakewood, [[Grubnade]] and Black Lotuses occupy the route. Living fruit is covered by [[Taking on Aruhe]].

### Occupants

[[Creatures/Grinning Ape|Grinning Apes]] patrol canopy. [[Wolfrabbit]]s den in the terrace caves and work the collapsed terraces at dawn and dusk.

### Likely actions

Follow terrace edges to skip hazards, take fallen fruit, climb toward [[Grasslands]], or enter the green route to [[The Quiet]].

## Depth

### History

The terraces are older than Hinewai's law and once carried water through planted steps.

#### Session 10: the landing party's crossing

The Session 10 landing party came up from [[Western Landing]] into the terraces on passed survival rolls. From above, the open terrace showed dark wet stone, broken steps and flooded ditches holding green stagnant water. Bowling-ball orange fruit fermented thick on the vine, and its rotting smell hung over the terrace walls. Many small holes opened through the stone. The Party read a stone settlement the jungle took back centuries ago.

Nothing on the terraces made a sound, and thousands of glowing eyes watched from the shadows without moving. The garden's plants grow muted rather than bright. Basketball-sized oranges, scarlet berries and large light-green guavas hang bright against that muted growth.

The crossing showed the gardens' hazards. A [[Black Lotus]] snapped shut around a wolfrabbit that walked past, one squeal and a crunch before the flower sat still. Red-eyed wolfrabbits nested in the terrace caves, and their dens held piles of bones and no fruit. A caterpillar on a leaf swelled as Jean-Claude's hand came near, and it settled once he drew back. A reed topped by a bulb swelled as he stepped toward it. Darts burst from it on every side, and he leapt clear. The stalk regrew until his arrow cut it down. Blood stood in front of nearly every fruit bush, marking at least six deaths, some with a skull set before the bush.

A [[Creatures/Grinning Ape|Grinning Ape]] watched from beyond the terrace wall, its grin split unnaturally wide. It ran as the unseen great shape bore down through the trees. A parrot hailed the Party from below the treeline and gave Delmar's own thoughts back to him in his own voice. The [[Unsaid Macaw]] has that trick, though the bird's own name never came up in play.

The first walk ended above the gardens. Trees moved in from several directions on Jean-Claude's position, and about a dozen wolfrabbits burst from the terraces toward the same spot. The Party hauled Jean-Claude into the air, and the pack killed its own first arrival for finding nothing. The [[Crown Squid]] beneath it all followed the Party to the treeline and stopped where the terraces begin. It snatched two wolfrabbits in frustration and dragged them beneath its mantle. Then it vanished into the trees.

### Hidden truths

Following the edge preserves a safer route while the tempting centre tests whether travellers understand receiving and taking.

An unseen watcher remains in the garden after lantern light drove it from a dead porcupine.
