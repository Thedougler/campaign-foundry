## Areas

The [[dravosi-crown|Dravosi Crown]] sends suspected plague ships here under a quarantine with no fixed end. Nobody sent here is known to leave.

- **Stockade gate.** ==The quarantine flag rots on its pole above grey timber. The gate bell's rope has frayed through.== The stockade decays without a keeper. Repairing the rope allows visitors to ring the bell, but nobody living is present to answer it.
- **Quarantine harbour.** ==Three ships ride at anchor without smoke from their galleys or anyone on deck.== All three crews are absent, with no visible route of departure. The hulls and stockade can be searched for evidence, but neither cargo nor the truth of the suspected disease is established.

## Hazards

The quarantine marks a disease risk without confirming its cause or present spread. Boarding a ship commits the visitors to that uncertainty. There is no established treatment or official release procedure.

## History

The Crown's flag remains raised over an indefinite quarantine. No returning keeper has ended it, and the empty harbour does not explain what happened to its crews.
