## Areas

Crews seek harbour decisions or bring complaints to the [[chain-council|Chain Council]] here. The seized fort stands on the height above [[kalowe|Kalowe]]'s dry docks. Its approach and entry arrangements are not established.

In the council's meeting place: ==One seat stands empty among the places where the council meets.== Kalowe refuses to install a local ruler. The fort's internal layout and hierarchy remain unknown.

## Occupants

The Chain Council meets inside the fort and controls part of the town's arrival arrangements. Its members are unnamed. Complaints about Grung raids have accumulated here, but the Council has not established a decision about changing beach trade with the [[verdant-teeth|Verdant Teeth]].

## Likely actions

Bring raided crews' testimony to the Council or seek a harbour decision. The Council's power to raise tribute gives it leverage over visiting ships.

## History

The fort was seized before becoming the Council's meeting place. Who held it before the seizure and how the Council took possession are not established.
