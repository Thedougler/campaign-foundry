## Areas

Otanu offers local readings of the smoke and a safe path across active volcanic ground.

- **Fishing shore.** ==Fishers look up from their nets towards a thin plume above the central cone.== Coarse sand and tide pools line the shore below ash-fed scrub. When the plume leans wrong, locals haul in their gear and refuse to sail. Visitors can ask the fishers for a reading before setting out towards [[quillane|Quillane]] or [[vetau|Vetau]].
- **Vent path.** ==A grey plume bends over black soil at the throat of the vent.== Local volcano-watchers know the safe path. The ground beside it remains hot enough to kill, and no safe independent crossing is established. Keep to the guided route or return to the fishing shore.

## Occupants

Fishers and volcano-watchers maintain the local practice of reading the plume. No named keeper, ruler or population count is established.
