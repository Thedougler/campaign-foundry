## Areas

Drovannis offers shelter and a chance to trace lost possessions carried home by migrating birds.

- **Lagoon perches.** ==Seabirds crowd floating platforms and rope perches. A druid moves between them, murmuring.== Approach by boat through the lagoon channel. The keepers control access to the birds and their perches. A loud approach scatters the colony and interrupts work.
- **Reed margin.** ==Reeds hide the ground behind driftwood pilings crusted with mussels.== Visitors must push through the reeds before stepping onto solid shore. At low tide the exposed pilings offer food without disturbing the roosts.

## Occupants

Druids tend the sanctuary and collect the small objects returning birds bring each year. They keep these finds for identification or return. Visitors can ask to examine an object with them rather than take it from a perch. No source for the birds' finds has been traced.
