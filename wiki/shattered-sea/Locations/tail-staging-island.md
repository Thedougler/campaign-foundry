## Areas

The Party can stop here to replenish supplies and hire hands before continuing towards the [[drowned-maw|Drowned Maw]].

### Staging stop

==Provisions wait beside boats whose crews are still hiring.== Staging crews help vessels prepare for the Maw approach. Supplies wait for loading while crews seek the hands they still lack. Visitors can ask what is available and who is willing to sail. Loading provisions changes what a vessel can carry on the next leg. Hiring hands means agreeing what work they will undertake before departure.

The island's local name, landing place, layout and route from the [[midchain|Midchain]] remain unknown. No keeper, prices or access conditions are established. This is a distinct staging island, not [[fathomrush|Fathomrush]].
