## Areas

Come for glasswork or an apprenticeship. The furnace lies below a black-glass slope, with [[barrow-ait|Barrow Ait]] to the north and [[chronehall|Chronehall]] to the south.

- **Furnace slope.** ==Heat ripples above black glass. Below it, a glass-blower turns a glowing gather beside the furnace.== The natural silica vent feeds the furnace. The glass-blowers control the working ground and guard their methods. Visitors can negotiate for finished work without taking on the risks of training.
- **Warm fissures.** ==Grey mineral crust gathers around cracks in the rock.== Apprentices collect this flux before using a blowpipe. Their first task supplies the furnace with a material that lowers the melt's working temperature.

## Hazards

The vent and heated rock make unsupervised work dangerous. Heat shimmer and the furnace glow mark the working ground from a distance. Apprenticeship kills most outsiders in its first month. Refuse training or stay with the guild's supervised work rather than treating the vent as an ordinary quarry.
