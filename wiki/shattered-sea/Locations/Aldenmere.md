## At a glance

- **Draws the Party because.** The island is the reference mark for Sandtable Shoal, where the bottom rises from two hundred feet to less than forty in two miles and the Blue Lane kinks south around it.
- **Entrance.** Open anchorage water off the island's shore.
- **Danger.** The shoal to the south, and the wreck-feeding giant octopuses that hold its limestone.
- **Prize.** A clean position line on the mid-strait lane, and a view of the salvage traffic that works the groundings.

> [!narration] Entering
> The island stands low off the bow with the shoal's water showing beyond it, the blue of the deep going sandy and pale along the shoal's whole length. A salvage hull works the shallows with her boats out, and the lead-man at your bow calls the bottom earlier than he has all voyage.

## Play

### Likely actions

- The Party fixes the lane by the island and works the shoal's kink under the lead.
- The salvage crew hails approaching hulls and prices a grounding claim.
- The bottom comes up fast, and the watch trades depth calls with the chart.

## Depth

### Hidden truths

The shoal's salvage business runs on a Crown contract worked by a [[Kalowe]] operator, and the island's landings watch every grounding. The traffic tells you which hulls the strait has hurt lately.
