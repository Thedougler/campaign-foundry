## Areas

The Fold offers a chance to investigate a path that loops through folded space. After walking far enough: ==You're back on the path you passed earlier. You haven't walked far enough to account for it.== The return happens sooner than ordinary distance permits. No entrance, exit, reliable landmark or way of measuring the loop is established.

## Likely actions

Continuing tests the loop. Retreat does not guarantee that retracing the path behaves normally. The cause, occupants and consequences beyond the early return remain unknown.
