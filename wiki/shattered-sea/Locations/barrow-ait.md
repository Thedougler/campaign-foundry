## Areas

Visitors can investigate the graves and the remains of failed attempts to rob them. The most direct recorded approach is by water from [[calders-verge|Calder's Verge]].

- **Shore mound.** ==An opened patch of turf breaks the first burial mound. Tools lie scattered by the unfinished hole.== The excavation provides evidence to examine before disturbing another grave. No grave-robber is known to have left carrying a prize.
- **Mound rows.** ==Grass bends along straight rows of raised earth. A low hum sounds through the wind.== The island was built from the burial mounds of a forgotten war. Open swales separate the graves. The grass reveals their outlines without excavation. The hum's source remains unknown.

## History

The graves form an artificial island. Neither the war's identity nor the explanation for the failed robberies is established.
