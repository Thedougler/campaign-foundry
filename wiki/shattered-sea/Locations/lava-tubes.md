## At a glance

- **Draws the Party because.** The tubes shelter the Party from surface hunters and carry them toward the island's centre.
- **Entrance.** A smoking skylight in [[the-long-meadow|The Long Meadow]], marsh breach or root descent.
- **Occupants.** [[luca-ferrante|Luca Ferrante]], [[ettore-ferrante|Ettore Ferrante]], [[piero-sorrentino|Piero Sorrentino]] and [[gianni-moro|Gianni Moro]] are out with the Party, and the pit stands empty. Snakewood is deeper in.
- **Danger.** Loose rock, vertical breaks and living roof roots. The deepest tubes may hold little air, and a flyer rising through a skylight stands out to anything waiting outside.
- **Prize.** Survivors and underground routes to the island's interior.

> [!narration] Entering
> The basalt tube runs as wide as a village street under the old lava, and its roof hangs with pale roots. Their glow lifts as you pass, a slow pulse spreading from where you step. Daylight falls in columns through the wells. Between the wells you go by root-light, and the loose rock shifts underfoot. Water drips somewhere out of sight, into dark pools, and each drop comes back off the stone. The tube forks ahead, east and west. The mapped route goes on for the burnt road.

## Play

### Areas

==Mind the skylight, fifteen feet up, and it's still smoking.== The 15-foot smoking skylight, 40-foot ledge, east and west tubes, daylight wells, seep and vents.

==Careful at the edge here. The floor just goes, down and away every way at once.== The skylight opens into a natural basalt pit that drops away deep in several directions, dimly lit by the white roots along its ceiling as they pulse.

### Hazards

Acrobatics DC 10 keeps footing. Success: ==You keep your feet, and the glow ripples pale and slow out of the stone a step behind you.== Failure: ==Your heel skates on a loose slab, and the fall knocks a bright pulse out of the roots under you.== Athletics DC 15 climbs breaks. Success: ==Each hold takes your weight, and you come up over the lip onto the ledge.== Failure: ==The hold crumbles in your hand, and you drop back to your last footing with stinging palms.== Snakewood and Stillbloom answer living movement near roof roots and skylights: ==The glow answers you, a pale pulse racing away over the roof, spreading, fading, gone.==

### Roots

The roof roots glow dimly at all hours. A footstep or a hand on the stone raises a pale pulse near the mover, and the glow spreads and fades. [[jean-claude-tabarnack|Jean-Claude]] read the pattern at Investigation 28, and Crissdalynn's test, everyone still and then wings alone, kept a steady glow above her hover. The light rises under pressure and movement of any kind, steps or wing beats alike. The mat itself is a tangle of pale, vaguely glowing root, thick in places and thin in others, different species side by side, all joined to the plants overhead. The only growth down here is the roots themselves, and the only bones are small animals that fell in and were eaten.

### Occupants

[[luca-ferrante|Luca Ferrante]], [[ettore-ferrante|Ettore Ferrante]], [[piero-sorrentino|Piero Sorrentino]] and [[gianni-moro|Gianni Moro]] are out with the Party, and the pit stands empty.

### Likely actions

Travel the tubes toward the burnt road, hide while a hunter passes above, follow seepage toward Clear Lake, or fly a skylight for bearings.

## Depth

### History

A Terror-Bird's pursuit put Luca, Ettore, Piero and Gianni down here nineteen days ago. Ettore broke his shin, and Luca scratched the tally into the wall.

#### Session 12: out of the pit, under the hunters

Delmar and [[crissdalynn-khinriss|Crissdalynn]] ferried the four survivors out of the pit, and the Party went below with all five civilians when a Terror-Bird stalked the skylight. The bird pushed its camouflaged head into the hole and moved on. It came back with the light after twenty or thirty minutes. Jean-Claude's Survival 28 read the tunnels as the safer road, since the hunters above are all too big for tubes this size. Crissdalynn mapped the route as she scouted, well enough to find the way back. The Party and the civilians then travelled the tubes toward the burnt road, and when the scouts pushed ahead to the stone-table lavatories, the smell of dead grung and burnt plant matter ran strong there. Jean-Claude's map brought the rest up from the opening quickly, and the whole group walked out onto the burnt road together.

Crissdalynn's cartographer eye placed the tubes' origin as lava tubes that volcanic pressure shaped long ago, natural tunnels rather than cut ones. The volcanic system is quiet today, and its deep branches may hold little air.

### Hidden truths

The tubes join the wider Aruhe Caves system. The island's taking law reaches underground.

### Threads

[[taking-on-aruhe|Taking on Aruhe]] reaches underground here, and word that four survivors live below the skylight joins the report owed to [[perrin-and-nona|Perrin and Nona]].
