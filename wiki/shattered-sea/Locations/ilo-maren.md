## Areas

Crews come to trade for kelp rope, learn the reef routes or witness the annual harvest fire.

- **Reef beds.** ==Divers surface with baskets of blue-green kelp, water streaming between the glowing fronds.== Harvesters work the rare kelp beds at dawn. Watching from shore allows visitors to follow the harvesting line before choosing whether to enter the same currents.
- **Braiding shore.** ==Women twist wet kelp into long ropes along the beach.== Shore work turns the harvest into rope. The matriarchal diving community directs the work and trades with visitors. Salt figs from the dune thickets also provide a sharp local wine.

## Occupants

The island's matriarchal free-diving community harvests the kelp and braids it ashore. No named leader or population count is established.

## History

The annual harvest-night bonfire consumes the year's kelp harvest. Its gold-blue light fills the bay until dawn.
