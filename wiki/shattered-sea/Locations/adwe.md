## Areas

Adwe offers a sheltered crossing through mangroves, at the cost of navigation that a recent storm may have made useless.

- **Channel forks.** ==Roots arch over the water, and three unmarked channels split beneath them. A heron stands at one fork.== Enter by boat. Storms rearrange the mud and channels. Local accounts credit the herons with knowing the new ways through. Losing sight of a guiding bird leaves the crew without its best route reference. Open water around the island avoids the maze.
- **Root hummocks.** ==Palms crowd the dry patches between roots. Crabs cling below them where the tide has fallen.== Mud crabs provide food at low tide. Mangrove apples can be boiled into a preserve for the ship's stores.

## Hazards

Submerged roots prevent a boat from cutting directly between channels. Keep to visible water instead of forcing a straight course through the root beds. After a storm, retracing an old chart is less reliable than returning by a channel the crew has just sounded.
