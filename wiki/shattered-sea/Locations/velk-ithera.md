## Areas

**Fungal hillside.** ==Fungus covers the slope in a continuous carpet.== The growth is one connected organism. Sound provokes a faint response, making controlled noise a way to investigate it without crossing the whole slope. The response's form and meaning are unknown. Its ecology, safety and any useful properties remain unrecorded.
