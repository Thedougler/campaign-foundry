## Areas

Disputants come to substitute a champion's contest for war. Asked how the court works: ==Champions fight here so their people don't have to go to war.== Corvenna provides neutral ground, but the arena, landing, current match and people who maintain that neutrality are unknown.

## Likely actions

Parties can bring a dispute, put forward a champion or watch a contest. The eligible combatants, equipment, witnesses and enforcement of the result are not established. No consequence for refusing a duel is recorded.
