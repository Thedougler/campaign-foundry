## Areas

Visitors can investigate the arch and chest or seek a settlement to the families' dispute. At the island's arch: ==A split runs through the arch. The chest here still has its lock.== The relationship between the two features and the marriage pact is unknown. Neither the chest's contents nor a means of opening it is established.

## History

Two unnamed families broke a marriage pact centuries ago and still feud over an agreement that remains technically unresolved. Its terms and what would finally settle it are unknown.
