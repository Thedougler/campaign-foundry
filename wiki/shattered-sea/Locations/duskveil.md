## Travel

Dusk never lifts from the island. The dim light comes from a permanent illusion, so it cannot tell travellers how much daylight remains elsewhere. Duskveil lies east of the [[verdant-teeth|Verdant Teeth]] amid the Midchain's increasingly unreliable reefs and soundings. Its landing, paths, buildings and distances are not established.

The illusion's origin, boundary and interaction with creatures or objects remain unknown. No keeper or population is established.
