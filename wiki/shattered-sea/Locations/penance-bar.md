## Districts

Near [[kalowe|Kalowe]] in the western Midchain, Penance Bar's houses follow one street along a sandspit. A stone causeway leads to the headland shrine, [[sorrowbell|Sorrowbell]], where [[corvin-ashe|Corvin Ashe]] preaches to Tyr. Ash on doorsteps and tally-stones mark the village's siege warnings, and the bell tolls at odd hours. The village is already afraid, but nobody has died here yet.

Beyond the last worked field, half a day's walk inland, [[sandy-grove|Sandy Grove]] shelters old-growth trees and the coven. The plough has always gone around that stand. The town's population is not established.

## Factions here

The [[sandy-grove-coven|Sandy Grove Coven]] trades herbs and willow bark for salt fish. Its people have never belonged to the village and were never invited into it. [[halda-wren|Halda Wren]] leads them. Their children are among those Ashe has put at risk.

## Disputes

Ashe blames the coven for the siege to preserve his own standing. [[sula-marn|Sula Marn]], the Reeve, tries to keep the peace. Townspeople are organising a mob to burn the grove. Fear itself is the antagonist. The crowd is faceless, without a named rabble-rouser.

Investigation and Marn's help can expose evidence of a frame-up. At Sorrowbell, the true ledger contradicts the public tally. The [[penance-bar-siege|Penance Bar Siege]] turns those discoveries into a race to get the truth to the mob, with stopping its march by force a harder alternative if the truth arrives late.

## Hidden truths

Ashe's lies and incitement are what draw and feed the demon swarm. The coven is harmless. Sorrowbell has lost Tyr's protection. Only the grove remains hallowed. [[sin-and-sanctuary|Sin and Sanctuary]] explains why the demons are here and why the shrine can fail.
