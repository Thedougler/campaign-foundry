## Areas

Crews come to trade without entering Veth's closed interior. Arriving by small boat: ==Pale sand runs twenty strides to a stair of living roots. There's no pier.== Blue-caste go-betweens handle exchanges on the beach, while higher castes watch from the forest. Visitors who take the root stair enter the closed mid-storey of the canopy.

## Likely actions

Beach trade exchanges bone, resin and pigments for food, timber, metal and rope, but an invitation to trade doesn't permit climbing. Staying beyond the invitation or taking the stair brings the [[grung-clans|Grung Clans]]' patrols into the visit.
