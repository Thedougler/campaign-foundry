## Areas

The hollow preserves a garden whose keepers have outlasted its vanished founders.

- **Garden paths.** ==Pale apes clear fallen wood between beds half swallowed by figs and flowering vines.== Old paths remain visible through the growth and allow passage without cutting across the beds. The apes work in loose pairs and watch visitors without immediate alarm.
- **Offering stone.** ==Soft fruit rests on a flat stone among the beds.== The fruit stains fingers and bruises at a touch. Taking it breaks the boundary the apes observe. A party that leaves the stone alone can watch the tending without provoking them.

## Occupants

An albino ape colony tends the beds, removes fallen wood and renews the fruit offerings. Their reason for the ritual-like care remains unknown. No member of the vanished druidic order is present.

## History

A druidic order planted the garden. The apes have maintained it for generations after the order's disappearance.
