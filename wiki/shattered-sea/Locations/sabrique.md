## Areas

Crews visit Sabrique for cheap salt or to investigate the labour behind its price. Its salt pans occupy an island of the western [[midchain|Midchain]], near [[kalowe|Kalowe]]. Local pilots guide vessels through the shallow channels and shifting sandbars. Sabrique's landing and harbour have not been established.

At the pans: ==People work across shallow strips of water, drawing salt from the bright ground.== The pans are worked by indentured labourers. Their number, the boundaries of the working ground and the rules enforced there remain unknown.

## Occupants

Indentured workers produce the salt. No worker, overseer, owner or local authority has been identified, and the means used to enforce the indentures are not established.

## Likely actions

- **Buy salt.** Buyers accept the low price without asking what makes it possible. The seller, quantities, terms and consequences of asking questions remain unknown.
- **Question the labour arrangement.** The work is visible, but the indentures' holder, terms and available means of resistance are not established.
- **Sail for Kalowe.** The free port is nearby. The route and sailing time have not been established.
