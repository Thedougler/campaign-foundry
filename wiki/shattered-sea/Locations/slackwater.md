## Areas

**Shared harbour.** ==Water lies slack among the southern knots, a harbour used in common.== Slackwater serves the southern islands as a common anchorage. Beaches in the south are very short and landing is negotiated, so a harbour that belongs to no island matters to travellers. Who maintains it, the rules for using it and the vessels that gather there are unknown.
