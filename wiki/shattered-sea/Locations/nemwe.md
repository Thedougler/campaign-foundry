## Areas

Holding the tower offers control over a signal visible from the shipping lanes.

- **Old shore path.** ==A leaning tower rises above salt pines and gorse, with an old path along the slope below it.== The path keeps the shore in view as it climbs. Leaving it for the scrub hides the shoreline within a few strides. Bayberry at the seaward edge can be rendered into candle wax.
- **Tower and signal platform.** ==The lower door hangs broken open. Above it, an iron brazier swings on its arm towards the shipping lanes.== The signal-fire still works. Its cradle holds warm ash from a recent unidentified visitor. Whoever holds the tower for a night can signal a passing cutter to approach or warn it away.

## History

The [[dravosi-crown|Dravosi Crown]] abandoned the outpost after moving its patrol route north. No permanent resident or present claimant is confirmed.
