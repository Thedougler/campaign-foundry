## Areas

Visitors can protect nesting turtles, investigate stolen eggs or negotiate with the beach watch.

- **Nesting beach.** ==Broad flipper tracks end in shallow nests between the water and the trees.== Sea turtles nest here each egg season. The beach is one of the Midchain's largest nesting grounds. Local guards watch it while poachers bring skiffs ashore for the eggs.
- **Dune edge.** ==Low scrub backs the nests, with succulent leaves spreading through the dune grass.== Beach purslane grows here. Locals dry it for a preparation that eases sand-flea bites and sell it alongside shifts on the turtle watch.

## Occupants

Local guards defend the nests against poachers. During egg season they expect an armed confrontation. Visitors can join the watch or investigate a missing clutch. No crew, guard captain or governing faction has been named.

## Pressure

Egg season brings both nesting turtles and arriving poachers. While guards and visitors argue over access, unattended nests remain available to the skiff crews. Agreeing on a watch shifts the dispute from competing claims to protecting a specific stretch of beach.
