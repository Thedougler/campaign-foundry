## At a glance

- **Draws the Party because.** The cliffs are the last charted mark before the [[Central Strait]] narrows, and the tower on the eastern edge is the strait's final position line.
- **Entrance.** The eastern cliff edge, where the Crown signal station stands above the water.
- **Occupants.** The signal station's Crown crew.
- **Danger.** The cliffs below, and the current that sets a missed ship north toward them.
- **Prize.** A position fix and the signal state, because a flag on the tower means the [[Drowned Maw]] is behaving unusually.

> [!narration] Entering
> The cliffs rise grey and sheer from water that runs fast along their base, and the tower stands on the edge above you, a squat crown of stone with its signal yard bare. The hoist lines hang ready at the yard. Whatever the tower has to say, it says to the whole strait at once.

## Play

### Likely actions

- The Party reads the tower's yard for a hoisted flag before committing to the Narrows.
- The signal crew trades the strait's news for news of the west.
- The current sets on the cliffs, and the helmsman works to hold off.

## Depth

### Hidden truths

A flag on the tower means the [[Drowned Maw]] is behaving unusually. Reading the hoist before entering the Narrows is free, and the signal crew checks the Maw hoist twice before it flies.
