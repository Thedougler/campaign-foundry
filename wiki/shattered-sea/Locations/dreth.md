## Travel

Dreth is a country-scale rainforest island in the eastern Teeth, where facing shores are hours apart and interiors weeks. Its forest stands permanently drowned, and no shoreline is charted. A hull must enter the flooded channels under the canopy or stay out. The islands' beach-trade protocol does not apply, because there is no beach. Rare dry hummocks serve as garrison and work camps. Shallow crossings lead to [[orak|Orak]] only until rain erases them.

A keel and mast are liabilities in the channels, which favour a shallow Grung proa. Fresh-cut hummocks and a smell of resin show timber work. Patrols thicken as the cut moves south.

## Powers

The [[grung-clans|Grung Clans]] hold Dreth as hidden, forbidden territory and patrol its channel. Root hollows and silt margins harbour specimens. Darker seams hold larger predators. Giant frogs, crocodiles, constrictors and channel axolotls share the water.
