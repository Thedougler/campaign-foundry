## Areas

Visitors can observe a huge fey-moth roost here. Approaching the roost: ==Moths gather by the thousand.== Sailors put out their lanterns a mile away because flame attracts the moths. No landing, caretaker or local route is established.

## Likely actions

A crew can darken its vessel before closing with the roost or approach by lantern light to test the attraction. The moths' response beyond their attraction to flame, and any danger it brings, remain unknown.
