## Travel

Orak is the raised karst island paired with [[dreth|Dreth]]. Stone towers climb into cloud, forest fills the clefts, and waterfalls drop towards drowned trees. Reef and cliff face give a poor landing, and no sanctioned beach exists. Shallow crossings link Orak with Dreth until rain erases them.

The useful island is vertical. Sinkholes breathe air colder than the day and are shafts, not doors. Falls and cold breath mark the water system, and sailors' tales of thin places trace to sinkholes. A path swallowed by a shaft stays a shaft.

## Powers

The [[grung-clans|Grung Clans]] hold Orak as hidden, forbidden territory. They count the known shafts and patrol the channel. Any nests lie in wet clefts beside water that can be entered, and no named settlement is established.
