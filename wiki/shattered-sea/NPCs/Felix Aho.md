## At a glance

- **Role.** Captured Grung labourer and informant.
- **Wants.** One month of protection and survival outside the clan's kill-on-sight category.
- **Voice.** Frightened, practical, and cooperative rather than ideological.
- **Found at.** Nona Black-Jaw's Warren safe house, guarded by Ruk.

> [!narration] First look
> A lean green Grung sits under guard, old leaves of colour on matte skin and amber eyes fixed on the door. He looks like a labourer who has decided to answer, whatever the answer costs him in breath.

## Play

- **Opens them up.** A clear bargain for protection.
- **Shuts them down.** Threats that sound like the clan's orders.
- **Will share.** The Calveno sewer bombing plan, powder sites, and the defector kill-on-sight category.
- **Will not share.** The discarded prep identity sometimes attached to his name.
- **If pressed.** He cooperates, flees, or freezes. He is no leader or combat threat.

## Depth

### History

Crissdalynn took Felix down silently in the dark when he ran for the sewer exit, and the Party woke him bound once the purple Grung's blood was on [[Jean-Claude Tabarnack]]'s blade. He traded the operation for protection. Powder barrels came across the ocean by ship, six hulls in all, and the boat the Party burned was the sixth. Workers were building scaffold in the sewers to brace the barrels against the ceiling, the underside of the city streets. The plan counted four attack points, each with at least two dozen guards, purple garrisons under red leaders, and gave the work two days, timed to the festival. He told Jean-Claude that Grung defectors are a recognised kill-on-sight category, which put Jean-Claude himself in it, and offered everything he knew for a month of safety. The Party walked him bound through the festival crowds to [[Nona Black-Jaw]]'s safe house, where he is held under [[Ruk]]'s guard.

### Hidden truths

- The older Vaka elder and organiser described in discarded preparation notes is not this Felix. The session-canon captive is the only established identity.
- He was hired for labour, not ideology, and trades information for time.

### Threads

He is a living witness in **Simone's Hunters** and the Calveno raid aftermath.
