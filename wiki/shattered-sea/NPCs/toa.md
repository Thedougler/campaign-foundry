## Roleplaying

- **Wants.** Find out how a prospective opponent fights before the squad takes the job.
- **Voice.** Warm and animated. He talks through an exchange blow by blow, with a question whenever the account skips something.
- **Opening move.** At [[the-flat-water|the Flat Water]] in [[kalowe|Kalowe]], he examines seals from other companies and asks a visitor about a recent fight. He starts friendly.
- **Leverage.** He can analyse an opponent for the [[five-blades|Five Blades]], but needs an accurate account and cannot commit the squad alone.
- **Opens them up.** A detailed account of a fight, especially what went wrong.
- **Shuts them down.** Boasting that skips the opponent’s response.
- **Will share.** His reading of a fight and what the squad would need to prepare.
- **Requests.** Asked whether an opponent is easy: ==Toa leans towards the account. “What did they do after the first hit? That’s the bit I need.”==
- **If ignored.** He returns to his seals and asks the next traveller for a better account.

## History

Toa is the largest and most readable of the four. His collection includes other companies’ contract seals, but why he keeps them is unrecorded. He is a [[moucheron|Moucheron]] from the company, unrelated to [[toa-kailani|Toa Kailani]] of [[sparhold|Sparhold]].
