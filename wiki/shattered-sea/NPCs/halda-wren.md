## Roleplaying

- **Voice.** Plain and blunt. She lets a direct question stand without dressing it up.
- **Opening move.** At [[sandy-grove|Sandy Grove]], keep splitting kindling on a stump while a visitor talks. Start with "Ask what you came to ask."
- **Leverage.** She leads the [[sandy-grove-coven|Sandy Grove Coven]], about a dozen hedge-druids, including children. They are harmless and have traded herbs for the village's salt fish for longer than the shrine has had a priest.
- **Will share.** The coven has never had a grievance against the village. Its people stayed out because they were never invited in.

## Quotes

> Ask what you came to ask.

Her first line to a visitor at the grove.
