## At a glance

- **Role.** Purple-caste Grung guardian and Still-Water Discipline master.
- **Wants.** To protect Magazine Gamma and prove patience, venom, and touch are enough.
- **Voice.** Tongue-clicks constantly. Otherwise calm, clipped, and precise.
- **Found at.** Room T1 of the Calveno Sewer Magazines. Dead after Session 06.

> [!narration] First look
> A lean purple Grung stands beneath a tattered grey veil, milk-white eyes fixed nowhere. Her tongue clicks without pause, mapping the room by echo before her hands move.

## Play

- **Opens them up.** Stillness, patience, and an opponent who understands discipline.
- **Shuts them down.** Noise that breaks her echolocation or threats to her room.
- **Will share.** She gives information only through touch and venom.
- **Will not share.** Her order's rites or the limits of her blind discipline.
- **If pressed.** She uses the room's mist and tongue to pull prey close. Ranged attacks meet her deflection.

## Depth

### History

Vashu leads the Still-Water Discipline, whose masters fight blind and wear the veil as achievement. She guarded Magazine Gamma with a purple-caste enforcer before joining Solange's defence. Crissdalynn killed her in Session 06.

- Vashu died defending Solange's ritual.

### Hidden truths

- Her tongue-click is echolocation, not nervousness. Within 40 feet she ignores darkness, fog, invisibility, and her own Weeping Veil.
- The veil is both her order title and the bone-vial toxic mist she releases.

### Threads

She was a guardian in **Simone's Hunters** and the Calveno sewer raid.
