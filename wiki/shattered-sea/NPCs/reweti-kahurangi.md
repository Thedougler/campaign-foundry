> Behind the bar across from you, a tortle woman is setting mugs along a dark timber plank. She works one at a time. Her shell angles towards the room, and both arms remain free as she pauses over the next mug to let someone finish speaking. Then she puts it down and looks your way. “Two hammocks or the room?”

## Roleplaying

Reweti runs [[the-flat-water|The Flat Water]] in [[kalowe|Kalowe]]. She wants customers to talk freely, but will not let their business disturb her room. She uses she/her pronouns.

- **Wants.** A paid tab and a quiet evening, with guests fed and lodged before she puts the lamp out.
- **Torn between.** She needs crews to trust the bar enough to bring their news, but a useful introduction can bring somebody else's quarrel through her door.
- **Voice.** Low, unhurried and practical. Short questions about meals, berths and payment. She lets other people finish before answering, and offers an observation without introducing it as advice.
- **Opening move.** At [[the-flat-water|The Flat Water]], Reweti sets a mug on the bar. She asks what the Party needs and starts with an Indifferent Attitude. Her shell stays angled towards the room, with her arms free.
- **Leverage.** She can offer lodging, what she has heard in the room and a hiring contact for the [[five-blades|Five Blades]]. An introduction risks the bar's peace if the enquiry becomes a dispute. She passes on enquiries, not orders, and cannot promise the Blades' terms or arrival.
- **Opens them up.** Polite questions, settled tabs and patience while she finishes serving someone else.
- **Shuts them down.** Threats, unpaid bills and demands to repeat herself. She stops the conversation rather than negotiating her limit.
- **Will share.** She knows how to reach the Five Blades. A polite paying crew may receive a useful fact from her without asking, once, with no preamble. Use something she could have observed from the bar or lagoon, or heard from a customer. Separate what she witnessed from what she merely heard.
- **Will not share.** She will not repeat a fact she has volunteered, however much money a customer offers. Her knowledge of a rumour extends only to what she heard.
- **Requests.** For meals and beds, she gives the [[the-flat-water#Buy food, drink or lodging|house prices]] and offers what is available. She puts a mug on the bar: ==Pay your tab and keep the room calm.== For a Five Blades enquiry, she waits for the job's details: ==Tell me the work. I can pass word along, but the price is theirs.== These services do not require an Influence check for polite paying customers.
- **If pressed.** Asked for the same observation again, she resumes her work: ==You heard me.== If someone insists on making a scene, she indicates the exit: ==Take it outside.== She denies further service until the trouble stops.
- **If ignored.** She serves the next customer. After the last guest leaves, she puts the lamp out. Her contact offer remains available on a later civil visit, but the useful fact she volunteered does not get a second telling.

## Quotes

## History

Reweti worked as a deckhand before taking over the Flat Water. After working decks at sea, she finds tending the bar restful. She serves alone from mid-morning until closing and attends to each order at her own deliberate pace.
