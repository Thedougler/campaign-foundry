## Roleplaying

- **Wants.** First take Crissdalynn's Fate Spinner for Vantyrus; when the chance comes, kill his master. If he wins the second attempt, keep the Spinner and use it against Vantyrus.
- **Torn between.** He is Vantyrus's apprentice and intends to kill him. The Rule of Two ends only when one kills the other.
- **Conviction.** He believes wholly in his cause and his right to choose his own fate. That includes refusing the future Vantyrus has chosen for him. His belief is sincere, with no hidden hurt, humiliation or hunger for recognition beneath it.
- **Voice.** Level, clipped and patient. He names people by what they carry.
- **Opening move.** He is fighting the Party at their inland camp on [[aruhe|Aruhe]], invisible but outlined by falling water.
- **Leverage.** He can call on Vantyrus, who has helped him from afar. His orders forbid killing Crissdalynn, but leave her companions unprotected.
- **Opens them up.** A clean defeat or talk that treats Vantyrus as beatable.
- **Shuts them down.** Questions about his master's name, threats or pity.
- **Will share.** Once his Kyzil half-truth fails, he offers the Party an equal alliance against Vantyrus, keeping his katana raised.
- **Will not share.** His routes or the reason for the Spinner. Asked where he flies or what the toy is for, he tips his head away: ==Where I fly is mine to know. The purpose of the toy is my master's to tell, not mine. Ask me something I can answer.==
- **Requests.** The Spinner on open stone where he can watch every hand: ==Set it on the stone, hands where I can watch. Everything else comes after.==
- **If pressed.** He threatens a companion to force Crissdalynn to surrender the Spinner. A talon closes on the fruit pack's strap and drags its carrier closer: ==My orders are silent on the fruit carrier. The toy, or his breath.==
- On his first casual mention of Kyzil: ==Kyzil picks his carriers for strong legs and quiet mouths.==
- When he asks Crissdalynn why Kyzil gave her the Spinner: ==Tell me, staff. Why did Kyzil give it to you? He didn't tell you what it does, did he.==
- He continues the half-truth: ==Sent his best apprentice into a storm with a toy she couldn't use. Ask yourself who he meant to come and find you.==
- When pressed about Kyzil: ==He learned to spin at my master's knee. Ask him.==
- He adds: ==He won't lie to you. Watch how long he takes to answer.==
- Once the half-truth fails, offering rebellion: ==Fine. No more stories. I want him dead, so do you, or you will.==
- He makes the offer: ==Give me the toy and I'll put it through his eye.==
- **If ignored.** If the Party refuses his offer, he fights to the death. If he wins the second attempt, he keeps the Spinner and turns against Vantyrus.

> [!narration] First look
> You see the glare high over the camp. Then something drops out of the glare. Brown wings half open, chains ticking over a black robe, a peregrine aarakocra braking hard for the last twenty feet to land standing on the stone ten strides from you, katana still sheathed. The wings fold. You see where his amber stare goes, past your faces to the carrier's straps. It stays there while the chains swing still. Then it comes up to the carrier's eyes. “Set the toy on the stone.”

## History

Skarn serves Vantyrus and studies every encounter as practice for killing him. He watched Matteo vanish after eating a ghost plum, then attacked Crissdalynn at the River Slack Basin for the Fate Spinner. The ambush opened at a watch change, his approach quiet enough that both watchers' perception missed it, and his first grab at her pack came despite her grapple. Her staff found him once, and the Stunning Strike he failed cost him one Legendary Resistance to shake off. He was still in her face, empty-handed, when the session's recording stopped.

In [[session-12-recap|Session 12 - Recap]], Skarn continued the river-camp attack before dawn, pressing his katana against Crissdalynn's staff as he searched for her necklace. He said he had come only for the toy. Vantyrus watched from afar through a device and manipulated fate to counter the disadvantage on his attacks. He pinned [[matteo-scola|Matteo Scola]] beneath a talon and stole Matteo's fruit pack, then threatened to kill him unless Crissdalynn surrendered the Spinner. As the Party wounded him, Skarn called out, “Vantyrus, help!” He stunned Crissdalynn and flew away, taking Matteo's hat as he rose. During the pursuit he ate nearly all the stolen fruit and disappeared from sight. The fruit was windfall that had fallen on [[aruhe|Aruhe]], and the island's law leaves fallen fruit free to any eater. He escaped inland without the Spinner. The DM reported that he had 12 of his 314 hit points left.

Skarn returned invisible during the Party's rest at their next camp. His dive broke Crissdalynn's arm, knocked her down and stunned her, but the falling spray from [[delmar-fisk|Delmar]]'s decanter outlined him. His grab for her necklace failed because the Spinner had been moved into Delmar's bag of holding. He lifted her by the throat and demanded its location. Delmar tried to convince him that [[jean-claude-tabarnack|Jean-Claude]] had it, but failed to deceive him. Skarn released Crissdalynn and turned his attention to Delmar. He repeated that his orders were to take the Spinner without killing her. He threatened her companions and offered them a meeting with Vantyrus if they complied. The fight remained unresolved when the recording stopped. Crissdalynn's final strike failed to stun him, but reduced his speed and gave advantage on the next attack against him.

## Hidden truths

- The Rule of Two makes Skarn Vantyrus's eventual executioner. The contest ends only when one kills the other.
- Skarn does not know the Spinner's purpose. If he learns it reaches the Soul Incarnate technique, he will keep it from Vantyrus and come for it himself.
- The [[vantyruss-shard|shard on his harness]] survives his death. The Party can take it from his body, carrying Vantyrus's unseen gaze with them.

Skarn knowingly wraps a false conclusion around true facts about Kyzil. He was present at the Sentinel Schism and saw Kyzil refuse to leave. He does not know Kyzil believes Vantyrus died decades ago. Every sentence in his half-truth is true. A read on him finds the sentences true, while seeing through him means recognising that he does not believe the conclusion they imply.

If the second attempt ends in defeat, Skarn will not flee or surrender. He dies fighting. If he wins, he keeps the Spinner instead of returning it to Vantyrus and intends to use it to break the Rule of Two on his own terms. Before fighting to the death, he tries the half-truth and then offers the Party an equal alliance against Vantyrus, with his katana still raised. If they refuse, he fights them to the death.
