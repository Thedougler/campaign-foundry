> In the brass cage in front of you, a pigeon-sized mosquito humanoid hums on his wings and pulls at the bars with four little hands. He's thin, with a long proboscis, and his eyes follow you as he rocks the cage. Then he slows, leans against the bars and speaks softly, the needle of his mouth still pointing through them. Ket says, "If you come close, I'll tell you a bit."

## Roleplaying

A starving prisoner who calls you a friend while coaxing you within reach of his mouth.

- **Wants.** Warm blood and the shortest way back to [[murrat|Murrat]].
- **Voice.** An addict's urgency under smooth, sweet talk. He repeats himself, promises a harmless conversation and offers secrets if you come closer. A glimpse of blood breaks the patter into impatient demands.
- **Opening move.** Freed from the Surety's brig, he flew home to Murrat. When encountered hungry, he coaxes someone nearer with an offered secret, asks for blood and works towards an open door.

- **Opens them up.** Food and an open cage. He makes conversation into an invitation to stand closer: ==Ket leans towards you. "It's a secret. It's a secret, but I trust you guys. I'll tell you 'cause you're not supposed to."==
- **Shuts them down.** Being reminded that he is small or caged, or finding everyone still beyond reach.
- **Will share.** What [[barnaby-rook|Barnaby Rook]] wanted and the Crown's interest in the [[five-blades|Five Blades]], for blood.
- **Will not share.** Why Rook took him unless asked directly.
- **If pressed.** He lies shamelessly and reassures whoever might release him. When Jean-Claude offered freedom under threat of combat, Ket replied, "I can assure you", then, "that we will not have any problems." Only the prospect of the corpse on the weather deck made him admit he might lose control.

## History

Rook took Ket from Murrat as a specimen and held him three weeks in a brass cage aboard the Surety. Jean-Claude offered him blood from a wineglass, which reddened his abdomen and left him calmer. Ket sweet-talked the Party, offered secrets and repeatedly lied to draw them closer. When Crissdalynn entered his reach, he stole blood from her arm without shame. Crissdalynn shielded him from Perrin's blade, and the crew used the dead ensign's body as a lure to open the cage from across the room. With the body waiting on the weather deck, Ket finally admitted he might not control himself and urged them to get behind closed doors. After feeding, Ket flew home to Murrat.

## Hidden truths

- Ket knows that Rook wanted to breed Moucherons for the Five Blades' strength and fervour. He knows that part of the Crown's investigation, but not the wider supply chain.
- His pigeon size is normal for a [[moucheron|Moucheron]]. Captivity left him thin, but did not make him a runt. Like his kin, he has four arms and wings on a little gnome-like body.
