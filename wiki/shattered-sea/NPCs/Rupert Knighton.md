## At a glance

- **Role.** Commodore of the Dravosi Crown's Knight Squadron.
- **Wants.** To avenge Captain Gorgeous and restore Crown order along the [[Central Strait]].
- **Voice.** One measured order, never a shouted threat. “He doesn't threaten. He replaces.”
- **Found at.** The Crown fleet and its search corridors, not yet in the crew's path.

> [!narration] First look
> A tall, broad-shouldered commodore fills the doorway, grey at the temples and one eye hidden by a black patch. His gloved hand rests on the rail while he gives one order and watches it happen.

## Play

- **Opens them up.** Clear results, useful intelligence, and officers who act without excuses.
- **Shuts them down.** Failure, delay, or anyone responsible for Captain Gorgeous's death.
- **Will share.** He does not share anything beyond what a Crown commander must communicate.
- **Will not share.** The Knight Squadron's true size or his personal grief.
- **If pressed.** He replaces the failing officer and sends the next ship. His vanguard folds a line before the real strike arrives.

## Depth

### History

Rupert commands the Knight Squadron and calls every Crown vessel under him a Knight. Captain Gorgeous was effectively his son. Barnaby Rook served as his tool, while Dorian Bishop is the escalation before Rupert acts directly. His ships have chased the wrong trail toward Calveno and still have not identified the Uncertainty as the Surety's alias.

#### Session 9: the private fleet

The [[HCS Ordinance]], a Dravosi Crown man-of-war built at Harwick, belonged to Rupert's private fleet when it intercepted the [[Uncertainty]].

[[Corbin Knighton]] told Catarina that Rupert had dispatched him and that he was newly in charge of the knights. Catarina said she had spoken with Rupert, who had strongly encouraged her to evacuate [[Calven and Calveno|Calveno]].

### Hidden truths

- Rupert has not yet learned that Captain Gorgeous and Rook are dead. Once he does, he will act outside ordinary Crown channels.
- He is Geoffrey Draves's uncle by blood, though neither man has met the other.

### Threads

He drives **The Crown Inspection** and is the late-campaign pressure behind Geoffrey's family connection.
