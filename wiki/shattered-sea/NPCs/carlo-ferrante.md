## Roleplaying

- **Appearance.** Tall and broad through the shoulders, with olive skin, brown eyes and a square nose. A grey streak divides his dark beard. His dark hair is tied at the nape. He wears a faded blue waistcoat over a coarse linen shirt with rolled sleeves, and a rope belt cinches his patched brown breeches.
- **Wants.** To bring his brother Ettore out and protect his nephew Luca.
- **Voice.** Clipped orders from the quays, with numbers murmured between them. He speaks more easily about a load to shift than a person he fears he lost.
- **Opening move.** Sits at the south edge of [[the-pantry|The Pantry]], facing the way he came, sure that Ettore and Luca died in the dark. Indifferent.
- **Opens them up.** Thanks for leading the camp to food, or Ettore's forgiveness.
- **Shuts them down.** The Long Meadow or questions about why he never went back.
- **Will share.** The march from Spoke Ring, Renzo's guidance, and the island's protection.
- **Will not share.** That he turned back from the four when he was near them.
- **If pressed.** With Ettore and Luca alive, he stays close to them and takes a way off Aruhe as soon as their safety allows. Passage is an offer he accepts without a roll (SRD 5.2.1, Influence). ==He reaches for Ettore's pack and slips it over his own shoulder. "Keep your feet under you. I'll take the weight."==

## History

Carlo worked the Calveno quays as a lighter foreman and got Ettore a bridge-toll clerk's post. After the wreck he kept survivors together until Renzo's rule took over. At Spoke Ring nineteen days ago, he rose first to follow Hinewai's voice and most of the camp followed.

## Hidden truths

- On the second day after Spoke Ring, Carlo returned to the Long Meadow and shouted Ettore's name once. Smoke poured from the lava-tube skylight and a Terror-Bird rose from its stump. He mistook the smoke for a grass fire and fled. He hides this from the camp because he believes he abandoned his family.
- Mentioning the meadow breaks his muttered count and makes him start the march's story again from Spoke Ring. Thanks for getting the camp to food can bring him to confess. Either Ettore or Luca asking him directly gets the truth without persuasion. The confession gives his account. Luca can explain the doubled day-two mark on his tally, while questions about Carlo's broken count expose his evasion.
- He walked back through the green unharmed. The island left him free to turn round. He can admit this once he admits the retreat itself.

## Threads

He carries the survivor strand of **Taking on Aruhe**, with the brother he believes he left behind at its heart.
