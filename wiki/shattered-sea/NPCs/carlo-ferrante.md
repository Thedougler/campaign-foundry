> [!narration] First look
> A tall man with a grey streak through his beard stares at the litter. His big hands open and tremble at his sides. He takes one step and says, "Brother."

## Roleplaying

- **Wants.** To bring his brother Ettore out and protect his nephew Luca.
- **Voice.** A dock foreman's short words. He counts under his breath.
- **Opening move.** Sits at the south edge of [[the-pantry|The Pantry]], facing the way he came, sure that Ettore and Luca died in the dark. Indifferent.
- **Opens them up.** Thanks for leading the camp to food, or Ettore's forgiveness.
- **Shuts them down.** The Long Meadow or questions about why he never went back.
- **Will share.** The march from Spoke Ring, Renzo's guidance, and the island's protection.
- **Will not share.** That he turned back from the four when he was near them.
- **If pressed.** Shown Ettore and Luca alive, he covers Ettore and takes any way off Aruhe the moment his family is safe, rather than stay at the Pantry. A way out is what he has wanted since the wreck, so he answers it without a roll (SRD 5.2.1, Influence). ==He takes the nearest pole of the litter in both hands and counts the steps under his breath. “Show me the way to the ship, and I carry.”==

## History

Carlo worked the Calveno quays as a lighter foreman and got Ettore a bridge-toll clerk's post. After the wreck he kept survivors together until Renzo's rule took over. At Spoke Ring nineteen days ago, he rose first to follow Hinewai's voice and most of the camp followed.

## Hidden truths

- Carlo went back toward the Long Meadow, saw smoke from the lava tubes, and turned round after mistaking the terror-bird's stump form for a grass fire. He believes he abandoned Ettore.

## Threads

He carries the survivor strand of **Taking on Aruhe**, with the brother he believes he left behind at its heart.
