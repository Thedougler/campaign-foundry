## At a glance

- **Role.** Named chaos entity and Mercatura crater opponent.
- **Wants.** To rend and consume everything in reach.
- **Voice.** He communicates through roars and tongue lashes, with the wet sound of a body moulting.
- **Found at.** The Mercatura crater, after emerging through Solange's body. Dead after Session 07.

> [!narration] First look
> Crimson hide rises above the crater, split by widening cracks that pour ichor and green vapour. A blank jaw opens, a tongue lashes toward movement, and the ground sinks beneath him.

## Play

- **Opens them up.** Nothing. Otar is a brute hazard rather than a negotiator.
- **Shuts them down.** Fire or acid suppressing his regeneration only makes him more violent.
- **Will share.** He does not communicate. His corpse and summoning circle prove someone knew his name.
- **Will not share.** Motive, allegiance, or a plan.
- **If pressed.** He attacks the nearest body and pulls prey into reach. Trapped townsfolk suffer when no one blocks him.

## Depth

### History

Simone Tabarnack contracted the summoning beneath Mercatura. Solange Barret completed the circle, and Otar consumed her body without killing her or taking her mind. The adventurers killed him in the crater after Kyzil arrived and the Warren's Rattle joined the fight. Otar emerged in the Mercatura crater and died there.

### Hidden truths

- Otar's regeneration fails for a round after fire or acid damage. His stalled caste transformation produces the foul miasma.
- A scrap of paper nobody has read stayed with the corpse, and the summoning circle remained beneath the rubble.

### Threads

He was the climax of **Simone's Hunters** and the Mercatura raid.
