> [!narration] First look
> A lanky boy's lips move over a count he has not finished, and he gives you the numbers, "We are four. One cannot walk. How many can you carry?"

## Roleplaying

- **Wants.** His father safe on the march, and his uncle Carlo found.
- **Voice.** Short sentences with numbers first. Counting keeps him calm.
- **Opening move.** Out of the lava tube, marching inland with the Party along the fruit-pile trail. The walk in search of Carlo still lies ahead.
- **Leverage.** He can guide the Party to the fruit piles and show his count. He carries a strip of bark copied from the lava-tube wall: twenty day marks since Spoke Ring, with the second cut deep and doubled.

- **Opens them up.** A promise to bring Ettore out of danger.
- **Shuts them down.** Calling Carlo dead.
- **Will share.** The voice at Spoke Ring, the route north-east, and every fruit pile.
- **Will not share.** His fear that the tally is running out.
- **If pressed.** He follows the party while light remains, then walks on alone after twenty minutes stopped.

## History

Luca led Ettore, Piero, and Gianni after Carlo followed Hinewai's voice. A terror-bird drove them over the smoking skylight into the lava tube. He has scratched nineteen days into the wall and arrives at the twentieth ready to find Carlo.

## Hidden truths

- The tally is a promise. Luca decided he would go after Carlo on the twentieth day, alone if he had to.
- On day two Luca heard Carlo shout Ettore's name across the meadow once. Ettore put a hand on Luca's shoulder, and both kept silent while the roof shook beneath the Terror-Bird. Luca has kept the shout to himself. Asked about the doubled stroke, he names his uncle as the caller and explains that an answer would have drawn the bird.

## Threads

He is a guide and survivor in **Taking on Aruhe**.
