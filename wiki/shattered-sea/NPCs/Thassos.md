## At a glance

- **Role.** Card-table fixture at [[Tallow Row]].
- **Wants.** To know a player's nerve before the stakes do.
- **Found at.** The [[Tallow Row]] tables.

> [!narration] First look
> A player at the corner table watches the new face longer than the cards, and folds a good hand without a shrug. The table waits on this player's word when the play turns.

## Play

- **Will share.** A test of a new player, played across the felt.

## Depth

### History

Across a run of hands, Thassos tested and folded [[Thunk]] while the silver left the tables with him. When [[Old Faas]] folded a hand he should have won rather than tip the table, Thassos called the game. Faas was still fast on his pegs.
