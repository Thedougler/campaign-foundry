> [!narration] First look
> Nobody has met Impuni. A healer going ashore at [[la-vasca|La Vasca]] spoke the name once, someone in Calveno he was short of time to reach. Whether Impuni is a patient, a bind, or a plain visit, only Grigori knows.

## Play

Impuni has not been met, so nobody knows how a meeting runs. Grigori's business sets the tone instead. He arrives warm and leaves owed, and the questions he asks stay long after he goes.

## Depth

### History

Leaving the Party at [[la-vasca|La Vasca]], Grigori spoke of a young heir in Calveno whose physicians had given up, and of Impuni, a name he tied to business that could not wait. The words were his. The Party has not tested them.
