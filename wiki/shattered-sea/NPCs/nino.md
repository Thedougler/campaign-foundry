## At a glance

- **Role.** Calveno captive. The name covers both the wordless companion beside [[sandro|Sandro]] and a Mercatura metalworker rescued at sea.
- **Wants.** To leave Aruhe without ever crossing the garden line.
- **Found at.** Aboard the [[uncertainty|Uncertainty]]: the metalworker since the Session 9 driftwood rescue, the beach companion since the Session 10 lift-off.

> [!narration] First look
> A second man keeps to the hull's shade, and his eyes move between your boat and the treeline. When the first man looks to the fruit shade, this one shakes his head and points instead at berries lying loose among the stones.

## Play

- **Opens them up.** A patient guest. He answers watching better than questioning.
- **Shuts them down.** The garden line. Talk of crossing it ends his help.
- **Will share.** Gesture by gesture: nothing from the island while he watches, and a pointed hand toward berries already loose on the stones.

## Depth

### History

The beach companion's side of the name: the same raid and the same wreck took him captive toward [[karath|Karath]], and he came ashore clinging to the same outrigger strut as Sandro.

The metalworker's side of the name: the morning after the Party's first night at sea, the [[uncertainty|Uncertainty]] sailed into a field of storm wreckage. [[crissdalynn-khinriss|Crissdalynn]] found three young men clinging to a shattered piece of hull, sunburnt and a day or two adrift without fresh water. She flew two aboard and ropes took the third. They came aboard wrapped in blankets, shivering, with a clear Calveno accent. [[perrin-black-jaw|Perrin]] greeted them as a rattkin with a black jaw, from Calveno. "You're in good hands. See? Safe." Nino gave his account aboard: a metalworker in [[mercatura|Mercatura]], taken in the raid while getting groceries. The raiders herded the captives toward the tunnels and loaded them aboard ships. The hurricane, he said, cut the captured fleet in half; the back half was pushed east, presumed lost at sea or shipwrecked. The Party berthed the men below, in spare crew beds in the galley.

The table has used the name for two men. The Session 9 cold open put a Nino under the [[vethka|Vethka]]'s shade beside Sandro, and the Session 10 rescue lifted a man the DM called Nino off that sand. A metalworker of the same name gave his account aboard days before the ship raised Aruhe. One man cannot have been berthed aboard and still on the beach, so the question of which man is Nino is open.

The beach companion stayed at the beach's edge from the island taking [[tomo|Tomo]] to the Session 10 rescue, and his caution kept both castaways unmarked.

### Hidden truths

- His caution marks the island's rule before he can put words on it. Watch him refuse living fruit and the survivors' law shows itself the quiet way.

### Threads

The rescue half of [[take-on-aruhe|Take on Aruhe]] is behind him: lifted off beside [[sandro|Sandro]] in Session 10, and the castaways' account feeds [[perrin-and-nona|Perrin and Nona]].
