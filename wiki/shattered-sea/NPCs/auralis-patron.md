## At a glance

- **Role.** The power behind Perrin's warlock magic: the Antheri guardian holding the fissure into the [[elemental-plane-of-water|Elemental Plane of Water]].
- **Wants.** The fissure kept shut, and a hand that can leave the trench and act for him above.
- **Voice.** One word at a time, each set down like a verdict. He counts elapsed time in geological units, and he addresses people at a slight angle, as if watching the conversation from just outside it.
- **Found at.** The trench beneath the [[drowned-maw|Drowned Maw]]. He acts above the water only through [[perrin-black-jaw|Perrin Black-Jaw]] and the whale-light.

> [!narration] First look
> The water turns cold, and the cold keeps gaining weight until it rests on your chest. You stand on worked stone scored with long channels that run down into black water. Pale lights drift above the floor where a face should be, and the attention behind them settles on you the way a lamp turns. The lights hold, and the water stills. One word arrives from far below. "Grow."

## Play

- **Opening move.** Contact comes when his need drives it: a word into Perrin's skull, or the whale-light when the bond needs Perrin to see. His attention is monitoring before it is anything else, and he answers rather than opens.
- **Opens them up.** Perrin putting direct questions to his patron moves him. The bond answers one word at a time, and a vision shows what a word cannot.
- **Shuts them down.** Any call for a long explanation. Every contact costs focus he cannot spare, so he cuts the exchange to a word and returns his attention to the fissure.
- **Will share.** Warning of what approaches Perrin, the next word the bond needs, and the plain fact of his attention turning.
- **Will not share.** Why Perrin. He cannot explain himself at length, and he does not volunteer the whole of what he knows.

## Depth

### History

The Antheri built Auralis about -2000 DR on the trench floor beneath the [[drowned-maw|Drowned Maw]], as a body and machine for a mind they had lost. He is not a rider inside the machine. He is the machine, bound to hold the fissure into the [[elemental-plane-of-water|Elemental Plane of Water]], and he slept through the fall of his makers. He woke on Day -5, roughly three hours after [[umberlee|Umberlee]] took Fisk's fleet in the water above him, when the [[pearl-of-souls|Pearl of Souls]]' signal crossed the boundary and drew the [[leviathan|Leviathan]] through. He read the Leviathan as a parasitic invader in the realm that is his alone to hold.

Soon after, the *[[vestra|Vestra]]* went down, and Auralis found [[perrin-black-jaw|Perrin Black-Jaw]] drowning in her hold. He held the rattkin alive, and the act forged a warlock pact hidden from Perrin. Perrin credits the cloak he wore. The rescue took the nearest mind within reach, and it never chose a champion. It stays because Auralis cannot leave the trench, so he needs a hand that can.

### Hidden truths

- **The pact is his own, unclaimed.** Perrin does not know he carries one, and he credits his cloak with the rescue. Exposure asks Perrin to weigh everything since Keth-Naar against a patron he has never met. The tell is the pale whale-light that gathers round his cloak when he reaches for his patron. The Party can put the question to Auralis through the bond. An Arcana read of Perrin's magic at DC 15 places its source far beneath the sea. The cold current at the *Vestra*'s open hatch leads a diver down to the working that kept him alive.
- **He is a machine, and no god.** Perrin, the [[waveservants|Waveservants]], the [[sentinels-of-the-eyrie|Sentinels of the Eyrie]] and the [[dravosi-crown|Dravosi Crown]] are all kept from it, because a patron outside every divine jurisdiction invites capture or destruction from any power that learns of him. Exposure confirms Perrin's pact and opens the heresy question in [[the-hound-of-god|The Hound of God]]. The tell is that his signs keep to one rattkin: the light shows where Perrin is, and the words come to him alone. The Party can press him through the bond across visits. The soul-pledge chamber lies in the Deep Works, and a diver who enters it can read its script. Comparing accounts of the whale-light ties the sightings to Perrin's whereabouts.
- **He has touched others.** Auralis touched other mortals more lightly than Perrin across the years, and he never named himself to them. Some did good with the gift and some did ill, and each touched soul knows only their own gift, so a face met in play may know more of Perrin's patron than Perrin does. The tell is a gift with no visible source. When the table needs a touched face, invent one fresh as background, never an existing NPC. Leave the list open on purpose.
