## Roleplaying

- **Wants.** See the client accept the feeding terms before the squad commits.
- **Voice.** A Kalowe accent with a Sylvan edge. Short clauses and exact terms. She repeats the feeding clause without apology.
- **Opening move.** At [[the-flat-water|the Flat Water]] in [[kalowe|Kalowe]], she reads the prospective task against its feeding terms. She starts civil and businesslike.
- **Leverage.** She speaks the contract for all four [[five-blades|Five Blades]] and can refuse a job that separates them. Committing the squad ties up their time and supplies.
- **Opens them up.** An employer who specifies both the task and the daily feed.
- **Shuts them down.** Treating blood as an optional extra or calling her a pet.
- **Will share.** The rate, the feeding clause and her home on [[murrat|Murrat]].
- **Requests.** Asked to waive feeding payment: ==Varet points at the clause. “You can hire us for a job without bloodshed. You still have to feed us.”==
- **If ignored.** She takes the next clear proposal to the squad.
