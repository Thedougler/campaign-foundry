## At a glance

- **Role.** Artificer and salvage engineer.
- **Wants.** To keep her workshop operating and continue private salvage research.
- **Voice.** Practical workshop terms, with no patience for romanticising mechanisms.
- **Found at.** Her workshop in Calveno.

> [!narration] First look
> A white-haired artificer in a worked breastplate looks up from oil, brass, and hot wire. Tool marks score her gloves, and she asks about the mechanism before the story.

## Play

- **Opens them up.** A mechanism she gets to study or repair.
- **Shuts them down.** Being asked to abandon Calveno or treat her shop as a standing resource.
- **Will share.** Practical answers on mechanisms and what repair or copying would require.
- **Will not share.** Her workshop or research without terms.
- **If pressed.** She returns to the work and says, “The work stays here.”

## Depth

### History

Catarina works on mechanisms and Antheri salvage from her permanent Calveno workshop. She completed work for Delmar, Crissdalynn, and Zort before the party left. She also tried and failed to stop Solange's final ritual. On the push into the [[calveno-sewer-magazines|Calveno Sewer Magazines]] she brought [[ragnito|Ragnito]] and the owl [[strix|Strix]], which joined the local defence.

In Session 9 she sailed with the Party aboard the [[uncertainty|Uncertainty]]. As the refit's bills reached [[nona-black-jaw|Nona Black-Jaw]], she told the others she was taking it on for them all: "Just Catarina's taking it on, of you all and looking, you know." She drew the travel role of navigation intelligence. When the council weighed flying [[crissdalynn-khinriss|Crissdalynn]] ahead to [[sparhold|Sparhold]], she sketched a bird-sized catapult to launch her from the deck, then built it below during the first watch with her opera turned up.

She met the voyage's troubles with [[ragnito|Ragnito]], her spider-like construct, refitted as a cannon mount. When three rescued captives panicked, she set Ragnito to staring them down until the intimidation took, then left the construct on deck as a deterrent, saying, "Don't touch Ragnito." She can make ginger essence steam and smell warm by magic, and she strengthened [[delmar-fisk|Delmar]]'s seasickness drink with it. As the [[hcs-ordinance|HCS Ordinance]] closed in she told the Party, "I don't wanna act abnormal and weird… I work for them." The time aboard counted as a long rest for her: spell slots back, seven temporary hit points on top. By her account, [[rupert-knighton|Rupert Knighton]] had strongly encouraged her to evacuate Calveno. When the inspection wore on she turned her umbrella's magical tinkering to imitating [[umberlee|Umberlee]]'s laughter around the ship, and her Persuasion 23 made [[aleksander-malone|Aleksander Malone]] step back, unsettled.

She later led the Party down to a crafting workshop she has set up aboard, her Calveno workshop staying home. There she unveiled [[lamaraes-fang|Lamarae's Fang]], a sword forged from whip shark bone infused with metal, and offered it to Delmar for aid in finding an axolotl, whose components she means to study. Delmar agreed to find her one.

During the crossing toward [[aruhe|Aruhe]] she claimed the night shift below, telling the deck she wanted to see what she could do with "that pearl power she bought back in Hell". About two hours in, the ship heard a bang, then a second, and something wet hit a floor. The sounds stopped and nothing more came from below. Crissdalynn asked whether she was all right, and the ruling put the noise down to an accident with the wild pearl and some change in her appearance, or so it ran. She was fine. She was also the only soul aboard who could read the spell scroll Shepherd Grigori left in the galley that night. Its arcane script was gibberish to everyone but her.

### Hidden truths

- Her completed work is with its recipients, and the source doesn't record unfinished commission.
- Her workshop's permanence is a boundary. No faction claims her.
- The wild pearl behind the night's bangs has no name on record. By her own account she bought its power back in Hell, and that is all anyone knows.

### Threads

She is a practical contact in the Calveno aftermath of **Simone's Hunters**. The Party still owes her the axolotl she priced [[lamaraes-fang|Lamarae's Fang]] at.
