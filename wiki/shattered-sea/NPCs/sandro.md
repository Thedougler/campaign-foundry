## At a glance

- **Role.** Calveno captive and castaway, lifted off [[western-landing|Western Landing]]'s beach with [[nino|Nino]] when the [[uncertainty|Uncertainty]] came through the reef gap.
- **Wants.** To get off Aruhe for good and home to Calveno, Nino with him.
- **Found at.** Aboard the [[uncertainty|Uncertainty]], carried off the sand from the broken [[vethka|Vethka]]'s shade.

> [!narration] First look
> A man steps out of the proa's shade when the ship clears the reef gap, and a signal fire smokes down to embers behind him. He stays where the sand meets the dry shingle and calls across the surf. "You came through the gap. Take us off this beach."

## Play

- **Opens them up.** Asking after the wreck, the reef gap, or a sail home.
- **Shuts them down.** Any thought of his going back onto the island. He told the Party plainly that he would not go back.
- **Will share.** The survivors' count, the taking-rule, the island's threats list, the wolfrabbit toll, and the fate he guesses for the ones who climbed to the terraces.

## Depth

### History

The Calveno raid took him captive toward [[karath|Karath]], and the storm hit the captured fleet on the way. By his count, 100 to 150 of the captured came ashore on [[aruhe|Aruhe]] alive, and how many of them live still he cannot say. He assumes not many. The storm put the [[vethka|Vethka]] proas on the reef, and he came ashore on a broken outrigger strut with [[nino|Nino]] and [[tomo|Tomo]].

Tomo broke the fruit taboo the first night, and the island took him. After that, Sandro and Nino kept to the broken hull's shade while the signal fire watched the gap for them, too frightened of the water and the forest to leave the sand. They lived on fish caught off the beach. The [[wolfrabbit|wolfrabbits]] took at least a dozen of the first captives who went into the woods. He watched a dozen die the first day alone, one of the beasts running right past the shade where he sat. Some survivors climbed to the stone terraces half a day inland. A few came back saying the terraces were safe, and none were seen again. In all that time he never stepped off the beach, the only ground he ever called remotely safe.

When the wolfrabbits swept the landing beach, the pack passed the hull's shade by. Sandro sat coiling thread to build himself a net while Nino stared at the water, shell-shocked, and neither of them was noticed.

The island's hate has an aim, in his telling. "The island really hates the Grung," he said, and its animals "will choose death if it means killing a Grung." He gave Perrin the same warning in person: the island truly, truly hates his kind.

The [[uncertainty|Uncertainty]] came through the reef gap in Session 10 and found him and Nino waving from the sand. While the ship held off about 60 feet from shore, [[delmar-fisk|Delmar]] and [[crissdalynn-khinriss|Crissdalynn]] flew the two of them off the beach, and the castaways gave their account aboard. He drew the Party the island's rule, "do not take anything from the island," and told them he had watched people die "horrible, quick deaths" for taking "just any, a fruit." The threats, as the survivors counted them, ran to wild boars, the rabbits, deer, bats and birds. Delmar called [[ruma-delacroix|Ruma]] and [[jean-claude-tabarnack|Jean-Claude]] out to hear it all, having assured the castaways these were friends.
