## Depth

### History

She served aboard the HCS Surety and was named to the prize crew when the crew took the cutter. The [[murrat|Murrat]] shore party returned with wounded, four of them, and they were hers to see to.

At Calveno she went ashore on shore leave and did not return to the ship. A man visited her at her [[le-paludi|Le Paludi]] boardinghouse, and she left the next morning. What can be found of her ends there.

In Session 9 the Party counted the [[uncertainty|Uncertainty]]'s five original crew and named her among them, with [[thunk|Thunk]], [[geoffrey-draves|Geoffrey Draves]] and the galley's Gregory ([[shepherd-grigori|Shepherd Grigori]]), one more besides. They recalled that she and Gregory went ashore at [[calven-and-calveno|Calveno]] together.
