## Roleplaying

- **Appearance.** A spare old man with sun-browned skin and deep-set hazel eyes. Grey stubble covers his scalp above a clean-shaven face and bent nose. He scrubs his big palms clean before handling fruit. His loose shirt is cream linen, worn over faded russet breeches. A strip torn from its hem binds one cuff.
- **Wants.** To remain beneath the vine and give the willing a safe place.
- **Voice.** Slow and certain: “The ground gives.”
- **Opening move.** Beneath the vine in [[the-pantry|The Pantry]] with Beppe Sarti and Marco Lenzi, sorting windfall. Indifferent.
- **Leverage.** He is the bonesetter and elder of the Pantry survivors, so he heals the injured and speaks for the three who would stay. He argues with nobody who chooses to leave, and nothing moves him from the vine.
- **Opens them up.** Seeing the party keep the island's rule.
- **Shuts them down.** Picking living fruit. He steps back until dawn.
- **Will share.** Eat only what falls and walk by day. Avoid still water and open grass. Leave before the sun is high, because a midday departure risks finishing in the dark. He has heard Hinewai say she has never held anyone who asked to leave.
- **Questions for a Grung.** Before sharing food, Renzo holds the fruit in his lap. ==He looks you in the eye. "Who sent you?"== His next question is, "Gold on you?" Finally he asks, "What do your hands take?" Plain answers and leaving the living fruit alone win his trust.
- **The voice's tree.** Asked about the grove, he repeats what Hinewai tells the camp at night: her tree bears fruit that shines like metal and gilds the hand. She allows people to take the fallen fruit. She stole the seed from the gold people's farms.
- **Will not share.** An argument with anyone who chooses to leave.
- **If pressed.** Offered a way off Aruhe, he turns it down for himself and blesses those who take it. He heals the injured and gives each PC a fallen fruit. The offer costs him nothing he holds dear, so he answers it without a roll (SRD 5.2.1, Influence). ==He folds your hand round a fallen guava. “The ground gives. Take it, and walk by day.”==

## History

Renzo heard Hinewai's voice first after the wreck and showed the camp how to survive. At Spoke Ring nineteen days ago, Carlo rose to follow the voice and most of the camp followed. Renzo now lives beneath the vine at the Pantry.

## Hidden truths

- Hinewai does count him as hers, as she does every Calveno who ate fallen fruit. Aruhe's responders spare them wherever they go.

## Threads

He anchors the survivor strand of **Taking on Aruhe** and would stay beneath the vine if the other Calveno took a way off Aruhe.
