## At a glance

- **Role.** Calveno wreck survivor, net-and-rigging mender, and the camp's runner for clean, claim-free food.
- **Wants.** Food brought back to camp without a claim on it, and to owe the woman in the woods nothing.
- **Voice.** Short declaratives in rope-and-sail idiom; rules stated as maintenance, never as blessings.
- **Found at.** The safe riverbanks between the [[Slack Basin]] and the fruiting margins, walking the edges with [[Oren Vask]].

> [!narration] First look
> A broad-shouldered woman with forearms nicked by a netting needle re-lays a fallen fruit pile on a broad leaf, then stands back to count breaths until the birds start again. Tar and crushed green come off her in equal measure. She says, "Birds stopped. Somebody's touched something. Walk soft and leave it be."

## Play

- **Opens them up.** Take only what lies loose, build from driftwood, and ask her to mend a line or judge whether a thing is safe to receive.
- **Shuts them down.** Treat the island as a benefactor or the woman as a goddess and she goes flat. Reach a careless hand toward living growth and she puts her body between the picker and the stem.
- **Will share.** The camp's survival rules in trade terms, and how she reads the tell of a claim: leaning stems and one silent breath, read the way a sailor reads weather.
- **Will not share.** Who made the rule. She has never asked, and the question feels like a debt weighed in a doorway. Her first week on the island.
- **If pressed.** The rule works, and why it works belongs to people with slack in their lives. She picks up her needles and walks the safe edge away from you.

## Depth

### History

Celia came off the wreck with the Calveno camp and walked its first weeks beside [[Renzo Canale]]. When the camp split ship against garden at Spoke Ring, she stayed inland for the rules, never for the voice. She walks the safe edges with [[Oren Vask]] now, foraging the margins and sent ahead when someone looks about to break the rule.

### Hidden truths

- In her first week she cut a living pole for a dying crew mate's shelter, was marked until dawn, and fought off a wolfrabbit in the grass. Fighting back was allowed, though she never learned the rule's wording. She tells no one, because her survival reads to her as proof the island runs on rules rather than reverence, and the frightened camp holds together on reverence. The tells are a wolfrabbit-width scar on her right forearm, and a camp mate's account of her return at dawn, bloodied and refusing to say with what.

### Threads

She is one of **Taking on Aruhe**'s law-keepers, and her quiet disbelief in the garden's godhood puts her beside Matteo Scola without sharing his panic.
