## Roleplaying

- **Wants.** Settle a petty score against the [[sandy-grove-coven|Sandy Grove Coven]] while protecting his standing in [[penance-bar|Penance Bar]]. He wants the Party and townspeople to do his violence for him.
- **Torn between.** Fear of public exposure and the need to appear a blameless servant of [[tyr|Tyr]]. He is too deep in his lie to hear the god's guidance.
- **Voice.** Grand public blessings become defensive assertions of rank when challenged. He insists that his life's work is Tyr's bidding.
- **Opening move.** At [[sorrowbell|Sorrowbell]]'s door, greet the people answering his call as noble heroes. He presents the woods coven as the threat and sends help against it.
- **Leverage.** His bishop's title and the frightened town give him others to act through. Public deniability matters to him, so he incites privately and keeps his own hands visibly clean.
- **Opens them up.** Acceptance of his accusation against the coven.
- **Shuts them down.** Treating the coven as framed or asking to see his locked quarters and the true ledger.
- **Will share.** His false account of the coven's guilt and his insistence that Tyr protects every temple.
- **Will not share.** The discrepancy between his public tally and his true ledger, or his private part in stirring the mob.
- **Requests.** Kill the people he calls witches before sundown.
- **If pressed.** Retreat into the authority of his office, then the accusation: "She was a witch!"
- **If ignored.** Continue stirring the mob and seek to use the demons themselves against the coven. Follow this escalation on [[penance-bar-siege|Penance Bar Siege]].

## Hidden truths

Ashe's fear-stirring and lies attract and sustain the swarm. He blames the coven to preserve his standing and settle his own petty score. The Party can suspect him through [[sula-marn|Sula Marn]] and investigation in the village. Comparing the ledger with the public tally proves fabrication. The demon's accusation at Sorrowbell identifies its cause directly.

Tyr warned him before withdrawing protection. Ashe became unable to hear those warnings. The shrine is already unhallowed, though its appearance has not changed. The demon's unopposed entrance and kiss expose both losses. [[sin-and-sanctuary|Sin and Sanctuary]] explains them. The locked confrontation and transformation remain future developments.

He is unrelated to [[corvin-knighton|Corvin Knighton]] or [[corbin-knighton|Corbin Knighton]].
