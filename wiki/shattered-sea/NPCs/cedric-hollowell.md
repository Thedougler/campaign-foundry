## Depth

### History

For years a young sailor returned to the spot where he and his daughter had danced, and for years she wasn't there. Then one evening Cedric was, and his terms were simple. Geoffrey was not a man of means. Until he could provide for a wife and family for the rest of their days, he was not to see her again. Geoffrey agreed. He did not see another choice.

### Hidden truths

Whether the terms were a father's protection or a door he expects a worthy man to walk through is not known.

### Threads

His terms are the arithmetic behind [[geoffrey-draves|Geoffrey Draves]]'s vow, and the reason the crew's carpenter sails with vagabonds.
