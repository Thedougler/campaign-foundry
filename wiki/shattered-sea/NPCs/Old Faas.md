## At a glance

- **Role.** Bosun and rigger aboard [[Uncertainty]], eight gold a week at the bosun rate. Rated bosun under [[Barnaby Rook]] before the capture.
- **Wants.** A ship, a crew moved safely along her lines, and the truth told without making a performance of it.
- **Voice.** Short declarative sentences, never raised. Little aboard surprises him. He elaborates once if asked, then the subject closes.
- **Found at.** The rigging above [[Uncertainty]]'s deck, in almost any weather.

> [!narration] First look
> A broad man comes down the shrouds and takes the deck on two pegs capped in brass, steadier aloft than he looks standing still. The salt has worked the creases around his eyes into a permanent squint. He sets a thumb against the nearest stay and weighs its tension before he looks at you, and nothing about the height or the drop has drawn a word from him. "Ja. You'll have questions."

## Play

- **Opens them up.** A direct question. He answers short and true, and if asked about the old days he tells of running cargo with [[Barnaby Rook]].
- **Shuts them down.** Pushing him to pick which account of his legs is true. *"No. That is not a story for the deck."*
- **Will share.** The state of every line aboard and how long each has left, and the Murrat account, told flatly: *"He took us to Murrat. Wanted a live one. Six of us went ashore. The surgeon saw the four who came back."*
- **Will not share.** The absence he has felt since he was seven. He tells it plainly if asked directly, but he has not volunteered it to a captain. To Faas it is simply how he is.
- **If pressed.** He elaborates exactly once, then the subject is closed. *"Ja. It was a busy week."*

## Depth

### History

He is a rigger of uncertain age who has outlasted several captains and more ships than he remembers. Under Rook he sailed to [[Murrat]] with the shore party that tried to take a live [[Moucheron]], and he was not among the four who came back to the surgeon's care. [[Alys Kuiper]] saw to those four. He told the Party this himself, between courses, the way a man reports weather he has survived.

He was named to the prize crew when the crew took the cutter. On the crossing to Calveno he showed [[Perrin Black-Jaw]] the ropes and swapped old cargo stories about Rook. He went ashore on Calveno leave and came back two peg legs lighter, both well fitted, good timber and brass ferrules, the socket work done properly, and he stands shorter than he used to on them. He gave three accounts of the loss in one week, each with the same conviction. A festival bull loosed near the Bridge took the right leg. A scaffold on the south side of the Bridge, climbed for a better view of the [[Il Gioco delle Beffe|Beffa]] in [[Le Paludi]], took the left. The canal under the [[Mercatura]] dock took both on a Tuesday. About forty minutes after coming aboard he went back up the rigging, and he is faster there than he was before.

At the [[Tallow Row]] tables he caught [[Thunk]] counting cards and walked him in on fifteen years of his own standing. There he coached Thunk to lose the first hour and folded a hand he should have won rather than tip the table. He was still fast on his pegs when [[Thassos]] called the game.

### Hidden truths

- He does not feel fear. At seven he fell from a hayloft onto a stone barn floor and woke after three days with the thing gone, the way a man born without a smell does not smell. He has no death wish, and bravery has nothing to do with it, because bravery wants something to overcome. Tell: he goes aloft in any weather without a flinch and works a squall with steady hands. Ask him and he tells you plainly. Watch him work a storm. Ask an old crewman why nobody bets against him.
- No one can say which account of the legs is true, and Faas offers all three with identical conviction. Confronted with all three at once he listens, nods, and says, *"Ja. It was a busy week."* The peg legs do not slow him on the rigging, and the reason is not on offer.

### Threads

He sits in the working of [[Uncertainty]]'s rigging and in [[Perrin Black-Jaw]]'s survey of the sea, one of the few aboard who can tell by feel when a line is close to failing.
