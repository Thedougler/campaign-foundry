> [!narration] First look
> At the edge of the fire's light, a broad-shouldered man turns his left forearm over and back, fine white scars crossing it. His eyes go past you, down the trail you came by. “You came up from the coast?”

## Roleplaying

- **Wants.** To get home to his wife and daughter in Calveno.
- **Opening move.** Among the seven at [[the-pantry|The Pantry]], a Calveno rigger and survivor of the wreck, watching the trail. Indifferent.
- **Opens them up.** Talk of the road back to the ship, which he would walk with the others.
- **If pressed.** Offered a way off Aruhe, he asks only when they leave. It is the road to his family, so he answers it without a roll (SRD 5.2.1, Influence). ==His hand stops on his forearm. “My wife will not believe it. Tell me when we leave.”==
