> [!narration] First look
> Out where the reeds open onto the water, a duck stands knee-deep and does not move. He is white and about eight feet tall, with a dockside wrestler's shoulders, and he wears a war-kilt, bracers and shoulder guards trimmed in gold. A golden crest burns above his head like a lion's mane, with a halo over it. Lightning and flame play across his shoulders, and you smell the sharp air of a lightning strike. A longsword rests in one fist and a great recurve bow hangs from the other, and his glowing eyes follow you across the water. He raises neither one. "Name. Business. And are you leaving my marsh the way you found it?"

## Roleplaying

A duck who sprang every trap meant for someone else, again and again, and now stands in front of a whole marsh so that no one else has to.

- **Wants.** A true name, a true business and a promise to leave [[yssenmoor|Yssenmoor]] the way the Party found it, said out loud by each of them. After that he wants to talk about his old master for as long as someone will listen.
- **Torn between.** The fear of failing the one place he was left, which makes him stop every stranger. Against it stands his gentleness. He knows what it is to be sent ahead to get hurt, and he hates to hurt anyone who answers him straight.
- **Voice.** Plain, short and courteous, like a doorman who has said the same three questions ten thousand times and means each one. He repeats the challenge word for word. For his master's titles he slows down and runs them off like a proud nephew, and he saves his dry jokes for small things. A slow, level delivery, with the flat vowel on "marsh" held a beat. He says little to strangers and a great deal about the wizard.
- **Opening move.** At the edge of open water in [[yssenmoor|Yssenmoor]], blade and bow lowered, waiting for a proper answer. Indifferent to every PC, and patient with all of them.
- **Leverage.** He can let the Party go or stay, heal and restore them (*Greater Restoration*, *Raise Dead*, *Cure Wounds*), put his blade and bow behind a cause, or call out any lie he hears. Using it leaves the marsh unguarded while he is away, so he will not leave Yssenmoor for long. He will not harm anyone who has answered him straight.
- **Opens them up.** Plain, true answers to his three questions. Leaving the marsh as it was found. Calling him Sir, a title he gave himself and is proud of. Asking about the wizard.
- **Shuts them down.** A lie, which he hears at once. Refusing to give a name or a business. Fouling or taking from the marsh. Mocking his old master.
- **Will share.** His name and titles, the lie of the marsh, every deed of his old master, and what it was to be a familiar who sprang the traps and came back after each death with every memory. He changed slowly, here, over years.
- **Will not share.** Where his old master went. He does not know, and says so, and the question stings. He also cannot say whether his master's tears made the sending worse or better.
- **Requests.** Leave to cross or camp: ==He lowers the bow a hand's width. "You can go, or stay. That's still your call."== Price: a true answer to all three questions. It makes him Friendly toward the speaker. Healing or restoration for a hurt Party that has answered him: ==He sets the blade across his knee and holds out a wing. "Hold still. I've done this before."== Price: they leave the marsh as it was found. It spends one of his daily castings. Taking something from the marsh: ==His wing comes down across the way. "No."== He will not move.
- **If pressed.** Asking him to leave the marsh and fight beside the Party is Hesitant. The check is Charisma (Persuasion), DC 16 (his Intelligence of 16 against the floor of 15), with Advantage once he is Friendly (SRD 5.2.1, Influence). On a success: ==He looks at the water, then at you. "A day. Then I'm back."== On a failure: ==He shakes his crest. "I stay with my marsh."== The same urging cannot be tried again for 24 hours. Lying to win him round is Unwilling, since he hears it.
- **If ignored.** A Party that walks past without answering finds him in its path again, and he asks once more, louder. If it still walks on, he blocks the way. He shoots only when something in the marsh is hurt or taken.
- **Invitations.**
  - [[crissdalynn-khinriss|Crissdalynn Khinriss]] charts the sea, and Yssenmoor is an unmapped corner of it. If her crew answers him straight, he will vouch for them, and the marsh becomes a safe place to put them ashore.
  - [[delmar-fisk|Delmar Fisk]] meets someone who hears every lie, including the ones he tells himself aloud about the five crews. Quackers' restoration magic is a bargain to weigh against the Pearl and [[umberlee|Umberlee]].
  - [[jean-claude-tabarnack|Jean-Claude Tabarnack]] meets a creature that was sent ahead to spring the traps and was freed. Quackers respects what Jean-Claude fights for, and he is also a bird eight feet tall, to a ranger who fears them.
  - [[perrin-black-jaw|Perrin Black-Jaw]] finds a place to enter on his survey of the sea, and an ally on the order of a patron who tests every promise Perrin makes.

## Quotes

> "Name. Business. And are you leaving my marsh the way you found it?"

The DM's line for his challenge to any stranger who comes into the marsh. He waits for a proper answer.

> "You can go. Or stay. That's still your call."

The DM's line for a stranger who has answered, to give them leave.

> "They cried, you know. Every time I came back."

The DM's line about his master, when Quackers talks of the deaths he remembers.

> "Best wizard I ever knew. Well, best two. Half of him couldn't reach the top shelf."

The DM's line about his master, who was two gnomes in one long coat.

## History

Quackers was the duck familiar of a wizard known as RTLB. That is two gnomes in one long coat, Reginald Toppington and Leeroy Bottomus, a master of duomancy, transfiguration and conjuration who tamed faeries and rescued the dragon clans. A familiar's job is to go ahead and spring every trap meant for someone else, so Quackers died to them over and over. He came back each time and remembers every death. His master cried every time, and Quackers never worked out whether that made the sending worse or better. Eventually the wizard freed him, gave him his own life and will, and left him the marsh. Quackers loves talking about his master, the titles, the feats and the reckless genius, and no wizard he has met since has matched him. Where the wizard went is unknown, and he is not in play.

A powerful ley line crosses [[yssenmoor|Yssenmoor]], and over time it changed Quackers into Sir Quackers. He became fully sentient and herculean in strength, and a celestial in the rules. He took the title himself, Sir Quackers the Fowl, Blade of the Wetlands, Guardian of the Marsh, Defender of the Weak, and decided to protect the place his master left him. Whether his master knew the ley line would do this is open.
