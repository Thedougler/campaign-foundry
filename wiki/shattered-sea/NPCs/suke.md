## Roleplaying

- **Wants.** Watch a prospective client without being cornered or crowded.
- **Voice.** Quiet, with long pauses before a short answer. He asks where people intend to stand.
- **Opening move.** At [[the-flat-water|the Flat Water]] in [[kalowe|Kalowe]], he puts his back against a wall and watches strangers. He starts reserved.
- **Leverage.** He contributes a fighter’s vigilance to the [[five-blades|Five Blades]]. Taking a position away from the wall costs him that view of the room.
- **Opens them up.** Space to watch and a direct account of the task.
- **Shuts them down.** Someone blocking his view or reaching for his blood keeps.
- **Will share.** What he has seen in the room and whether someone is crowding the squad.
- **Requests.** Asked to join a crowded table: ==Suke looks at the wall behind him. “I can hear you from here.”==
- **If ignored.** He watches who approaches the squad and lets Varet speak.
