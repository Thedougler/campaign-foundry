> [!narration] First look
> Dark olive-green scales with darker banding make Ruk broad and large for a lizard folk. Scars cover his forearms and the left side of his neck. He braces against the wall with both hands flat on the table, watching every door. “Who sent you?”

## Roleplaying

- **Wants.** To protect Nona and the young people under her protection with his body and presence.
- **Torn between.** His absolute loyalty to Nona and the need to let people make their own choices.
- **Voice.** Measured, flat, and literal. He states threats and lies plainly.
- **Opening move.** Nona's employee Ruk is aboard her Passage ship [[short-manifest|Short Manifest]] as messenger to the Party.
- **Leverage.** His body shields Nona and the people under his protection. He observes physical danger and notices a liar's fear-sweat.
- **Opens them up.** Direct statements, food offered before demands, and respect for territory and the safety of the young.
- **Shuts them down.** Lies, threats to Nona or a protected person, and crossing him twice.
- **Will share.** What he has literally observed, including a liar's fear-sweat and the position of a threat. He warns the Party about the HCS Ordinance and Malone, advising them to avoid the ship and let the Dravosi handle their own problems.
- **Will not share.** A way past the people or territory he is guarding.
- **If pressed.** He names the threat without raising his voice. “You threatened her. I heard you.”

- **Combat profile.** AC 16, 104 HP, speed 30 ft. Swim speed 30 ft. CR 6. He uses [[ruks-baseball-bat|Ruk's Baseball Bat]] and his bite to grapple, then uses Pin Down. Relentless Endurance, Hungry Jaws, Hold Breath, and Territorial Senses keep him standing.

## Quotes

> "YOUR NONA WAS WORRIED SICK ABOUT YOU. Sorry Ma'am"

Rounding on [[perrin-black-jaw|Perrin Black-Jaw]] for letting his grandmother, [[nona-black-jaw|Nona Black-Jaw]], go on thinking he had died in the sinking of the [[vestra|Vestra]], then catching himself before her.

## History

Ruk has guarded Nona for twenty years, understanding her through territory, loyalty, feeding rituals, and care for the young. He was one of [[vincenzo-black-jaw|Vincenzo Black-Jaw]]'s original fixers, with [[enzo|Enzo]] the other, and he stayed on when Nona took the operations over. He and Enzo divide the work. Ruk reads physical danger while Enzo reads social danger. At the safe house he guards Felix Aho, a captured Grung prisoner. He pushed Perrin into a seat when the Party first came to the table, and pinned Felix to his chair when they marched him in bound through the festival crowds. When [[nona-black-jaw|Nona]]'s people catalogued the sewer loot, Ruk handled its six vials of [[grung-toxin-vials|Grung tincture]] with care and filed them as ordinary Grung poison, the whole of what they looked like to him.

## Hidden truths

- Ruk cannot ignore a lie or threat he observes. A direct question can expose what a more diplomatic guard would conceal.
- His loyalty is built from repeated acts of protection rather than a contract. The Party can see it when he places his body between Nona and danger.
