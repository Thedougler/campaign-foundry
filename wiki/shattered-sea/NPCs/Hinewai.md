## At a glance

- **Role.** Undead guardian bound to [[Aruhe]] and the Death Bloom.
- **Wants.** Aruhe untaken, her graves unharmed, and the [[Calven and Calveno|Calveno]] survivors to remain in her garden.
- **Voice.** Low, calm, and old, with long silences between short sentences.
- **Found at.** [[Memorial Grove]]. Elsewhere she appears only as the woman in the woods.

> [!narration] First look
> Between a pair of trunks the dark is deeper than it should be, and two orange eyes open at the height of a tall woman's face. Silence stretches until a low voice says, "You ate what my island gave you. You are already mine."

## Play

- **Opens them up.** A Grung without a gold seal who stands before her without the pull toward the graves.
- **Shuts them down.** Taking from the island or threatening fire.
- **Will share.** The Grung send expeditions against her graves. She explains her garden's rules and permits anyone who wishes to leave to do so.
- **Will not share.** Her history or any reason to trust the Grung.
- **If pressed.** She claims fruit-eaters as hers and can bind an intruder with roots. She released Jean-Claude when the Party agreed to bring him before her and obey her garden's rules. Damage to the Death Bloom brings her Blight form.

## Depth

### History

Karath captured Hinewai, an elf arch druid of a [[Midchain]] island, and worked her in its hatcheries. The raid that took her struck a beach four islands east of Karath at night, a coast meeting grung for the first time. A toxin went in at the base of her skull at the same hour each morning, and the antidote that followed was small enough to keep her hands at the bench and her mind too dulled for plans. She tore the magic loose to raise the pens' hound-sized vermin. On the days the toxin ran weak, she and [[The Unnamed Companion|a companion]] taken there before her whispered of fire and open water. One night they fired the long hut with a lantern and took the eastern path for the ridge and the cliffs. An arrow found him above the channel; she reached the sand first, and he lay face down in the surf twenty feet behind her. The wound closed under three castings, and his chest stayed still through all of them. She cursed [[Mystra]] by name over his body. At first light she carried him inland, and her bare hands opened his grave beneath a fruit tree that bore, a month early, the fruit he used to eat green off the ground. The dawn after she finished it, black flowers stood open along the treeline, and the wolf and deer she passed kept their heads lowered. Fruit came down within reach on her weak days, leaves held rain through the nights, and she lived against the tree's north side until a grung sail traced the treeline without landing in the third summer. She fused her soul and body to the land, and the land kept both. Her grief became the island's law.

#### The spring expeditions

Grung compelled by [[Grung Authority Seal|authority seals]] burned a straight firebreak about twenty feet wide through Hinewai's garden in spring. Their orders led them toward the grove and its graves. The road was lined with the clean white skeletons of more than a hundred grung and their spent seals.

#### Session 12: the summons

Hinewai's beasts killed the grung who had accompanied the shipwrecked Calveno, and she began protecting the survivors. About two dozen remained further inland. The Party's four rescued survivors trusted her, while [[Matteo Scola]] had left them because he distrusted her. One survivor told the Party that she hated the Grung.

At the burnt road, Hinewai appeared as deep shadow between trees, with burning orange eyes. Her telepathic voice reached only [[Jean-Claude Tabarnack|Jean-Claude]]. She called him an invader and a kidnapper for taking people she considered hers. Hinewai said, “Mine do not need rescuing.” She insisted that they were already home. When Jean-Claude challenged her claim over sentient beings, she accused him of murder and manipulation.

[[Delmar Fisk|Delmar]] honoured the garden and its dead with a toast, then explained that Jean-Claude had escaped the Grung's chains. Roots bound Jean-Claude as Hinewai demanded that the Party bring him before her. Delmar asked for a guarantee of his safety, and she promised he could leave if he followed her rules. [[Crissdalynn Khinriss|Crissdalynn]] asked whether anyone could willingly leave the garden, and the answer was yes. Hinewai released Jean-Claude on condition that the Party bring him before her and obey the garden's rules. She told them, “Meet me in the grove.” The eyes vanished and the surrounding shadows looked ordinary again. The Party followed the burnt road toward her.

### Hidden truths

- She is a tragic villain. Her love for Aruhe is real, and she is unhealthy for the island's flora and fauna all the same.
- Removing her presence realistically means burning down most of Aruhe, an obviously horrible solution.
- The Death Bloom is her body and place-bound phylactery. Destroying it before killing her prevents her return.
- Every Calveno who ate fallen fruit belongs to her garden, and Aruhe's responders spare them. The Grung curse comes from the old capture and torture.

### Threads

She drives **Taking on Aruhe** and the island's conflict with the Grung.
