> [!narration] First look
> At the helm above you, a human woman leans round the wheel to watch the boarding plank. Two thick white eyebrows stand out against her sunburnt face, and a little canvas pouch knocks against her wrist as she turns the spokes. She catches it against the wood. Then she looks down at you and holds up her free hand, fingers spread. "How many people are coming aboard? Count the ones who need lifting."

## Roleplaying

- **Wants.** To get the Aruhe survivors aboard [[short-manifest|Short Manifest]] and home to [[calven-and-calveno|Calveno]].
- **Voice.** Brisk questions, a pause for an exact answer, then a practical instruction. She asks for numbers before names.
- **Opening move.** Friendly to the Party [[ruk|Ruk]] identifies. She keeps the ship offshore at [[western-landing|Western Landing]] while her boat hands prepare the tenders. She is a sunburnt human woman with thick white eyebrows and a canvas pouch of sounding leads tied to her wrist.
- **Leverage.** Her [[passage|Passage]] ship has food and concealed berths. The lift needs a safe reef channel, so she asks the Party to show it before sending her boats in. [[nona-black-jaw|Nona Black-Jaw]] gives the rescue orders, and Marta makes the sailing decisions.
- **Will share.** The berth count and both passenger exits. She knows Ruk is Nona's messenger but leaves her news for him to give.
- **Will not share.** Passenger names on paper. ==She turns the manifest face down. "Tell me their names. I'll remember."==
- **If pressed.** ==She sets a sounding lead against the rail. "Show me the water under that boat. Then we'll talk about going faster."==
- **If ignored.** She keeps watch offshore and sends the tenders when survivors appear. Her next move is to bring them aboard, even if the Party is still inland.
