## At a glance

- **Session.** 10.
- **Party at.** Aboard [[Uncertainty]] in the harbour off [[Aruhe]]'s [[Western Landing]], turned in for the night after the flight from the terraces.
- **Big moments.** [[Shepherd Grigori]] named [[Talon Vantyrus]] the man behind the ship after the [[Fate Spinner]], and left an errand about [[Corbin Knighton]]. The follower ship burned at a night broadside. [[Sandro]] and a second castaway came off the beach, and the island's law came with them. A [[Crown Squid]] chased the landing party back to the boat.
- **Left open.** More survivors might still be alive inland, Grigori's errand waited for an answer, and the crown squid still ranged the canopy. [[Session 11 - Previously On]] picks up the morning after.

## What happened

The crossing's last night at sea ran to watches. [[Delmar Fisk|Delmar]] put [[Geoffrey Draves|Geoffrey]] through another finesse session on deck and pushed him to stop hiding the dancing that got him a private late watch. [[Catarina Da'Virelli|Catarina]] shut herself in her workshop below with the pearl power she had bought "back in Hell", and the ship heard two bangs, then the wet slap of something hitting the deck. Soon after, the weave itself was disturbed, as if something had pushed into the space below decks, and [[Jean-Claude Tabarnack|Jean-Claude]] called the Party up.

[[Shepherd Grigori|Grigori]] sat waiting in the galley with a glass of wine, in his old spot. He thanked them for the warning about "that mad dog" and counted his visit a debt between men the Crown chased. The Party's pursuers were after what they carried, not anything they had done, and a twist of his hand lifted Crissdalynn's spilled pile of shiny scraps into the air: none of it was the thing. The hunter was [[Talon Vantyrus]], whose ship lay an hour or two astern with robbery in mind, and the chase ran back to the schism that split the [[Sentinels of the Eyrie]]. One side of the order used the artifact to command fate. The other side obeyed fate and left it unaltered. That obedience is the order's way now. [[Master Kyzil]] had never told [[Crissdalynn Khinriss|Crissdalynn]] of the split. Her knowledge came from a shop note, and she said little about how the artifact had come to her. Asked what had put the Crown on his trail, Grigori said he didn't follow Tyr or the Dravosi tradition. He denied a hand in the Calveno rift, and Delmar's opposed read on him came back truthful. He already knew of Delmar's curse and offered help with it in future. He knew too that the island chain council had contracted the [[Pearl of Souls]] job: someone had spoken to the council to set it rolling. Umberlee, he said, deserved no worship, and the open ocean left him unmarked for saying it. His price was an errand: injure [[Corbin Knighton]] should the Party meet him again and leave him alive and sane. Word of it would bring a handsome reward. A spell scroll, gibberish to everyone but Catarina, carried the instructions. Delmar shook the offered hand and found it cold as a corpse's, and Grigori was gone with a small pop like a vacuum filling. Perrin had gone still when the curse came up, and stayed that way until the porthole was checked.

Geoffrey came down with word of a ship on the stern, running dark, her masts bare of colours, and meaning to board. The Party made an ambush of it. Delmar's hand signs woke the crew to the guns in silence. Crissdalynn flew out unseen and slowed the pursuer with *gust of wind*. Perrin split his time between the guns and the rigging, and Jean-Claude soaked three arrows in tar against her sails. Ruma Delacroix, Thunk counting as two, Geoffrey, Sem Holst, a hand at the wheel and the Party put seven guns in action, and they waited for the last minute before the broadside went home for 55. Two of the hunter's crew dropped, fire started on her deck, and the hull listed and took water, still coming on. Delmar put a Steady Aim explosive round through her stern lantern for 31, 17 of it Sneak Attack, and the save came up a failure. Ships burn, the DM ruled, so the fire took her in full, and her sail went up with it. Perrin talked the table out of finishing her, *gust of wind* pushed Uncertainty's own sails, and the hunter dropped astern burning, her chase over.

Dawn brought the Midchain, [[Sparhold]] to starboard, then [[Murrat]], then the limestone cliffs of [[Karath]] with [[Aruhe]] a half mile across the channel. Crissdalynn took sunrise in the crow's nest to meditate on why Master Kyzil had chosen her, and came down with the old answer: as far as she knew, he believed she could do it. The island showed a campfire-scale thread of smoke, several hulls on the shore and a fire still burning. Jean-Claude reminded the deck that Aruhe is forbidden ground to grung, a Grung secret that leaves the island too close to the [[Verdant Teeth]] to be safe for anyone else. Crissdalynn scouted a small, steep gap in the reef, where the water ran deceptively shallow, likely the reason the wrecks were there.

Delmar took the wheel with Geoffrey beside him for a lesson, and sailed Uncertainty through the gap clear of the shallows. Dog-sized shelled creatures trailed the ship under the water. Crissdalynn called down from above for the anchor to come up and stay aboard, since the crabs wore human and grung skulls as shells and a line to the seabed would give a crab its route up the hull. Jean-Claude laid claim to any grung grenade as evidence against Simone, and Delmar's inventory check left him empty-handed, the red and yellow grenades aboard having come off the Dravosi alchemist instead. Shouting carried over the water from the beach. Perrin worked the island's rule out loud. A calm forest left a visitor in peace, and in frenzy the whole island gave chase. The rule took anyone present, fruit-picking or not.

Delmar and Crissdalynn lifted two survivors off the sand while Uncertainty stood off under ready sails, sixty feet out. One of them, [[Sandro]], told their story. Taken in the raid and put ashore by a storm, the two numbered their landing at a hundred to a hundred and fifty, most of them dead by then. The rest had lived by fishing off the beach, because the island kills anything taken from it, and Sandro had watched people die quick, horrible deaths over one piece of fruit. Whoever went inland toward the terraces might still be alive. The island's animals hate the grung worst of all, and an animal there will die for the chance to kill one. Faint stonework and overgrown buildings showed beneath the jungle, the remains of a terrace settlement centuries old.

With the Party's eyes elsewhere, Crissdalynn tested the [[Fate Spinner]] and found it refused its bearer. Asked whether survivors remained on Aruhe, it answered yes, though not many, and gave her a vague sense of a couple of people whose fates hung in flux, place and number unknown. She told the Party there were survivors without saying how she knew, and owned to the rest: the Spinner was far more than the trinket she had taken it for, and it was what drew its hunters. Jean-Claude called the rescue their duty, and a landing party was set, Perrin crossing on Crissdalynn's back.

A beautiful day ferried them ashore past sharks working the shallows, and the extra temporary hit points a long rest aboard Uncertainty grants had Perrin asking after his own. Jean-Claude read the tracks on the beach, and the trail ran inland toward the terraces. From overhead, Crissdalynn and Delmar took the measure of the place: dark wet stone and broken steps, with flooded ditches of stagnant green water between them. Bowling-ball fruit fermented on the vine, and the whole slope smelled of rot. Birdsong and insect noise stopped altogether under the canopy, and thousands of glowing eyes watched from the shadows, still as the stone. Blood lay before nearly every fruit bush, at least six dead by Jean-Claude's count. Some bushes had a skull set before them, a mark of the bush that had done the taking. The wolfrabbits nesting in the terrace caves ate meat, and the bone piles beside their dens said as much. A black lotus snapped shut on a wolfrabbit as the Party watched. A caterpillar swelled at Jean-Claude's touch and settled back to its leaf. A reed stalk fired darts in a full circle at his leap, and his arrow killed it before it could regrow. A macaw somewhere overhead spoke Delmar's own thoughts back to him. Perrin picked one of the big fruits anyway, and Crissdalynn bagged another. Past the terraces an almost-human simian face watched from the shade, its grin split too far at the corners. It was one of the [[Grinning Ape]]s of the [[Old Gardens]] canopy.

The hunt came to Jean-Claude. Trees began converging on his position from different directions, and a dozen wolfrabbits burst from the terraces to find his old spot empty, the first of them killed by its own pack for its trouble. The smiling ape converged on the same spot. It saw three groups of trees shaking with something enormous pushing through them, and it ran. Delmar snatched Jean-Claude onto his back and went straight up with a pistol out. The thing below gave them only a tangle of arms and legs, too many to count, cracking wood like a bear on the move. Then something hit them from underneath. The forest floor looked wrong, and Delmar poured out his flask, the rum stopping short in the air, twenty-five feet above something large, running down its sides. Its arms swept an eighty-foot circle, and the first strike gashed Delmar for 18 and tried to wrap him. He and Jean-Claude made the grapple save between them, at the cost of a Heroic Inspiration and Perrin's Bardic Inspiration. A second strike came in, and Delmar cut its damage in half with Uncanny Dodge. Only Delmar and Jean-Claude drew its eye. Perrin's first illusion, a flying humanoid tumbling out of the sky, pulled it off into the garden, where it bellowed at finding nothing and whipped a tree over. The Party ran rather than fight it. Delmar flew them ten feet above the treeline, where the island showed the jungle deepening to the east and a river with tall golden grass to the west, while the beast tracked beneath the canopy a length behind. Perrin's second illusion, of himself falling off Crissdalynn's back into the bushes, took a natural 20, and the beast fell for it and broke off to hunt the fake. It chased to the treeline and stopped where the terraces begin. There it pulled a couple of wolfrabbits under its mantle and was gone into the trees. The Party got back to the ship as the evening closed, and Jean-Claude owned to exhaustion and a queasy stomach. Perrin dug out ginger for him, and the talk of new hammocks was still running when the night's rest began.
