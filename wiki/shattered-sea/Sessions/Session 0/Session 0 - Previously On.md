> The Shattered Sea had already taken its toll. When the fleet sank, Crissdalynn Khinriss and Delmar Fisk spent two days afloat before Beaumont Sel gave them berths on the Saltwright. Perrin Black-Jaw arrived from Keth-Naar with a family obligation still unexplained. Jean-Claude Tabarnack joined at the final island, four months after escaping the Grung who wanted him back. All five ships had disappeared over the Drowned Maw with the stolen Pearl aboard. Umberlee had answered. Something had passed through the seal. With the four of you aboard, the Saltwright made its last run towards Calveno. Then a Crown vessel drew alongside. Barnaby Rook hoisted the inspection signal and began examining the holds and the ship's papers.
