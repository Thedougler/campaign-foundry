## What happened

The Shattered Sea was introduced as a remote chain of islands, reefs, open water and unfinished charts. The Galewall, Ashwall Islands, Crown Islands, Midchain, Tail and Drowned Maw set the routes, while every harbour demanded a price.

The Dravosi Crown, Tessarine Concordat, Passage and Grung clans were established as powers contesting the sea. Official passage brought inspections and records. Unofficial passage brought debts and favours.

Six days before the story began, a privateer fleet stole the Pearl of Souls from the shrine at Vel Orn. Umberlee answered over the Drowned Maw. All five ships sank, the water changed, and something came through the seal.

[[delmar-fisk|Delmar Fisk]] and [[crissdalynn-khinriss|Crissdalynn Khinriss]] survived two days in open water before Beaumont Sel brought them aboard the Saltwright. [[perrin-black-jaw|Perrin Black-Jaw]] came aboard from Keth-Naar, and [[jean-claude-tabarnack|Jean-Claude Tabarnack]] took a berth while four months ahead of the Grung hunters pursuing him.

The Saltwright approached Calveno when the Crown cutter HCS Surety closed for inspection. Its captain, [[barnaby-rook|Barnaby Rook]], raised the signal and began reading the cargo and manifests.

## Changes
