## Opposition

- [[hinewai|Hinewai]] grabs the hearing and every Scene before it, one speaker at three speeds, never three speakers. Play her tempos as one voice changing pace, and let the gallery's faces announce each shift a half-beat early. Her cue at the convening: ==One stands accused. Speak for him, or sit and hear what the garden already knows.==
- [[talon-skarn|Talon Skarn]] grabs the ambush and drops into witness work if taken. Play him professional and spent, a fighter on the far side of his own order's arithmetic. His cue at the hostage turn: ==Drop your steel, or I drop the toy.==
- [[matteo-scola|Matteo Scola]] grabs the gallery's backbench and the walk's flank. Play him in short wet bursts, alligator-eyed, allergic to her voice. His cue when the court convenes: ==She talks and people walk off. I saw it happen twice. Nobody walks me anywhere.==
- [[renzo-canale|Renzo Canale]] grabs the treeline at dusk. Play him slow and certain, a bonesetter's hands and a gardener's patience. His cue as the Party leaves: ==The ground gives. Take what falls.==
- [[carlo-ferrante|Carlo Ferrante]] grabs the gallery testimony. Play him as a man carrying a stone he has decided to put down, and let the truth do the lifting. His cue under the ring's law: ==I turned back. I left them. I'm sorry, Ettore.==
- [[ettore-ferrante|Ettore Ferrante]] grabs the reunion. Play him countable and exact, apologies kept like invoices, and let one name wipe the ledger. His cue across the ring: ==Stand up, Carlo. We will count it later.==
**Unopposed timeline**, her Session run left alone by the Party:

1. Night, the ambush's noise. Her responders gather at the trees' edge, watching the burn. The sign is paired eyes in the dark, and fire drawn to the camp brings them in against everyone near it. Interference, a small fire, leaves them watching.
2. Predawn. She lays the lane from the road camp to the grove. The sign is an eight-foot band of grass lying flat to the north-east, and the walk on it runs safe. Interference, a step off the band, restores the island's ordinary rules.
3. First light. Her voice calls the Pantry. The sign is Renzo and Carlo walking the lane with the four behind them, and the gallery fills by late morning. Interference, the Party routing survivors to the coast instead, thins her jury.
4. Midday. The court convenes at the flowers. The sign is the ground opening before Jean-Claude's feet, and the hearing opens on her terms. Only leaving the island itself dodges it.
5. Dusk. With counsel silent, rage has the floor. The sign is the scream finishing its climb, and the verdict condemns by default. Interference, steering any tempo before sundown, changes the verdict.
