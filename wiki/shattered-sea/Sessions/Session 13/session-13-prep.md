## Session question

What is the Party to [[hinewai|Hinewai]], and what will they do about the army she means to send against [[karath|Karath]]? The Party starts in a clearing beside [[the-burnt-road|The Burnt Road]], several miles inland on [[aruhe|Aruhe]], their long rest broken by an invisible [[talon-skarn|Talon Skarn]] with the fight still going. The Session runs four to five hours.

A break of about fifteen minutes falls after row 3, close to the two-hour mark.

1. **Skarn's last stand.** The fight from Session 12 is still going at the road camp. Skarn is invisible but outlined and slowed. He offers a half-truth and then an alliance between equals, and when the Party refuses he dies fighting. [[vantyruss-shard|Vantyrus's shard]] survives him for the looting. Every outcome leads to row 2 along the fruit-pile trail.
2. **The Pantry.** Jean-Claude walks into a camp of seven people whose captors were grung, which makes it a small rehearsal of the trial at the grove. [[carlo-ferrante|Carlo]] sits at the south edge sure his brother died in the dark, and he went back once and turned round. [[ettore-ferrante|Ettore]] limps in on his splint with [[luca-ferrante|Luca]] counting on day twenty. Offered a way off Aruhe, Carlo, Tommaso, Sandrino and Ilario go without a roll, and [[renzo-canale|Renzo]], Beppe and Marco stay and argue with no one. Those who would leave wait at the Pantry with the rest while the Party goes on. Whatever the camp says of Jean-Claude, the Party can carry to Hinewai as testimony.
3. **The walk.** The Party travels alone towards [[memorial-grove|Memorial Grove]]. Aruhe's seeped hatred acts without Hinewai: a pack of about five [[wolfrabbit|wolfrabbits]] with two [[young-bloodhawk|young Bloodhawks]] diving from the canopy, all going for Jean-Claude past softer targets. This is High difficulty by `cfcli encounter-budget`. Hinewai neither sends nor stops them, and fighting them off doesn't break her rules. The [[lava-tubes|Lava Tubes]] are the Party's choice of a way out. The pack won't follow into the stone, but the air is bad and the drops are deep, and pale roots pulse with every step. The Party arrives at row 4 wounded or whole.
4. **The grove.** A couple of fallen golden peaches wait beneath the tree from the moment the Party enters. Hinewai judges whether Jean-Claude and his companions are Grung under Gold compulsion or messengers from Karath, or more survivors of Calveno. Each thing the Players touch draws out one disjointed piece of her. The peach and its gilding bring out Karath's gold farms and the stolen seed, and the graves bring out her companion. Jean-Claude himself carries [[simone-tabarnack|Simone]]'s scent. *Detect Magic* shows her the tether, which she reads and explains, and the canister draws her *Legend Lore*. The tether lands last and hardest. A guilty verdict, or the Party resisting her, ends the Session at the break-out (row 6, bad branch).
5. **The army.** Just after her verdict the procession arrives: animals from across Aruhe bow their heads around the Party, and she says, "They'll have bigger problems soon." Her army is every wild thing on the island, most likely months from crossing the channel, and once across it kills every grung on Karath, Jean-Claude's blue caste included. The Party may ally with her or refuse her. They may also bargain to aim her at the Gold caste alone, or lie to her. Her answer is what the Party is to her: allies, tolerated guests or Karath's spies.
6. **The way out.** On the good branch the Party returns to the Pantry for those who chose to leave and walks them towards the coast. The Stinger is [[nona-black-jaw|Nona]]'s sail on the horizon, with [[ruk|Ruk]]'s news held for Session 14. On the bad branch every bowed head at the grove comes up at once and the Session cuts there. The run downriver to the coast, a couple of days, opens Session 14.

## Threads

- [[rule-of-two|Rule of Two]]. Resolves in row 1 when Skarn dies fighting. His shard passes to whoever loots it, and Vantyrus watches its next carrier.
- [[take-on-aruhe|Take on Aruhe]]. Tested in row 2 by the Pantry's choice and in row 4 by Hinewai's verdict, and it resolves in row 5. Angle: the survivors' word for Jean-Claude.
- [[the-canister|The Canister]]. Raised in row 4 if the Party brings it before her. Angle: her *Legend Lore*.
- [[perrin-and-nona|Perrin and Nona]]. Carried by the survivors who leave in row 2, then by Nona's sail in row 6.
- [[journey-of-jean-claude-tabarnack|Journey of Jean-Claude Tabarnack]]. Tested by the hunt in row 3, the tether in row 4 and the army's cost to his people in row 5.

### Spotlights

- **Lead PC.** [[jean-claude-tabarnack|Jean-Claude]] ([[journey-of-jean-claude-tabarnack|his journey]]). The Pantry's former captives meet him first (row 2), and Aruhe's animals hunt him alone (row 3). Hinewai judges him and shows him the tether he thought was gone (row 4), and her army puts his own people under a clock (row 5).
- [[delmar-fisk|Delmar]], rows 2 and 4: he carried Ettore out of the pit, so the Carlo reunion is his to witness. At the Burnt Road he asked Hinewai for Jean-Claude's safety, and at the grove he can hold her to that promise.
- [[perrin-black-jaw|Perrin]], rows 2 and 6: the survivors who leave pay down his debt to Nona, and her sail is the Stinger.
- [[crissdalynn-khinriss|Crissdalynn]], rows 3 and 4: she read the island's law past the fruit rule and asked Hinewai whether anyone can leave, so she reads the hunt and the procession first.

## Opposition

- [[talon-skarn|Talon Skarn]] wants the [[fate-spinner|Fate Spinner]] and an equal alliance. He fights to the death in row 1.
- **Aruhe's wildlife** wants the only grung on the island dead. Unopposed, the pack runs Jean-Claude down on open ground in row 3. Stone, water and tight ranks blunt it.
- [[hinewai|Hinewai]] wants to protect Aruhe and to know what the Party is. Unopposed, she keeps her promise to Jean-Claude while he follows her rules, and her army waits until it can cross. A guilty verdict, taking from the grove or harm to the Bloom turns the island against the Party.
