## At a glance

- **Contest.** Standing the summons without breaking the garden's peace, with [[jean-claude-tabarnack|Jean-Claude]]'s life held by a scream and the Party named as his counsel.
- **At risk.** Jean-Claude's life, the Party's welcome, and the two graves nothing may touch.
- **Where.** [[memorial-grove|Memorial Grove]], the flower ring around the tree and its two mounds.
- **Opposition.** [[hinewai|Hinewai]] as the court, one attention in three bodies, with the still predators beyond the flowers.

> [!narration] Opening
> The Terror-Bird lies down at the treeline like a mourner at a burial, and it ignores you. Black flowers close a fence around a circle ahead. One fruit tree inside it shades two unmarked mounds, and the windfall shines soft gold in the grass. Saplings crowd the circle's rim. A figure wrapped in thorn waits beyond the flowers, and a chair of pale root faces the tree, empty. Every animal outside the ring has its head low. Then, ahead of Jean-Claude, the ground opens quietly to bar his way.

## Play

### Stakes

The court's demand is simple and it will not repeat itself. The accused enters the ring, and the people who brought him out of her garden stand beside him as his counsel, inside her truth with him. Compliance opens the hearing. Bargaining changes its terms, a plea, a witness order, a sentence served outside the graves, and whichever body is speaking sets what can be bargained. Defiance ends the welcome, and the roots answer defiance at once.

The gallery has already chosen. Benches of living root line the north rim, grown for these people, and [[renzo-canale|Renzo Canale]] heads them with [[carlo-ferrante|Carlo]] and [[ettore-ferrante|Ettore]] arm in arm at the front. [[matteo-scola|Matteo]] takes the backbench alone. Wherever a survivor looks, the saplings lean a degree after them.

A bound [[talon-skarn|Talon Skarn]] crosses the ring unmarked, because the roots have no claim on him, and the court ignores him completely. He is the Party's prisoner, visibly, and the whole gallery can see the law leaving him alone.

### Pressure

The thorn figure never takes its attention off Jean-Claude, tracking him by scent with its head swinging like a dog on a rope. Each time a body names him grung, its scream climbs a step. A third naming unbroken by other business brings the scream.

Crossing the flowers changes everyone who steps over. No deliberate lie can be spoken inside the ring. Silence, evasion and careful truth are all available to anyone with the nerve for them. The law arrives like water closing over the head: ==Something older than you grips the heel of your tongue. The rule arrives wordless, and you know it anyway. The ring refuses to pass a lie for you.==

Top of the second exchange, if the Party still stands at the rim: ==The tree breaks its long silence, a voice like a root through a wall. He came off the night sea, and he took what was mine. Who speaks for him?==

### Terrain

The flower ring marks a circle about forty feet across. The fruit tree and the two unmarked mounds fill its centre, the Judge's ground, and a hand on the tree, a mound, a flower or the soil ends the welcome. The root chair faces the tree from ten feet away, and it is empty. The saplings crowd the ring's inner edge on every side, and the thorn figure keeps to the black-flowered ground outside the ring, twenty feet further out where the trees begin. The gallery benches line the north side inside the flowers. The treeline stands another twenty feet on, Wolfrabbits and a Deer-Stalker bedded at the trees in stillness, the Terror-Bird largest among them.

Triggered change: the first seizure is roots through soil, Strength DC 19 or Restrained. The second takes the offender to the ground. The third, or any touch on the graves or the tree, wakes what waits outside the ring in full.

### Checks

- **Read the three bodies.** Insight DC 15, once, before addressing the court. Success: ==Leaf, shoot and thorn all bend to the same wind, a heartbeat apart. Orange eyes light one body at a time, and the voice follows them. One of her, speaking from three mouths.== Critical: ==One animal and one attention, walking between all three of her mouths. It hears through whichever mouth it occupies at the time. Words aimed at the other two fall on deaf ground.== Failure: ==You read it as three judges in three tempers, and the tree as senior for its age. You will open with the tree, calmly, the way one addresses the head of a table.==
- **Speak to the body that is speaking.** Address the body whose tempo is live for Advantage on the persuasion point at issue. Address a body gone cold and the same point rolls at Disadvantage. Which body is live is plain to anyone watching the eyes and the gallery's faces.
- **Bargain before entering.** Persuasion DC 15 with the body whose tempo is live. Success: ==The tree considers, and the saplings shiver through their leaves. Terms are possible, the slow voice allows, if the accused stands where the advocate sits and the witnesses remain.== Failure: ==The thorn figure answers for the whole court, three fragments of sound, mine, ground, now, and the tree says nothing after them.==

## Encounter

### Battlefield

![[Session 13 - The Hearing - Battle Map.webp]]

### Creatures

![[hinewai-the-blight#Statblock|Hinewai the Blight]]

![[terror-bird#Statblock|Terror-Bird]]

This fight exists to punish defiance, and it cannot be won. One Hinewai the Blight, CR 19, one Terror-Bird, CR 13, and every still predator at the treeline if the graves are touched. Destroying her body reforms it at the tree within days, and the ground she fights from is hers. Opening, if it comes: ==The thorn figure unfolds into something once shaped like a woman, rot-green claws unhooking joint by joint, and the black flowers all turn toward you at once. The Terror-Bird's scream arrives through the ground before it arrives through the air.==

Weapons dropped and knees on the ground end it, because the trial is what she wants. The bent lane out of the grove is the one road she leaves open, and running it means running on her, with her voice in the grass the whole way. Interference short of violence, words, an offered hand, a returned peach, can pull the scream back down a step at the DM's judgement.

## Depth

### Hidden truths

- The tree, the saplings and the thorn figure are one disjointed consciousness speaking with itself and with the Party from three perspectives, law and grief and rage, one voice and one "I" throughout. The tell is the shared bend of leaf and limb on the same delayed wind, and the orange eyes that travel mouth to mouth.
- The empty chair is the advocate's place, and it is his. Nobody has sat in it for hundreds of years, and the italic half-voice belongs to the seat rather than to the bodies. Asked who sits there, the tree says only that the court does not testify.
- Inside the ring, the ground shows one dead stride of stillness on either side of the two mounds, because no root crosses either grave. The court can be asked anything. The truth answers only what it is asked, exactly.
