## At a glance

- **Pressure.** The recorded instant itself, played out: the outlined falcon flying slow, and the advantage blow already dropping toward him.
- **Choice.** Finish him, take him alive, or trade the [[fate-spinner|Fate Spinner]] for the camp's safety.
- **Where.** The fire-ring clearing beside [[the-burnt-road|The Burnt Road]], deep inland on [[aruhe|Aruhe]], hours before dawn.
- **Who.** [[talon-skarn|Talon Skarn]], with [[matteo-scola|Matteo Scola]], [[ettore-ferrante|Ettore Ferrante]], [[gianni-moro|Gianni Moro]], [[luca-ferrante|Luca Ferrante]] and [[piero-sorrentino|Piero Sorrentino]] behind the stones.

> [!narration] Opening
> The decanter's spray keeps raining over him, and his shape hangs in it like a falcon pressed into wet glass. Crissdalynn's staff is already moving, his wings beat at half speed, and the falling blow is one breath from his chest. Behind the ring of stones, Matteo has both arms over Luca's head. Ettore is trying to stand on a leg that answers him only in part. Perrin comes back to himself, one hand flat on the ground. The shimmer banks for the trees and stops dead, the amber stare finding Crissdalynn over the ticking of his chains. Nobody breathes. "The toy. Last time of asking."

## Play

### Situation

The recorded state, resumed exactly. [[talon-skarn|Talon Skarn]] stands at 150 of his 314 hit points, the stolen windfall fruit having closed his wounds. All three of his Legendary Resistances are spent. His speed stays halved until the start of his next turn, and the next attack roll against him carries Advantage as that blow falls.

He is Invisible, and the decanter's spray draws his outline while the water keeps running. The Party targets him normally. If the water stops, Invisibility comes back. Perrin's stun lifts at the start of Skarn's next turn. Delmar rides invisible somewhere above the clearing on the [[ghost-plum|Ghost Plum]]'s last hour, and the hundred-pound [[fleet-commanders-chair|Fleet Commander's Chair]] lies where it fell.

The Party is level 6 from the first roll of this Session. Each player takes the level when and how they choose, even mid-fight, and new hit points raise current and maximum together.

Skarn wants the toy, not corpses. His clock: the hostage turn arrives at 75 hit points, or after two stalled rounds. The garden runs its own clock beside his, and fire or felled trees brought responders down on the last camp, who answer both sides alike.

Crissdalynn wears the [[nightmantle|Nightmantle]]. Attacks against her roll at disadvantage, and the curse draws wild ranged fire onto her, which Delmar's explosive rounds have paid twice.

### Handles

- **Finish it.** The advantage blow is loaded and the spray marks him. His reaction still blunts the first solid hit by 18, and his escape magic is spent. Finishing him ends the hunt tonight, and cornered eight strides from five unarmed people, he fights hardest.
- **Take him alive.** Restraint ends this fight rather than damage. Pin his wings or drop the chair on him again, and hold what you catch, because a live falcon is a witness for whatever waits at the grove. Binding him spends turns on chains instead of wounds, and he buys his exits with hostages.
- **Trade or stall.** He hears any offer out and repeats his price. Persuasion DC 16 buys a full minute of talk, and the meeting with [[talon-vantyrus|his master]] is still on the table. The only coin he wants rides in Delmar's bag.
- **Shield the civilians.** Move Matteo, Ettore, Gianni, Luca and Piero east behind the burnt road's lip, twenty-five feet out and dead ground below a low flyer. The move costs a turn and removes his hostages.
- **First actions.** Crissdalynn's advantage blow is already falling. Delmar can shoot from above, minding the Nightmantle's pull. Jean-Claude keeps the decanter running with a free hand and shoots. Perrin, once the stun lifts, holds the inspiration dice and walks the five survivors behind the stones.

- **See the lines snap.** When Skarn acts against the garden's people, the hostage grab first among them, Wisdom (Perception) DC 18, free to attempt. Crissdalynn has spun a [[fate-spinner|Spinner]] and knows the sight, so she doesn't need a roll. Success: ==Threads you can barely see cross the spray around him, tight as rigging. One by one they part with a sound like a snapped line, and something that was holding him up lets go.== Critical: ==Understand what you just saw. A Spinner's thread can break. Something outside this island holds him, and its grip has just failed.== Failure: ==The spray shows nothing but his outline. The water shivers as if the wind had changed, though the leaves above hold still, and in Delmar's bag the quartz top gives one little click with no hand near it.==
- **Ground and bind him.** Grapples and pins contest his Acrobatics at +16, and Step of the Falcon buys him a Dash or Disengage each turn even so. Trip him instead while his speed stays halved, as Delmar's explosive trip did at the river, and standing costs him half of what movement he has. Bind the wings and you bind the falcon.
- **Read the hostage turn.** Insight DC 14 when he drops toward the fire ring. Success: ==His eye stays on your hands and his wing beats keep him between you and the dark. He wants the toy produced, and he would spend a life to make you open a pack.== Failure: ==He looks spent to you, wings ragged, beaten. His chains have stopped their ticking, and you take that for rest.==
- **Buy a minute's talk.** Persuasion DC 16. Success: ==The shimmer sinks to a man's height and stops. He hears out the whole offer with the chains still.== Then he repeats the trade in his own words: ==Lay the toy down where I can see it, and show me your hands. The rest comes after.== Failure: ==The stare slides off you the way it would slide off furniture.==

### Pressure

Each round spends his patience. Round one, he repositions and tests the Party's line. Round two, he quits fencing people and starts cutting straps, pouches and packs wherever the Spinner might ride. Round three, or at 75 hit points, he drops on Matteo or the splinted Ettore with the demand, "The toy, or he falls."

Any fire or felled tree changes the fight for everyone. The bushes around the clearing rustle with things running, and the responders arrive the following round to take whoever is near the burn.

Top of round two: ==The diving has stopped. He circles now level with your own eyes, chains ticking, and every pass crosses the fire, the survivors, the packs.==

#### Creatures

![[talon-skarn-creature#Statblock|Talon Skarn (Creature)]]

One Skarn, hovering ten feet up at the clearing's heart, Crissdalynn beneath him. Opening tactic: ==The spray betrays him and he knows it. He quits the plume and banks for the dark trees, dragging your eyes off the fire ring and into open sky.==

Interference answers: if packs are hidden or moved, he goes for the survivors' bundles and the survivors next. If grounded and pinned, Wingbeat Step and the chain's ten-foot pull buy him space, and the Tempest sweeps everyone within twenty feet. If the Party shows the Spinner, he shifts at once to the trade.

The hostage turn arrives at 75 hit points, or after two stalled rounds. From there, denied the trade and pressed, he shoves the hostage away and climbs the burnt road into the dark. He goes free, because the island's law has no claim on him. When a blow can drop him: ==The chains slack across his forearm and his wings stall, and the shaped water collapses around him as he falls toward the stones. He is yours to finish.==

## Depth

### Hidden truths

- The remote help is [[talon-vantyrus|Vantyrus]] working fate through a shard on Skarn's harness. When the lines snap, the shard crackles once with a voice, "Talon. Bring it.", and goes dead. Skarn believes a second Spinner is working against him, the woman of the island in his telling, and that report goes to his master with him.
- The toy's purpose is his master's secret, kept from him. The lesson inside it, the road to Soul Incarnate, would turn his master from teacher into target. He would keep the Spinner for himself.
- He didn't break any law of this island. The fruit he healed on was windfall, already fallen, and taken from a man rather than a living plant. If the Party walks him bound into the grove, the roots leave him alone, and the court's ruling on him will be lawful indifference.

### Threads

- [[rule-of-two|Rule of Two]] moves here by his fate. Killed, the contest breaks and Vantyrus follows in person. Captured, the Party can question him under a truth that binds everyone in the ring. Escaped or traded, the hunt resumes on new terms.
- [[drowned-maw-awakening|Drowned Maw Awakening]] presses Perrin in the same breath, the demand arriving under the stun, and his answer stays his player's.

### Spotlight

The Session's first blow belongs to [[crissdalynn-khinriss|Crissdalynn Khinriss]], on the arm he broke and the sky he took, both hers again at half his speed. The Spinner he has come twice for is the gift her own master regrets. The toy rides in Delmar's bag under a spent lie, and Jean-Claude's running decanter is the reason the falcon can be hit at all. Behind the stones wait Perrin's five charges.
