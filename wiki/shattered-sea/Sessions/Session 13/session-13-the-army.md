## Situation

Hinewai has judged, and the Party stands cleared inside the black-flower ring of [[memorial-grove|Memorial Grove]]. The army she means to send across the channel to [[karath|Karath]] is now theirs to answer. They can stand with her or against her, and a third road runs between the two. Each answer makes them something different to her. At stake are the grung of Karath of every colour, including the blue caste of Jean-Claude's kin. The captives in the pens lie in the path of the crossing, and the Party's own road off the island depends on her answer.

[[session-13-the-grove|Session 13 - The Grove]] hands over its good branch only. Her warm half decided the verdict and spared Jean-Claude, the tether has been read aloud, and Delmar still holds her promise. She hasn't yet said what the Party is to her, and she does that here. A guilty verdict or resistance at the grove goes straight to [[session-13-the-way-out|Session 13 - The Way Out]] and never reaches this Scene.

### Where everyone stands

- **The Party.** Inside the ring, a few strides from the fruit tree and the two unmarked mounds beneath it. The Calveno survivors of [[the-pantry|The Pantry]] are elsewhere, because those who want to leave wait there.
- **Hinewai.** Her figure stands across the clearing, and her voice also comes from the tree and the saplings. They are one disjointed mind talking to itself and to the Party. Each line has two halves, one for the listener and one for the companion only she hears.
- **The procession.** Wolfrabbits, deer-stalkers, terror-birds and young bloodhawks stand a rank deep along the flowers, and none steps inside. See [[wolfrabbit|Wolfrabbit]], [[deer-stalker|Deer-Stalker]], [[terror-bird|Terror-Bird]] and [[young-bloodhawk|Young Bloodhawk]]. They are the island's own wild, in a number that changes by the hour. She didn't call them. The bow is the island's habit after two hundred years of her shaping it.
- **The clock.** The bow lasts ten to twenty minutes in the fiction. Count each statement a Player addresses to her as an exchange. At the fourth, the first ranks turn and file out in silence. By the seventh the ring is empty, and each animal's wildness has returned with it. For Jean-Claude that is the walk to the Pantry. Hinewai's army has a clock of its own, and it runs for months.

### What she says

Each piece comes when a Player asks, or when the DM spends the pause after the opening. She speaks in her two-conversation voice throughout.

- **Who they are, and what they are for.** The trigger is a Player asking what the beasts are for. ==A green-clawed hand turns palm upward, and the antlered heads dip together. "They will cross the water to the hatcheries, child, every grung there, and not here, and not you. *She asks to leave, love, and does not see she is asked to stay.*"== The animals are her garden. They don't share one mind, and she doesn't call them slaves.
- **Who dies, and when.** The trigger is Jean-Claude pressing her on whether every grung on Karath dies, and how soon. ==Every sapling bends Jean-Claude's way, and her voice grows softer and shorter. "Each one, child, months yet, no day set, and I am growing what will ferry them over the water, and my wild things won't tell one from another. *He asks for the chained, love, as I once did.*"== She doesn't give a date. The Party can read it as months, and weeks are unlikely. The Snapping Turtle Whale grows in [[the-mangroves|The Mangroves]], which she mentions only if asked where. Jean-Claude's blue caste is among the dead, and so are the green labourers and the children.
- **What she wants from them.** ==She tilts her head, speaking to one half and listening to the other. "Gold lit my road. *And sent the small ones to burn on it.* Will you stand with the garden, or with the ones who sent them?"== This is the ask, and every Player answer follows it.

> [!narration] The army
> Hinewai turns one hand palm up and holds it there. The nearest wolfrabbit lifts its head, the deer-stalker beside it follows, and a bloodhawk shakes out its wings. Heads rise down the rank and over the whole ring, until every animal looks towards the water you can't see, eyes open and fixed.

Read the army callout when a Player asks what "bigger problems" means, or when the DM spends the pause. It lands before her explanation, and nothing follows it.

### Angles the Party has gained

> [!tiles]
>
> - **The tether read aloud.** With it, Jean-Claude can say that the gold drinks the small ones as it drank her, and her quiet half answers him. Her aside to the companion recognises him as a captive of the same pull. Terms that spare the lower castes are DC 15 with it and DC 20 without. Without it, the argument for the small ones rests on Jean-Claude's word alone.
> - **The seal samples.** The dead along the Burnt Road were compelled, and she knows it. Every order on the seals is in the gold's own script. A seal laid in her hand makes the "gold works first" term automatic, and without one it is a roll.
> - **Delmar's promise.** She keeps her word. If Delmar holds her to it, Jean-Claude's safe leaving isn't open to argument, and any term she grants binds as that promise does.
> - **The captives on Karath.** If Perrin or anyone raises the Calveno held in Karath's pens, she stops for a breath, because she was taken and her companion was taken before her. If the Party leaves them unmentioned, she never thinks of them, and the feral beasts overrun the pens with everything else on the shore.
> - **Crissdalynn's reading of intent.** The island weighs what a creature means, and Crissdalynn read that at the river camp. The army can't do the same, and Hinewai hasn't allowed for the beasts that cross the water.

### The Party's answers

- **Ally freely.** They stand aside, or help against the Gold caste's fire. She counts them friends. Her Cue: ==She bows her head slowly, like something rooted deciding to bend. "Guests you are, child, and allies if you bring me what I ask. Eyes and word I will take, but the wild does not choose, and I cannot pick out the Gold alone. *He offers, love, and does not know what he offers.*"== The cost falls on the grung below the gold, and on Jean-Claude's conscience first.
- **Refuse plainly.** They say no and mean to leave. She counts them guests she tolerates, keeps her word to Delmar and lets them go. Her Cue: ==Not a plant moves, and every head remains bowed. "A guest may speak, child, and no hand is raised, so none is taken, but the water is mine to cross and you will not stop it. *She would stop me, love, so let her look.*"==
- **Bargain.** They ask to aim her. She offers what is in her hand: the order of the killing, a warning and a wait. Beyond the shore she can promise nothing, since the beasts are feral once across. A bargain she accepts makes them allies on terms. Her Cue: ==She lowers her head to his height, and the moss curls back from his boots. "The Gold first, then, child, so show me the hall and the farms, and I will try to hold the rest. *He wants to buy the small ones, love, with his own road, and I cannot promise what I cannot hold.*"==
- **Lie.** They pretend to ally so they can leave. Her magic hears truth, and a lie told in the ring is a bet against it. A lie that holds makes them allies on false footing, and a caught one makes them Karath's spies. Her Cue when she accepts it: ==The vines lean to the hands that made the promise, and she says nothing for a breath. "Warm words, child, and I will take them and watch the hands that gave them, because roots remember who said what. *They shake, love, and someone is always a breath late saying yes.*"==
- **Threaten to warn Karath.** Anything that tells the gold, or tells Karath's grung at large, that the beasts are coming reads to her as a spy's errand. It ends the talk.

## Pressure

Her first move is to tell them of the army and put her question. When a Player speaks to the voice only she hears, she slows and listens. That is her answer to interference. Her desperate move is the test of the small ones, and her exit terms are the Party's own answer, whichever one it is.

- **The bow.** Trigger: the verdict ends. Signal: ==The ring holds so still that you can hear a bloodhawk shift its claws on the sapling above you.== Opportunity: ask what she means, read the ring or read her. Three routes lead to the army. She tells it herself, the ring shows it (Nature DC 13), and the quiet half gives the date away (Insight DC 15).
- **The ask.** Trigger: the army is understood, or the fourth exchange arrives. Signal: ==The ears in the ring turn towards you together, and the old deer-stalker's eye stays on you longer than the rest.== Opportunity: answer her. Each answer above has its own handling.
- **The test.** Trigger: a Player argues for sparing the grung. ==She doesn't raise her voice. "Name me one grung on that island who never bowed to the gold or looked away from the pens. *He couldn't either, love.*"== Nobody can truthfully give a name. An offer in its place opens the bargain, such as cutting the tether or opening the pens first.
- **The break.** Trigger: a caught lie, or a blow against the grove itself. A threat to warn Karath triggers it as well, and so does any wound dealt to a beast. Signal for a lie or a threat: ==Her aside has stopped. Her mouth hangs open on half a word.== Signal for a raised weapon: ==The thorned vines stop trailing. Black blooms and young trees draw in round the Party without a sound. "A hand was raised in my garden, child, so let the soil have what you carried, and it will keep you well. *Not the loud one, love, the loud ones tire.*"== Opportunity: one breath to step back (Persuasion DC 18, a plain retraction with no excuse). If that fails, every bowed head comes up at once and the Session cuts to [[session-13-the-way-out|Session 13 - The Way Out]].
- **When play stalls.** She turns to Jean-Claude and asks what he would do with the gold. ==Her eyes fix on his, and her voice goes gentle. "What would you do with them? *He freed us once, love, this one would too.*"== She asks it once, and the question is for the Player behind him.
- **The procession leaves.** Trigger: the fourth exchange. Signal: ==The first rank turns without a sound. The ones behind follow, and the gap in the flowers closes after each of them.== Their wildness goes with them into the forest, and the Party has to decide how it will walk out through it.

If the table is short of time, skip the exchanges. Hinewai gives the army in one speech and asks her question once, and each Player answers once. Resolve each answer from the Outcomes table and the Checks, with the army callout spent first.

A contrast beat is ready for the middle of the talk, if any of the walk's pack lived. A young bloodhawk with a scorched wing-feather bows at head height beside Crissdalynn. ==The bloodhawk with the scorched feather bows so low that its beak touches the moss. An hour ago it dove at Jean-Claude. Now it looks anywhere else.== The beat also gives the Clue that the island's hatred of grung is seeped into the land and is no rule of hers. The army callout follows it as the next image. The table turns from a laugh to dread at once.

The old deer-stalker with the pale burn is the planted detail. The Gold caste's fire marked it, and it bows last and leaves last. It can return at the shore in Session 14.

## Encounter

The Party can start a fight, and the grove forces none. Hinewai steps forward as the Blight when anyone strikes the tree, the graves or the flowers, when a Player harms a beast, or when the break comes. The fight is unwinnable, and its job on the page is to give the DM a clean break-out. She is a powerful caster beyond the spells her statblock names, so any fitting druidic or necromantic magic is hers to use.

- 1 × Hinewai the Blight (CR 19, 22000 XP each) = 22000 XP
- Party budgets (4 characters at level 6, from recorded levels 5, 5, 5, 5 plus the +1 combat offset) are Low 2400 and Moderate 4000, with High at 5600
- **Total: 22000 XP (Beyond High difficulty)**

The figure is 16400 XP over High, and it leaves out the ring. The wild things along the flowers are uncounted and all of them take her side, and she can draw most of the island to her. Her body also re-forms 1d10 days after it falls while the Death Bloom lasts. Breaking the graves destroys one part of her among many, and ending the Bloom means burning most of Aruhe. Nothing in the grove is a victory. The route out is the one [[session-13-the-way-out|Session 13 - The Way Out]] opens.

- **Opening move.** The plants lean towards the nearest PC before she moves. ==Thorned vines climb out of the moss beside the nearest of you, and the blossoms turn your way like a crowd.== She opens with Grasping Roots on the loudest voice.
- **While the ring stands.** The beasts don't enter until she calls them. Her aside keeps pace as she fights, and she answers it between blows.
- **Break or escape.** She follows only as far as her roots run. A Party that leaves the ring and doesn't return has left her behind, and the wild runs it down on the walk.
- **Kill hand-off.** ==The light in the cracks of her skin gutters out, and her blue eyes go dark. Her gown of roots slackens and she folds into the moss beneath the gilded boughs, and blossoms close over her. Who lands the last blow, and how?== The body re-forms in days.

## Hidden truths

- The army has no date. The Snapping Turtle Whale is too small, and her dread of the water and her distaste for leaving Aruhe hold back the rest. Months is most likely. Her will extends as far as her roots, and the roots end at the shore. Once across, the beasts are feral. That is why she can promise a route and a warning, and can't promise mercy. A promise to try for the small ones depends on her standing at the far shore when they cross, and she hates the water.
- A blue-caste handler tallied her mornings in the hatchery pens. This is invented, to give her anger at Jean-Claude's colour a cause. She can raise it as a charge against him, and he can answer it with Pell. Her anger at the blue is old, and her quiet half pities the same hands.
- The Gold caste's scorched earth has wounded her. A Party that discovers this can use it as common cause, and one that finds out later may see why she can't simply cross now.
- The scorch on the old stag's flank is the Gold caste's mark, and the animals know whose fire it was.
- Hinewai has never considered the captives in Karath's pens. She was one, and a Party that raises them touches the part of her that still loves open water.

## Spotlight

- **Jean-Claude.** She watches him, and he is the cost. His blue caste is among the grung she is sending the army against, and the tether shows that his people are drained too. He answers her question about the gold and decides whom he speaks for.
- **Delmar.** He asked for his friend's safety on the Burnt Road, and she kept it. He can hold her to it and drink to the garden again, and he can speak the terms aloud.
- **Perrin.** Nona's captives on Karath are his family obligation. He can make the pens a term, and he can make her remember being taken.
- **Crissdalynn.** She reads the ring from the air as an aarakocra, and she read the island's law past the fruit rule. The bloodhawks give the army the sky, which touches her fear of losing it. She sees the army's shape first.
