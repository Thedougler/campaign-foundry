> [!narration] Opening
> Wind has shoved the canopy around behind you all the way here, and at the black flowers it stops. The grass within stands dead still and greener than the soil outside, and one fruit tree rises over two bare mounds, near enough that an arm could span the gap. It smells of peach under there, sweet and thick as a distillery, and saplings crowd the mounds from every side. A deer-stalker, a Terror-Bird and a loose pack of wolfrabbits wait outside the ring in the shadows like mourners, and none of them looks your way. Two golden peaches lie beside the roots. They shine like new brass, a few paces inside the ring.

## Situation

The Party reaches [[memorial-grove|Memorial Grove]] on the summons [[hinewai|Hinewai]] spoke at the [[the-burnt-road|Burnt Road]], "Meet me in the grove." They come from [[session-13-the-walk|Session 13 - The Walk]], wounded or whole. Only the four of them stand here. The Pantry's leavers wait at [[the-pantry|The Pantry]]. The wolfrabbits and the Terror-Bird that stalked [[jean-claude-tabarnack|Jean-Claude]] across the island stand outside the black flowers like mourners.

**The turn.** Hinewai sorts Jean-Claude and the companions who brought him into one of three kinds: Grung under Gold compulsion, messengers from [[karath|Karath]] or more survivors of [[calven-and-calveno|Calveno]]. When she has judged, the Party knows what the island's keeper is, what the [[gold-caste|Gold Caste]] still takes from Jean-Claude and whether the island stands with them. She also watches that nobody takes a Calveno survivor, since only those who want to leave may go. The Party won't force anyone, and the survivors' choice stays out of this Scene's drama.

**The place.** A round clearing ringed by black flowers, with saplings along its edge and pale roots running down toward the [[lava-tubes|Lava Tubes]]. One fruit tree stands over two unmarked mounds, [[the-unnamed-companion|the companion's]] and Hinewai's own. Fallen golden peaches lie beneath it from the moment the Party arrives, free to any guest. Living fruit hangs on the branches and is a claim. Wind outside never stirs the grass inside.

**One mind, three bodies.** Hinewai speaks through the tree, the sapling ring and a tall figure at the clearing's far edge. They are one broken mind talking to itself and to the Party from different sides. Give each piece of her a body and let the others answer it.

- **The tree.** Grief and the dead. It speaks to someone kneeling at the mounds who isn't there.
- **The saplings.** The law and the accusation. Thinner and quicker, they speak to the Party.
- **The figure at the edge.** The watcher, with orange eyes at the height of a tall woman's face, fixed on Jean-Claude. She asks questions and gives the verdict.

**How the hearing runs.** Each thing the Players touch draws exactly one broken piece from her, and she never lectures. A question about a touched thing gets that thing's piece. The pieces below come only when their trigger happens.

- **A fallen peach.** Trigger: a PC picks one up, bites one or questions the shine. Every fruit on Aruhe so far has done roughly what it looks like it does, and the Players know the pattern. A metallic-gold peach invites a bite at once, so don't stage a puzzle. Stage the reaction and Jean-Claude's understanding, since eating one is lawful and the gilding doesn't anger her. See Checks for the roll. Cue to Jean-Claude's Player when he sees the shine: ==You know the fruit before anyone bites it, from the gold-green skin and the heavy smell. The highest caste eats it behind walls no blue hand has crossed. Here it lies in the grass for anyone to take.== When anyone but Jean-Claude eats one and gilds: ==The saplings bend to the new gold on the eater's palm before the figure moves. "That was given, child, so sit and tell me what the fruit tastes of to you. *Look, love, it is only gold, and it is ours.*"== When Jean-Claude eats one: ==Orange eyes flare at gold on a grung's skin. The vines behind her rise, then sink into the moss. "Only our fruit on you, child, so tell me whose voice you hear besides your own. *It is not theirs, love, it is ours.*"==
- **The graves.** Trigger: a PC kneels at the mounds or asks who lies there. ==The moss goes soft where the knee rests, and the grass leans in to meet the palm. "Him, child, and one beside him, though that is a longer thing, and the rain comes here first, and then the roots, and then, where was I, yes, both are kept. *He kneels as you used to, love, and does not dig.*"== If someone asks the buried man's name: ==She lifts a green claw toward the nearer mound and stops short of it. The claw closes slowly on nothing. "He was only him, child, and he ate the green ones off the ground, and I keep him, and that is enough. *She asks what you were called, love, and I reach for it and there is only soil.*"== Pressed on the law, the saplings add: ==I lay down beside him alive and said it into the soil, and the island heard. The law is a promise kept in the dirt.== A hand on either mound, a spade, a pulled flower or a blade against the tree is a claim, covered under Checks.
- **Jean-Claude's scent.** Trigger: he steps within reach, a PC names his sister or someone asks why she watches him. ==Her head swings slowly to the blue grung, and the thorned vines behind her go still. "I know the scent on you, child, so say it plainly, did she send you for more fruit, or did the Gold send you for my tree? *It is the thief's, love, the one who took.*"== If he names [[simone-tabarnack|Simone]] or answers at all, she adds: ==She came with her hands out and took what she could reach, and left with her arms full. *A diet is years of fruit, love, and she carried off an armful.* She thought it enough.== The Players now know that Simone has stood in this grove and stolen the fruit that makes grung gold.
- **The hunt.** Trigger: a PC mentions the wolfrabbits, the hawks or the wounds from the walk. The saplings answer. ==I didn't send them, and I didn't call them off. *It comes up out of the ground now, love, from my crying.* If you killed them to live, I won't hold your hands against you.== Aruhe's hatred of Grung is her own hatred seeped into the land and never her rule.
- **The seal samples.** Trigger: Jean-Claude shows the spent seals he took from the Burnt Road. She turns one over with a root as fine as a finger. ==Gold's words. They send spades and never ask the spade. *Over a hundred came to dig up him.*==
- **The canister.** Trigger: a PC lays one of the [[grung-toxin-vials|toxin vials]] before her or tells her of the canister at the Saltwright's gangplank. She takes it in a root and goes still as long as a long prayer, while the saplings change which way they lean. She is casting *Legend Lore*. ==She does not touch the canister. Saplings and black flowers lean in as her head tilts. "A woman with gold in her seams, child, grinding by lamplight with exact hands, and she sold it by the crate to coats from another fleet. *Simone, love, the one who took, and her kin may owe the debt.*"== If the Party presses on the poison itself: ==It's cousin to what they put in my skull each dawn, and you didn't look surprised.== The Players now know who made the Crown's toxin and that Jean-Claude has known since the gangplank. What he does with that is his choice.
- **The tether, last.** She saves it. Once three pieces are out, or the Party has nothing left to touch, or a PC stalls the hearing with talk, the figure walks in from the edge and says, "Stand in the light." She casts *Detect Magic* on Jean-Claude, and the Party sees what she sees, since no PC carries the spell.

> [!narration] The tether
> Jean-Claude stands in the clearing as blue as ever, with his red beret and the one neon-purple patch. Then gold lifts off his skin, thread after thread, faint as breath, like steam off a wet dog. Every thread drifts the same way, east, out over the trees, and not one turns back.

She reads it to him first. ==The orange eyes brighten, and the nearest saplings bend low to listen. "A thin leash, child, and it drinks from whoever believes, and it lasts only while you allow it, so do you? *No seal on him, love, only the threads, drifting east, as mine did.*"== When the Party asks what the wisps are, she turns to them. ==She turns her head toward the others and keeps her eyes on the blue grung. "It drinks from whoever believes, and the drink goes east, and he walks free and he feeds them, both at once. *It drank from me in the pens, love, the same way.*"== She can't cut it, because the door is in him, and the saying is all she gives.

If Jean-Claude carries [[solanges-authority-seal|Solange's Authority Seal]], it shows to her as a small hard coin of the same gold. ==And a coin of theirs against your back.== See Checks.

**The clock.** If the Party stalls or talks past the pieces, her drift takes her and unprompted lines come out, each a little closer to the reading. The tether always comes, because she will look at Jean-Claude whether the Party offers him or not.

- Saplings, in unison: ==The saplings sway together, though no wind comes in. *Not yet,* the thinnest says to nobody. *Soon.*==
- The tree: ==The tree starts a word and stops, the way you stop at a name you've lost.==

**The verdict.** She decides on the pieces and the answers, and no roll decides it. A Party that leaves the Bloom alone and answers truly is spared, and Jean-Claude with it. ==Her vines settle into the moss, and the saplings straighten one by one. "I keep my word, child, and her debt is hers and not yours, so you may go. *No gold on him, love, only the thread, and he has not decided.*"== If anyone asks about the Calveno or the leavers: ==Her vines slacken. Her chin dips toward each of them, one at a time. "Anyone who wishes may leave, child, so go and ask them, and the thread is yours to loosen. *They never wish to, love, and he keeps his word, so I keep mine.*"==

A guilty verdict ends welcome and promise together. ==The vines rise from the moss, and every plant leans toward the blue grung before she moves. "The thread feeds them still, child, and the thief's scent is on you, so my word was for a clean grung, and the guests may step aside, because the island takes him back. *Her kin, love, and drunk as I was.*"== Resistance ends them the same way, and the posture Cues under Angles cover it.

Delmar can force the reason. At the Burnt Road he asked for Jean-Claude's safety and she promised it while he follows her rules. If she leans guilty, Delmar naming the promise makes her state the rule broken. When nobody broke a rule, she finds none, and her own word spares him.

## Angles

> [!tiles]
>
> - **[[hinewai|Hinewai]].** As the three-bodied keeper above. **Goal:** learn what the Party is, keep her promise to Delmar while Jean-Claude follows her rules and watch that nobody takes a survivor. **Knows:** the seed and the farms at Karath, her companion's grave and her own, Simone's scent, the orders on the spent seals, what the tether is and that the hunt is her seeped hatred. She is a powerful caster, an archdruid lich, and any fitting druidic or necromantic magic is hers when the fiction calls for it. *Detect Magic* and *Legend Lore* are two among many. **Offer and price:** the Party may leave whole, Jean-Claude may live and any Calveno who want to go may follow them. In return the Party's hands stay off the Bloom, nobody who doesn't wish to go is taken, and Jean-Claude answers truly. **Withholds:** her companion's name, which she lost. She also withholds what she means to do with the island. Tell: the tree stops mid-word and the saplings stop swaying together, and she turns her eyes back to Jean-Claude. She never lies. **Posture changes**, and each closes on a Cue:
>   - Toward kindness, when the Party eats, asks and listens: ==You took what fell. *He was thin once, like this one.* Sit, child, and the grove won't hurt you.==
>   - Toward the survivors waiting at the Pantry: ==They may go if they want to. *They never want to.* I'll let nobody drag one off unasked.==
>   - Toward the first hand on a grave: ==Nobody gave you that. *It's crying.* Return it and I'll forget the hand that took it.==
>   - Toward a taker who persists: ==The island will have you back, then. *Don't watch.* It's gentle, always.==
>   - Toward a lie she can disprove, or a bowed head for Karath: ==That is gold's answer, in gold's voice. *They answered the same way.* My promise stops where my hand stops, and the island has hands of its own.==
>   - Toward anyone who flees: ==Go, then. *They went too.* The roots have learned your weight by now.==
> - **[[jean-claude-tabarnack|Jean-Claude]].** Is what the hearing turns on. He wears no seal and still leaks, because a part of him still believes the caste order and the golds' power. He may admit it, hide it or rage at it. He may also pretend the leak is Simone's or the Crown's. Hinewai judges the ring of each answer, and the wisps stay out of it.

## Checks

> [!checks]
>
> - **Eat a fallen peach.** No check to pick one up. Constitution saving throw DC 10 for the first peach of the day, DC 12 for the second and DC 14 for the third, one action each.
>   - **Success.** A patch of skin as broad as a palm turns soft metallic gold for eight hours, wherever the eater chooses. ==Juice runs down your wrist as a patch of skin turns soft metallic gold, bright as a fresh coin. Saffron lingers on your lip.==
>   - **Failure.** Only a saffron stain at the lips for the day, and the next peach is harder. ==It's the sweetest thing you've eaten in weeks. The gold doesn't take, and a saffron stain is all you have to show.==
>
>   The gilding is a passing disguise. Armour, saves and senses stay as they were.
>
> - **Read which half of her is speaking.** Wisdom (Insight) DC 14, one action, on any exchange.
>   - **Success.** ==She says one thing to you and a gentler thing to someone at the trunk's foot, and both are about you.==
>   - **Failure.** ==Her warm half sounds like laughter at the cold half's charge, and you take it for mockery.==
>   - **Critical.** ==You catch the rule of her. She takes her own sentence apart, and the half the room answers gets the last word.==
> - **Read what the pale roots do while she speaks.** Intelligence (Nature) DC 12, one action.
>   - **Success.** ==The pale roots under the flowers brighten with each word she says and dim when she drifts.==
>   - **Failure.** ==The glow looks like the grove breathing in its sleep, on its own slow time.==
>   - **Critical.** ==The glow spans the whole of what she touches, and the roots the fire killed have gone dark.==
> - **Identify the wisps.** Intelligence (Arcana) DC 19, one action, for any PC who sees them.
>   - **Success.** ==The threads gather far to the east at one place, a heart where they all end.==
>   - **Failure.** ==The wisps look like a hunter's tracer, a leash drawn after him by someone who knows where he walks.==
>   - **Critical.** ==Here's what you'd know. Break the heart and the drain ends, though a believing mind still believes.==
>
>   Hinewai explains the tether regardless, and a missed roll costs the Party nothing.
>
> - **Hands on the grave.** A PC who touches a mound, pulls a flower, digs, picks living fruit or strikes the tree gets her first warning from Angles, and the hand comes off. She forgets it. A second claim is a taker's, and so is a first one made with intent to dig or break. Then the roots rise and she comes to fight. Intent decides, and fingers resting on a mound by accident are forgiven. A roll never decides it.
> - **The unspent seal.** If Jean-Claude brings Solange's seal into the reading, he may hand it to her or explain it. Handing it over unasked reads as trust. Hiding it after she names it reads as a lie she can disprove. No roll decides it, and his answer does.

## Hidden truths

- The tether is [[golden-presence|Golden Presence]]. Jean-Claude broke his chains and wears no seal, but he still believes the caste order and the golds' power, and that belief leaves the door open. Leaving doesn't close it. Only giving up the belief does, and he can't give it up tonight. Hinewai knows the wisps from the hatcheries and explains them in her broken way. Her aside is as true as her accusation, since he feeds the caste and the caste drinks him.
- The golden peach is the Gold Caste's diet fruit, and any grung who eats enough of it can turn gold, which undoes the caste's claim to divinity. Of the Party, only Jean-Claude can read the shine as Karath's gold farms and the Gold caste. He won't know the whole of the biology unless the Players put it together. The tree is Hinewai's revenge on the Grung, grown from seed she carried out of the farms.
- Simone stole peaches here, an armful that could never finish a gold diet. She had already cast Ossketh before the colour was done, so her gold stays unfinished. Hinewai knows only the theft, and Detect Magic on Simone is what would show the rite caught mid-execution.
- The canister's lore is true. Simone made the toxin the Crown deployed, and it is a cousin of the hatchery dose Hinewai suffered. No page states that kinship, and this Scene supplies it. Jean-Claude recognised the toxin at the gangplank and has told nobody.
- Her promise to Delmar covers her own hand and her law and stops there. It never shielded Jean-Claude from the wild. The wolfrabbits could hunt him on the walk because her promise binds only her.
- The tree, the saplings and the figure are one mind. Her three speakers are never different people.
- Aruhe's hatred of Grung is hers, seeped into the ground over two hundred years. She doesn't punish self-defence.
- The glowing pale roots are her reach and the limit of her phylactery. No root crosses either grave.
- A Party that leaves the grave alone and answers truly can't be judged guilty.
