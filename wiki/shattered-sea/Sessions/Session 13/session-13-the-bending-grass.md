## At a glance

- **The turn.** The island has chosen the road, an eight-foot lane of grass lying whole and flat toward [[memorial-grove|the grove]], and her people are already walking it.
- **Changes.** The gallery assembles on the lane, [[renzo-canale|Renzo Canale]] and [[carlo-ferrante|Carlo Ferrante]] included, each PC crosses roots that show them one true flash, and the Party knows the court convenes at midday.
- **Where.** The bent lane out of the road camp, northeast through the grasslands' edge.
- **Who.** [[matteo-scola|Matteo Scola]], [[ettore-ferrante|Ettore Ferrante]], [[gianni-moro|Gianni Moro]], [[luca-ferrante|Luca Ferrante]] and [[piero-sorrentino|Piero Sorrentino]], with Renzo and Carlo joining where the paths meet.

> [!narration] Opening
> Dawn comes grey through the burnt road's smoke haze, and the grass ahead of camp changed in the night. A lane about eight feet wide lies flat between the trees and your fire, every stem bowed and whole. It runs northeast into the gold-green sea until it bends out of sight. Warmth rises from the soil where the lane goes, a slow pulse of light under the world. Gianni is already three strides down it, walking like a man following a voice, and far off along the flattened grass, small figures walk it too, spaced like a procession. Matteo will have none of it. He stands at the lane's edge with his sling in both hands.

## Play

### Situation

The lane is the anchor, there to walk or to refuse. Nothing on the lane is prey and nothing on it is attacked, because the island set it down itself. The court convenes at midday, and the walk runs about three hours at a survivor's pace. Gianni feels the pull worst at dusk but walks now, and the gallery seats itself without the Party if the Party lags.

If the Party talks of fleeing the island instead, [[jean-claude-tabarnack|Jean-Claude]] alone hears the answer, in the voice that has spoken to him before, and can pass it on: ==Run if you like. *They ran.*==

A captured [[talon-skarn|Talon Skarn]] walks the lane bound and ignored. The roots never take him, and the Party can see it plainly.

### Handles

- **[[matteo-scola|Matteo Scola]]** wants the ship and nothing else. His offer is the same as at the river, walk me to the coast and I guide, and his price is distrust of the woman. He skirts the lane thirty feet off in the standing grass, and at the grove he takes the gallery's backbench. Asked why he hates her, his answer comes in short wet bursts: ==Mad woman in the trees. She talks, people walk, I watched it happen. I'm not walking anywhere she wants.==
- **[[ettore-ferrante|Ettore Ferrante]]** wants Carlo, and the lane runs toward both the graves and his brother. He says his leg is fine, which is arithmetic away from true, and he counts apologies when he believes he slows the column. Offered an arm, his posture changes: ==Forgive me. For this part I would stand on my own. Arithmetic again, but the numbers should be mine.==
- **[[luca-ferrante|Luca Ferrante]]** reaches his twentieth day, the one he marked to go after Carlo alone. He walks ahead of everyone, counting under his breath. When Carlo comes in sight at the lane's bend, the count stops mid-number, and Luca is running before it finishes.
- **[[gianni-moro|Gianni Moro]]** walks first and will not say why. Pressed on the voice, he looks at the ground and answers: ==It sounded kind. That's all. It sounded kind, and I got up.== He walks in the middle of the column after that, where someone can grab him.
- **[[piero-sorrentino|Piero Sorrentino]]** comes for the promise of salt water at the walk's end and calls the lane a towpath and the grove a Customs house. His one demand is the coast after: ==There's a hull with my name on it when this walk ends, or I swim for one.==
- **[[renzo-canale|Renzo Canale]] and [[carlo-ferrante|Carlo Ferrante]]** come down the lane from the Pantry with the morning, Renzo slow and certain, Carlo counting under his breath with his eyes on the stretcher he expects to carry. At the bend, when the two columns see each other, the brothers' names cross the grass, Ettore first, then Carlo's answer cracking on the second try. Renzo blesses the joining: ==The ground gives. Bring them to each other.==

### Checks

- **Read the lane.** Nature or Survival DC 12, once. Success: ==Every stem bows whole, bent and living. The grass lies down under something's attention, and the soil gives a body's warmth.== Critical: ==Understand what you walk on. The island picked you up when you stepped onto this lane, and it moves you now the way a hand moves something precious.== Failure: ==It reads as a game trail to you, worn by many feet. The lane feels safe because it looks used, and you mark it as a road other people survived.==
- **Follow the pulse.** Arcana DC 15 while walking. Success: ==The pulse underfoot quickens as the lane nears the grove. Step as one, any two of you, and it beats twice, close together. Every root on this island is one long attention, and it counts feet.== Failure: ==The light beneath the roots runs on its own time, quick and slow by turns. You cannot find its rhythm, then you cannot tell whether it answers your steps at all.==
- **Spot the converging column.** Wisdom (Perception) DC 13 from the lane's high bank. Success: ==Half a mile off, a second line of people walks a second bend of the same flattened grass. An old man leads it, and a tall one behind him counts on his fingers.== Failure: ==The far grass bends and rights itself in the wind, and you cannot tell people from swaying stems until the columns are close enough to hear.==
- **Keep Matteo with the column.** Persuasion DC 13 at the lane's head. Success: he skirts the grass beside you, talking the whole way about the ship. Failure: ==Matteo drops a hundred feet back and matches your pace like a debt collector. He keeps close enough to witness, and far enough to be first away if this goes the way he thinks.==

The lane gives each PC one flash, once, when the pulse first passes under their feet. These arrive on their own, without a roll.

- For Crissdalynn: ==In your mind's eye the quartz top turns and turns. A thread of light runs from it to the horizon, taut as a fishing line. When the pulse passes, the thread remains.==
- For Delmar: ==Cold water closes over your boots, though the lane is dry. Something too deep to read moves in that water, and it sounds like your own name in another mouth.==
- For Jean-Claude, alone: ==A root tip lifts the hem of your cloak and breathes you in, and the voice that follows is almost amused. She is speaking of someone beside you. The one you smell of came here by night, and the root lingers on your collar like a witness.==
- For Perrin: ==The pulse under your feet dies for three strides. A room hushes like that when something large is passing the door, and CONSUME arrives through your soles, cold as deep water. Then the pulse takes you up and hurries on.==

## Depth

### Hidden truths

- The DM key to the flashes. Delmar's water-word is the sea's name for his debt, and it arrives whole at the hearing as "Fisk's debt". Jean-Claude's flash means Hinewai knows [[simone-tabarnack|Simone]]'s scent on him and remembers her night visit. Perrin's dead patch means the island itself flinches from the thing under the water. Crissdalynn's thread is her Spinner, one strand of the same loom as the lines that will snap around Skarn.
- The lane is her escort, and her voice travels the roots ahead of the Party the whole way. Where the lane meets the grove's rim, the pulse stops dead for one stride on either side of two low mounds, because no root crosses either grave.
- Renzo came because the keeper called her garden's people, and Carlo came because Ettore is alive and his guilt needs somewhere to stand. [[beppe-sarti|Beppe Sarti]] and [[marco-lenzi|Marco Lenzi]] keep the Pantry.
