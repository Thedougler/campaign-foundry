## Payoffs

Table time is about fifteen minutes. The Session's question was what the Party is to [[hinewai|Hinewai]] and what they will do about her army. Each branch answers it differently, and the army has no date in any of them. Her beasts most likely cross the channel in months, and when they do they kill every grung on [[karath|Karath]], Jean-Claude's blue caste along with the [[gold-caste|Gold Caste]].

- **Allies.** Hinewai takes the Party as allies against the Gold Caste, whether by a free pact, a bargain on terms or a lie she believed. The Party owes her a report on Karath's pens and works before the crossing. Her voice follows the column from the treeline at the Pantry, and she lets the walkers pass unmolested. On terms, she also promises to try for the small ones and keeps what she grants. A lie that worked lasts until she hears otherwise. The wisps leaving Jean-Claude still drift east.
- **Tolerated guests.** Hinewai lets the Party go. They owe her nothing, and she owes them nothing. The island's welcome has cooled, and her voice from the treeline is faint and half addressed to someone else.
- **Karath's spies, or Guilty or resisted.** Hinewai has spoken her sentence and withdrawn her own hand, and the island sees the Party out. The Session ends at the break-out, before any walk or sail. The Party stands together at the grove, hurt only by what a resisting Party did, and Jean-Claude is whole. Behind them, an hour south-west, the survivors wait untouched at the Pantry, and the Party's promise to come back for them stays unkept. The run downriver to the coast takes a couple of days and opens Session 14, with the island's hunters closing in from the first mile.

Both good branches share one walk. Carlo, Tommaso, Sandrino and Ilario leave from the camp, and Ettore, Luca, Gianni, Piero and Matteo, who came in with the Party, leave with them. [[renzo-canale|Renzo]], [[beppe-sarti|Beppe]] and [[marco-lenzi|Marco]] stay beneath the vine. Hinewai counts all three as hers, and her protection covers them wherever they stand. The column leaves by the quarter-mile fruit-pile trail to [[the-burnt-road|The Burnt Road]], bare common ground where the island's animals don't hunt, and follows it towards [[western-landing|Western Landing]]. Nobody walks after dark on Aruhe. If dusk is near, the column sleeps one more night under the vine and goes at first light. The island's responders spare anyone who ate fallen fruit. Fruit doesn't grow on the burn, so Ilario's dried guava and the Party's rations feed thirteen people for the days of the walk.

## Reactions

[[renzo-canale|Renzo]] reacts first and does not stand. The rest follow as the table finds them.

- **Renzo, who stays.** He lifts two fingers to the line of leavers as it passes. ==He does not rise from the fruit. "The ground gave you your people back. Go, and don't look at it as if it owes you more."==
- **Beppe, who stays.** He still believes her protection ends at the clearing. ==He ties a knot and pulls it tight. "Go, then. The road will tell you when she stops counting."== He is wrong, and the Party can tell him so. He stays regardless.
- **Marco, who stays.** He carries a bundle of poles to the column's edge, sets it down and says nothing.
- **Hinewai, from the treeline.** She speaks to one PC in her two-conversation voice. If allies, the nearest vine bends towards Delmar: ==The shade over the clearing deepens a shade. "They chose it, child, so take them by day, and bring me word of the gold's doings. *He lifted the hurt one, love, as I once lifted you.*"== If tolerated, it turns towards Jean-Claude: ==The leaves hang still while a long root settles into the moss. "Go by day, child, and take nothing that was not given. *They all go, love, and this one goes thinking of his own.*"==
- **Tommaso.** He leaves the raft moored for the three, and it knocks against the bank as the column files out.
- **Ettore, on the litter.** ==He catches [[delmar-fisk|Delmar]]'s sleeve. "You carried a stranger out of a hole. I'd bow lower, if the leg allowed it."==
- **Luca.** ==He checks the line twice and gives the count before anyone asks. "Nine going, three staying. I stop counting at the coast."==
- **Piero and Matteo.** This is the contrast beat, a light moment that tells the table both men want the ship. ==[[piero-sorrentino|Piero]] looks the length of Matteo and back. "You walk behind me, eel, where I can see you." [[matteo-scola|Matteo]] grins. "Behind you is closer to the ship."==
- **Gianni.** He still feels the pull at dusk, and the first dusk on the road brings it. He walks in the middle of the column. If he turns inland, nobody grabs him and Hinewai doesn't hold the willing. The Party can talk him round, and a Player who wants it can have him stand looking inland until the fire is lit.

Prompts for the Players, each answered as they choose:

- **Jean-Claude.** Grung held these people. Ask what he says to the Calveno who keep a step's gap round him, if anything. The survivors' word for him is already in the grove.
- **Delmar.** He carried Ettore out of the pit and has Hinewai's promise in his pocket. Ask what he does with the litter's second pole.
- **Perrin.** Every Calveno on the road counts towards the survivors the Party owes Nona beyond the favour already paid. Ask what he tells the others about her.
- **Crissdalynn.** She read the island's law and asked Hinewai whether anyone can leave. Ask what she watches for on the road, and whether she flies ahead to read the burn against the horizon.

The bad branch has no reactions to play. When the Players protest the sentence or raise a hand, Hinewai speaks the cut: ==Not a petal stirs. The black blooms swing round to face the clearing, and the ring of saplings draws shut one step at a time. "Hush, child, the ground is ready for all of you, and it holds softly. *Close your eyes, love.*"== The Players answer the heads rising, and the Session cuts there.

## Stinger

- **Allies and Tolerated guests.** The second sail is [[short-manifest|Short Manifest]], [[nona-black-jaw|Nona]]'s Passage ship, with [[ruk|Ruk]] aboard as her messenger. Perrin carries Nona's sending stone. He can say whose hull it is, or the stone can warm in his pocket. The cut falls before anyone speaks, and Ruk's news waits for Session 14.
- **Karath's spies, or Guilty or resisted.** The cut is the stinger. Session 14 opens on the break-out at the grove and plays the flight back through the island, a couple of days downriver to the coast. [[short-manifest|Short Manifest]] is unmet. It waits offshore for survivors who now stand an hour from a grove that has turned the Party out.
