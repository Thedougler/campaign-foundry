## Play

### The rules of the ring

The truth binds everyone inside the flowers, the court, the counsel, the gallery and the accused. Deliberate lies refuse to come out. Silence and careful truth remain available to anyone with the nerve for them, and an answer covers the question asked and nothing wider. Counsel question and object, and the evidence is theirs to present. If the Party refuses that role, the court calls each of them to the stand as witnesses instead. [[talon-skarn|Talon Skarn]], brought bound, crosses the flowers unmarked and testifies as a witness. Asked what she claims on him, she says plainly that he took nothing of hers.

One body of the court has the floor, and the floor is where the verdict goes. Testimony hands it around. A grung-naming sends it to the scream at the treeline. Survivor grief draws it to the saplings, and a question of law pulls it back to the tree. Counsel steer it by address, and the whole gallery's faces track it before she does.

### Structure

1. **The charge.** The tree lays both counts in full sentences, and the naming begins. Each grung-naming without other business between throws the floor to rage and climbs the scream. An objection on form, the charge conflating rescue with kidnapping or the arrow with the accused's hand, keeps the floor at law.
2. **Jean-Claude on the stand.** The truth presses him in order, and the scent comes first. He names [[simone-tabarnack|Simone]], his sister. She came by night and stole peaches off this tree. The canister follows, what he recognised at a gangplank and told nobody. That toxin is Karath's old method, the skull-base preparation worked on Hinewai herself for years. A clean answer or an evasion, that choice is the Party's counsel work. Evasion inside the ring is legal and visible.
3. **The gallery testifies.** Rescued Calveno, one after another, speak of the smoke, the walk and the fruit, and of trusting the woman of the trees. Then [[ettore-ferrante|Ettore]] says his brother's name across the ring, and [[carlo-ferrante|Carlo]] breaks apart on the benches. He turned back from the four that day. The guilt has kept him ever since. The gallery weeps with him, and the saplings droop. [[matteo-scola|Matteo]] answers with nothing but distrust in short wet bursts, and his answer feeds the scream a little.
4. **Counsel's case.** The exhibits and levers sit below. [[perrin-black-jaw|Perrin]] says it plainly, whenever the chance comes. A voice under the water orders his guest to eat her garden, and he has refused it three times. Every predator outside the ring shifts at once. The floor goes to the tree, because a taker larger than Jean-Claude is now named. [[delmar-fisk|Delmar]]'s two questions come here too, the tree's origin and his own debt.
5. **Hinewai on the stand.** The Party's standing as counsel gives them the right to examine the court itself. First the seeds. Her answer must be true, and it is. The seed came out of Karath's gold farms in her hand, a thief's harvest. The law's founding theft now stands in the open ring. Then the Question of the empty chair, who advocates for the accused. The long silence, the italic half-voice, and the admission, that she no longer remembers his name. Grief breaks open through all three bodies at once. The saplings fold and the scream cracks mid-climb. Even the tree's sentences come apart.
6. **The verdict.** She steps down last, and the court rests. The verdict is the tempo left with the floor.

### Checks

- **Address the court.** Persuasion DC 15, Advantage addressing the body that has the floor. A success addressing a cold body hands the floor to that body. A failure leaves the floor where it was. Charisma of any applied kind, Performance for a display, Intimidation for a dare, works at the DM's judgement.
- **Object.** Persuasion DC 15, then DC 17 for each further objection. Success strikes the question. Failure overrules counsel, and the witness must answer, defy or take contempt, and defiance sends the floor to rage.
- **Present an exhibit.** Each exhibit below lands once, and its effect is stated with it. Only a contested exhibit calls for a roll, and Deception is never available inside the ring.
- **The Question.** Asking who advocates from the empty chair requires standing rather than a check, one successful address to the tree in open court, any phase. The grove does the rest. It is a question of law, and the law must answer it truly.
- **Question Skarn.** If he is the Party's bound witness, the ring's truth strips him like anyone else. Who feeds your fate, counsel asks, and the answer comes true. A coin-clicking falcon far away. The name under that falcon's wing is Osset. To the Party a name. To [[crissdalynn-khinriss|Crissdalynn]], who alone has heard [[master-kyzil|Kyzil]]'s message, the schism come true in front of her.

## Depth

### Hidden truths

- The verdict is a self-reckoning rather than a judgement. Whichever tempo counsel fed last wins her own argument. The gallery's faces run a half-beat ahead of every shift.
- The empty chair is the advocate's place and it is his, the drowned companion's, hundreds of years empty. The italic half-voice is what she still hears from it, and the seat is where she listens rather than speaks.
- The name is gone from her truly. Nobody in the gallery or the Party can supply it. After the verdict, the Party may offer to carve "him" on the second mound, his marker and his word.
- Skarn's testimony is the only route by which Osset's name reaches the table this Session. Crissdalynn alone hears it in full, because Kyzil's message reached her and nobody else. What she does with the collision is her player's.

### Bench lines

- **Law, unhurried.** "The garden does not accuse lightly. Speak what is so, and the soil will weigh it."
- **Grief, interrupting itself.** "I called it kidnapping. I meant, I meant, did I mean it."
- **Rage, fragments from the treeline.** "Thief. Kin of thieves. Ground."
- **The italic half-voice, from the empty chair.** "*He would have stood for this one.*"

### Spotlight

[[hinewai|Hinewai]] is the Spotlight. Her own court, built so nothing untrue can be said inside it, forces from her the one admission she cannot bear. The name underneath all of it is gone. Play her three tempos as one speaker changing speed. Let the gallery's faces mark the floor's movement for the table. The silence after her admission is the moment. Give it room before the verdict.
