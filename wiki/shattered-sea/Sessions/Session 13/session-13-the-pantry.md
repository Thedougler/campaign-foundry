> [!narration] Opening
> One vine thick as a mainmast roofs the clearing ahead, strung between the highest trees over ground the size of a threshing floor. Guavas and grey, scaly pears hang from it in heavy clusters, and the wood groans whenever a breeze touches it. You smell guava drying over a small fire, and along the far edge a lashed raft thumps against the lip of a channel. Six people stop what they are doing, and every face swings to Jean-Claude's blue skin and the neon-purple patch across it. A rope hangs forgotten in one man's hands, and a woman's fruit slice stops halfway to her mouth. Off to your right a heavy man with grizzled whiskers sits alone, and his eyes slide past the Grung to the man limping in behind you on a splint. He gets to his feet. "Brother."

## Situation

[[session-13-skarns-last-stand|Session 13 - Skarn's Last Stand]] hands the Party over at first grey light. Skarn is dead, and the Party has followed a fruit-pile track that appeared off the Burnt Road in the night. [[matteo-scola|Matteo]], [[ettore-ferrante|Ettore]] on his splint, [[luca-ferrante|Luca]], [[piero-sorrentino|Piero]] and [[gianni-moro|Gianni]] walk with them. The clearing is [[the-pantry|The Pantry]], whose layout, fruit and hazards stand on that page. It is morning by now, and [[memorial-grove|Memorial Grove]] lies about an hour north-east.

**The turn.** The people who live here were taken by the [[grung-clans|Grung clans]] and wrecked on Aruhe, and Jean-Claude is the first living Grung they have seen since. [[hinewai|Hinewai]] will ask the Party at the grove what it is: Grung under Gold compulsion, messengers from [[karath|Karath]] or more Calveno survivors. The camp asks first, in plainer words. The Party leaves with a count of who will wait for it, what the camp says of Jean-Claude and what Hinewai's voice has told the camp about her tree.

**The anchor.** Luca has copied the tally from the wall of the lava tube onto bark: twenty strokes for the twenty days since Spoke Ring, with the second stroke cut deep and twice.

> [!narration] Luca Ferrante
> Read when someone asks Luca about his count.
>
> Luca turns a length of bark over in his fingers. Twenty strokes march down it, even and neat, and the second pair sits doubled, one cut slashed across the other.

**Pressure.** Hinewai waits at the grove, and [[renzo-canale|Renzo]] holds to the island's day rule, which forbids walking the Quiet at night. He tells the Party to be on the path before the sun is high, since whatever starts at midday ends in the dark. Whether the Party lingers or hurries, the leavers wait under the vine and the Party goes on alone. A Party member who picks living fruit makes a claim, and up to three [[vine-lash|Vine Lashes]] wake at the end of that turn. Renzo steps back until dawn, and the camp turns cold.

### Renzo's questions

Renzo puts Hinewai's verdict to Jean-Claude in plain words before anyone eats, in front of everyone. The Party can answer through Jean-Claude or beside him.

- **Who sent you.** Nobody did. The Party gave its word to bring him before her.
- **Gold on you?** None that shows. The patch across his skin is the colour of his bracers' curse, and the tie that still drains him is out of sight (see Hidden truths).
- **What do your hands take.** Windfall only since the landing, and arrows into whatever attacked the Party.

How he answers sets what the camp says of him at the grove.

### The camp's word on Jean-Claude

What each survivor says of Jean-Claude if the Party asks them to speak for him at the grove, and what wins or loses it.

| Survivor | Their word | Won or lost by |
| -------- | ---------- | -------------- |
| Renzo | "He keeps the rule." | Plain answers to his questions, and nothing picked under the vine. |
| Ilario | "He ate what I gave him." | Taking the dried slice he offers without a flinch. |
| Tommaso | "He asked before he touched the raft." | Jean-Claude asking leave before he handles the raft or its cord. |
| Sandrino | "He stood where I could see him." | Staying in view with his hands off his bow. Talk of the road to the ship thaws him. |
| Carlo | "They lifted my brother out of the dark." | Ettore and Luca standing alive beside him. He speaks for the whole Party. |
| Beppe and Marco | Nothing, and Beppe warns against him. | Neither will vouch for a Grung. Beppe's fear is his own, and no roll turns it. |

The Party takes this testimony to [[session-13-the-grove|Session 13 - The Grove]]. Hinewai counts these seven as hers and weighs their word on that basis.

### The reunion

Carlo believes his brother and nephew died in the dark. Ettore believes Carlo walked to his death at [[spoke-ring|Spoke Ring]]. Luca knows his uncle called out once across the meadow, and he has told nobody. [[delmar-fisk|Delmar]] carried Ettore out of the pit and can stand where the family sees him. The reunion plays at once. Carlo hides what he did on the second day: he went back for them and turned round.

With the family together Carlo asks for nothing and takes the nearest end of any load. He leaves the hour a way out is offered, once Ettore is on his feet and Luca beside him.

### Staying and going

Any offer of a way off Aruhe gets its answer at once. The Party can promise a ship at the coast, a walk of a couple of days down the river and a return for those who wait. The raft is no way home, since the coast is days away by water.

- **Carlo, Tommaso, Sandrino and Ilario go.** They want a way out and take it as soon as it is offered. Hinewai watches that nobody is taken and the walk is dangerous, so none of them goes to the grove with the Party.
- **Renzo, Beppe and Marco stay.** Renzo blesses those who go and argues with none of them. Beppe warns them off, and Marco keeps to his work and his rules.
- **Ettore, Luca, Piero, Gianni and Matteo wait with them.** Matteo won't go near the grove, and Ettore can't walk an hour on that leg.

The Party will not force anyone, and nobody needs forcing. Nine wait under the vine, which is the raft's whole count, and the raft is left moored for the three who remain.

## Angles

- **[[renzo-canale|Renzo Canale]]** speaks for the three who stay and wants the willing to have a safe place. He knows the island's law and what Hinewai's voice has told the camp at night: she has never held a hand that asked to go. A tree at her grove bears fruit that shines like metal and gilds the hand. He offers to set bones and gives each PC a fallen fruit, and he asks one thing, that the Party keeps the rule. He lies about nothing, and he is slow to talk of anything but the law. Plain answers to his questions settle him. ==He sets down the fruit and looks at Ettore's leg before anyone else. "Sit him under the vine. I'll see what the knot has done."== Once he is satisfied: ==Renzo lowers himself onto a root and rests his big palms on his knees. "Then you are guests. Eat what falls, and you'll sleep dry."== When offered passage for the others: ==He presses a windfall pear into your palm. "Go well, and go by day."== When asked about the tree: ==He keeps his eyes on the vine. "She talks of a tree at her grove, with fruit that shines like a new coin. A fallen one gilds your hand and she doesn't mind. She says the seed came out of the gold people's own farms."==
- **[[carlo-ferrante|Carlo Ferrante]]** wants his brother out and his nephew safe. His lips move over numbers while he works. He hides that he went back to the Long Meadow on the second day, and his tell is a count that breaks off whenever the meadow is mentioned. Thanks for leading the camp to food open him, and pressing him about the meadow shuts him down. With the family together: ==He grips Ettore's shoulders, and the count in his throat breaks. "I went back. The ground was smoking, and something stood up out of the grass, long as a boat. I thought the whole meadow was burning, and I ran."==
- **[[ettore-ferrante|Ettore Ferrante]]** wants Carlo near and never wants to be a burden. He knows only that his brother walked towards the voice. He hides how much the leg hurts and how much he blames himself, and his tell is an apology for taking up room. When Carlo confesses: ==He takes his brother's wrist and holds it. "You are here, and so am I. The rest is arithmetic, and I have done enough of that."==
- **[[luca-ferrante|Luca Ferrante]]** wants his father safe and his uncle found. He knows his uncle's voice crossed the meadow once on the second day and that nobody answered. He hides his fear that the count is running out, and his tell is that he stops counting when someone calls Carlo dead. A promise to see Ettore safe opens him. Shown the tally, he explains it: ==Luca lays a thumb on the doubled stroke. "Day two. He called 'Ettore' across the grass, just the once. Father put a hand on my shoulder and we froze, because the roof was shaking."==
- **[[tommaso-brasca|Tommaso Brasca]]** wants the first way out of Aruhe, and he wants the camp's rules obeyed on the road. He built the raft and knows which river stretches the River Otter families fish. He lies about nothing. ==He ties off the cord and pockets it. "We go by day, and nobody touches a stem."== Of the raft: ==He knots its mooring cord twice round the stake. "This stays here. The old ones will want it."==
- **[[sandrino-vale|Sandrino Vale]]** wants to see his wife and daughter in Calveno. He watches the trail, and Jean-Claude's hands draw his eye before his face does. He hides how afraid he is, and his tell is the forearm he turns over and back. Talk of the road to the ship changes him: ==He lets go of his forearm. "My wife will never believe it. Tell me the hour we leave, and I'll be standing there."==
- **[[ilario-pozzo|Ilario Pozzo]]** wants a crowd to feed on a march, and he hands every guest a dried slice, Jean-Claude included. The slice is no test. Offered passage: ==He bundles the slices in a cloth. "Then I'm packing for all of us, for two days of walking."==
- **[[beppe-sarti|Beppe Sarti]]** wants to stay under the vine. He believes Hinewai stops counting a person at the clearing's edge, and he says so as a warning. He hides nothing, and his cords pull tighter as he talks. ==The cord in his hands snaps taut. "She counts what sits under the vine. Walk out from it and see how long you stay counted."== His belief is his own and no roll turns it. Carlo's account of the walk back through the green contradicts it, and Beppe says nothing more and goes on knotting.
- **[[marco-lenzi|Marco Lenzi]]** wants to stay at Renzo's side, afraid Hinewai will stop counting him if he leaves. He mutters the rules as he works and says little else. ==He mutters it under his breath without lifting his head. "Fallen is food and standing is hers, and nobody walks at night."==
- **[[piero-sorrentino|Piero Sorrentino]]** wants water and a hull, and he has no patience for talk of the inland. ==He spits on a fibre and twists it into cord. "Water at the end of the road, and I'll haul whatever you give me. Inland, I won't."==
- **[[gianni-moro|Gianni Moro]]** remembers the night voice word for word and tells it gently, if nobody laughs. ==His thumb stops on the iron bracelet. "She talked of a tree at the end. Fruit that shines like a new coin. She took the seed out of the gold people's farms when she left, and she said 'Mine now' to someone else."==
- **[[matteo-scola|Matteo Scola]]** wants a ship, and he relishes Renzo's surprise at seeing him alive. ==Matteo hooks a thumb in his sling and grins at the old man under the vine. "Told you, Renzo. Still breathing, and a ship on the way."== When the grove comes up: ==The grin goes. "I'm staying right here. She talks to me as well, and I don't answer her."==

## Checks

- **Read Carlo's evasion.** Wisdom (Insight) DC 13, one action, when someone asks how he came to the Pantry or about the meadow. Success: ==Each time somebody says the word meadow, his count drops a number and starts over. Whatever happened out there, he is still walking it.== Failure: ==He recites the march from Spoke Ring step by step, like a man who has told it a hundred times. You put his silence down to grief.==
- **Draw out Carlo's confession.** Charisma (Persuasion) DC 14, one action, by thanking him for leading the camp to food. When Ettore or Luca asks him instead, he answers without a roll. Success: he confesses as in his Angle. Failure: ==He faces the south trail and starts the march again from the top. He has nothing more to give you this morning.== Ettore and the tally still carry the truth. Critical: ==The count stops dead. "The voice didn't stop me, and a root didn't. I turned round and walked back through all that green, and not one leaf touched me."==
- **Notice the lean.** Intelligence (Nature) or Wisdom (Survival) DC 12, one action, when Jean-Claude stands under the vine. Success: ==The air lies dead still, yet the hanging cords tilt towards Jean-Claude. Above the sleeping mats they hang plumb.== Failure: ==A little air slides off the channel, and the cords sway with it.== Under the vine the cords never strike without a claim, so Jean-Claude is safe there while he takes nothing. Hinewai's hatred has seeped into the plant, and its tips lean at him from habit.

## Hidden truths

- Carlo reached the Long Meadow on the second day. He saw smoke pour from the skylight and a Terror-Bird rise out of its stump, took it for a grass fire and ran. Luca heard him call from the grass, and nobody answered because the bird was overhead. The Party learns it from Carlo, from the tally or from Insight.
- Hinewai counts Beppe, Marco and every Calveno who ate fallen fruit as hers wherever they walk, and Beppe is wrong that she stops at the clearing. Carlo's walk back through the green proves it.
- The cords lean at Jean-Claude because Hinewai's hatred of the Grung has seeped into the land. It is no order of hers. The Party can learn this from the Nature or Survival check.
- The Golden Presence tether on Jean-Claude is out of sight here. Only Hinewai's *Detect Magic* shows it, in [[session-13-the-grove|Session 13 - The Grove]].
