## At a glance

- **What is true now.** The hearing is done, the survivors have chosen aloud, seaward or back under the vine, and the forest is becoming itself again.
- **Cost.** The name under the second mound is gone beyond recall, and Jean-Claude's sister and his long silence are the whole Party's knowledge now.
- **Where.** [[memorial-grove|Memorial Grove]], the lane out to the treeline, and the reef beyond it at dusk.
- **Reactions.** [[renzo-canale|Renzo Canale]] remains beneath the vine with fruit for the departing, [[matteo-scola|Matteo Scola]] goes seaward, and the Terror-Bird screams as forest again.

> [!narration] Closing image
> The lane lets you go as it let you come, grass lying whole behind your boots. The survivors walk out in a loose column at last light, and each one chooses where the lane runs out, toward the boats or back to the garden, aloud, unhurried. Renzo remains. With him stay two of the rescued four. Somewhere north a Terror-Bird screams, and only forest sounds come back, none of them hers. The windfall in your packs smells of distillery and gold. Behind you the tree stands over its two mounds, and the flowers have closed for the night.

## Play

### Payoffs

The Session's question answers itself at the treeline rather than in the ring. Whose authority over the survivors is legitimate was never hers to keep or the Party's to claim, because each Calveno speaks the choice aloud there, ship-bound or vine-bound, with their own mouths. Both authorities live where consent lives. That holds under every verdict.

- **Law.** Jean-Claude walks free with the murder count redirected to the Gold caste. The court's offer of witness-penance against Karath stands open past sundown, and taking it or declining it can happen at any later port.
- **Grief.** The garden opens, the graves are shown to anyone who asks, and the windfall peaches become lawful to take away. The Party may offer to carve "him" on the second mound, and her answer sets the ending's tone.
- **Rage, answered by the chair.** Jean-Claude in the advocate's seat stalls the scream into grieving, and the condemnation loses its voice with the scream's collapse. He leaves as the grove's advocate, welcome under the flowers whenever he returns. The seat is his now, wanted or not.
- **Rage, unanswered.** A condemned Jean-Claude has the lane and whatever the Party makes of it, with the whole garden watching and none of it friendly. His fate is the table's to play.
- **A broken peace.** The court that broke into roots resumes coldly at kneel-point, shorter and grimmer, and the survivors' choice still comes at the treeline.

### Reactions

- **[[hinewai|Hinewai]], by verdict.** Under law she drafts the redirect like a growing contract, roots printing it into a bench for whoever returns for it. Under grief she walks the ring once as one woman, briefly, before the bodies take her back. After the chair, she looks at the seat oftener than at anyone.
- **[[renzo-canale|Renzo Canale]]** remains beneath the vine with the ones who stayed. To each departing PC he gives a fallen fruit with both hands, and he says the ground gives, the way he says everything.
- **[[matteo-scola|Matteo Scola]]** goes seaward with whoever goes, distrust intact and his offer standing, walk him to the coast and he guides.
- **The Ferrante brothers** leave together or remain together, and [[carlo-ferrante|Carlo]] walks lighter than he sat down.
- **[[talon-skarn|Talon Skarn]], if captured.** The court has no claim on him and says as much. He walks the lane free or remains the Party's prisoner, the table's call, and if Osset was named he knows the far help is spent here.

### Rewards

- **Windfall peaches.** About two dozen lie golden under the tree by dusk, lawful to take under the verdict's wording, [[gold-fruit|gold fruit]] with its skin-turning shine and its save-by-the-piece rule. Renzo's gift rides with each departing PC.
- **Standing welcome.** Each Calveno who ate her fruit belongs to her garden, and Aruhe's responders spare such people wherever they go on the island.
- **The court's testimony.** Under law and taken, witness-penance means the seals' chain laid out as evidence, the Party's strongest paper against Karath when that day comes.

### Stinger

Dusk, from the treeline, whichever verdict held: ==A grey hull stands off the reef, blindfolded Tyr at her figurehead and her colours left down. Her hunt at Calveno has ended empty. She ranges wider tonight, and her boats will come with the dawn.==

That hull is the [[hcs-ordinance|HCS Ordinance]] with [[aleksander-malone|Aleksander Malone]] aboard, his Grigori hunt come up empty at Calveno and widening now toward the Midchain's teeth. If the DM would rather point Session 14 at Calveno, the alternative stinger is the Tessarine heir's taint stirring in the [[khlysty|Khlysty]] thread.
