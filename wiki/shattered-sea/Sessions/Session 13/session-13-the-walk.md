> [!narration] Opening
> Red eyes open in the dark beyond the roadside trees, three pairs and then more, and each one watches Jean-Claude. The nearest wolfrabbit crouches forty feet off across the burnt road, long in the leg, with a rabbit's ears and a wolf's jaw. Leaves thrash overhead with the beat of wings. A hundred feet ahead the ground has caved into a ragged black hole, its lip laced with roots that glow faintly pale. The crouch tightens.

## Stakes

The Party leaves [[the-pantry|The Pantry]] without the survivors ([[session-13-the-pantry|Session 13 - The Pantry]]) and follows [[the-burnt-road|The Burnt Road]] towards [[memorial-grove|Memorial Grove]], about an hour on foot. Partway along, the island's hatred of the Grung finds [[jean-claude-tabarnack|Jean-Claude]]. A pack of [[wolfrabbit|wolfrabbits]] and two [[young-bloodhawk|young Bloodhawks]] come for him past every softer target. [[hinewai|Hinewai]] hears it through the roots and neither sends the pack nor stops it.

- **Pack.** Each beast goes for Jean-Claude over any other creature it can reach, and turns on someone else only when that creature blocks its path. A bleeding PC does not draw it off. Bait does not either, since in Session 9 a thrown spear failed to turn the wolfrabbits.
- **Party.** The aim is to arrive at the grove with Jean-Claude alive and enough strength left to face her. The Party can lose him outright, or bleed hit points and spell slots before the verdict.
- **Winning.** The pack breaks and its survivors head off towards the grove, or the Party drops into the Lava Tubes and the pack stays at the lip. Either way the Party arrives, whole or wounded, and Jean-Claude has seen that the island's hatred acts without its keeper.
- **Losing.** Jean-Claude goes down under a ring of claws, and the others haul what is left of him up the road.
- **Abandoning.** Running on open ground fails, since a wolfrabbit moves 50 feet a round and the Party about 30. Only stone stops the pack.

The danger is several small enemies in place of one large one. Each is a skirmisher, and together they put one target on the ground and rend it. The DM brings in a second wave only if a PC makes a claim (see Terrain).

## Pressure

The fight runs three to five rounds. The escape through the stone adds about a quarter-hour at the table.

- **Round 1.** The nearest pair of wolfrabbits clear the road in one bound each and rake Jean-Claude. A young Bloodhawk dives on him out of the canopy while the other circles high. A PC who failed the Perception check below rolls Initiative at Disadvantage.
- **Round 2.** The far wolfrabbits close. A creature knocked Prone with another wolfrabbit within 5 feet takes the extra rend. The second hawk dives. If the Party has not yet moved for the hole, the pack covers the road end to end.
- **Round 3.** The ring closes on Jean-Claude. The first death among the pack brings Hinewai's aside to him. If nothing has died by the end of Round 2, the aside comes at the end of Round 3.
- **Rounds 4 and 5.** The wolfrabbits break once only two are still on their feet. A hawk wheels off at 19 hit points or fewer, or when the fight moves more than 10 feet into the regrowth.
- **Counters.** Fight at the regrowth edge, where sight fails past 10 feet and the wolfrabbits lose their running bound. Kill a wolfrabbit more than 5 feet from its fellows so none can feed. Ready a weapon along a hawk's dive path. Splash Jean-Claude with Delmar's rum to throw the pack's noses off him for a round. Or run for the hole, and the pack stays at the lip.

Round 3 repaint, when the ring is closing: ==The pack has closed its ring, and the claws have chewed the road black. Wings beat lower overhead. The hole still waits on the south lip, its roots giving off the same pale glow.==

Hinewai's aside, once, heard only by Jean-Claude's player: ==A voice only you can hear says, softly, "I do not stop what is hungry. I do not punish what is hunted. *Both are only living.* Come up to the grove, child."==

## Terrain

- **The scar.** The burnt road is 20 feet wide and open to the sky. A white skeleton sits sunk to its shoulders every 20 to 30 feet, and each gives half cover to a Small creature behind it. Pulling a body free is a claim.
- **The regrowth.** Charred trunks stand 10 feet deep on either side, thick enough to hide whatever hangs among them, and sight fails past that band. A wolfrabbit cannot make its Pouncing Bound toward a space it cannot see, so inside the trees its leap runs about 10 feet and the Prone rider on it fails. A hawk cannot dive through the canopy more than 10 feet in from the road, and must land and walk at speed 20.
- **The cleft.** The compelled Grung burned this stretch hot enough to crack a tube roof, and the scorched ground has slumped into the gap. A ragged hole six feet across lies on the south edge of the road, 100 feet ahead of the Party's first position. A steep slope of rubble and root drops 20 feet into the [[lava-tubes|Lava Tubes]], and pale roots lace its lip. The wolfrabbits do not jump in, and the hawks cannot dive into the stone. The tubes run on under the whole region and meet the grove.
- **Triggered change.** A claim here, such as lighting a fire or pulling a skeleton free, marks the taker until the next dawn. Cutting living growth and taking a kill's flesh count as claims too. Responders find a marked creature within 60 feet by scent and root, so one more beast joins the pack each round the taker stays above ground. Underground the roots pulse hard toward the grove instead. Fighting back is not a claim.
- **Underground.** The tube is as wide as a village street, with a roof hung in pale roots that glow brighter as the Party passes. Two dead stretches of about 200 feet each lie between daylight wells, one near the cleft and one past the middle, and the air in them is thin. A vertical break of 30 feet lies before the last well. The route to the grove takes about two hours.

Each step sends a ripple up the roots: ==Each step sends a pale ripple up the roots overhead, and the ripple keeps going past where you stand. It runs ahead of you down the tube, faster than you walk.==

At the lip, when the Party is below and the pack is above: ==Above you the pack paces the lip of the hole with ears flat and heads low, and each of them stops at the edge.==

Contrast beat, once the fighting is behind them: ==Down here your own breath is the loudest sound, and the roots overhead rise and fall with it, slowly, as if something above were listening.==

## Checks

- **Spot the pack before it springs.** Wisdom (Perception) DC 17, no action, once per PC as the road opens up. The wolfrabbits' Stealth sets the DC. Success: ==A low body between the trunks sits too still, with its ears cocked into the wind.== Failure: the PC is Surprised and rolls Initiative at Disadvantage. ==You see trunks and hanging dark and nothing else, until the quiet gets up and runs at you.==
- **Read why they hunt.** Intelligence (Nature) DC 12 as an action. A PC who spends a breath watching the plants makes the check for free. The law's tell is plants leaning towards a taker within 30 feet. Success: ==The flowers lie flat and the roots lie still. Nobody here has taken a thing, and the pack comes anyway.== Failure: ==You watch the flowers for a lean and think you see one, so you start counting who picked what.== Critical: ==You know the island's law only ever came for those who took. This is something else, a grudge older than the law, and it looks at him the way the law looks at no one.==
- **Run for the hole.** Dash, with Opportunity Attacks from any adjacent wolfrabbit. The cleft is 100 feet from the Party's first position, about two rounds of Dashing for anyone who moves 25 to 30 feet. Crissdalynn flies, and a young Bloodhawk's Flush Dive drives a flier 30 feet straight down with any rider falling too, so flying out is the trap. Success: ==You hit the lip with claws behind you and drop over it, and the pale roots flare bright under your hands.== Failure: ==A wolfrabbit drops onto the road across your path, and the hole is still thirty feet off.==
- **Keep your footing down the slope.** Dexterity (Acrobatics) DC 10, part of the move down. Success: ==You skid down the rubble on your heels while a pale ripple spreads across the roots and chases you down.== Failure: 1d6 Bludgeoning and Prone. ==The rubble slides out from under you, and you ride it down on your back.==
- **Splash Jean-Claude with rum.** One action for Delmar, no check, with Jean-Claude within reach. Wolfrabbits that have not yet attacked him choose the nearest creature until the start of their next turn. The hawks hunt by sight and keep their dive. This is a design ruling from Session 12, when Delmar soaked a coat in rum to mask his grung scent. Success: ==The rum stink rolls off him in a wave, and the wolfrabbit in mid-leap swings its head as if it had lost something.==
- **Climb the break.** Strength (Athletics) DC 15, one action, or DC 10 with a rope anchored above. Success: ==You find a hold and then another, and you haul yourself up onto the ledge.== Failure: a fall of 30 feet, 3d6 Bludgeoning and Prone. ==A hold crumbles under your hand, and the ledge drops away below you.==
- **Cross a dead stretch.** Constitution saving throw DC 12 for each creature, once per stretch. A PC who stops at the last well and breathes for a minute first has Advantage, and one who Dashes through has Disadvantage. Failure: 1 level of Exhaustion, gone at the next long rest. Success: ==The air is flat and thin and tastes of nothing, and you hold your breath until the next column of daylight.== Failure: ==Your torch bends short and blue, and your head goes light. A step later the floor tilts.==
- **Pick the fork.** Wisdom (Survival) DC 12, one check for the Party at each fork. The mat of pale roots thickens towards the grove. Success: ==The roots run thicker down the left branch, and the glow pools there like water running downhill.== Failure: ==The right branch looks the better road, and an hour later it ends in rock.== The wrong fork costs an hour and one more dead-stretch save.

## Encounter

### Battlefield

The Party starts in the middle of the burnt road with Jean-Claude among them. The road runs east and west, 20 feet wide, with 10 feet of charred regrowth on each side. The cleft lies 100 feet ahead on the south edge. The Pantry trail lies a quarter-mile behind them. The cleft is the only entrance to stone within a hundred feet.

### Creatures

![[wolfrabbit#Statblock|Wolfrabbit]]

Four wolfrabbits at the sheet levels below. Two start 40 feet north of the road and two 75 feet south, all inside the regrowth. If the Party has taken level 6, as the Session 12 Recap records, a fifth starts 90 feet behind the Party and cuts off the retreat. One has a split left ear, so the table can call it by that. It reappears at the grove.

- **Opening tactic.** ==The first one uncoils out of its crouch and clears the road in two bounds, claws already swinging.==
- **Answer to interference.** ==It ignores the blade in its flank and the bodies in its way, and swings round them for the small blue one.==
- **Break.** Once two wolfrabbits are left, the pack stops fighting. ==The last two stop on the torn ground with their ears flat and their eyes still on him. Then they wheel round and lope into the dark.== They go to the grove, and the pack will not follow into the stone.
- **Kill hand-off.** ==The wolfrabbit stumbles in the open with its flank bare and its jaw wide. How do you finish it?==

![[young-bloodhawk#Statblock|Young Bloodhawk]]

Two young Bloodhawks, 60 feet up in the canopy, one north and one south of the road. Each dives on Jean-Claude first. A hawk stoops on a flier only when the flier is the nearest quarry.

- **Opening tactic.** ==Wings rattle once overhead, and the hawk drops straight through the leaves with its talons forward.==
- **Answer to interference.** ==Its dive meets a blade at the bottom of its path, and it jerks away across the road, screaming.==
- **Break.** At 19 hit points or fewer, or when the fight moves more than 10 feet into the regrowth, a hawk wheels off and does not come back.
- **Kill hand-off.** ==The hawk skids wing-first across the dirt and rakes at the ground. How do you end it?==

### Balance

The source is `bun run cfcli -- encounter-budget`, with the four PCs at their sheet levels (Crissdalynn Monk 5, Delmar Rogue 5, Jean-Claude Ranger 5 and Perrin Warlock 2 with Bard 3). The Creature XP comes from the [[wolfrabbit|Wolfrabbit]] and [[young-bloodhawk|Young Bloodhawk]] pages.

- 4 × Wolfrabbit (CR 4, 1100 XP each) = 4400 XP
- 2 × Young Bloodhawk (CR 2, 450 XP each) = 900 XP
- For four characters at level 6 after the +1 combat offset (recorded 5, 5, 5, 5), Low is 2400 XP and Moderate 4000 XP. High is 5600 XP.
- **Total: 5300 XP (High difficulty)**

The Session 12 Recap records the Party starting this Session at level 6, though the PC pages still read level 5. At level 6 the DM adds the fifth wolfrabbit, the force the Prep names, and the same command prints it.

- 5 × Wolfrabbit (CR 4, 1100 XP each) = 5500 XP
- 2 × Young Bloodhawk (CR 2, 450 XP each) = 900 XP
- For four characters at level 7 after the +1 combat offset (recorded 6, 6, 6, 6), Low is 3000 XP and Moderate 5200 XP. High is 6800 XP.
- **Total: 6400 XP (High difficulty)**

XP does not measure the pack's single-minded focus. Four wolfrabbits that reach Jean-Claude can deal over 100 damage in a round against his 51 hit points, and Pack Rend and the frenzy on a dead pack mate make a bad round worse. XP also leaves out what lowers the danger. The regrowth blunts both the bound and the dive, and the pack arrives in stages. The stone ends the contest outright. Responders joining on a claim are the only reinforcements, and every other opponent is on the page from the start. Hinewai hears the fight and takes no part in it.

## Hidden truths

- **The hunt is not her law.** The pack's hostility is Hinewai's hatred of the Grung, seeped into the land. No one has made a claim. The Party can learn this from a Nature check that finds the plants lying flat. It can learn it from the pack passing softer and bleeding targets to reach Jean-Claude. Hinewai's aside also says that she neither stops the hungry nor punishes the hunted.
- **She is watching and could end it.** As an archdruid lich she could stop the hunt with a word, and she doesn't. Fighting back is not a claim, and she doesn't punish self-defence.
- **The pale roots are her reach.** The pulse that runs ahead of the Party down the tube tells her where they are, and the mat thickens towards the graves. The Party can learn this from the Survival check at a fork or from a hand on a root. Hinewai also tells them at the grove.
- **The tubes lead to the grove.** The cleft opens into the same system as the root shaft at the sapling ring. The pack stops at the lip, and the DM may let the Party guess why.
