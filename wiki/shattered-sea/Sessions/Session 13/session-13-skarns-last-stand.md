> [!narration] Opening
> Rain from Jean-Claude's decanter hisses over the dying fire, and in the falling water a falcon takes shape, drawn drop by drop with his katana level and one wing hanging. A chain swings at his forearm, and he moves like a man wading through tar. Behind you the Calveno press themselves into the road's black verge, Ettore's splinted leg stuck out stiff, and thirty feet overhead a patch of stars has gone missing where Delmar hovers. Crissdalynn's last blow left Skarn's guard open, and the outline has just turned its head towards Perrin. Perrin, your next swing at him lands with Advantage. What do you do?

## Situation

The Party stands in a clearing beside [[the-burnt-road|The Burnt Road]], several miles inland on [[aruhe|Aruhe]], in the last hour of dark. [[talon-skarn|Talon Skarn]] came back for the [[fate-spinner|Fate Spinner]] a second time, and the fight from [[session-12-recap|Session 12]] resumes mid-round. [[perrin-black-jaw|Perrin]] acts next and Skarn follows.

- **Entry state.** [[crissdalynn-khinriss|Crissdalynn]]'s Stunning Strike halved Skarn's speed (20 feet walking, 40 flying) and gives the next attack against him Advantage, both until the start of her next turn. Her arm has healed and the cooked guava still holds her Dexterity at 25. [[delmar-fisk|Delmar]] hovers about thirty feet up, Invisible from a [[ghost-plum|ghost plum]], with the Spinner in his Bag of Holding. [[jean-claude-tabarnack|Jean-Claude]] carries a new neon-purple patch and the decanter keeps pouring. Skarn knows Delmar lied to him about the Spinner, though he doesn't know the bag is magical. The PCs play at their level 5 Sheets, and a long rest has refilled every resource. Each takes level 6 at a moment of their Player's choosing during the Session.
- **The Calveno.** [[matteo-scola|Matteo Scola]], [[ettore-ferrante|Ettore Ferrante]], [[luca-ferrante|Luca Ferrante]], Piero Sorrentino and [[gianni-moro|Gianni Moro]] crouch forty feet from the fire, frightened and unarmed.
- **Skarn's state.** He stands at about 200 of 314 hit points. The river camp took him down to 12, the stolen fruit healed him, and the two fights since have cost him again. The record never counted the figure, and the DM set it for a good, fair fight. His three Legendary Resistances are spent, so every save is his own. The ghost plum keeps him Invisible, and attacks against him have Disadvantage. The decanter's spray outlines him for as long as he stands inside it, about thirty feet around the fire, and the outline gives half cover (+4 AC). Outside the spray he shows as nothing at all.
- **Skarn's want.** The Spinner, and with it his own fate. He will not retreat, surrender or be taken. His orders forbid him to kill Crissdalynn and leave everyone else with her fair game.
- **The clock.** Skarn works through his options in order and takes the next step on each of his turns.
  1. His first turn, while the slow holds. He plants himself in the spray, katana up, and demands the Spinner on open stone. When that fails he opens the Kyzil half-truth.
  2. His second turn, if no blow has landed. The half-truth has failed, so he offers an equal alliance against [[talon-vantyrus|Talon Vantyrus]] and keeps the katana raised.
  3. The first blow, a refusal, or a full round of silence after the offer. He fights to the death and neither flies off nor falls back.
  4. Each round of the fight. [[vantyruss-shard|Vantyrus's shard]] on his harness lets Vantyrus straighten one Disadvantage attack of Skarn's, as at the river camp, until [[hinewai|Hinewai]] snaps the thread (see Hidden truths).
- **The commitment.** The Hook ends when the Party answers the alliance and the fight settles, with Skarn dead or gone with the Spinner.

His lines for each step are on his NPC page, and he names people by what they carry, so Crissdalynn is "staff" to him. The Party can break in at any moment, and the first blow ends the talk.

At first light a line of windfall piles, each on a broad leaf, leads east off the road through the flowers, and Matteo knows how they are stacked. Skarn took Matteo's hat at the river camp and wears it still. When the body lies still, Matteo takes it back with a grin he can't help.

## Angles

- **Strike now.** The obvious first move, because Skarn is slowed, outlined and past his last Legendary Resistance. **Cost.** The Party never hears the half-truth, so Kyzil's teacher stays a name Skarn dropped. **Answer.** The full fight runs, with Vantyrus's thumb on the scales each round until Hinewai breaks the thread.
- **Hear him out.** Crissdalynn lets him talk, or Delmar plays for time. The upside is the Kyzil lead and a round or two for the Calveno to crawl clear. **Cost.** Each line shows Skarn where the Spinner is, and his stare finds Delmar's bag. **Answer.** He offers the alliance once the half-truth fails, and the first blow still ends the talk.
- **Take the alliance.** The Party hands him the toy and he walks. The upside is a Talon turned against Vantyrus. **Cost.** Kyzil's lesson ends up in Skarn's hands. **Answer.** Skarn takes the Spinner into the dark with the katana never lowered, and Vantyrus follows it in person.
- **Use the garden.** Skarn grabs a Calveno survivor, or the Party baits him towards one. The upside is Hinewai snapping Vantyrus's thread and leaving Skarn staggered. **Cost.** A frightened civilian stands inside his reach. **Answer.** His chain hauls the survivor ten feet closer before Hinewai's will breaks what he draws on.

Each PC has a first action.

- **Perrin.** He acts first, with Advantage on the next attack against Skarn. He can swing, laugh the falcon down with *Tasha's Hideous Laughter* (Skarn's save is his own now), or mock his tail feathers again, as he did at the river camp to spoil a dive.
- **Crissdalynn.** She can grapple him and follow with Stunning Strike. She can answer his question about her master with what she knows, that Kyzil gave her the Spinner blind and seemed sorry about it.
- **Delmar.** He can shoot the outline from the air, drop on Skarn or keep the Spinner out of sight. His lie about Jean-Claude already failed once, so a second bluff has to be better.
- **Jean-Claude.** He can keep the spray on Skarn or swing it to follow him, and he can shoot or mark him. Aruhe's rules are on him, and fighting for his life breaks none of them.

## Checks

- **Hear through the half-truth.** Wisdom (Insight) DC 15, one action during his half-truth. Success: ==Each sentence he says is so, and he knows you will check. He has laid the facts out like a road and walked himself to the far end of it.== Failure: ==Every word rings true, and so does the conclusion they point to. Kyzil handed it over blind, and nobody has told you why.== Critical, a total of 25 or higher: ==A man who believed it wouldn't say "ask yourself". His eyes never leave the straps, and he doesn't think Kyzil sent you into anything.==
- **Weigh the alliance.** Wisdom (Insight) DC 12, one action once he makes the offer. Success: ==He means it, and he holds the katana up only because he trusts nobody. What he wants is his master dead.== Failure: ==It looks like one more feint, with the katana creeping a hand's width towards Delmar while he talks.==
- **Catch the thread.** Wisdom (Perception) DC 15, when Hinewai snaps Vantyrus's hold. Success: ==Threads finer than spider silk run from his harness up past the rain and go taut, and then they part.== Failure: ==His whole body jolts as though he leaned on a wall that was never there. You can't tell what he lost.==
- **Hit the outline.** Attacks use the Disadvantage and half cover already given for an Invisible foe in the spray, and a spell that needs sight can target the outline. Hit: ==The rain bends round a wing, and the blade bites something that was not there a moment before.==

## Encounter

![[talon-skarn-creature#Statblock|Talon Skarn (Creature)]]

- **Forces.** Talon Skarn alone, at about 200 of 314 hit points.
- **Battlefield.** A clearing beside the road with black flowers underfoot and the low fire at its centre. The decanter's spray falls over the fire out to about thirty feet. The Calveno crouch forty feet off at the road's edge, and black trees close in past the twenty feet of open ground. Dawn is an hour away or less.
- **Tactics.** His first turn is the half-truth (see Situation). Once it is a fight, he opens with Kusarigama Tempest when it is ready and hauls the nearest carrier ten feet with the chain. He goes for Delmar first, because he knows who lied. Stunning Strike goes to whoever stands closest, and Legendary Actions buy him a step or a strike. Vantyrus straightens one Disadvantage attack of his each round until Hinewai breaks the thread. Below 30 hit points he spends everything on whoever carries the Spinner.
- **Break and escape conditions.** He never flees or surrenders, and whatever drops him kills him.

### Balance

- 1 × Talon Skarn (CR 13, 10000 XP each) = 10000 XP
- Party budgets for the four PCs, whose Sheets read level 5 and whose combat offset makes them level 6, come to Low 2400 and Moderate 4000, with High at 5600
- **Total: 10000 XP (Beyond High difficulty)**

The figure is 4400 XP over High, and it counts none of Skarn's state. He is at about two thirds of his hit points, slowed through his first turn, outlined in the spray and out of Legendary Resistances, and he spends turns talking. Expect four or five rounds of fighting once the alliance is refused. A Player who levels mid-fight gets the new level's hit points and features at once, which makes the fight's middle a strong moment to take it.

## Hidden truths

- Every sentence of Skarn's half-truth is true, and he doesn't believe the conclusion they imply. The Party can see through it with the Critical Insight result or with Crissdalynn's own sense of Kyzil's regret, and [[master-kyzil|Master Kyzil]] can explain it later.
- The alliance offer is sincere. Skarn wants Vantyrus dead and wants the Spinner as the weapon. He keeps the katana up because he trusts no one, and he doesn't mean to cut.
- [[vantyruss-shard|Vantyrus's shard]] survives Skarn's death, a flat grey sliver riveted through the harness strap. Whoever carries it takes Vantyrus's gaze along. This Scene gives the carrier no power and the Party no way to find the watcher.
- Hinewai watches through the roots. If Skarn seizes or strikes a survivor she counts as hers, she overpowers Vantyrus's hold and the thread parts as if cut (see the Perception check).
- The fruit-pile trail is the survivors' own marker and leads to [[the-pantry|The Pantry]], a quarter-mile off the road.

## Threads

- [[rule-of-two|Rule of Two]]. His death ends the contest here. If he wins the Spinner, the contest becomes open war and Vantyrus comes in person.
- [[fate-spinner|Fate Spinner]]. Delmar holds it, and Skarn is its last hunter in this Scene.
- [[take-on-aruhe|Take on Aruhe]]. Hinewai's hand shows for the first time without her speaking.

## Spotlight

[[crissdalynn-khinriss|Crissdalynn]] carried the Spinner first, and Skarn's half-truth aims at her trust in her master. She also knows the parry he uses. Perrin is up first, Delmar holds the toy Skarn is hunting, and Jean-Claude controls the spray that shows Skarn at all.
