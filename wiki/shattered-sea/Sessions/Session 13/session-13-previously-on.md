> You woke at dawn with a falcon monk's katana on Crissdalynn and one demand, her Fate Spinner. Jean-Claude surfaced from his catatonia in Delmar's rum-soaked coat, and the magic words of his spell were curses at birds. Delmar opened the falcon with his shark-tooth sword, and the rifle blew him out of the burning roost. When he dived at Crissdalynn, Perrin sang that his tail feathers look like baby carrots, and the dive hit grass. Skarn fled with Matteo's fruit and a dozen hits left, but Jean-Claude's blind arrow brought blood down. Between fights you hauled survivors out of a smoking skylight, and a woman's voice from the trees bound Jean-Claude to her grove while Perrin refused Auralis's "Consume or die." Skarn returned unseen, and Crissdalynn's arm broke at the elbow. Where the decanter's falling water traced him, Delmar rose, a ghost plum in him, and let his invisible command chair fall on the falcon. Crissdalynn ate the kebab, stood up whole, and said, "Buddy, you've made a terrible mistake." Her blow meant to leave him senseless only slowed his wings. He is still up, slower now, and your next blow is already falling. He is not here to kill you. He is here for the toy.
