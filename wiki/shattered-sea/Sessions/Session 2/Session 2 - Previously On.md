> [!narration] Previously on
> With his followers gone, Barnaby Rook faced you. Beaumont Sel signalled Bisou, who entered a gun opening and fouled its powder. The charge failed as Delmar Fisk fired through the other opening, felling Ensign Wouters. Crissdalynn Khinriss diverted Rook's first shot and caught his next against her bracers. Rook fell from the rigging when Delmar threw a chair, then sank into black water as an eel-like creature rose nearby. Perrin Black-Jaw claimed Mira's Blade. Afterwards, Beaumont told Perrin the Passage regarded him as a Friend. Jean-Claude Tabarnack held the Truth Stone and admitted his former kin were hunting him. Ket waited below deck behind the bars of a brass cage. Feeding him the ensign, you released him towards Murrat. In the galley, Grigori offered any hand a free walk ashore before you selected a prize crew. Rain drove sideways. Then three impacts beneath the hull heeled the Surety sharply to port.
