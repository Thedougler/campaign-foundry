## What happened

[[barnaby-rook|Barnaby Rook]] came at the Party alone because the rest of his men were dead or standing with them. Beaumont Sel sent Bisou through a gun port, where she fouled the powder. [[delmar-fisk|Delmar Fisk]] shot [[ensign-wouters|Ensign Wouters]] through the other port, and the Surety's guns stopped firing. Crissdalynn Khinriss turned Rook's first shot aside with wind and caught the second on her bracers. Delmar knocked Rook from the rigging with a thrown chair. Rook fell into the dark water, something long and eel-like rose beside him, and he did not return.

Rook's cabin held letters of marque, a pendant marked for Mira, a crate of flintlocks and twenty vials of Grung tincture. [[perrin-black-jaw|Perrin Black-Jaw]] took [[miras-blade|Mira's Blade]]. The crew had taken the HCS Surety as a prize.

Beaumont named Perrin a Friend of the [[passage|Passage]]. He said [[nona-black-jaw|Nona Black-Jaw]] was hunting her lost grandson and believed someone had already been sent. He passed a Truth Stone to [[jean-claude-tabarnack|Jean-Claude Tabarnack]], who spoke plainly under it. His former kinsmen were hunting him. He had stowed away, and nobody had sent him.

Below deck, Ket was found starving in a brass cage. Jean-Claude offered blood, Crissdalynn stood between Ket and Perrin's blade, and Perrin's magic put himself on the floor laughing. The Party fed Ket with the ensign's body and opened the cage from across the room. Ket flew towards [[murrat|Murrat]].

[[shepherd-grigori|Shepherd Grigori]] set his terms in the galley. Anyone who wanted off at the next port would walk free. He shared what he knew about Cap'n Gorgeous and Rupert Knighton and said he would leave the crew at Calveno. The prize crew was named as Geoffrey Draves, Sem Holst, Alys Kuiper, Old Faas, Thunk and Noor. Perrin told them he was a Black-Jaw. A tropical storm came in from the east that night. The hull took three impacts from below, and the Surety lurched hard to port.
