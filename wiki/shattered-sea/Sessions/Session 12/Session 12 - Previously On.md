> [!narration] Previously on
> You slipped the four-winged hunter overhead and pressed inland by hugging the treeline. Soon the island taught you its law, for fruit off the ground mended you and picked fruit brought hunters. By the river's slack, Crissdalynn snatched Matteo Scola from the otters, and his ghost plum dropped her from sight. When an eight-legged cat sprang at you, Perrin leapt from her shoulders onto its back and rode it until he rolled off. At camp, a macaw told Delmar, "Perfect fire starter. Here, take it." and something in the treeline kept saying, "Admire my garden." Jean-Claude never answered, and Delmar carried him swaddled on his back. As the watches changed, a falcon stooped on Crissdalynn for the Fate Spinner's pack. She marked it once with her staff, but it shrugged off the strike that should have floored it. A pouch of black powder turned up in her hand. She bit into a plum and vanished, the falcon still upon her.
