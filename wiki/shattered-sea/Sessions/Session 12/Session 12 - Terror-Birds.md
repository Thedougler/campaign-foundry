## At a glance

- **Contest.** Cross the Long Meadow's open Gap.
- **At risk.** A Terror-Bird can swallow and carry away one body.
- **Where.** The 100-foot Gap between bands of tall grass.
- **Opposition.** Two [[Terror-Bird]]s, with civilians running into their lanes.

> [!narration] Opening
> The earth jumps under your boots, and the mossy stump under the trees unfolds a pair of scaled legs. It heaves itself up, ferns swaying off its shoulders, head higher than a mounted rider's, and its hooked beak swings round toward the hole. The tall grass behind you ends a few strides away. Before you, bare ground runs to the next wall of green. Black water runs along its foot, and halfway across, a low patch of white blades throws back the sun. The bird lowers its head. Pebbles skip on the packed dirt.

## Play

### Stakes

Reach the east band with everyone, or lose a civilian to a bird's rim. The first acid tick leaves a swallowed civilian at 1 HP, and the second kills them.

### Pressure

- Round 1: the South Bird charges the nearest creature.
- Round 2: civilians nobody has steadied run 30 feet into open ground.
- Round 3: the North Bird charges the northern half.
- Round 4: a bird swallows a grappled creature and runs for its rim.

### Terrain

Tall grass ends a charge. The channel is deep and difficult to cross. Razer-Grass blocks a charge. The skylight drops 40 feet, or 1d6 down the root chute.

### Checks

| Intent | Approach | DC | Success | Failure |
| ------ | -------- | -- | ------- | ------- |
| Steady civilians | Action, or Charisma (Persuasion/Intimidation) as a Bonus Action | 12 | They follow orders. | They run into open ground. |
| Turn a charge | Attack beside its line, or Dexterity (Acrobatics) | AC 16 or DC 14 | The charge overruns. | The charge connects. |
| Break a beak grip | Deal 20 damage in one turn | none | The creature drops. | Swallow follows. |
| Escape a grip | Strength (Athletics) or Dexterity (Acrobatics) | 18 | Free and Prone. | Still held. |

## Encounter

### Battlefield

The Gap is 100 feet east to west and 60 feet north to south. Tall grass lies on both sides. The channel runs along the east edge, and a 15-foot Razer-Grass stand sits in the middle.

### Creatures

- [[Terror-Bird]] × 2, each hunting one body and backing off at 140 HP.
- [[Commoner]] civilians, Matteo, Luca, Piero, and Gianni. Ettore is carried and has 4 HP.

### Balance

The birds charge in straight lines, never enter tall grass or deep water, and will not charge through Razer-Grass. Each wants one body rather than a prolonged fight.
