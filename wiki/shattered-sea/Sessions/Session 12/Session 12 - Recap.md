## At a glance

- **Session.** 12, with all four PCs present.
- **Party at.** A clearing beside [[The Burnt Road]] several miles inland on [[Aruhe]], with [[Matteo Scola]] and four rescued [[Calven and Calveno|Calveno]] survivors.
- **Big moments.** [[Talon Skarn]] escaped the dawn fight with stolen fruit. The Party rescued the skylight survivors and found [[Grung Authority Seal|spent seals]] ordering expeditions into the island. [[Perrin Black-Jaw|Perrin]] refused [[Auralis]]'s demand to pick living fruit.
- **Left open.** Skarn's second ambush was still in progress. [[Crissdalynn Khinriss|Crissdalynn]] had recovered from a broken arm and slowed him, while [[Delmar Fisk|Delmar]] kept the [[Fate Spinner]] hidden in his Bag of Holding. [[Session 13 - Previously On]] resumes the fight.

## What happened

At the dead fire in the river camp, a falcon monk pressed a katana against [[Crissdalynn Khinriss|Crissdalynn]] and demanded the [[Fate Spinner]]. She owned to keeping it under her feathers, and the monk, [[Talon Skarn]], said he was only there for the toy. [[Jean-Claude Tabarnack|Jean-Claude]] woke from his catatonia swaddled in a coat Delmar had soaked in rum, and the fight began.

[[Delmar Fisk|Delmar]] opened with a critical sneak attack through [[Lamarae's Fang]] and shot Skarn out of a burning tree with an explosive rifle round. Skarn shrugged off poison, hexes and [[Perrin Black-Jaw|Perrin]]'s Tasha's Hideous Laughter, and a watcher with a device like the Party's own fed him advantage. Perrin's Cutting Words taunt about his tail feathers spoiled a dive at Crissdalynn. Skarn seized [[Matteo Scola|Matteo]]'s pack of fruit and called on [[Talon Vantyrus|Vantyrus]] for help. The Party had dealt 292 of his 314 hit points when he fled inland. Crissdalynn and Jean-Claude chased him through the dark, and one blind arrow from Jean-Claude drew blood from the unseen flyer after a gifted inspiration. Both turned back rather than leave the camp and civilians exposed.

Perrin stabilised Matteo at the camp, and dawn brought fishing, trout, and a fallen [[Giant's Guava]] the size of Perrin. Eating it set one ability score to 25 for an hour, and cooking made kebabs of the same gift. Crissdalynn found the island's life reads intent: wind that happens to knock fruit down is safe, and pulling fruit down on purpose draws the native beasts. She mapped the thought in her own words at the fire.

While Crissdalynn foraged, voices in a [[Calven and Calveno|Calveno]] accent drew the Party to a smoke-wreathed skylight. Matteo knew the four survivors in the lava tube below, one with a broken leg that Crissdalynn's [[Redheart Berry|red heart berry]] mended. In the [[Lava Tubes]] the pale roots pulse wherever pressure or movement passes, and a [[Terror-Bird]] stalked the Party from above until they stilled. Crissdalynn mapped the tunnels, and the Party followed them to the burnt road.

The road ran straight and fire-cleared through Aruhe, every twenty or thirty feet a white grung skeleton with a spent [[Grung Authority Seal]]. The earliest seals read "Report the lives and land", later ones "Find the grove" and "Find the graves at the grove". Jean-Claude read his kin as compelled bombers sent by the [[Gold Caste]] and gathered samples as evidence. The woman of the island spoke into his head alone, called him kidnapper and murderer, and bound him in roots. Delmar's toast and his plea won her word. Jean-Claude would live if he followed her garden's rules and was brought to the grove, and anyone could willingly leave her garden. She left them "Meet me in the grove."

The Party camped at dusk where the corpses stopped. [[Auralis]] pressed Perrin at the fire with a vision of swimming small in dark water and the word "Consume". Fresh [[Stonepear]] and guava hung on the vine before him, and he turned his back on both, choosing his new crew over his old vengeance. Jean-Claude watered the land from a decanter for the keeper's regard, and Crissdalynn found three black [[Rotheart]] fruit that mend rolled healing at its full number.

During that rest, Skarn came back invisible. His dive broke Crissdalynn's arm at the elbow and stunned her. The decanter's falling spray gave the Party his outline. Delmar ate a [[Ghost Plum]], rose into the dark, and dropped his hundred-pound invisible [[Fleet Commanders Chair|command chair]] on the shimmer. A natural-1 arrow from Jean-Claude's cursed bracers turned him neon purple and pinned Crissdalynn's leg. Crissdalynn ate her kebab, then a Rotheart that healed her whole and mended the arm. Perrin refused Auralis again under stun, Skarn was slowed, and the fight was still joined when the recording stopped.
