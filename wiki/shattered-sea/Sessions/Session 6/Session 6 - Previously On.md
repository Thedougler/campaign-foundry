> You came back beneath Calveno with the artificer from the canal workshop, her cannon and owl taking up positions around the powder stores. A defender laid down his arms, so you passed through untouched. Bazzoth met you at his still and poured his finest remaining drink into a bomb before he touched it off. The water took him, two workers, his still, and the stores, and another powder cache went under with them. And that left only the primary chamber. A red figure knelt inside a circle chalked on the floor, chanting while four amphibious guards held the approach, one of them already down against a wall. The air carried powder and river water, and the chant came nearer with every step. Once you reached them, the circle still shone and three guards remained between you and the ongoing ritual.
