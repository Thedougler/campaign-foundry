> [!narration] Previously on
> After defeating its boarders, you took control of the Crown cutter. Perrin became a Friend of the Passage and learned Nona Black-Jaw was pursuing her missing grandson. Meanwhile, one of you answered the Truth Stone. He admitted his former kin were hunting him. Descending into the ship, you found Ket, a Moucheron starved thin behind the bars of a brass cage. When you fed him and opened the cage, he flew towards Murrat. Before you formed the prize crew, the cook offered anyone freedom at the next port and shared his knowledge. Perrin told the six new hands he was a Black-Jaw as they joined. Then a tropical storm caught the cutter. Three times, something hammered the hull from below. The vessel lurched hard to port.
