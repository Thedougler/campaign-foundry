## At a glance

- **Session.** 11.
- **Party at.** Matteo's river camp in the River's slack basin on Aruhe, the long rest made and Crissdalynn's watch begun.
- **Big moments.** The Party slipped an adult Bloodhawk's hunting line, baited wolfrabbits and young Bloodhawks into fighting each other, drove off a Spiguar that pounced Crissdalynn, and pulled Matteo Scola from the otters' game.
- **Left open.** The falcon that stooped on Crissdalynn's watch burned one Legendary Resistance and was still over her vanished shape when the recording stopped. The Fate Spinner was still in the Party's keeping, and the woman in the woods was still only heard.

## What happened

Above the Old Gardens, Jean-Claude went catatonic on Delmar's back and Delmar caught him and swaddled him into a jacket carrier. An adult Bloodhawk dove past the Party, struck the crown squid down through the trees, and lost interest in low quarry, so the Party flew low along the treeline rather than fight.

At a cold camp upriver, Crissdalynn tested the fruit. A fallen redheart berry healed her thirty hit points, her insight named the juice healing, and the berry she picked from its stem drew two wolfrabbits and two young Bloodhawks. Bird speech, a ship's recall whistle, and Perrin's illusions, including a fatter Perrin tossed out as bait, turned the beasts onto each other, and the Party slipped away north.

The grass ambushed them next. A Spiguar leapt from eight-foot grass at the Party and pounced Crissdalynn, who fought off its prone save with a resource to spare. Delmar's steady blunderbuss round tore a burning hole in its shoulder, Perrin leapt from Crissdalynn's back onto the cat with a Hex and a green-flame blade, and the Spiguar fled north bloodied with Perrin dropping off mid-run. It swerved wide of the razor-grass patch where a [[Terror-Bird]] waited.

In the slack basin, three giant otters kept Matteo Scola in a game, and the Party bought him out with acrobatics and play rather than blades. Matteo confirmed the island's law, eat only what the ground gives, and told of a woman in the woods whom the survivors obey or die. He shared his fruit: redheart pears that heal eight dice plus eight, and ghost plums that turn the eater unseen. Crissdalynn ate one and vanished on the spot. A stonepear read granted resistance to all damage for a minute.

The Party took Matteo's camp for a long rest. Crissdalynn picked a crackling black lotus flower, Perrin identified it as bottled wild magic and eased it into the River with Mage Hand, and its disturbance burst Tasha's Hideous Laughter on Perrin before the water carried the bloom away. Delmar met a macaw that spoke his own worry back to him, saying "Perfect fire starter. Here, take it." and "I worry the woman's real."

On Delmar's first watch, a voice from the trails repeated "Admire my garden," and he let it be. Lantern light later drove an unseen watcher from a dead porcupine it was eating, with an inhuman scream. Delmar butchered the carcass during his rest and cooked the meat. He saved the quills for Perrin.

At the watch change, a falcon that had crept in unheard stooped on Crissdalynn and went for her pack. Its grab slipped her grapple, her Bardic-inspired staff found it once, and the Stunning Strike it failed cost it one Legendary Resistance to keep its feet. A bag of black powder pressed itself into her hand, leaving her craving a sniff of it. She pulled out a fruit and bit down once. She vanished with the falcon still on her, and the recording stopped there.
