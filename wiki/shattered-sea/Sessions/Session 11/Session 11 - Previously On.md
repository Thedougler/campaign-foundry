> [!narration] Previously on
> Shepherd Grigori appeared in your galley with a warning about hunters after Crissdalynn's Fate Spinner. Delmar shook a hand gone cold as the grave, and Grigori vanished. Soon a ship crept up behind you with its lanterns out. Crissdalynn stalled it with a headwind for your broadside, and Delmar shot a lantern that set its sails burning, so you sailed on to Aruhe. There you rescued two castaways. But more were missing inland. Jean-Claude tracked them into the terraces and shot down a dart-spitting plant. Creatures swarmed towards him, and a Crown Squid struck Delmar even above the treetops. You fled above the canopy. The creature tore along beneath you. Perrin conjured a double of himself falling into the bushes, and the squid chased the decoy. As you flew back across the terraces, it snatched up two wolfrabbits and disappeared into the jungle.
