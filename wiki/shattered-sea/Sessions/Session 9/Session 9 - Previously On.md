> [!narration] Previously on
> The thing beneath Mercatura fell, and families came into the crater to ask after the missing. Amid the dust, Iacopo Fieschi placed your crew under Calveno's protection. Nona took charge of the search, counted more than three hundred fourteen absent men, and sent ships along the raiders' wake. While you spent Fieschi's payment on tools and a cloak, a scrap and a falcon-featured stranger supplied the same troubling name. It belonged to a monk who had left the Sentinels during their split, yet Crissdalynn did not know the falcon at the shop. A craft worker finished the last commission while Cobb nearly finished the refit. Perrin accepted Nona's promised favour. By the time *Uncertainty* could sail, the raiders had distance, the writ was fresh, and your first course was open water.
