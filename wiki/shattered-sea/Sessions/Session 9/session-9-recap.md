## What happened

The session opened on [[aruhe|Aruhe]], away from the Party: the table shared one purple Grung whose fleet hull a storm had ripped on a reef two days out of Calveno, with [[karath|Karath]] the goal ashore. Group checks took the Grung through a [[wolfrabbit|wolfrabbit]] pack's charges and a parkour escape along the beach. The swim against the current failed. One guard drowned in the surf, and the DM left the fate of the last two undecided. Captives crouched under the broken hull, unnoticed.

Two days later the refitted [[uncertainty|Uncertainty]] came out of La Vasca's cradle into a festival morning. [[geoffrey-draves|Geoffrey Draves]] brought a map of headings and a tour of the work: the captain's quarters were a break room now, and an icebreaker blade rode the prow. The ship drew four feet of water with four more guns aboard. The money was [[thunk|Thunk]]'s doing, and [[perrin-black-jaw|Perrin Black-Jaw]] called the spending out. He took the bill to [[nona-black-jaw|Nona Black-Jaw]] over [[sending-stone-nonas|her sending stone]], and she judged the guns useful and the break room unnecessary. [[ruma-delacroix|Ruma Delacroix]] signed aboard and took Perrin's ledgers and the galley with them. Travel ran on the [[shipboard-travel|Shipboard Travel]] roles, where three passes out of five held the course and her cooking turned each long rest into a roll of temporary hit points for everyone. The Party's roll call of the old crew named [[thunk|Thunk]], [[geoffrey-draves|Geoffrey Draves]], [[alys-kuiper|Alys Kuiper]] and cook [[shepherd-grigori|Shepherd Grigori]], and Alys and Grigori stepped ashore at Calveno before the refit. The map offered [[sparhold|Sparhold]] a day north in the [[midchain|Midchain]], [[kalowe|Kalowe]] a day or two southwest, and [[fathomrush|Fathomrush]] far east by the Maw. The Party set a course for Sparhold, with Crissdalynn to fly ahead and ask after the fleet.

A couple of hours east, [[master-kyzil|Master Kyzil]] dropped out of the sun onto the stern, spoke to Crissdalynn alone and left. She kept his message to herself.

Jean-Claude took the first watch, rolled a natural twenty on it, and reported the night clear. Catarina spent the hours below on a bird-sized catapult for Crissdalynn's scouting flights. Crissdalynn joined the second watch, and near dawn both watched wreckage break the waterline. An hour later the sea was full of driftwood.

Crissdalynn flew over the wreckage and found a Grung-made boat shattered the way the [[red-lady|Red Lady]] had been: surrounded and crushed by water. A voice called help from a piece of hull, and she hauled two survivors out by the feet while ropes took the third. The survivors were young Calveno men, a day or two adrift without fresh water. Their account ran that a hurricane out of the east cut the captured fleet in half, and the back half was pushed east and given up for lost. [[nino|Nino]], a metalworker of Mercatura, had been taken on a grocery run and loaded through the tunnels. One survivor blamed the storm on tribute unpaid to [[umberlee|Umberlee]], and Crissdalynn went stiff at the name. Terror then sent one captive over the side, and a second followed him. Crissdalynn dove after the first and caught him above the water, and Delmar hauled the second back over the rail. Jean-Claude watched the survivors from the rigging and read them as blistered, split-lipped and honest. Delmar's persuasion calmed them, and the captives took the spare crew beds in the galley, with Catarina's construct on deck as a deterrent.

Delmar flipped his coat to the Admiral and paid a coin over the side in that voice. Perrin counted his second blatant swap in two days. The table worked the sea-toll out as common knowledge, and most people flick a coin into the water when they board. Ginger drinks went around against seasickness, warm with Catarina's magic, and the DM ruled advantage on seasickness saves for the next three and a half days.

Three hours into the second day a shoal hid a huge hull turning to intercept: the [[hcs-ordinance|HCS Ordinance]], about twice the Uncertainty's length. The man-of-war belongs to [[rupert-knighton|Rupert Knighton]]'s private fleet and mounts ninety-six guns. The Party chose merchant colours over a fight. Perrin, whose kind carries the Dravosi bounty, went below with Ruma, and Jean-Claude climbed into the middle masts under his cloak, fifteen poisoned arrows ready. Delmar dressed the deck for inspection from a commander's chair and ran up friendly flags. Something sharp hidden in the chair dealt him three piercing damage. The Ordinance came alongside under a scarred young officer and a gaunt grey high elf with six swords under his coat: [[aleksander-malone|Aleksander Malone]], the Hound of [[tyr|Tyr]], a Dravosi Inquisitor hunting what he calls perfects. The officer introduced himself as [[corbin-knighton|Corbin Knighton]], newly in charge of the Crown's knights under Rupert's dispatch, and Catarina, who does work for the Dravosi, traded words with him about Rupert's evacuation advice.

The inspection ran on lies. Delmar played the hired captain on a maiden voyage for [[iacopo-fieschi|Factor Fieschi]]. He placed Grigori in Calveno and urged wiser hunting there. Malone refused Delmar's toll coin and named Tyr the one real god. He answered with a Command of confession; the save failed, the personality die came up six, and a cold admiral persona surfaced to confess in detail to the murder Delmar had planned and to the victim who saved his life. Malone believed it. He slapped Crissdalynn and caught her deflection for his trouble. His confession spell on her met a natural twenty and failed so cleanly he thought it had worked, and her false confession about lifting a shark impressed him. Catarina's umbrella put Umberlee's laughter around the ship until the inquisitor stepped back, Delmar flipped the refused coin over the rail, and Knighton drew a flintlock on his own inquisitor: "You heard them. I believe them. Go." The Ordinance turned for Calveno.

The Party let her go. Jean-Claude proposed flooding her lower decks from a decanter of endless water, but Catarina urged caution and Delmar found the approach too exposed. Delmar's sending stone took the warning to Nona instead: the Ordinance and its Hound were Calveno-bound, hunting Grigori. Nona asked which ship and whether they meant to occupy the harbour. Delmar retold the hour before departure as well. A call on an old flame had rolled a natural one and turned comic. He spent twenty-five words warning his most recent mate [[serena|Serena]] about the warship and the elf, then scrubbed the stone's returning contact for a burner crystal.

Below, Catarina's workshop was ready. She unveiled [[lamaraes-fang|Lamarae's Fang]], a sword of whip shark bone fused with metal, and priced it at one axolotl for study. Delmar flipped the blade, swore he would get her one, and Perrin stood on the table to toast the new crew and the new sword. Talk turned to [[zort|Zort]] and the Midchain contact the Party carried out of Calveno, and the session closed with the Midchain's broken line coming up over the horizon.
