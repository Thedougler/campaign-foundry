## At a glance

- **Goal.** Worship. The caste rules the Grung as living, walking gods on earth, with the sages' religion, the clans' obedience and the seals' compulsion holding the claim up.
- **Next move.** Another burning party leaves [[Karath]] for the two graves on Aruhe.
- **Led by.** The caste rules as one body from Karath.
- **Base.** [[Karath]], with works hidden across the [[Verdant Teeth]].
- **Strength.** Authority Seals that overwrite a will, a sage-kept religion, hatcheries, captive pens and gold farms the lower castes never see.

> [!narration] Public face
> A gold grung walks the line of pens with two red sages a step behind, and every head at the work goes down before his shadow crosses it. He stops where the river mud has taken a row of hatchery stakes, says one word, and the grung hauling stone repeat it like a breath. One of them glances at your boat, then back to the mud, and keeps hauling. The blue ones on the beach use the sages' name for them, the gold presence, gods walking the earth.

## Play

- **When met.** Gold-caste grung keep inland at [[Karath]]. The Party meets their works first: script on a spent seal, the fixed devotion of a compelled bearer. At the reef gaps, an agent erases evidence, and inland a hatchery runs on captives.
- **When opposed.** They spend seals and send fire. A captured grung leaves with a lifelong order, and a burning party sails for whatever has threatened the works.
- **Offers.** Beach trade belongs to the clans, and the caste keeps to the interior. What it gives a lower caste comes pressed in by a seal.
- **Costs.** An order laid in by a seal runs for life and feels like the bearer's own wish. Crossing the caste means becoming its instrument.
- **How to notice or interfere.** Read the gold script on spent seals and watch for gold bands sealed onto skin. A bearer whose devotion outruns the clan's interest reveals the order's source. Proof that its gods are grung breaks the claim that rules the clans.

## Depth

### History

The Gold caste leads the [[Grung Clans]] and rules from [[Karath]], where its hatcheries, captive pens and gold farms hide inland, secret even from the lower castes. The clans' water power failed, and the caste answered with compulsion. Seals pressed to lower-caste grung sent expedition after expedition toward Aruhe. The orders ran from report, through replacing the lost, to destroying the two graves beneath the memorial tree. At the last came the burning of the forest along [[The Burnt Road]]. It branded [[NPCs/Ozzeth, the Twiceborn|Ozzeth, the Twiceborn]] when his self-cast colour rite stalled. The suppressed rite called Ossketh shows a grung can be sealed partway toward gold, as [[Simone Tabarnack]] did to herself.

### Hidden truths

- The gold presence the sages preserve and preach is the caste itself, mortal grung who have named themselves gods. The religion of the Grung is the worship of the Gold caste. The script on a spent [[Grung Authority Seal|Authority Seal]] and the sages' decrees share one author.
- The caste maintains a species-wide spell. Grung who believe in the caste system all fall under it. Power a grung gives up willingly crosses to the caste, and so does magic a grung believes it lacks. The caste spends both freely as its own, and that constant drain lifts the gold toward something like demigods.
- The enslavement runs in both directions. The clans enslave other peoples and take their agency, while the same belief figuratively enslaves the grung to the gold.
- A seal's compulsion holds for life, and the bearer believes the order was always a conviction of their own. Reading a spent seal shows the whole chain of orders it held.
- Any grung can eat its way to gold. The colour comes from diet, the [[Gold Fruit|gold fruit]] grows on the farms at [[Karath]], and a casting of [[Ossketh]] locks the change in for life. [[Simone Tabarnack]] proved it with fruit bought from outside the farms, and the caste could not deny her gold without exposing the biology, so it treats her as showing signs of divinity.
- Breaking the caste means convincing clan after clan across five separate islands that their entire belief system and religion is wrong and a lie. The caste's power weakens once the grung public can hear those facts with proof behind them.
- The authority mark on [[Solange's Authority Seal]] matches the spent seals on Aruhe, so the same caste's command reached her too.
