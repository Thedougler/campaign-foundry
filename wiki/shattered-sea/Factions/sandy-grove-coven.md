## Dealings

- **Goal.** The coven's specific aims are not established. Its people have no grievance against the town.
- **Faces.** [[halda-wren|Halda Wren]] leads them at [[sandy-grove|Sandy Grove]].
- **When met.** About a dozen hedge-druids live among the old trees, children among them. Their leader splits kindling while visitors talk.
- **When opposed.** They are harmless. The [[penance-bar|Penance Bar]] mob threatens every person in the coven.
- **When ignored.** They remain in the woods while the village's fear turns against them. Their further development is not established.
- **Offers.** Willow bark and herbs, traded for salt fish.
- **Costs.** The village treats them as outsiders and calls them "the witches". They were never part of the town.
- **How to notice or interfere.** Visit the grove beyond the last worked field and hear their account instead of accepting [[corvin-ashe|Corvin Ashe]]'s accusation.

## History

The herb trade predates Sorrowbell's first priest. The coven never entered the town because nobody invited them. That distance gave Ashe outsiders to blame, though the coven itself had neither harmed the village nor sought a quarrel.
