## At a glance

- **Goal.** Collect [[Umberlee]]'s tribute from every hull afloat, and witness the sea's payments while they do it.
- **Next move.** Collection continues port by port, and the Calveno shrine keeps an open appointment for [[Delmar Fisk]].
- **Led by.** [[Umberlee]], through agents such as [[Umberlee - Branca|Branca]].
- **Base.** Basin shrines at the waterline of every working harbour.
- **Strength.** Collectors in every port and a collection hull on the water.

> [!narration] Public face
> Attendants in salt-bleached grey work the basin as you tie off, taking a coin from each crew and marking it in a booklet. Their hems are damp, and tallow and brine carry across the quay. People call them the Waveservants.

## Play

- **When met.** They collect, witness and keep the rites, and they treat the exchange as business already settled. Coin is preferred, and rope, fish bones, brass buttons, a broken compass needle, a carved token or a spoken promise will serve.
- **When opposed.** They refuse almost no one, because an enemy's tribute spends as cleanly as a friend's. A shrine that takes sides stops collecting from half the harbour.
- **Offers.** A recorded blessing, a place to acknowledge the sea, and a shrine appointment when one is owed.
- **Costs.** The tribute itself, which buys the admission that the sea can take you.
- **How to notice or interfere.** Grey robes, damp hems, basin and booklet. Pay on the way out, or count on the weather to remember the slight.

## Depth

### History

The shrines have outlasted every flag raised above them, and collection has run at the same rate from merchant, pirate, navy, smuggler and pilgrim for as long as the record holds. The Sentinels of the Eyrie founded their Kensei tradition at [[High Eyrie]] in 895 DR to counter this collection, and their watch has run since. An older charge stands apart from the harbour basins: the hidden shrine on [[Vel-Orn]], where the work leans toward the drowned.

### Hidden truths

- They do not discuss the [[Drowned Maw]]. Their working position holds the Maw as an anomaly that will one day fall back inside her dominion, and they leave the rest alone.
- Some among them suspect tribute is the plug holding the fissure shut, and the clergy do not advertise the suspicion. Shrine records and Maw pressure can reveal it.
- They know [[Delmar Fisk]] on sight, and attendants have looked at him with recognition before they speak. A watcher at the Calveno shrine can catch it. Branca's report of the night communion travelled the maritime religious network, and harbour clergy watch for the man in the distinctive coat who came to the shrine by night. Gossip in the harbour says someone who stole from Umberlee is in the city, without his name.
