## Dealings

- **Goal.** Secure reliable feeds through fighting contracts while keeping each squad intact.
- **Faces.** [[varet|Varet]] speaks the terms and wants a client who hears the feeding clause before agreeing. [[toa|Toa]] wants to understand an opponent's fighting habits. [[suke|Suke]] wants room to watch strangers without being crowded. [[wirra|Wirra]] wants medical work paid before it starts. All four can be reached through [[the-flat-water|the Flat Water]] in [[kalowe|Kalowe]].
- **When met.** Varet gives the rate and the feeding terms together. The company is sociable when fed, sipping from flasks throughout the day. Each member is eager for the next drink even while doing business. Across the Sea, people count them among their greatest fighting forces.
- **When opposed.** A squad contains four [[moucheron|Moucherons]], each with four swords and a stinger. Fast flight, cunning and evasive fighting make them hard to pin down. Battle sustains them through the blood of their enemies. They stay with a client under fire while fed, but hungry members can turn on allies quickly.
- **When ignored.** They remain available through the Flat Water or factors who know to ask, taking another suitable contract rather than pursuing an unwilling client.
- **Offers.** Varet offers the whole squad's fighting service in exchange for the stated blood payment. Wirra's medical work costs extra and is paid up front. They leave the site clean after their work.
- **Costs.** Squads will not split. An assignment without a fight still owes blood. Each member's clay keeps store preserved blood for two to three days, decanted into flasks for frequent sips. Their fast metabolism makes them hungry within hours and desperate within a day without a feed. Seven days is the longest they can survive without blood. Prudent admirals and captains stock chilled blood for twice the voyage, enough to pay the squad even when no enemy appears.
- **How to notice or interfere.** Ask [[reweti-kahurangi|Reweti Kahurangi]] at the Flat Water for their terms. Offer a reliable feed and a clear task. Trying to hire one member separately fails.

## History

Moucherons leaving [[murrat|Murrat]] sell their skill through Kalowe. Their company takes its name from the Way of the Five Blades, in which a fighter's stinger joins the blades wielded by all four hands.

In Session 2, the Party learned of the company's swordsmanship and habit of cleaning up after themselves. [[barnaby-rook|Barnaby Rook]] wanted to breed that strength and fervour. He took [[ket|Ket]] as a specimen, and the Party fed and freed Ket from his cage.

## Hidden truths

The company's reputation fuels the Sea's talk of an almost unbeatable Moucheron army. That army remains a hypothetical. Murrat's billions are too disorganised and bloodthirsty to drill, and nobody could supply enough blood when fighting stopped. Varet can explain the daily feeding clause. The short supply inside a member's keeps shows its practical limit, while Kalowe's pilots explain why trying to recruit on Murrat ends in death.
