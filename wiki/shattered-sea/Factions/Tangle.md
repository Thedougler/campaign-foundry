## At a glance

- **Goal.** Set wider policy for the [[Passage]]'s Runs, Holds and kitchens.
- **Next move.** The elders name none that the Runs would recognise in advance.
- **Led by.** Senior elders of the Run families. [[Nona Black-Jaw]] of the Black-Jaws is one.
- **Base.** No fixed seat. The Tangle speaks where elders gather.
- **Strength.** The elders' word across every Run, and the Runs' habit of obeying it.

> [!narration] Public face
> You will look for a hall and find a kitchen. Policy leaves the elders' tables already known to every Run that must obey it, set by elders who gather where the food is and argue until the ruling comes out plain. Ask who decided, and the answer, every Run and every harbour, is the Tangle.

## Play

- **When met.** Rarely and at one remove. [[Nona Black-Jaw]] speaks Passage policy at the Warren that the Tangle set wider.
- **When opposed.** The Tangle does not argue. It reroutes.
- **Offers.** Passage-wide sanction, and elders who can vouch a whole Run.
- **Costs.** Obligations counted across every Run at once.
- **How to notice or interfere.** Matching rulings surfacing in kitchens far apart, and elders arriving quietly to hear a case.

## Depth

### History

The [[Passage]] grew from the [[Warren]] around 1240 DR without a public hierarchy, running through travelling families, Runs, Holds and kitchens. The Tangle is where that loose order sets wider policy. Anchor Runs like the [[Black-Jaw Run]] execute it, and elders like Nona carry it.

### Threads

The Tangle underlies the Passage's standing with the Party and the rescue work in [[Bring the Pearl of Souls to Umberlee]].
