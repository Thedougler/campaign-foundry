## Dealings

- **Goal.** To bargain with the desperate mortals who end up at the [[huahei-crossing|crossing]], where the magic is thickest.
- **Faces.** A [[green-hag|green hag]] in the bog, with illusions and false lights over the black water. A [[sea-hag|sea hag]] on the tide line. The third hag is not recorded. The coven keeps to [[huahei|Huahei]].
- **When met.** They don't hunt anyone who comes ashore. They wait for the Party, and they bargain.
- **When opposed.** The coven can confront intruders with three hags. The green hag's Coven Magic becomes available while her two hag allies are within 30 feet of her. She uses her bog illusions to misdirect attackers, while the sea hag keeps to the tide line.
- **When ignored.** They keep waiting at the crossing, and every desperate mortal who comes to Huahei ends up in front of them. On a night when a Wild Hunt comes through, they bar their doors and bargain with nobody.
- **Offers.** The green hag offers guidance through the bog to someone desperate enough to accept her price. Visitors seeking a different bargain can ask at the crossing.
- **Costs.** For guidance, she demands a favour payable on the visitor's next return to Huahei. She names the task only when they return.
- **How to notice or interfere.** Investigate the bog's false lights or approach the sea hag at the tide line. Bargaining takes place at the crossing, and the hags don't guard access to it.
