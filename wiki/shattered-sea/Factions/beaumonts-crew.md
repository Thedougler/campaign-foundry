## At a glance

- **Goal.** Wages paid and a working ship beneath them, nothing grander.
- **Next move.** Crew the [[saltwright|Saltwright]] wherever [[beaumont-sel|Beaumont Sel]] signs her next. The next watch is the whole of their plan.
- **Led by.** [[beaumont-sel|Beaumont Sel]], captain and employer.
- **Base.** The [[saltwright|Saltwright]].
- **Strength.** An able hand for every station, four in all, and all of them good at the work.

> [!narration] Public face
> You come aboard to the sound of work. Rope runs through a block overhead, and something salted and hot drifts up from the galley hatch. A woman bends over charts at the stern table without once looking up, and a lad coils line before anyone has told him to. Nobody stops working when you cross the deck. The bosun speaks without turning. "If you're sailing with us, find a rail and keep clear of it. If you're not, find the shore." These are Beaumont's Crew, the hands people mean when they say the Saltwright sails well.

## Play

- **When met.** Polite, busy and short with nonsense. They speak for their own trades and pass the rest up to the captain.
- **When opposed.** No pride in it. They side with [[beaumont-sel|Beaumont Sel]] and the ship, and leave the fighting to whoever holds her.
- **Offers.** Honest labour: navigation, deck work, a hot meal, and muscle that shows up on time.
- **Costs.** Coin on the wage roll, and no loyalty past it. A better-paying deck takes them without ceremony.
- **How to notice or interfere.** They are the ones visibly working. Outbid the wage or endanger the ship, and they move faster than any threat could push them.

## Depth

### History

Beaumont's hired complement aboard the [[saltwright|Saltwright]], taken on to fill the ship's required stations. [[lenne-vor|Lenne Vor]] navigates while [[drav-holke|Drav Holke]] runs the rigging as bosun. [[wessa|Wessa]] keeps the galley, and [[fen|Fen]] works the deck as the ordinary sailor. The Party shared the ship with them through the HCS Surety's boarding, though the record of that day names none of them in the fight.

### Threads

None. The crew goes where the [[saltwright|Saltwright]] goes, and no further.
