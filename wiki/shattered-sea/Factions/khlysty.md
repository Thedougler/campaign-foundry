## At a glance

- **Goal.** Ascension beyond what a Hierarch can be, powered by heirs healed into unwitting blood-bonds.
- **Next move.** Grow the flock through cures nobody asks the price of, and keep [[aleksander-malone|Aleksander Malone]] a step behind.
- **Led by.** [[shepherd-grigori|Shepherd Grigori]], Korabl of the Flock.
- **Base.** Nowhere and everywhere: threaded through Dravosi and Tessarine noble courts.
- **Strength.** Healing that leaves a dormant thread in every patient, and noble houses that owe it their children.

> [!narration] Public face
> Nobody meets the Flock outright. A warm man with cold hands saves a life nobody else could save, and the house he saved stops asking questions. His name arrives later in a heresy file, or in a whisper between factors, and by then the debt is yours. People who speak of the flock at all call it the Flock.

## Play

- **When met.** So far the Party has met the Flock only as [[shepherd-grigori|Shepherd Grigori]]'s warmth, and as the Crown's confirmed-heresy file.
- **When opposed.** [[aleksander-malone|Aleksander Malone]] pursues its infiltration with scripture-cadenced certainty. The Flock's work hides behind the gratitude of healed houses.
- **Offers.** Cures for afflictions past medicine, asked of no one and paid to no one.
- **Costs.** A dormant blood-thread in the patient, and the question nobody in a saved house asks.
- **How to notice or interfere.** A DC 22 Arcana check detects a thread. Counterspell disrupts the healing and the binding together. The sect has a small mark, carried quietly and read by those who have seen its iconography elsewhere: a DC 18 Religion or History check names it Khlysty.

## Depth

### History

The Khlysty is a secret mystical flock threaded through Dravosi and Tessarine noble courts. Its shepherd heals heirs whose illnesses are past cure and binds them as unwitting phylactery threads, building toward an ascension beyond what a Hierarch can be. The Calveno cure is done, and a Tessarine heir rose from a bleeding sickness overnight. The grateful household guards the story, and the Flock's living anchors grew by one. Confirmed heresy has [[aleksander-malone|Aleksander Malone]] on the flock's trail with a port and a ship class in hand, though not yet the crew that brought the shepherd to Calveno. Faction records and mail trails place the Flock among those reading the [[pearl-of-souls|Pearl of Souls]] as a route to transcendence.

### Hidden truths

- Grigori's healing anchors a patient's blood to him. A DC 22 Arcana check detects the thread. Counterspell disrupts healing and binding alike.
- The healed heirs never learn what the cure anchored in them. The roll of living anchors is the shepherd's record alone.
- The Flock treats the [[pearl-of-souls|Pearl of Souls]] as a route to transcendence. Mail trails and faction records show the interest.

### Threads

The Flock drives the **Growing Flock** front. [[the-hound-of-god|The Hound of God]] and [[grigori-and-the-crown-hunt|Grigori and the Crown hunt]] close on it, and [[bring-the-pearl-of-souls-to-umberlee|Bring the Pearl of Souls to Umberlee]] holds what it waits for.
