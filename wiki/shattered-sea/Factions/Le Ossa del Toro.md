## At a glance

- **Goal.** To play loud to a crowd that moves. Placing has never been the point.
- **Next move.** Out-volume every stage at the [[Il Palio delle Voci Contese|Palio]] and let the surge carry.
- **Led by.** The orc and the tortle, the two of them and no one else.
- **Strength.** Drums and a massive horn, and a crowd that surges and compresses and occasionally knocks people into the canal.

> [!narration] Public face
> The horn hits first and the crowd hits back, shoulder into shoulder, surging against the barriers. The orc keeps time like a heartbeat and the tortle works the horn like a siege engine. Nobody in the front rank is standing still, and the people behind the barriers are shouting Le Ossa del Toro.

## Play

- **When met.** From the front rank of a crowd that will not stand still.
- **When opposed.** Third is the highest any Palio has put them, and placing was never the point.

## Depth

### History

No Palio has yet put them higher than third place, and the pair does not care.
