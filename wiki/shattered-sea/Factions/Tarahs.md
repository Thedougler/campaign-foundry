## At a glance

- **Goal.** Unknown, and Rattkin communities do not stay long enough to learn what the Tarahs want.
- **Next move.** No move is known.
- **Led by.** No name has surfaced.
- **Base.** At sea. Their ships put into ports the Rattkin trade.
- **Strength.** Ships, and a reputation that makes matriarchs move.

> [!narration] Public face
> Sailors discuss the Tarahs as they discuss weather, in someone else's lowered voice. A Rattkin matriarch counts their ships and calls off a family reunion because one put in at her port. Nobody volunteers what they do to the families they find. Crews who sight their sails report only the sighting. The Tarahs are out, they say.

## Play

- **When met.** The Party has met them only as the threat Nona watches for.
- **When opposed.** Nothing on record.
- **Offers.** Nothing on record.
- **Costs.** Nothing on record.
- **How to notice or interfere.** One reported ship of the Tarahs is enough for [[Nona Black-Jaw]] to assume the worst about a missing grandson.

## Depth

### History

[[Cobb]] reported a ship of the Tarahs in port while [[Perrin Black-Jaw]] was at sea and beyond sending word. [[Nona Black-Jaw]] called off the attacks her people were running, fearing the Tarahs had taken him, and she watches for their ships still. Rattkin in the area count the Tarahs a known threat.

### Threads

The Tarahs prompted one called-off attack in [[Perrin and Nona]], and Nona keeps watching.
