## At a glance

- **Offered by.** No quest giver is established.
- **Reward.** Protection, leverage or information from choosing Grigori's fate.
- **Deadline.** Before [[aleksander-malone|Aleksander Malone]] reaches [[shepherd-grigori|Shepherd Grigori]].
- **Done when.** The Party chooses and acts to protect Grigori, hand him over or withdraw.
- **Failed when.** Malone reaches Grigori before the Party chooses.
- **Advances.** Khlysty / the Flock and [[the-hound-of-god|The Hound of God]].

> [!narration] The offer
> Shepherd Grigori helped you, and now Aleksander Malone pursues him for the Dravosi Crown. Shield the healer or give him up, because if you walk away, the hunt goes on without you.

## Play

- **Leads.** Malone's public trail, Crown pronouncements, the recovery Calveno's servants whisper about and the recoveries like it still to surface, and the Party's direct knowledge of Grigori.
- **Opposition.** [[aleksander-malone|Aleksander Malone]], the [[dravosi-crown|Dravosi Crown]] and Grigori's hidden network.
- **Complications.** Protecting a helpful healer leaves blood-anchor infiltration active. Interference makes Malone trace the Party.
- **Payoff.** The Party gains Grigori's cooperation, Crown leverage, evidence or time to protect a targeted house.

## Depth

### Hidden truths

[[shepherd-grigori|Grigori]] leads the Flock and leaves dormant blood-threads in powerful bodies while healing them, building an army for ascension. Malone acts on his own authority after confirmed heresy and has the port and ship class, but not yet the crew. The Party transported Grigori to Calveno; Malone suspects the Party of it, though he still lacks confirmation.

The Party heard his request to wound [[corbin-knighton|Corbin Knighton]] without killing him and agreed to contact Grigori should the opportunity arise. They do not know his purpose is to bind House Knighton, and they know him only as an oddly powerful healer whom the Crown calls a demon in public.

The Calveno cure is done, and the Tessarine heir recovered as his newest anchor, bound without the household's knowledge. The grateful family could become a contact one day, and word of the Party's part in the voyage has yet to reach them. The trail resumes when a healed noble surfaces in front of the Party or when Grigori reaches for them again.

Grigori put the request to the Party in person when he appeared aboard the [[uncertainty|Uncertainty]] on the approach to Aruhe. He named [[corbin-knighton|Corbin Knighton]] "the man who was shepherding the dog around yesterday" and thanked the Party for the Calveno warning "from one pursued man to another". The terms came word for word. "I will reward you handsomely if you injure him," he said. "Do not kill him, do not break his mind, and leave him of sound mind, and let me know so that I may heal him." A spell scroll came next, its arcane script gibberish to everyone aboard but [[catarina-davirelli|Catarina Da'Virelli]], left in Delmar's keeping with instructions to send word once the wound was dealt. The reward, Grigori promised, would come "for dethroning or defanging one of the Dravosi's dogs". Delmar took the scroll, but no one pledged the deed. Grigori knew of "the two that are chasing you" and was sure there were "more in your order than two". The handshake that closed the visit was corpse-cold, and Grigori vanished with a small pop.
