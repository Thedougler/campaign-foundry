> [!narration] The offer
> What falls from a stem is yours to eat, while anything still rooted remains untouched. Pick living growth and the island marks you until dawn, then its creatures come for you. Learn that law, keep your hands off anything living, and the inland survivors may let you pass.

## Terms and leads

- **Offered by.** Aruhe survivors who know the island's rules, including Matteo Scola and the Calveno group.
- **Done when.** The Party gets to the inland survivors and can distinguish receiving from claiming.
- **Failed when.** The Party strips living growth or loses its marked members to responders, or the graves at Memorial Grove are destroyed.
- **Deadline.** Before the graves at Memorial Grove are destroyed or the island's response spreads beyond control.
- **Reward.** Safe passage, living knowledge of the island, and access to fallen fruit and materials without triggering a hunt.
- **Leads.** Follow the river and survivor signs. The Party can use fallen fruit, deadwood, loose stone and shed shell as safe supplies.
- **Opposition.** [[hinewai|Hinewai]]'s law, responders that find marked creatures within 60 feet, and anyone who wants the Death Bloom destroyed.
- **Complications.** Fighting back is allowed, but taking a responder's flesh is a claim. Open grass, still water, night travel and living plants each carry different risks. An alliance with Hinewai is an Angle: if she sees [[jean-claude-tabarnack|Jean-Claude]] as a Gold-caste messenger or otherwise non-hostile, he may draw out the army's lore. She can assist the Party against the Gold caste. Both oppose it. When her beasts cross the channel, they will kill every Grung on [[karath|Karath]] without distinction.
- **Payoff.** The Party learns to travel Aruhe without turning every resource into a hunt and gains the survivors' account of the island. The Quest advances [[drowned-maw-awakening|Drowned Maw Awakening]] by giving the Party a route and evidence in the sea's changing eastern frontier. Its rules are recorded in [[shattered-sea/Lore/taking-on-aruhe|Taking on Aruhe]].

## Hidden truths

- Hinewai hears every claim through the roots but leaves punishment to responders. The Party can learn this by watching the mark and the island's reaction.
- Fallen fruit is safe, and fighting back against an attacker is not a claim. Nature DC 12 recognises the tell when plants lean and insects stop calling.
- The law runs on roots spreading from the Death Bloom across the island. The Gold caste of Karath wants it destroyed, believing that would end Hinewai.
- The guardian's motive shows through three kinds of evidence. The survivors' accounts separate looking from taking. The Old Gardens still read as a planted refuge. At the grove, black flowers encircle two graves, where fauna bow in silence.
