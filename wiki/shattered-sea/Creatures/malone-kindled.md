> [!narration] First sight
> You face a high elf about ten feet tall. His arms hang too low, and his joints bend a beat after he moves. White-gold flames burn above him. His eyes are coals. Thin cracks run across his skin, bright with holy light, and six swords hang at his sides, their blades catching fire as he turns towards you. He speaks in a chorus.

## Tactics

Malone closes with four Burning Sword attacks, or uses Bayonet Barrage when several targets stand inside its Cone. The flames on the six blades warn the Party before he strikes. Step beyond the swords' 10-foot reach or attack from range. Anyone can scatter outside his 30-foot Cone, and a successful Dexterity save halves the barrage's damage. KNEEL begins with one word. Deafened creatures cannot hear it. Creatures beyond 120 feet are out of range, and a successful Wisdom save resists it. Hellfire Aura burns within 10 feet. Step outside the aura or have Fire Immunity. Necrotic damage stops his regeneration through his next turn. Three times a day, his Legendary Resistance can turn a failed save against a spell or magical effect into a success.

Step Through Fire moves him to a visible space within 30 feet that contains fire or Bright Light. Remove the fire or leave the bright area to deny that destination. His Psalm runs along a 60-foot line. Move clear of the line or succeed on its Dexterity save to halve its damage. If the unfinished mission no longer matters to him, or the Party completes or stops it, the conflict can end without killing him.

The Solar in the 2025 Monster Manual is the CR 21 celestial peer. Malone's swords, hellfire and regeneration give this design its distinct threats and the Party its answers.

## Hidden truths

Malone will die late in the Campaign. His god will return him as Kindled to finish the mission he had when he died. Which mission that is remains undecided. While it remains unfinished, the Party cannot keep him dead. They can finish or stop the mission, or make him question it. When the mission ends, the god leaves him and he falls to ash where he stands.

Malone and the god inside him possess one another. Sometimes his faith hauls the god's power around and points it where he judges. At other times the god surfaces and Malone disappears behind his eyes. The ten-foot body keeps the elf's face, but its stretched limbs and wrong-moving joints mark the cost. Each time the god surfaces, less of Malone comes back. His fatherly warmth goes first. He stops saying “God forgive me” last. The god spends him, and does not reward him.

This is Aleksander Malone's later form: [[aleksander-malone|Aleksander Malone]].
