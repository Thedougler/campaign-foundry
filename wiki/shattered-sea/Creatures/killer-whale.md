## Play

### Tactics

The [[outer-reach|Outer Reach]] record has a pack testing the rudder line of a wounded hull until the steering suffers.

## Depth

### Ecology

They follow wounded ships along the eastern road, from the chart edge to the water past [[keth-naar|Keth-Naar]]. Pods also work the cold water on both sides of the [[galewall|Galewall]] storm belt, where they follow lifeboats and debris for hours. Not always attacking. The circling is the part survivors describe as worse.
