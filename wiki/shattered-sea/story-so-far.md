## At a glance

- **Through.** [[Session 12 - Recap]], with the Party at level 6 and still fighting [[Talon Skarn]] on [[Aruhe]].
- **Arc position.** Rising. The Aruhe arc of Sessions 10 to 12 climbs towards [[Memorial Grove]], where [[Jean-Claude Tabarnack|Jean-Claude]] is owed to the island's keeper. The sea-wide pressures wait offshore with [[Uncertainty]].
- **Authority rung.** Lords. The Party overcame the authorities of the Surety's boarding crew and now contends with lords: [[Aleksander Malone]], [[Corbin Knighton]] with [[Rupert Knighton]]'s private fleet, and [[Talon Vantyrus]], whose hunter is Skarn. The false gods of the [[Gold Caste]] showed their hand for the first time on [[The Burnt Road]]. [[Umberlee]] waits above every rung.
- **Central question.** Which crew do these four become where every sea route has an owner, and whose authority will they accept?

## The story so far

### Sessions 0 to 2: the hold and the prize

[[Delmar Fisk|Delmar]]'s privateers stole the [[Pearl of Souls]] and lost all five ships over the [[Drowned Maw]], and four strangers met aboard the [[Saltwright]] ([[Session 0 - Recap]]). When [[Barnaby Rook]]'s Crown cutter boarded for inspection, the Party ambushed the boarders below decks and [[Geoffrey Draves]] changed sides. Jean-Claude recognised his sister's toxin in a Crown canister and told nobody ([[Session 1 - Recap]]). The Party took the cutter and Rook went into the water. They freed [[Ket]] from a cage and chose a prize crew, while the cook, [[Shepherd Grigori]], promised anyone freedom at the next port. [[Perrin Black-Jaw|Perrin]] became a Friend of the [[Passage]], and a storm closed the chapter with three blows from below ([[Session 2 - Recap]]).

### Sessions 3 to 8: Calveno

A whip-shark died under the hull, and a vision left Perrin the word "Grow". In Calveno the prize became [[Uncertainty]]. Perrin reported the [[Vestra]]'s loss to [[Nona Black-Jaw]] and accepted a favour without hearing its terms ([[Session 3 - Recap]]). Delmar confessed the Pearl theft, and [[Umberlee]] named the Pearl as her price. The crew burned a Grung powder boat and took [[Felix Aho]] two days before a festival bombing ([[Session 4 - Recap]]). With [[Catarina Da'Virelli]] the Party raided the sewer magazines, [[Ruma Delacroix]] surrendered, and a magazine went into the water ([[Session 5 - Recap]]). [[Solange Barret]]'s ritual cost two defenders their lives and brought the chamber ceiling down ([[Session 6 - Recap]]). [[Otar the Foul]] rose into [[Mercatura]], Delmar killed him with the [[Fleet Commanders Chair]], and [[Iacopo Fieschi]] signed the Party as Calveno's Defenders while hundreds were missing ([[Session 7 - Recap]]). Fieschi paid in credit and [[Casa Falier]]. Nona's list of the taken passed 314, Jean-Claude called it slaving, and the name [[Osset]] came up twice ([[Session 8 - Recap]]).

### Session 9: after the missing

Refitted, with Ruma aboard as bookkeeper and cook, Uncertainty ran east after the raiders. [[Crissdalynn Khinriss|Crissdalynn]] hauled Calveno captives out of a wreck, [[Nino]] among them, and learned that a hurricane had split the Grung fleet. The [[HCS Ordinance]] stopped them, and the inspection ran on lies. Delmar's cold admiral persona confessed under Malone's spell, Malone believed it, and the warship turned for Calveno to hunt Grigori. [[Master Kyzil]] sent Crissdalynn a message for her alone ([[Session 9 - Recap]]).

### Sessions 10 to 12: Aruhe

Grigori appeared in the galley and named Vantyrus as the hunter behind them. He left an errand: wound [[Corbin Knighton]] and leave him alive and sane. The follower ship burned at a night broadside, and the Party went ashore on Aruhe, an island that kills for anything taken from it ([[Session 10 - Recap]]). Inland they learned its law and bought [[Matteo Scola]] out of the otters' game. The survivors, they heard, obey a woman in the woods. Then Skarn stooped for the [[Fate Spinner]] ([[Session 11 - Recap]]). They drove Skarn off at 12 of his 314 hit points and rescued four Calveno survivors. They followed a road of compelled Grung bombers whose seals ordered them to find the graves at the grove. The island's keeper bound Jean-Claude to her rules. Perrin twice refused [[Auralis]]'s command to consume, before Skarn came back invisible ([[Session 12 - Recap]]).

## Pressures

- [[Bring the Pearl of Souls to Umberlee]] is the debt at the top of the ladder. It feeds [[What Sunk the Vestra]], since the [[Chain Council]] arranged the theft and the same water took both ships.
- [[Drowned Maw Awakening]] presses Perrin harder each Session through Auralis, and [[Take on Aruhe]] makes the island evidence of the sea's changing frontier. It meets the Pearl debt in the Maw's water.
- [[The Crown Inspection]] and [[The Hound of God]] carry Crown pursuit of the renamed prize. Delmar's lie sent Malone to Calveno after Grigori, which ties them to [[Grigori and the Crown hunt]] and to Grigori's errand against Knighton.
- [[Simone's Hunters]] run along the same Grung captive route that took Calveno's missing. The compelled bombers on the burnt road put the Gold caste behind that route.
- [[Rule of Two]] carries the hunt for the Fate Spinner through the Sentinel schism, with Vantyrus and Skarn closing on Crissdalynn.
- [[Perrin and Nona]] carries the Passage's rescue work, and the Aruhe survivors are the first living witnesses Nona can use.
- **Collision, a possibility.** The captive route, the Crown hunt for Grigori and Umberlee's price all lead back to Calveno and the Maw's water. Returning to Uncertainty with survivors aboard puts the Party where Nona, Malone and the debt can all reach it.

- **The scrap in Otar's rot**, Session 7, the Players. Nobody has read it.
- **Rupert Knighton's order**, Session 7, the Players. [[Rupert Knighton]] ordered an evacuation before the collapse, and nobody has asked who warned her.

- **Kyzil's message**, Session 9, Crissdalynn alone.
- **The axolotl**, Session 9, the Players. Delmar owes Catarina one for [[Lamarae's Fang]].
- **Grigori's errand**, Session 10, the Players. The instructions for wounding Knighton are written on a scroll, and Grigori has offered help with Delmar's curse.
- **The fruit's power**, Session 11, the Players. Perrin asked whether they are meant to use it at all.
- **The watcher's device**, Session 12, the Players. Someone fed Skarn advantage through a device like the Party's own.
- **The graves at the grove**, Session 12, the Players. The seals' orders and Jean-Claude's summons both lead to Memorial Grove.
- **The chair**, Session 7, the DM alone. Its secret is written on [[Fleet Commanders Chair]].
- **Auralis**, Backstory, the DM alone. What it is and what it seals are written on [[Auralis]].

## PC trajectories

- [[Perrin Black-Jaw]] chases the Vestra and the crew's survival. Auralis's demands and his Passage obligations pull against both. Twice he chose the crew over consuming, which points at a confrontation with his patron.
- [[Delmar Fisk]] keeps the crew together and free. The Pearl debt and Umberlee's judgement pull against him, and the admiral persona under Malone's spell showed a self he keeps buried. His path points at the Pearl choice.
- [[Crissdalynn Khinriss]] maps the sea and keeps pulling Delmar out of danger. The Fate Spinner made her a target, and Kyzil's message ties her to the schism. Her path points at Vantyrus.
- [[Jean-Claude Tabarnack]] protects fugitives and works to expose the captive route. His silence about the canister pulls against the crew's trust, and he now answers to the grove's keeper. His path points at the Gold caste behind his sister.

## Pacing

- Downward beats led Sessions 10 to 12. The Party fought Skarn twice and fought a Spiguar and a crown squid besides, while Jean-Claude went catatonic and then fell under the keeper's binding. The upward beats came from the island's gifts and the rescued survivors.
- Three Sessions on one island ran heavy on exploration and fights. Talk scenes were few: Grigori, Matteo and the keeper.
- Crissdalynn and Perrin held the spotlight. Delmar's own story and Jean-Claude's secret stayed still.
- [[The Canister]] has not moved since Session 1. The Pearl, the Crown Inspection, Simone's Hunters and the Vestra Thread have each sat still for three Sessions or more.
