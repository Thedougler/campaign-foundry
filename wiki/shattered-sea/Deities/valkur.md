## At a glance

- **Domains.** Sailors, ships, favourable winds and protection at sea.
- **Symbol.** A shield bearing a thundercloud with three descending lightning bolts.
- **Alignment.** Chaotic good.
- **Worshipped by.** Sailors who trust courage and seamanship first, and crews who mutter his name below decks.
- **Asks of followers.** Take the risk, keep faith with shipmates first and him second, and solve the difficulty rather than wait for rescue.

> [!narration] Invocation
> Pay the basin if you must, and keep a breath for the Captain of the Waves. Where she counts coin, he counts courage.

## Play

- **Boons.** Protection in weather another power sent, and help backing a crew's own plan that leaves the doing of it to them.
- **Costs.** Donations for temples and voyage blessings, and the expectation that a crew works its own difficulty before it prays.
- **Clergy and shrines.** Captains, officers and privateers who serve aboard ship and in port, aid sailors cast ashore and sponsor exploration. Ashore they wear white and blue with silver trim, and aboard ship they dress for their rank. Cutlasses and seawater holy symbols ride at their belts.
- **How it intervenes.** He stands between ships and the sea powers that would break them, and he answers crews that help themselves first.

## Depth

### Myth

A giant of a sailor wades ocean to the knee with a great shield on his arm, or takes the wheel of the flying galleon Windjammer. Below decks he is the quiet counterweight, invoked when the basin's price feels wrong and the weather turns against a paid passage.

### Rivals and allies

Sworn enemy of the Gods of Fury and of [[umberlee|Umberlee]] above all, whose collection he answers with a fair wind and a fighting chance. He looks to Selûne for navigation.

### Hidden truths

- Valkur-minded sailors hold that tribute is superstition and courage alone carries a ship through. The shrines keep ledgers of paid and wrecked hulls, and the clergy read them aloud when a deck sceptic speaks too loudly.
