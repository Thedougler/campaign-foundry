## Depth

### Myth

The Crown carves him blind. The figurehead of the [[hcs-ordinance|HCS Ordinance]] shows him robed and blindfolded, holding up gold scales, his face left bare. His faith counts him the one real god and names [[umberlee|Umberlee]] the sea bitch heathen, its claim spoken through its Hound. The office his inquisitor serves under is the Hound of Tyr. Sailors such as [[delmar-fisk|Delmar Fisk]] have heard of the title, and to them its holder is a fanatic let loose to deal with religious problems within the Dravosi Crown. The hunt he runs now is the one [[the-hound-of-god|The Hound of God]] follows.

### Rivals and allies

[[aleksander-malone|Aleksander Malone]] refused and returned the toll coin [[delmar-fisk|Delmar Fisk]] offered for the crossing. To his faith the sea's goddess is a heathen. The captain then persuaded the Hound that Tyr himself had guided his knowledge of [[shepherd-grigori|Shepherd Grigori]]'s trail, a claim that stayed the captain's.

### Hidden truths

- The leash on the Hound is his own. [[aleksander-malone|Aleksander Malone]] is a self-directed zealot of Tyr, and his prey is what he calls "perfects": anyone against Dravosi law. The Party can learn it by watching whom he answers when his faith and his orders disagree.
