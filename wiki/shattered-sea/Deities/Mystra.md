## At a glance

- **Domains.** Magic and the Weave.
- **Symbol.** A circle of seven stars.
- **Alignment.** Neutral, a keeper of the gift rather than a patron of causes.
- **Worshipped by.** Wizards, sorcerers and others who work the Weave, and the sages who study it.
- **Asks of followers.** Treat the Weave as a gift given in trust, and work it with care.

> [!narration] Invocation
> The Weave was given like a promise. Work it the way it was given, and it answers.

## Play

- **Boons.** A careful caster's spells answer as written.
- **Costs.** Magic wrenched loose rather than worked through the Weave calls her attention down on the caster.

## Depth

### Myth

Mystra keeps the Weave, the working that turns raw magic into the spells mortals cast. The gift came like a promise, and spell casters of every tradition take their magic through it. A caster answers for what the working takes.

### Hidden truths

- Hinewai, once a Weave-caster, cursed Mystra by name over her [[The Unnamed Companion|companion]]'s body on Aruhe's shore, after three castings left his chest still. The Party learns it only if Hinewai ever speaks of that night.
