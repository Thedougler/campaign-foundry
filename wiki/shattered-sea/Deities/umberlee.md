## At a glance

- **Domains.** Tempest, sea, wrecks and fear.
- **Symbol.** A wave curling left to right on harbour stones and prows.
- **Alignment.** She is the sea's claim rather than a moral patron.
- **Worshipped by.** Sailors, merchants, pirates, navies, smugglers and pilgrims.
- **Asks of followers.** Admit that she can take you by paying tribute before casting off.

> [!narration] Invocation
> Pay the basin before casting off because the sea does not care whether your purse is empty.

## Play

- **Boons.** A paid tribute is recorded as a blessing, and a shrine offers a sailor a place to acknowledge the sea. When [[shepherd-grigori|Shepherd Grigori]] named the Party's job a curse, [[perrin-black-jaw|Perrin Black-Jaw]] froze until [[delmar-fisk|Delmar Fisk]] checked a porthole. He said, “I’m paying his toll on the ship.”
- **Costs.** Coin, rope, fish bones, buttons, a compass needle, a carved token or a spoken promise. Refusal risks her attention. The payment is common knowledge on any quay: most people flick a coin over the side as they board any ship, and the amount is nebulous so long as something goes in. One rescued crewman of the raid took the tribute for a myth until the hurricane that shattered his hull. His words on deck: "You always gotta pay the sea bitch."
- **Clergy and shrines.** [[waveservants|Waveservants]] collect at the same rate in working harbours, with basins and booklets by the quay. A rescued sailor claimed [[calven-and-calveno|Calveno]] keeps a shrine for paying the sea toll. The black shrine on [[vel-orn|Vel-Orn]], [[umberlees-shrine|Umberlee's Shrine]], serves debts of an older kind.
- **How it intervenes.** She can sink a fleet on material-plane water and press captains into a mortal survivor. She speaks through her clergy when the debt needs a voice, and she sends a collection hull such as [[the-dead-lady|The Dead Lady]]. A stalled debtor finds the appointment rescheduled rather than a pursuit, and the deep water calls to him harder the next time he touches it. [[shepherd-grigori|Shepherd Grigori]] called her a god unworthy of worship in open water, and the sea beneath the [[uncertainty|Uncertainty]] kept its calm.

## Depth

### Myth

Umberlee is simply Umberlee at sea. People call her the Bitch Queen in anger and Queen of the Depths when avoiding the first name. She owns waves, storms, wrecks and the fear that makes a captain pay. Her claim is categorical over material-plane water. She never appears in person: storms, her [[waveservants|Waveservants]] and the [[the-dead-lady|Dead Lady]] carry her attention where she wants it.

She is tempestuous and petty, and her title came from her wrath and her greed. She deserves no worship. The gods and the sea stand as the ultimate authority over the Shattered Sea's rungs of power, and Umberlee is both at once.

### Rivals and allies

[[valkur|Valkur]] is a sailor's courage, [[tyr|Tyr]] is the land-and-paper counterweight, [[auralis|Auralis]] is an older Antheri machine rather than a rival, and the [[sentinels-of-the-eyrie|Sentinels of the Eyrie]] oppose Waveservant collection through observation. [[aleksander-malone|Aleksander Malone]] counted the sea's goddess a heathen beside his one god, and he threw the toll coin [[delmar-fisk|Delmar Fisk]] offered back across the deck.

### Hidden truths

- Umberlee's claim ends where trench water becomes elemental water at the [[drowned-maw|Drowned Maw]] fissure. She cannot reach into it to reclaim the [[pearl-of-souls|Pearl of Souls]].
- Tribute may be the procedural plug holding the fissure shut, though clergy do not advertise this suspicion. Her shrine ledgers and a reading of the Maw's pressure could confirm it.
- The Pearl remains a mortal recovery path after the theft from Vel-Orn, and the five captains ride [[delmar-fisk|Delmar Fisk]] while their crews remain inside it.
- An older shrine of hers stands on [[vel-orn|Vel-Orn]], cut into black stone: it marks debts and keeps drowned names, and the older worship of the [[blue-hole|Blue Hole]] is preserved there. [[keth-naar|Keth-Naar]] petitions her for terms there, and the shrine's protection of the [[sunken-crown|Sunken Crown]] fails without the [[pearl-of-souls|Pearl of Souls]].
- [[shepherd-grigori|Shepherd Grigori]] read the Pearl job's curse at a glance and named it “quite the curse” unasked. He offered help with it in future, calling himself “fairly familiar with matters of the divine, being a shepherd.”
