> [!narration] First sight
> A hundred feet of dark hull turns towards you beneath two slanting sails, pale patches showing through the canvas. She sits low. You can't see a Crown flag anywhere on her. As she draws alongside, water knocks inside the little boats lashed on deck, and her captain with white eyebrows turns the wheel above Ruk and the deckhands. They open the rail beside him. Then the hands lower a broad plank across to you, with a rope running at waist height along either side.

## Statistics

Gargantuan sailing ship, 100 feet long and 20 feet wide, with an 8-foot draught. Her slanting sails and pale repairs identify her beyond reading distance. She belongs to [[nona-black-jaw|Nona Black-Jaw]]'s [[black-jaw-run|Black-Jaw Run]] and serves the [[passage|Passage]]. Captain [[marta-venn|Marta Venn]] commands her.

| Armor Class | Hit Points | Speed | Damage Threshold |
| ----------- | ---------- | ----- | ---------------- |
| 15 | 300 | Sail 2 mph; tactical speed 30 feet | 15 |

Twenty crew are needed, and twenty are aboard, including Marta. She has room for twenty passengers besides the crew. [[ruk|Ruk]] occupies one passenger berth on the outward run. Cargo capacity is 75 tons after the rescue refit. Concealed berths count towards the twenty-passenger limit.

The hull's AC, HP, travel speed, damage threshold, crew and passenger limits use the 2024 [Sailing Ship](https://www.dndbeyond.com/equipment/480-sailing-ship). Dimensions, tactical speed, cargo reduction and component rules below are homebrew for this ship.

## Crew and stations

The nineteen hands besides Marta are rescue sailors without marine training. Use [[commoner|Commoner]] if one must defend themselves. They move passengers below and escape rather than hold a captured deck. Ruk travels as Nona's messenger, outside the sailing complement. His warnings and physical protection come from [[ruk|his NPC page]].

| Station | Aboard now | Minimum | Unattended |
| ------- | ---------- | ------- | ---------- |
| Helm | Marta | 1 | She keeps her last heading until the next turn, then drifts with wind and current. |
| Sails and lines | Twelve hands, six to each mast | 12 | With fewer than twelve, halve sailing speed; with fewer than six, the sails cannot drive her. |
| Lookout and sounding | One hand at the bow | 1 | Marta doesn't get warnings of shoals or approaching boats. |
| Pumps | Two hands below | 2 | Hull breaches keep flooding until someone returns. |
| Boats and boarding plank | Two hands | 2 | Nobody launches a boat or secures the boarding plank. |
| Galley and passenger care | Two hands | 2 | Hidden passengers lose their attendant and their quickest warning to move. |

Twenty is the working minimum for a rescue under sail. A reduced crew can keep moving under the penalties above, but leaves stations empty. The boat hands take the two tenders to shore when needed. The ship stays outside the reef until they return.

## Components and weapons

She is unarmed. Attacks target each component separately, and damage to a component does not also damage the hull. At hull 0 HP, she begins sinking and remains afloat for ten rounds. Two crew working the pumps extend that to twenty rounds, giving passengers time to leave. These are homebrew sinking rules.

| Component | Size | AC | HP | Damage threshold | At 0 HP |
| --------- | ---- | -- | -- | ---------------- | ------- |
| Hull | Gargantuan, 100 by 20 feet | 15 | 300 | 15 | Sinking begins. |
| Wheel and rudder | Large, 10-foot steering assembly | 15 | 50 | 0 | She cannot make controlled turns. Wind and current turn her from her last heading. |
| Sails and rigging | Gargantuan, two 40-foot masts | 12 | 100 | 0 | Sail speed becomes 0. Below 50 HP, halve sail speed. |
| Two tenders, each | Large, 10 by 5 feet | 11 | 50 | 0 | The tender sinks. |

Each unarmed tender takes one rower and three passengers and rows at 1.5 mph or 15 feet per round. Their figures and capacities are homebrew. Two shuttles by both boats can lift the nine survivors even if a litter reduces one boat's passenger capacity to two. A fifteen-foot boarding plank, four feet wide with rope handrails, serves a quay or another ship alongside.

## Errand, chase and boarding

**Current orders.** From her home berth at [[la-vasca|La Vasca]], Nona sent her to [[aruhe|Aruhe]] with Ruk as messenger and room to bring the [[calven-and-calveno|Calveno]] survivors home. Food, water and blankets fill the ordinary hold. Ruk is her only outward passenger. She waits off [[western-landing|Western Landing]] rather than abandon people because the Party is late. [[session-13-the-way-out|Session 13 - The Way Out]] determines whether the nine survivors board after Hinewai lets them leave, or remain inland while the ship waits.

**Meeting the Party.** Marta brings her alongside [[uncertainty|Uncertainty]] and lets Ruk make contact. She asks how many people need lifting. She sends the tenders through the reef gap only after a pilot or the Party shows the channel. A litter comes aboard before walking passengers. Nobody pays for a berth or signs away their freedom. Ruk gives Nona's news when conversation begins, after the Session 13 cut.

**Chase.** In each six-second round, the helmsman moves her up to her tactical speed and makes one turn of up to 90 degrees. With the sails properly crewed and intact, the helm can dash for another 30 feet instead of turning. These are homebrew manoeuvres. Her eight-foot draught prevents her following Uncertainty across four-foot shallows. To escape a pursuer, Marta favours a known channel and a reef between the hulls. She will not throw passengers or provisions overboard for speed. Damaged rigging or missing hands forces a choice between helping the sails and keeping the pumps or passenger exits staffed.

**Boarding.** The deck stands six feet above the waterline. Waist-high rails give Half Cover against attacks from the water. Grapples offer a climb. The broad plank offers a level route when the hulls lie alongside. There are no guns to seize. Boarders can reach the helm by either side of the main hatch, while the crew tries to keep them away from the galley and let the passengers leave by the stern.

**Stowing away.** Someone can enter through the stern hatch from a boat and crouch in the locker, then reach the galley without passing the hold. The passenger attendants check that route whenever they serve food. A stowaway who stays until the next meal must find another hiding place or meet the person bringing the bowls.

## Hidden truths

The commercial manifest lists twenty crew and no passengers. Marta keeps a separate headcount without names for rescue work. The ship's name is the crew's answer to anyone who notices the difference. No passenger is locked into a berth. The attendants demonstrate the inside release before anyone beds down.

The false bulkhead has three discoverable signs. Hammock-rope fibres cling to its lower edge. Air moves through the slatted cupboards, and an attendant takes more bowls below than the manifest accounts for. Marta shows the exits openly to people Ruk vouches for. A hostile search can expose the refuge, but a trusted visitor need not search at all.
