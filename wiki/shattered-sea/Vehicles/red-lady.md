## Crew and stations

Her stations stand empty. The storm drowned her crew with the fleet, and their souls are inside the [[pearl-of-souls|Pearl of Souls]].

## Components and weapons

Her armament went down unlisted. Below decks sits a Fleet Intelligence Room, and the crow's nest [[crissdalynn-khinriss|Crissdalynn]] pulled [[delmar-fisk|Delmar Fisk]] from still marks her rigging.

## Errand, chase and boarding

She does not sail. The way to her runs through the [[shelfworks|Shelfworks]] and down past the boundary of [[umberlee|Umberlee]]'s claim, and the question her last voyage left waits in [[what-sunk-the-vestra|What Sunk the Vestra]].

> [!narration] First sight
> The [[red-lady|Red Lady]] rests keel down in the clay of the [[shelfworks|Shelfworks]], past the line where the water stops belonging to Umberlee. Rigging still stands on her masts. The sea has opened her deck, and a crow's nest hangs above it. Spars and planks lie scattered on the shelf around her, and past her the ground falls away into the deep. The nearer you come to her hull, the stronger the feel of the dead in the water ahead. Her opened deck is the way in.

## History

She sailed with [[delmar-fisk|Delmar Fisk]]'s five when the fleet stole the [[pearl-of-souls|Pearl of Souls]] from Umberlee's shrine on Vel-Orn. Umberlee's storm sank the five together over the [[drowned-maw|Drowned Maw]], and the Red Lady went down with the Pearl aboard. Her wreck settled on the shelf's outer edge, below the boundary of Umberlee's claim, where the Pearl still collects what drowns near it. Crissdalynn brought Delmar up alive from her crow's nest while she went down. Umberlee's Dead Lady is a separate ghost-ship copy of this vessel.

## Hidden truths

- The Fleet Intelligence Room below her decks keeps the commission records in cipher, and the cipher hides the contractor's name.
- The [[pearl-of-souls|Pearl of Souls]] lies whole inside her, below the boundary where [[umberlee|Umberlee]]'s claim ends, and it goes on collecting.
- Whether the theft's arrangers meant her to sink beyond the claim is what her records may settle.
