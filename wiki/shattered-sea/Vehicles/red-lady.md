> [!narration] First sight
> The [[red-lady|Red Lady]] rests keel down in the clay of the [[shelfworks|Shelfworks]], past the line where the water stops belonging to Umberlee. Rigging still stands on her masts. ## Depth

### History

She sailed with [[delmar-fisk|Delmar Fisk]]'s five when the fleet stole the [[pearl-of-souls|Pearl of Souls]] from Umberlee's shrine on Vel-Orn. Umberlee's storm sank the five together over the [[drowned-maw|Drowned Maw]], and the Red Lady went down with the Pearl aboard. Her wreck settled on the shelf's outer edge, below the boundary of Umberlee's claim, where the Pearl still collects what drowns near it. Crissdalynn brought Delmar up alive from her crow's nest while she went down.

### Hidden truths

- The Fleet Intelligence Room below her decks keeps the commission records in cipher, and the cipher hides the contractor's name.
- The [[pearl-of-souls|Pearl of Souls]] lies whole inside her, below the boundary where [[umberlee|Umberlee]]'s claim ends, and it goes on collecting.
- Whether the theft's arrangers meant her to sink beyond the claim is what her records may settle.
