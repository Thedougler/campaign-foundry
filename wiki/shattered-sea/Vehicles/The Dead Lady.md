## Play

She works collection routes from harbour to harbour, sent where tribute has stopped and the debt has not. Her standing commission is the fleet's. Those captains ride [[Delmar Fisk]] while the [[Pearl of Souls]] remains beyond her mistress's reach at the [[Drowned Maw]], and the route holds open until the debt closes.

## Depth

### History

Umberlee pressed the five captains into the survivor of her striking, and the red wake has been seen since wherever payment stopped.

### Hidden truths

- Her wake runs red, and pilots read it as collection come to your water. Ask a pilot what the red wake precedes and watch him choose his words.
- Her original keel is a blank the clergy keep willingly. Sailors who put her name beside the [[Red Lady]]'s, the fleet hull that went down with the Pearl, hear the subject change at the shrine.
