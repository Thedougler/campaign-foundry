## Depth

### History

The Loud Argument sailed as one of [[delmar-fisk|Delmar Fisk]]'s five ships, alongside the [[red-lady|Red Lady]], the Narrow, Heft and the [[fernen|Fernen]], that stole the Pearl of Souls from Umberlee's [[umberlees-shrine|shrine]] on [[vel-orn|Vel-Orn]]. Umberlee's blow sent the whole fleet down over the Drowned Maw, and Delmar lived through it.
