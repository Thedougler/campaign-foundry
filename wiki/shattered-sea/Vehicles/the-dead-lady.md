## Crew and stations

The Pressed are people from emptied boats under the fiends' control. Each boat adds hands to the Dead Lady's deck. The fleet's own crews remain souls inside the Pearl. A few fiends steer from the wheel and brace at the rails with their oars; the Pressed haul lines with charmed, empty faces.
Umberlee hires [[merrenoloth|merrenoloth]] fiends, ferry pilots who steer ships anywhere for pay, rather than sail the Lady herself. She pays them from the tribute she collects.

## Errand, chase and boarding

She works collection routes from harbour to harbour, sent where tribute has stopped and the debt remains. The fleet's standing commission is hers. The ship takes the right lines and the right red from Umberlee's own water. The fiends steer her, and the Pressed haul lines. A fight aboard pits the Party against innocent Pressed and guilty fiends.

## History

Umberlee pressed the five captains into the survivor of her striking. The Red Lady was Delmar Fisk's old ship, sunk with her crew and the Pearl of Souls inside. Umberlee made the Dead Lady as a ghost-ship copy to taunt Delmar until his debt is repaid. The copy has the Red Lady's lines and colour, but none of the ship's crew or soul. The original wreck remains keel-down in the Shelfworks clay, past the line where the water stops belonging to Umberlee. She cannot raise it. The red wake has been seen since wherever payment stopped.

## Hidden truths

- Her wake runs red, and pilots read it as collection come to your water. Ask a pilot what the red wake precedes and watch him choose his words.
- The Dead Lady is Umberlee's copy of the Red Lady, remade from the goddess's own water. The original keel rests with the Pearl of Souls inside it, beyond her reach in the Shelfworks.
