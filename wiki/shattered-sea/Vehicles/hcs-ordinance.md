## Crew and stations

The Ordinance needs 50 crew and carries 240 at full complement. An Admiral's staff commands the ship, and the Crown has not named an individual captain. The Admiralty Court, Registry Vault and Gunner's Magazine are staffed facilities.

## Components and weapons

Her armament is 84 × 24-lb long cannon and 12 × 12-lb chasers, 96 mounts across the gun decks. The Admiralty Court issues Crown legal instruments. The Registry Vault produces or detects certificates (Investigation DC 18), while the Magazine prepares grapeshot, chain shot or alchemical incendiary orders.

## Errand, chase and boarding

The Ordinance is the Crown's court on the water, hearing cases and making rulings. It does not patrol like a cutter. Its name ends pirate gangs for miles around. She is berthed at Harwick Naval Yard and operates in Crown waters. Her hold takes 160 tons, and she typically impounds cargo in transit.

> Gun decks rise in three rows above a grey hull ahead of you. A white stripe and the Crown flag mark her as a floating court, with blindfolded Tyr at her bow. Metal gleams at every corner as she moves slowly because she does not need speed.

## History

Built at Harwick as the biggest Crown ship in the Shattered Sea, the Ordinance turned naval force into visible court authority.

### Session 9: the intercept and the turned inspection

The Crown sent the Ordinance out under [[rupert-knighton|Rupert Knighton]]'s private fleet after the Calveno incident. She is about twice the [[uncertainty|Uncertainty]]'s length and slower at fifty miles a day. She took a shoal for cover and turned onto an intercept under Dravosi colours, then flew signal flags whose intent read as "pull over", the call to heave to. [[aleksander-malone|Aleksander Malone]] came across to hunt [[shepherd-grigori|Shepherd Grigori]], and the inspection ended with [[corbin-knighton|Corbin Knighton]] drawing a flintlock on the inquisitor and overriding him. The Ordinance unfurled its sails and turned back for Calveno with its hunters. There Malone broke the limbs of men who resisted and threw some overboard. The remaining Dravosi sailors complied quickly with his order to make sail at once, and the ship left under his command.

## Hidden truths

The source calls the HCS Sovereign the definitive Tier 4 flagship. The Ordinance is the secondary vessel. Whether it is refitted or renamed is not established.
