## Depth

### History

The Fernen sailed as one of [[delmar-fisk|Delmar Fisk]]'s five ships, alongside the [[red-lady|Red Lady]], the Narrow, Heft and Loud Argument, that stole the Pearl of Souls from Umberlee's [[umberlees-shrine|shrine]] on [[vel-orn|Vel-Orn]]. Umberlee struck the fleet down over the Drowned Maw, and Delmar came out of the wreck alive.
