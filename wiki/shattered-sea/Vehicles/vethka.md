## At a glance

- **Kind.** Grung raiding proa, wrecked.
- **Size.** Not established beyond an outrigger hull.
- **Speed.** None. She will not put to sea again.
- **Crew.** Grung raiders and Calveno captives when she sailed. [[sandro|Sandro]] and [[nino|Nino]] sheltered in her hull until the Session 10 rescue.
- **Captain.** The raid's command went unnamed.
- **Berth.** Broken above the tideline on [[western-landing|Western Landing]]'s beach, [[aruhe|Aruhe]].

> [!narration] First sight
> A dark proa hull lies on its side above the tideline, outrigger snapped and ribs hung with wet cloth, riding so low that her deck rail is barely shoulder-high. She is empty now, her fire burned down to white ash in her shade, the castaways' water jars still in their row where they kept them. The only way aboard is the gap where her stern lifted clear of the sand.

### Crew and stations

The raid's Grung crew drowned with her or died ashore within hours of landfall, taken by the island's corrupted fauna. Her living complement is the two captives who came ashore, Sandro and Nino.

### Components and weapons

The snapped outrigger lies beside her. Water jars, a fire ring and whatever the raid left make up her fit.

### Underway

She sails no more. Her work now is shelter, and while she holds, the beach keeps its one defensible place.

## Depth

### History

#### Session 9: torn open two days out of Calveno

The storm tore her hull open on the reef two days out of [[calven-and-calveno|Calveno]], and she lay wedged against the coral. Her captives huddled beneath the broken hull, and two purple Grung guards came ashore with them and held the beach against the island's [[wolfrabbit|wolfrabbits]]. The tide erased the drag marks across the sand.

The storm of the Calveno raid put the Vethka proas on Aruhe's reef and beach line. This hull came to rest above the tideline with her outrigger gone, and three captives rode a broken outrigger strut ashore. Sandro, Nino, and [[tomo|Tomo]] reached the beach, and the first night took Tomo. The other two held her shade from that night until the Session 10 rescue, their signal fire marking the gap.

### Hidden truths

- The hull is failing. When she goes, the beach loses its last shelter.
- She marks the raid's far end for [[nona-black-jaw|Nona Black-Jaw]], who is looking for a dark hull.
