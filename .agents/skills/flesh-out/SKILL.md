# Flesh out

An **elaboration** pass on one existing page. The page's kind, template, rules and part in play stay as they are. Its job is **presence**, which means the subject exists before the Party arrives, while the Party is there and after it leaves. It writes DM-facing facts into the page's own sections, and `theatre-of-the-mind` later selects what the table hears from them. It succeeds when the richer page is easier to run and its Narration can be drafted without inventing World state.

- **Signature:** the smallest image, behaviour, relationship, contradiction, physical law or recurring pattern that identifies the subject.
- **Anchors:** the few facts that support the signature, each doing a different job.
- **Layers:** the order in which the table meets it, from first sight to what their meddling changes.

Give every added fact a use at the table. It sets up a choice or a Clue, or it supplies a reaction or a callback. Prefer evidence over labels, the specific over the dense, motion over portrait, cause over decoration, and interaction over spectacle.

## Restraint

Flesh out only the facts the DM would not improvise comfortably. Treat the result as a toolbox. Its signature and anchors, with their reactions and layers, lift into any situation. The DM recovers the table-facing path from the page in under 30 seconds. Plot and a full lore history belong to their owner skills, as does any fixed sequence of events. Stock one striking, supported image and a visible motion when they help the table act. Keep the rest sparse. Sparse, flexible facts let the DM wait on the Players while the World remains clear to them.

## Steps

### 1. Bound the truth

The caller is the DM, or any task that met the page thin or generic. Reuse a caller's completed orientation. Otherwise orient as root `AGENTS.md` **Orient** sets out. Read the subject's page, its template in `wiki/templates/`, and the linked pages needed to understand it. Search QMD and the Backup (`.omp/AGENTS.md` § Wiki access) for its name and aliases, and for the people, places, Factions, Items, Threads and Lore already tied to it, and read each tied page. Note its kind, its established look, behaviour, rules, relationships and history, its part in play and any intended feeling.

Canon precedence follows `CONTEXT.md` **Canon**, with the DM first and the Wiki before Raw. Narration heard at the table and played Session records stay as written. **Weave** first, building new detail from the tied pages. Invent only in the space they and the rest of Canon leave open, reusing material in the order root `AGENTS.md` **Sourcing** sets. Each new fact that conflicts with no Canon goes straight onto the page, decided as Canon (ADR 0003), and the reply lists it. Read the page's `revealed` property (`CONTEXT.md` **Revealed**). A revealed page takes additive detail only. On an unrevealed page, a draft fact that is itself what reads generic may be replaced outright, and the reply lists each replacement beside the fact it replaced.

When the page is missing, or its structure fails its owner skill's Done criteria, run the owner first and return here for presence:

| Need | Owner |
| --- | --- |
| Region, Settlement or ordinary Site | `location-design` |
| Site explored area by area | `dungeon-design` |
| NPC drives, secret, voice and first meeting | `npc-design` |
| Creature statistics, signature and ecology | `creature-design` |
| Item rules and identity | `item-design` |
| Vehicle statistics, crew and decks | `vehicle-design` |
| Faction engine, faces and agenda | `faction-design` |
| Lore truth, accounts and Clues | `lore-design` |
| A Scene's job and stakes | `hook-scene`, `development-scene`, `cliffhanger-scene`, `climax-scene` or `resolution-scene` |

A Deity has no design skill. Its template gives the structure, and this pass is its craft.

**Done when** the fixed facts, the tied pages, the open space, the owner and the intended effect at the table are known.

### 2. Diagnose the flat

Spend detail only on the two to four weakest of these:

1. **Image:** what can the Players picture at once?
2. **Identity:** what makes this unmistakably itself rather than its category?
3. **Life:** what is it doing, changing, maintaining, wanting, suffering or causing?
4. **Contact:** what happens when the PCs approach, touch, question, disturb, follow, threaten, help or study it?
5. **Depth:** what shows only under closer attention?
6. **Connection:** what does it reveal about the wider World?
7. **Emotion:** which facts create the intended feeling without naming it?

**Done when** the pass has named deficiencies in place of a general mandate to add detail.

### 3. Find the signature

Choose one dominant fingerprint, then anchors that differ in job:

| Scale | Budget |
| --- | --- |
| Minor | one signature, one or two anchors |
| Supporting | one signature, two to four anchors, at least one interaction or reaction |
| Major | one signature, three to five anchors, layers, discoverables and ongoing motion |

A useful set mixes one fact perceptible at once, one behaviour or process, one physical or social consequence, one invitation to interact and one hint of something larger. Use a sense where it sharpens the picture: temperature, texture, vibration, pressure, smell, echo, weight, taste and balance often beat sight. A Faction's or other force's signature is its motif, per [narrative-devices.md § Motifs](../../../docs/agents/narrative-devices.md#motifs). Run the swap test with a neighbour's or rival's name, and sharpen every line that is still true under it.

**Done when** the identity would survive aggressive trimming.

### 4. Make it lived-in

Ask which forces have been acting on the subject, and keep only the strongest answers:

- **Traces:** wear, repairs, stains, shortcuts, missing pieces, polished surfaces, scars, nests, habitual arrangements, graffiti, improvised fixes, offerings, bite marks, paperwork, erosion.
- **Routines:** what happens when nobody important is watching. Who sweeps, what the beast does between fights, what the noble grumbles about at breakfast, where the wet boots go, what the guard actually watches for.
- **Pressures:** hunger, weather, debt, fear, upkeep, status, rivalry, scarcity, migration, illness, orders, secrecy, grief, ambition. State what makes the current state unstable.
- **History made physical:** a repaired gate says there was an attack. Three sizes of handprint say a family stayed. New locks on old doors say trust failed recently.
- **Connections:** each detail points to a cause. Weave it first from the tied pages step 1 read, asking who supplies it, who it owes, who wants it and what it echoes elsewhere in the World. Food points to supply and fashion to aspiration. Damage points to whoever caused it, ritual to belief, neglect to priorities and scars to rivals.

Optional colour stays uncommitted, as [narrative-devices.md § Planted details and connection](../../../docs/agents/narrative-devices.md#planted-details-and-connection) sets out. An essential Clue belongs to the owner skill's three-route rule.

**Done when** the subject is embedded in causes, relationships and ongoing processes rather than placed on an empty stage.

### 5. Layer contact

Prepare responses in layers and leave the method to play:

| Layer | Question |
| --- | --- |
| **Surface** | What is obvious at once, enough for a first picture? |
| **Attention** | What shows when a Player looks, listens, talks, follows, compares, smells, remembers or asks a sensible question? |
| **Contact** | What happens when they handle it? Objects resist, ring, stain, unfold, jam, warm, leak or draw notice. People deflect, bargain, remember, posture, misread, soften or retaliate. Creatures stalk, retreat, bluff, circle, guard, feed, signal or use the ground. |
| **Disturbance** | What changes because they interfered? A moved object leaves evidence, an insult changes later behaviour, a broken ward changes the room, a frightened animal warns something else, a humiliation becomes gossip. |

**Done when** at least two plausible engagements each produce a concrete response from the World.

### 6. Tune the register

When the page means to produce a feeling, read that heading in [references/registers.md](references/registers.md) and build the facts it lists. Emotion is an effect of facts. The page states the causes and leaves the Players' feelings to them.

**Done when** every fact the loaded register requires is on the page and no sentence tells the Players what to feel, or no register applies.

### 7. Compress and stock

Write into the page's existing sections, following each section's `%%` guidance in its template, and keep good existing prose. Each fact is written once, in one section. The page gains facts and keeps its headings. Prefer short concrete bullets, verbs, distinctive nouns, observable behaviour, interaction cues and consequential details. Write every fact in the World's own terms, per root `AGENTS.md` **In-world voice**.

Leave more **prose stock** than the page's Narration slot will speak, so `theatre-of-the-mind` can select. The slot's Build in [recipes.md § First meetings on World pages](../theatre-of-the-mind/references/recipes.md#first-meetings-on-world-pages) names what the stock must cover for this kind. The stock holds:

- one signature image;
- Surface facts perceptible at a glance;
- at least two facts for senses beyond sight;
- one thing the table can touch or use;
- the Attention, Contact and Disturbance layers.

Sort each added fact by what the characters can sense or know, the split behind [theatre-of-the-mind § Hard lines](../theatre-of-the-mind/SKILL.md#hard-lines) rule 2. A perceivable fact is one they can sense, and a knowable fact is one they already know or can learn by asking. Both go in `At a glance` and `Play`. A hidden fact is neither, and it goes in `Depth` with how the Party can learn it. A requested look or illustration goes to `generate-image`.

**Done when** every added fact is in its page section and `theatre-of-the-mind` could draft the page's slot from the surface layer's facts without inventing World state.

### 8. Presence test

Test every page this pass changed, and stop adding once every applicable test passes. Detail beyond that point is sediment.

| Test | On a fail |
| --- | --- |
| **Distinct:** swap the name for a generic tavern, noble, forest or beast, and much is lost | sharpen the signature |
| **Concrete:** the DM can picture or perform the important details | replace abstractions with evidence |
| **Active:** something happens without the PCs | add behaviour, process, pressure or change |
| **Interactive:** the Players can investigate or interfere | add responses or things to use |
| **Layered:** closer engagement reveals more than the first look | add discoverable depth |
| **Causal:** details imply makers, users, history, ecology, relationships or consequences | cut disconnected decoration |
| **Emotional:** the feeling arises from what is happening | strengthen causes and cut mood instructions |
| **Memorable:** one fingerprint is ready for a callback | strengthen the signature |
| **Runnable:** a glance says what to stress and how it reacts | compress until it does |
| **Faithful:** the page keeps Canon and the owner's rules, and the Players keep their choices | correct the overreach |
| **Speakable:** the surface layer's facts can become Narration without new invention | add the missing signature, sense or thing to touch |

**Done when** every applicable test passes on every changed page.

### 9. Check and log

Check each changed page per root `AGENTS.md` **Gate scope** and rewrite the text behind every finding. Then close per `skill://lint` § Commands with every changed page, `--op create` and `--title "Flesh out <Page>"`: run standalone, this pass logs once. Run inside another skill, this pass leaves the log entry to the enclosing operation. When spoken Narration is also part of the work, this pass ends here and `theatre-of-the-mind` continues from the stocked page.

**Done when** the page gate reports `ok: 0 findings` on every changed page, and the reply lists the touched paths, each fact decided as Canon and the gate result.
