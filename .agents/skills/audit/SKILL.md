name: audit

Runs in a fresh subagent, dispatched after every Ingest over the pages it touched and their linked neighbours, when the DM asks to audit or tidy — named pages, a named claim, or the whole Wiki only on that ask — and whenever one page is found saying what another contradicts.

# Audit

The gate checks each page alone. Audit checks that the pages agree with each other and with what has happened since, and fixes what it finds by Canon precedence, never by asking the DM (ADR 0003). An answer is marked **contradiction**, **stale** or **duplicate** when it is a finding, and **current** (left as written) when it is none of them.

## Steps

1. **Scope.** One of four runs:
   - **An Ingest's pages.** The dispatch an Ingest requests, or a DM request for one ("audit the pages the last Ingest touched"). The active Campaign folder's `log.md` lists them as the `- [[slug|Title]]` bullets under that Ingest's `## [date] ingest | <file>` entry.
   - **The DM's pages.** The pages the DM lists.
   - **The DM's claim.** The DM states one claim or question ("the claim about who holds this office"). The run compares that question's answers only, never every claim on the pages it reads.
   - **The whole Wiki.** Every page in the active Campaign folder and the shared top-level kind folders, run only on the DM's request for the whole Wiki.

   A page run adds its neighbours to the list: every page a listed page links to, and every page that links to a listed page (a `[[<slug>` search across the vault finds the inbound ones). For a claim run the scope is the question, answered wherever the Wiki states it (Compare), and no page list grows. Write the page list down and state the slice in words: the Ingest entry's title, "the pages the DM listed, plus neighbours", or the claim. The report uses that wording. The slice bounds which subjects get audited, never which pages a fix may touch (Fix). Done when the list is written and the slice is stated.
2. **Claims.** Read every page in scope, records included, and list each claim that can go stale or conflict, with its page and line. A claim run lists one claim. What counts: `summary` first, then the At a glance lines (**Held by.**, **Ruled by.**, **Found at.**, **Occupants.**, **Prize.**, status), Play facts, rumours told as current truth, Depth (history and hidden truths), and sentences inside a `[!narration]` callout. Each line that says something is true now about a subject counts, whether it refers to the subject by link or in plain words.

   Records are evidence and stay outside every edit. They are each Session's Recap and Previously On, plus the Prep and Scene pages of a played Session (played once its Recap exists). A Prep line about where a thing is gives the Scene's purpose at planning time, never the current truth. `index.md` and `log.md` belong to their tools (`skill://lint` § Commands). `hot.md` is orientation and never evidence for a finding. Fix restates each of its lines a decision overturns. Done when every page in scope has yielded its claims.
3. **Compare.** Work one subject at a time (an NPC, Location, Item, Faction, Creature, Thread, Quest) and one question at a time. The unit of comparison is the **question** a claim answers. Questions include where someone or something is, who holds or rules this, whether someone is alive, who has this thing, what an allegiance or a status is, who leads or serves whom, when, and how many. Two present-tense answers to the same question are one comparison. Gather every answer past the slice by search. Search QMD by meaning (`.omp/AGENTS.md` § Wiki access). Find links with the `grep` tool (pattern `\[\[<name>`, path `wiki/`), and plain-text mentions of the subject's name, its variants and the question's own phrases with the same tool (pattern `<name>|<variant>`, path `wiki/`). The plain-text search matters because a page can state an answer without linking its subject, or without mentioning it by name at all. Searching replaces reading the Wiki page by page. The subject's own page is one answer, and so is every mention. A `summary` line, a rumour and a hidden truth are all answers. Set the answers against each other and against the latest record that touches the question: the newest Recap, Previously On or archived Transcript. The DM's own words count as a record too. Compare questions without a touching record too. Their answers stand against each other, and the only possible finding there is a contradiction. Mark every answer:
   - **Current.** No finding against it.
   - **Stale.** A present-tense answer a later event overtook. An event differs from a dispute: a Recap recording an NPC's death makes "is alive" stale. Decide staleness per proposition, never per sentence, by setting the fact's meaning and time against the event and marking stale only where the evidence entails the claim is no longer true, while each fact the event leaves true stays independent with its meaning and tense. Current state and explicit history are told apart by meaning alone, never by tense, keyword or a cited record. A historical claim is never stale, and a present claim is not history because it cites one.
   - **Contradiction.** Answers to one question that cannot both be true now, where no later event overtakes either one.
   - **Duplicate.** A pair of pages for one thing under different names. The sign is a page whose subject fits inside another's. Signs include a shared kind with a shared role or place, a shared Creature link, and one name being a variant or nickname of the other.

   Done when every present-tense answer to every question the run raised has a mark. Search finds the answers. The marks decide done.
4. **Decide** each finding by Canon precedence:
   - What the DM says, to the Agent or at the table as a Transcript records it, takes precedence over the Wiki. The Wiki takes precedence over Raw.
   - The latest event is the current truth. The past stays as history.
   - Between Wiki answers with no event to decide them, rank by how the answer is stated, regardless of which page the run started from. Find the fact's subject, the page about the thing the fact describes. That page states the fact **directly**, as an NPC's own **Role** does for an office or an Item's own **Held by** does for custody. Any other page that states it gives a **mention**. One line can be both. A Settlement's **Ruled by** is direct about who rules the Settlement, and it is a mention of the office that the office-holder's own **Role** states directly. Direct takes precedence over mention, whichever page the DM listed. Between answers of the same rank, the answer backed by a `sources` trail or a later log entry is kept. When rank and evidence leave a tie (two equally direct answers included), restate each answer as an attributed, conflicting claim using only what the answers say. The pages then record that the question is open and who claims what. One log entry states the question and both answers.

   Done when every finding has its outcome: a kept answer with its reason and the pages that will change, or a tie restated as conflicting claims with its log entry.
5. **Fix.** Rewrite a stale or overruled answer wherever a page states it, pages outside the slice included. The kept answer's own page stays as it is unless it is itself stale. Change only the proposition the finding is about: restate it to the evidence, and keep the overtaken fact as history in the past tense. Surrounding words may shift where grammar requires. Independent facts keep their meaning. The rewrite uses only facts the deciding evidence already states. A claim stated in `summary` is fixed there too. A fact told on two pages stays on the subject's own page, and the other page links to it. A page with nothing to change stays byte-for-byte.
   - **Duplicate:** merge into the page more links point to (count them). Keep every fact from both. The absorbed page's history and hidden truths go into the kept page's template sections along with its Creature link, and the kept page's Narration stays as written. Retarget every link to the absorbed page, then delete it without a redirect stub.
   - **Narration:** a stale answer inside a `[!narration]` callout is still an answer. Hand `theatre-of-the-mind` the one callout whose words state the now-false claim, and only those words. Leave a first look that does not state it as written.

   Done when every decided change is on its pages and each overtaken fact remains as history.
6. **Close.** Make a final sweep before writing any log entry. For every decided question, search again as Compare gathered and reread every page an answer was found on. Any answer the decision retired or left unresolved that still reads as current truth goes back to Fix. Then log one entry per finding with `--op audit` and `--title "<what changed> (<why>)"`, reading like `--title "Tie restated as conflicting claims (nothing settles it)"` (`skill://lint` § Commands). Each finding gets its own entry. A merge's `--page` flags list the kept page and the retargeted pages, leaving out the deleted page. Then close per `skill://lint` § Commands with every page this run edited, retargeted pages included. Done when the final claim sweep, each finding's log entry and the close are complete.
7. **Report.** One short line per subject fixed, and one per gate rule you suspect matched wrongly, with its rule and sentence. A slice run states its slice (the Ingest entry, the DM's pages, or the claim), never "the whole Wiki". The report states results only and leaves the DM nothing to answer.

## Done

- Every question the run raised (for a claim run, that question alone) had each of its present-tense answers marked current, stale, contradicted or duplicate. Findings were decided by precedence: each overruled answer fixed, or a tie restated as conflicting claims and logged. Records and untouched pages are byte-for-byte apart from gate repairs.
- Each finding has its own `audit` log entry saying what changed and why; the past stayed as history, every fact the evidence leaves true survived, and nothing was invented past the evidence.
- The close is complete (`skill://lint` § Commands) over every page this run edited, and the report asked the DM nothing.
