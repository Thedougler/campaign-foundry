# Resolution Scene

The Resolution is the short Scene after the Climax where the World shows what changed. It lets the result land and points at the next Session. Follow `docs/agents/scene-pages.md`, with this craft.

## Craft

- **One branch per Climax outcome.** The Resolution can't know how the Climax went, so Play holds a short branch for each of the Climax's outcomes: what the World shows, who reacts and how, and the cost still showing.
- **Show, briefly.** Five to fifteen minutes at the table: the consequence, people reacting through what they do, and room for each PC to react as their player chooses.
- **Threads move.** `## Threads` says what each Thread now stands at, which the next Ingest will carry to the Thread pages.
- **A seed.** Each branch ends on something that pulls toward the next Session: an arrival, a sign, a new question, grown from what the Party did.

## Cards

| Card | The ending |
| --- | --- |
| Happy Ending | the Party won, and the World is visibly better for it |
| Villain Escapes | the opposition got away, and the sign of where it went |
| Villain Surrenders | the opposition yields, on terms |
| Heroes Captured | the Party lost and wakes in someone else's hands |
| Heroes Escape | the Party got out, but not with everything |
| Greater Threat | the win reveals something worse behind it |
| Ending Cliffhanger | play stops on a new danger, cut before anyone acts |
