# Resolution Scene

A Resolution lets the Climax's result land. Its scale follows the Session's stakes. A personal choice changes a relationship, and a Faction's defeat changes who holds power. The Players should be able to name what is different and choose what they want next.

The shared procedure is [`docs/agents/scene-pages.md`](../../../docs/agents/scene-pages.md). Each step below references the section of it that applies. Chart assembly and the next Session's Prep belong to `prep-session`.

## Steps

1. **Orient and ground.** Read `docs/agents/scene-pages.md` whole, then apply its **Orient** and **Ground**. The template is `wiki/templates/Scene - Resolution.md`. **Done when** both sections' Done criteria hold.
2. **Trace the aftermath.** Read the Climax's `## Outcomes` rows and the entry state each row hands over, alongside the Session's opening question, involved Threads, PC Spotlights and earlier costs. For each reachable outcome, identify what was achieved, lost or left unsettled, who holds the relevant people and Items, where the Party ends up and which pressures remain live. Use the actual chart's Minutes allocation and plan a short closing play, normally ten to twenty-five minutes. **Done when** every Climax exit has a supported Resolution entry and its consequences follow from choices or events already established, the exits where the Party lost included.
3. **Choose endings from play.** Pick a card from **Catalogue** below for each materially different entry, recorded as scene-pages **Card** directs. The row's card expresses its intended payoff, not a guaranteed result. Preserve what the Party achieved. A death, a blocked escape route, a spent resource and an accepted bargain each remain in effect. An apparent reversal needs a specific earlier source for its means and a condition under which those means still work. **Done when** every branch has a card that fits its entry and no escape, rescue, capture or revelation requires overruling a resolved action.
4. **Resolve the cast.** Apply scene-pages **Cast**. **Done when** its Done criterion holds.
5. **Pay off at the table.** Start the page as scene-pages **Page format** directs. Under each `###` of `## Play`, write one bullet per branch, led by its Climax outcome in bold, such as `- **Skarn escaped with the Spinner.**`. Branches may share a bullet only where their visible facts, costs and choices agree.
   - `### Payoffs`: the answer to the Session's opening question, the concrete World change and the cost still visible.
   - `### Reactions`: each relevant NPC's or Faction's response as an action or offer, and its effect. Give each PC an opportunity to answer a goal, bond or Spotlight as their Player chooses.
   - `### Rewards`: write a row only for a branch that grants something. Give the amounts, linked Items and the grantor. State the practical effect of favours, titles or access, and advancement under the Campaign's actual practice where applicable. State availability and any remaining choice, such as accepting terms or dividing a reward.
   - `### Stinger`, only for a grounded next pressure, sign or arrival, built per `docs/agents/narrative-devices.md` § Closing stinger.
   - `## At a glance`: what is true now, the cost, where, and who reacts first, by branch where they differ.

   **Done when** each branch contains its actual payoffs, persisting losses, playable responses and precise rewards, and the Players retain control of their reactions and decisions.
6. **Leave a usable state.** In `## Threads`, link every Thread the Session touched and state, by outcome, whether it resolves, remains open or changes purpose, together with the concrete reason and remaining pressure. Record the next Session's starting Location, knowledge, ownership, conditions, resources and clock state wherever the branch changes them. Give the Party accessible next choices and their immediate consequences. Allow plain closure where nothing calls for pursuit. Write these as conditional Prep records and leave current Thread and World pages as they are. **Done when** the next planner can reconstruct each branch's aftermath from the page alone and tell the played branch from the unplayed alternatives.
7. **Closing image.** Write the `Closing image` with scene-pages **Narration**, recipe `### Closing image`, from each branch's visible change and cost. Branches share one image only when it is true for each of them. Otherwise each branch gets its own variant in the Narration section's shape, selected by the same label as its `## Play` bullets. **Done when** the DM can choose the correct closing material from the played result and the Narration section's Done criterion holds.
8. **Cold read and complete.** Apply scene-pages **Cold read** with the **Cold-read criteria** below, then **Completion**, Standalone or Composed as **Orient** resolved. Return the branch triggers, resulting states and touched owner paths with the Scene, so the completion owner can finish the operation and the next Prep can use the played aftermath. **Done when** both sections' Done criteria hold.

## Catalogue

Select the ending the outcome supports. Card names belong in planning material only, outside Scene properties and spoken Narration.

| Card | Condition | What to make runnable |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Happy Ending | The Party achieved the Session's stakes. | Visible benefit to the people affected, the rewards granted and the costs that remain. Leave space for the PCs' own closing responses. |
| Villain Is Killed | The opposition's death was established in play. | What remains at the death Site, who can claim its possessions and which existing interests move into the power vacuum. Keep the death effective. |
| Villain Surrenders | The opposition yields and can offer terms. | Its offer and what it can actually trade. The consequence of each Party answer: accept, counter or refuse. Custody, mercy, exchange or release remain Party choices rather than narrated decisions. |
| Villain Escapes | An escape established by the Climax leaves the opposition free. | The previously available means that worked, what it lost, its next aim and any evidence the Party can actually obtain. Treat routes the Climax closed as unavailable, and reveal the destination only through evidence the Party finds. |
| Heroes Captured | The Climax's resolved defeat or accepted terms place the Party in custody. | The captor's reason to keep them alive, holding Location, conditions, confiscated possessions and where they are kept, plus a concrete opening for future action. Carry the actual injuries and separated PCs, if any. |
| Heroes Escape | The Party's choices or an established interruption enabled withdrawal. | Their reachable refuge, who and what escaped, what was left behind and what the opposition now controls. Any interruption has a prior cause. The departure and its price both follow from what the Party did. |
| Greater Threat | What the Climax uncovered exposes a larger existing pressure. | Linked owners, discoverable evidence, the pressure's concrete implication and accessible responses. Preserve the achievement that made this discovery possible. |
| Ending Cliffhanger | The outcome causes or exposes an immediate new danger. | The perceivable warning and the next decision it presents. Stop at the first point the Players could act. Carry the unresolved danger forward without playing a new contest in this Resolution. |

## Cold-read criteria

Apply these in the shared procedure's cold read:

- Every reachable Climax outcome selects an explicit branch. Its `Closing image`, payoffs, reactions, rewards and Thread states agree with that entry.
- The Session's central question receives the answer play produced or a precise statement of what remains unresolved. Costs persist, and each reversal cites established means that still function in that branch.
- The DM can run each offered choice and NPC response from the page and linked owners. PC prompts leave actions, words and feelings to the Players.
- `## Threads` spells out each conditional state and the next-Session carry-forward on the page itself.
- A stinger grows from the Session and stops before a new contest. Plain closure also satisfies the ending. No branch requires the Party to accept the next offer, pursue a threat or feel a prescribed emotion.
