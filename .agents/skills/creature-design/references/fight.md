# Building the fight

## Difficulty target

Standard, Hard, Deadly or Terrifying, from the caller's brief. Default to Hard for a named threat and Terrifying for a boss. The target is measured against the Party read, never the CR label.

## Role

One primary role, shown on its first turn. Rank (minion, standard, elite, solo) is separate.

| Role | Does | Trades | Players answer by |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Ambusher | hides, strikes, withdraws | durability for burst | revealing it, readying actions |
| Artillery | pressure from range | HP or AC for offence | cover, closing in, disruption |
| Bruiser | dangerous up close | AC, speed or saves for damage and HP | kiting, control, focus fire |
| Controller | terrain, movement, conditions | damage for control | breaking the setup, rescue |
| Defender | holds ground, guards allies | damage for durability | going around, shoving, isolating |
| Leader | commands, heals, buffs | its own offence for its team's | killing or cutting it off |
| Skirmisher | moves through the Party | durability for mobility | pinning it, denying routes |

## What makes it fun

A good Creature is a puzzle with teeth:

- **A rule to learn mid-fight.** Round one shows something strange; by round three a player says "it only hunts by sound" and acts on it.
- **A choice every round:** save the grabbed ally or finish the wounded foe.
- **One moment** the table retells next Session.
- **Their tricks matter.** Each PC's best feature works somewhere in the fight, even when it costs something.
- **Stakes beyond hit points:** someone carried off, a hull taking water, a prize getting away.

## Answering a strong Party

| The Party wins by | The Creature answers with |
| --- | --- |
| nova damage in round one | enough durability to act twice; a split or second form when Bloodied; bodies that soak attention |
| hard control | Legendary Resistance on a boss; conditions it can end with an action; more than one body |
| action economy | legendary actions, a bonus action and a reaction that matter, allies |
| kiting and flight | reach, pulls, terrain that closes range |
| one tank | reach past the front, forced movement, a reason to go for the healer |
| resting and retreating | a clock or a pursuit that makes retreat cost |

Damage that moves people (grab, drag, shove, carry off) changes everyone's next turn; aim at least one feature at the Party's weak saves.

## Signature move

Write it as **tell → threat → answers → payoff**: the visible windup, what happens if it's ignored, at least two ways to answer it, and the opening it leaves.

| Pattern | Tell | Threat | Answers |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Windup blast | a sound stops, a body draws in | area damage and a condition next turn | spread out, cover, interrupt |
| Grab and haul | a limb locks on | a PC carried off | damage the limb, escape, hold the victim down |
| Terrain reshape | the ground cracks, water rises | the battlefield changes | move early, use the new cover |
| Split | the body tears at a seam | two threats at half strength | focus one, control the other |
| Mark and hunt | it fixes on one PC | focused pressure | shield, decoy, break line of sight |
| Command | horn, banner, shriek | allies act again or rise | kill or silence the leader |

## Terrifying, and fair

Terror is a visible threat the Players believe can kill or take something, plus a way out they must find: separation, a clock, the impossible made legible, "unkillable until", escalation when Bloodied, dread before contact, loss beyond death. Keep it fair:

- Every lethal move has a tell a round ahead or a visible recharge.
- Every hard condition has a save each turn or an ally action that ends it.
- Every defence has a bypass, and every immunity has a reason in the fiction.
- No PC loses two turns in a row to the same effect.
- Losing has an off-ramp: retreat, capture, surrender, a bargain.

## Legendary and off-turn actions (2024)

Usually three legendary action uses (four in its lair), each taken right after another creature's turn and all returning at the start of its turn; offer a move, an attack and one limited signature option. Legendary Resistance is 1–2 uses a day through CR 10 and 3 above, each use showing on its body so the Players watch it drain. A reaction answers a player's choice rather than giving it a free turn.
