# Item design

An Item matters through its rules or its story, and the DM's ask can call for anything. The best ones fill a niche the Party lacks without taking a PC's own, and they show on the object what they do. Its page contains the full rules text (ADR 0005), and the DM runs the Item from it alone. Only the 2024 rules, Canon and the template's page shape are firm. Beyond them, this skill is craft: its examples point a direction, and a design they missed is welcome when it serves the table.

## Steps

1. **Read the Canon and the bearer.** When the DM's ask ties the Item to no World, Campaign or PC, the page is World-free (`CONTEXT.md` **World-free page**). It has no Canon or bearer to read and stays setting-neutral in every name and line. Otherwise, with qmd, find the Item's page if it exists, every page that links to it, and where it lies, who made it, who owns or wants it, and its Lore. Change each page as its `revealed` property allows (`CONTEXT.md` **Revealed**). For a PC's Item, read their Sheet and the Party's other gear, and note the bearer's class, level, attunement slots in use and the niche the Item should fill.
2. **Kind and branches.** State its kind (consumable, magic item, artifact, plot object or mundane gear) and any branch: cursed, sentient, growing. A hazard nobody can pick up belongs to its Site instead. A Creature passing for an object (a mimic) gets an Item page for what it appears to be, with its truth under Hidden truths linking the Creature.
3. **Source it** in the order root `AGENTS.md` § Sourcing sets, starting with the Wiki's Items even when the ask supplies its pages: the Campaign's own `Items/` and the vault-root corpus `wiki/Items/`, over 1,100 published items with full rules text. Glob them by name (`wiki/**/Items/staff-of-*`), grep them by effect (`regains .* charges`), and search them with qmd by fantasy ("a compass that finds wrecks"). Turn to the SRD (`dnd5e-srd-api`), then to official and reputable homebrew items on the web, for what the Wiki lacks. List the closest candidates with links, rarity and what each does, and take the nearest as the rules anchor. Decide how much of it to keep, anywhere from reuse or a reskin through adaptation to new design built on what it teaches, and state that choice in one line. When the DM's ask specifies a published form or figure (a +3 weapon, an Immovable Rod, a Rare), that form is the anchor and the design keeps it. The candidates and the chosen path stay in your reply, while the page wikilinks the live Wiki pages its own facts draw on (Canon subjects, PCs, Locations, Factions).
4. **Make it this Item.** Find what sets it apart, in its concept, its bearer or its past. Show each property on the object (a water-breathing charm has gills cut in its rim) and in play, with the look, sound or landing of its strongest use, and let its marks point at what it does or where it has been. In a Campaign, tie its making, material or history to Canon (a blade forged from a wreck's anchor chain that pulls toward drowned iron), and check each fact that depends on a Canon subject or a rule against its source before writing it. When the ask cites an inspiration, carry its themes retold fresh (root `AGENTS.md` § Sourcing). Where the design should be its own, the swap test checks it. Give it its nearest published neighbour's name: a name that still fits means the Item has yet to find its identity. A filed Item whose rules are sound but whose presence reads generic gets a `flesh-out` pass in place of a redesign.
5. **Mechanics** with [references/rules.md](references/rules.md). Measure it against the rules anchor and a couple of peers. Each power traces to the ask, its concept or the chosen candidate. A power that traces to none belongs to another Item. Write the full item text in 2024 wording, shaped as `wiki/templates/item.md` § Properties sets out. Play the bearer's best turns with it to find what stacks, and write each interaction this audit settles into the page (In use on a Campaign page, the rules text otherwise). Done when each situation the ask names plays out from the page, the text gives every figure the table needs, rarity holds as a ceiling, and nothing multiplies unpriced.
6. **Branches** that apply, per that reference's § Branches, with a sentient Item's personality from `npc-design`.
7. **Narration.** When the Party will see the Item, hand `theatre-of-the-mind` the First look slot.
8. **File** to `wiki/templates/item.md` in `<campaign-folder>/Items/` (a page already filed keeps its folder, and a World-free page and its Campaign versions file per `docs/wiki-layout.md` **One vault, many Campaigns**). A World-free page stops at Properties and Links, with no `revealed` in its frontmatter. A Campaign page adds each section the template marks `Optional.` whose condition holds, and its In use speaks to this Party, from its PCs' recorded abilities and gear.
   New facts the Canon lacked are decided as Canon (ADR 0003) and listed in your reply. For an Item the Party can make, the page and reply state how it is made. Stock, holdings and played events stay as the Wiki records them, and new History tells only past that no Session saw. `sources` lists only the Raw and Archive files a page was ingested from, and a designed Item keeps it empty.
   Hand the filed page to `visuals`, then close per `skill://lint` § Commands with the page path.

## Done

- The reply lists the candidates and states the chosen path, or why none fits.
- The Item is its own, or its anchor as asked, and shows what it does on the object and in play.
- The text is complete in 2024 wording, rarity holds as a ceiling, and nothing multiplies unpriced.
- The page gate over the Item's page reports `ok: 0 findings`, and the reply lists every new fact decided as Canon.
