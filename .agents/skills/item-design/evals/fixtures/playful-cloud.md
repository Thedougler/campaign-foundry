# Playful Cloud (遊雲, Yū-un)

The concept in one sentence: a special-grade cursed tool with no technique at all, a three-section staff whose only power is that it hits exactly as hard as the body swinging it, so in weak hands it is a toy and in a monster's hands it breaks gods.

## Form

- A three-section staff: three short rods joined end to end by rings at the joints (Fandom). Each rod is decorated with a flame-like pattern near its ends (Fandom).
- Fought as a staff, a flail and a whip by turns. A swung end comes from an angle that's hard to read.

## Power

- Graded **special grade**, the top tier of cursed tools. Fandom lists its effect as "pure solid power" (Fandom infobox).
- It is the only special-grade tool with **no innate cursed technique** (ScreenRant). Its destructive power **scales with the wielder's physical strength** (ScreenRant; Swordslice).
- It needs no cursed energy to work, so it suits fighters with little or none: Toji Fushiguro and Maki Zen'in, both under Heavenly Restriction (ScreenRant; Swordslice). It is less useful to a technique-reliant sorcerer such as Megumi (ScreenRant).
- A wielder can still put cursed energy into it: Todo comments that he did when he used it (r/Jujutsushi thread). *(unconfirmed beyond fan discussion)*
- Said to be valued at around **500 million yen** (Swordslice). *(unconfirmed)*

## Wielders and key scenes

- **Suguru Geto** fights Yuta Okkotsu with it in *Jujutsu Kaisen 0* (Fandom image gallery: "Geto using Playful Cloud against Yuta", "Geto's mastery of Playful Cloud").
- **Maki Zen'in** wields it in the Kyoto Goodwill Event and strikes the special-grade curse Hanami with it (Fandom gallery). Per Swordslice, it hurt Hanami despite the curse's toughness, because its damage is physical.
- **Toji Fushiguro**, revived during the Shibuya Incident and acting on pure instinct, takes Playful Cloud from Maki (Fandom Toji gallery: "Toji takes Playful Cloud from Maki"). He uses it to batter the special-grade curse Dagon (ScreenRant image caption "Toji attacks Dagon"). This is its signature showcase: a man with zero cursed energy beating a disaster curse to death with a stick.
- **Aoi Todo** and **Megumi Fushiguro** are also listed as users (Fandom infobox).

## Themes to carry (DM concept)

- **Strength is the only key.** The weapon contributes nothing of its own: no element, no trick. That makes it the purest measure of whoever holds it.
- **Inheritance by violence.** It passes hand to hand: a villain's prized tool becomes a student's weapon, then gets ripped from her grip by someone stronger.
- **The non-magical beating the magical.** Its fame comes from people with no power humbling those built of power.
- **Playful.** The name is a joke: the flail-staff's loose, almost lazy swings land with absurd force.

## Notes for a D&D 2024 conversion (research only, not Canon)

- The base weapon could be a flail or quarterstaff analogue: a three-section staff fits Bludgeoning damage and the Versatile or Reach properties.
- Scaling with the wielder means damage keyed to Strength (modifier or score) rather than spell slots or charges. A `+3` weapon is Very Rare or Legendary in the 2024 DMG.
- No technique fits an item with no spells and no charges, whose features are all physical. Anti-magic flavour could come from ignoring resistance to bludgeoning, or from extra damage against creatures made of magic.
