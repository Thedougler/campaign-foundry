# notes for the brack - tidewell etc

ok so theres a village called Tidewell out past Reedholt where the eel-wives live. stilt houses, nets everywhere, they only come in to Saltwick to sell smoked eel before the Long Ebb. maybe 60 people. run by the eel-wives council, oldest is Marrow Jessup - she's got one eye (lost the other to a pike as a girl), laughs at everything, wants the eel grounds under the chapel back since the flats dried up there. she hates Nib Ashwater because his grandmother wrote Tidewell's boats out of the tally 30 years ago and they lost their fishing rights.

so theres a feud: Tidewell vs Reedholt over the eel traps on the Marl channel. Reedholt boys cut Tidewell traps at night. Tidewell's answer is gonna be to stop selling eel to Reedholt before the Ebb (Reedholt needs the smoked eel to get through the six dry days).

Marrow's grandson Ossie Jessup (15) runs messages for the Reedrunners, she doesnt know.

also Hobb Tarrow is the harbourmaster's son and has never kept the lighthouse - he just commands the watch.
