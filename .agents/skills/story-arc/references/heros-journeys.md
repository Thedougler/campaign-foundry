# Hero's journeys

The Party and every PC each walk a hero's journey (`CONTEXT.md` **Hero's journey**) across the whole Campaign, on Christopher Vogler's twelve stages. `story-arc` alone writes them: a PC's under `## Hero's journey` on their journey Thread, and the Party's on the **Hero's journey.** bullet of `story-so-far.md` § Arc position. The sources are in `docs/research/creative-writing/story-arc.md`.

## Stages come from play

A hero's journey is a way of reading play after it happens. After a Session, read its Recap and Session Ledger and judge which stage the PC's own choices put them at. Record that stage with the Sessions and Ledger lines that show it. Stage counts, pace and order are never targets. A PC is at whatever stage the evidence shows, for as long as it shows it. Stages may come out of order, repeat, be skipped or reverse. When the evidence is ambiguous, keep the earlier stage and write one clause saying what would settle it. A hero's journey may end in a fall or a poisoned elixir. Stage names stay on the DM side and never enter Narration (root `AGENTS.md` **In-world voice**).

## Scales

- **Party.** One hero's journey across the whole Campaign, read from the Party's shared choices.
- **PC.** One hero's journey per PC across the whole Campaign, read from that PC's own choices.
- **Arc.** A stage normally shows across several Sessions. When the page retells an Arc (`CONTEXT.md` **Arc**), note which stages the Party's choices crossed during it, as observation.

## The twelve stages

Each row gives what the stage looks like in a Recap and the kind of Player choice that shows a PC is at it. Refusal, failure, delay and walking away are Player choices, recorded like any other.

| Stage | In a Recap | The choice that shows it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ordinary World | The PC's life before the Campaign's trouble finds them, as the Backstory and first Sessions show it | The PC acts from the habits, ties and wants they arrived with |
| Call to Adventure | A pressure or offer comes to the PC and asks something of them | The PC engages with it, even only to question it |
| Refusal of the Call | The PC hesitates, bargains or turns the call down | The PC declines, delays or looks for another way out |
| Meeting the Mentor | Someone or something gives the PC counsel, a gift, a test or a reason | The PC seeks out, accepts or tests that guidance |
| Crossing the First Threshold | The PC commits past the point of easy return | The PC acts in a way that closes off their old life or safety |
| Tests, Allies, Enemies | The PC finds out the new world's rules and who stands where | The PC picks allies, makes enemies and takes on trials |
| Approach to the Inmost Cave | The PC closes on the place, person or truth they fear most | The PC prepares for, plans for or walks towards that danger |
| Ordeal | The PC meets their greatest crisis so far, often a near death or a loss | The PC stakes something they can't take back |
| Reward | The PC comes away with what the Ordeal gave them, a thing, a truth or a change in themselves | The PC takes, keeps or pays for the prize |
| The Road Back | The prize's cost comes due, and the PC turns towards home or the larger fight | The PC recommits, often under pursuit or a fresh pressure |
| Resurrection | A final test makes the PC prove what they have become | The PC acts on the change under the highest pressure, or fails to |
| Return with the Elixir | The PC brings what they gained back to the people it changes | The PC shares, spends or refuses what they won, and its effect shows |

## Possibilities for next Session

Forward material is two or three possibilities the DM can put in the PC's path. Build each as a situation from a live Campaign Thread, Quest or Faction pressure. It lets the Player take their hero's journey further if they choose, and you mark it a possibility (`SKILL.md` **Anchor, possibility, player-owned**). Write what the world puts in front of the PC, and leave what the PC does, feels or decides to the Player. Each possibility holds up when the Player ignores it, refuses it or goes another way. Where two PCs' possibilities come from one Thread, write the crossing on both journey Threads. The Campaign's `campaign-config.md` § Themes shape what each situation tests.

## Lead PC rotation

Each Session has one Lead PC (`CONTEXT.md` **Lead PC**). Read past Lead PCs from the **Lead PC.** line of each Prep's `### Spotlights` and from `story-so-far.md` § Pacing, and count a Session without one as nobody's. The next Lead PC comes from the PCs who haven't led since every PC last led. Among them, pick the one play has pressed hardest, from observed evidence only: their choices and the table's fixations in the last two Recaps and Session Ledgers, and the live Threads now converging on them. Give that evidence in one clause. Leading means the Session's strongest possibilities are built around that PC, and whether anything turns stays with the Player. A guest character (`CONTEXT.md` **Guest character**) never leads and has no journey Thread. The PCs who aren't leading each still get a Spotlight from their Goals and bonds.
