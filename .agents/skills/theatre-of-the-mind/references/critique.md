# Critique

One bounded pass after Revise, run once per block. It reads the block the way the table hears it and spends its fixes where the **table cost** is highest. The four-part finding, the bound, the simulated listener and the failure hunt are adapted in our own words from [haowjy/creative-writing-skills](https://github.com/haowjy/creative-writing-skills) (prose critique, critic, reader simulation, failure modes, prose, voice and antipattern guidance).

## Steps

1. **Table read.** Take the Players named in the Campaign's `campaign-config.md`, with their tastes and PCs, and hear the block once as they would, from the top and at speaking pace. Mark where the picture forms, where attention drifts and what a Player would ask before acting. Done when you can quote the sentence where the picture forms and any sentence where attention drifts, and can state the first question a Player would ask.
2. **Find.** Record up to five findings, most damaging first. Weigh the slot before the lines: does the block do its Job and stop at its End? Then hunt the lines. Write each finding in four parts:
   - **Quote**: the exact words from the block.
   - **Table cost**: what the table loses at that point. It is the picture, the pace, a choice or a PC.
   - **Fix**: the rewrite or cut that recovers it.
   - **Severity**: one of three levels.
     - *critical* when a hard line breaks or the table cannot act
     - *major* when the table read loses the picture, attention or an NPC's voice, or a line is below root `AGENTS.md` **Print grade**
     - *minor* when the table hears it fine and it could still be sharper

   Done when every finding has all four parts and the list holds five or fewer, ordered by table cost.
3. **Fix.** Apply the fix for every critical and major finding. Keep every hard line, every Canon source, exact document payload and quoted table speech as they stand. Minor findings go into the DM reply. Done when each critical and major finding has its rewrite in the block and every kept element still reads word for word.
4. **Re-read.** Hear the revised block once more and confirm the fixes landed without opening a new defect. This is the last read: the critique runs once, in this context. Done when the re-read is complete and any new defect the re-read finds is fixed or named in the DM reply.

## Hunt

These are the table costs to listen for, each a sentence- or beat-level pattern. Word-level tells belong to the style layer of `bun run cf -- check` (Vale `ai-tells` and `Narration`), so this hunt reads sentences, beats and voices.

- **Past the End.** Elaboration after the reaction point, while the table is ready to act.
- **Commentary.** The narrator telling the table how to feel about what it just heard.
- **Early release.** Tension resolved or a threat explained before a Player can answer it.
- **Over-intensity.** Every beat pitched at the same high volume, so nothing stands out. Dread or wonder named as a mood where an object, an absence or a number belongs.
- **Closed ambiguity.** A question the sources leave open answered by the block.
- **Info-dump.** History, plans or lore arriving faster than the table can use in one round of play, or lore no player asked for and no roll bought.
- **Templating.** Sentences built on one rhythm and shape, fluent and flat. The table stops hearing them.
- **Placeholder prose.** A sentence that would fit any Scene unchanged. Anchor it in this Scene's facts or cut it.
- **Metaphor cluster.** Several figures crowding one moment where one image would hold.
- **Metagame spotlight.** A mundane detail given more weight than its neighbours because the writer knows it matters. Emphasis follows what the Party would notice, not what the plot needs.
- **Bare handoff.** A closing "What do you do?" with nothing of this situation in it. Put the pressure inside the question, or offer the either/or the place gives.
- **Blank failure.** A failure Cue where nothing happens. Give it a false belief, a constraint or a consequence with its cause.
- **Unplayable space.** A description the table cannot move through: no door states, heights, distances or counts where the next action needs them.
- **NPC line.** A line that misses its target voice. The target line keeps a want beneath the words and its feelings unstated, in a diction the table could name blind and a length that matches the speaker's power and comfort. It tells listeners only what they lack, in that NPC's voice rather than the narrator's.
- **Stock tells.** A borrowed gesture (a jaw tightening, a breath released) where this person's own physical cue belongs.
- **Tidy ending.** A closing sentence that sums up or moralises instead of stopping on the live moment.
