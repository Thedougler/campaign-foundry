# Hook Scene

The Hook puts one pressure within the Party's reach and ends at the moment they commit to a response. The Session's problem is still unsolved at that point. Use Mike Pondsmith's *Scripting the Game*. In it the Hook sets the pace and the middle Scenes change the stakes, and the Climax resolves the question the Hook opened.

The shared procedure is [`docs/agents/scene-pages.md`](../../../docs/agents/scene-pages.md). Each step below references the section of it that applies.

## Steps

1. **Orient and ground.** Read `docs/agents/scene-pages.md` whole, then apply its **Orient** and **Ground**. The template is `wiki/templates/Scene - Hook.md`. **Done when** both sections' Done criteria hold.
2. **Choose the opening.** Start from the previous Session's ending or a live Thread. Select a card from **Cards** below, recorded as scene-pages **Card** directs. Identify the Session question it opens, a memorable perceivable detail, and whether the pressure is action or cerebral. A resumed Scene keeps its recorded positions, initiative, conditions, resources and unresolved action. A previous Session that ended on a stinger resumes per `docs/agents/narrative-devices.md` § Closing stinger. **Done when** one pressure connects the entry state to something the Session will use later, with about thirty minutes allotted rather than a whole investigation or journey.
3. **Resolve the cast.** Apply scene-pages **Cast**. **Done when** its Done criterion holds.
4. **Make commitment playable.** Start the page as scene-pages **Page format** directs, then fill:
   - `### Situation`: state the opposition's goal and its next clock change. Give the observable commitment that ends this Hook.
   - `### Handles`: give at least three materially different responses. Each has an upside, a cost and a World response. Include an obvious first move and an alternative that rewards using the cast or space. Give each PC present an available first action.
   - `### Checks`: each uncertain action in the Page format table.
   - `### Encounter`, only when the Party may fight: scene-pages **Encounters**.
   - `## At a glance` and `## Depth`. The row's Spotlight sharpens one PC's personal pull in `### Spotlight`, while every other PC keeps a reason to act.

   **Done when** the Party has a move within minutes, and the page rules the expected approach plus hesitation, refusal and interference.
5. **Open at the decision.** Write the `Opening` and any entering or Revelation callouts with scene-pages **Narration**, recipe `### Hook`, from the finished Situation and owner pages. The Opening starts inside the disturbance and stops before an unresolved seizure, attack or escape succeeds. Already-played events remain established. New events remain answerable. A memory gap or revelation that rewrites a PC's history needs that Player's established agreement. Without it, make the uncertainty external evidence the PC can investigate. **Done when** the first spoken sentence presents the pressure, the table has something to act on, and no callout chooses a PC's response or resolves the live contest.
6. **Cut on commitment.** Fill `## Outcomes` with scene-pages **Outcomes**. Give each handle's result, then a concrete pressure advance if the Party waits and the World response if they refuse. Write the changed state into the linked destination or standalone carry-forward instead of forcing every approach through the same success. An action Hook normally hands to a Development as the first middle Scene, and a cerebral Hook to a Cliffhanger. With no middle Scenes, use the chart's Climax destination. **Done when** every prepared result has a trigger and conditional handoff, and the Climax's question has been planted.
7. **Cold read and complete.** Apply scene-pages **Cold read** to confirm that one pressure opens the Session question and the Opening stops at the commitment point. Check each PC's first action, then the results of waiting and refusal. Then apply **Completion**, Standalone or Composed as **Orient** resolved. **Done when** both sections' Done criteria hold.

## Cards

Choose the card that serves the fiction. The card guides the Scene's design and leaves the Party's answer open. The catalogue paraphrases *Scripting the Game* for 2024 D&D play. **Stranger** also covers a direct opening request built around an urgent offer.

| Card | Build the pressure | Keep the result in play |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Kidnapped | A seizure in progress or a sourced abduction's aftermath. State the taker, captive, motive, route and deadline. Leave usable traces. | If the grab is live, rule rescue, pursuit, negotiation and escape. An abduction already established by play can stand. A new one succeeds only through the contest. |
| Coronet Blue | An agreed memory gap places the Party in a role others expect them to understand. Give immediate demands and recoverable evidence of what happened. | Use only with established Player buy-in. Clues restore access to the truth, and each Player chooses whether to accept the assumed role. |
| Play a Cliffhanger | A brief physical contest introduces an ally or opponent and an objective beyond killing. For interrupted action, resume the recorded unresolved moment. | Read `cliffhanger-scene`'s Locate the test, Choose the contest and Build the engine steps, its `## Rulings` branch for the contest, and its `## Cards` row. Retain `kind: Hook` and stop on commitment. Use scene-pages **Encounters** where Creatures can be fought. |
| Play a Development | A meeting, mistaken identity, alliance or betrayal puts a major relationship in question immediately. | Read `development-scene`'s Turn, Evidence, People, Choices and rulings, and Pressure and handoff steps, plus its `## Cards` row. Retain `kind: Hook` and end when the Party chooses what to do with the information or offer. |
| Discovery | A concrete find matters to a later Scene. State what it shows or does, who wants it, how they learn of it and distinct leads onward. | Keeping, using, hiding, trading or surrendering it changes who acts next and what the Party takes forward. |
| Crisis | A disaster is already moving. Place people at risk, exits, a discoverable cause and triggered stages of deterioration. | Saving someone, limiting damage and leaving each have ruled costs and different aftermaths. The opening stops before an avoidable loss happens. |
| Looming Threat | Visible signs announce a danger. State its arrival clock and how preparation, warning or intervention can change it. | Preparation produces concrete changes in timing, access or severity rather than merely delaying a fixed catastrophe. |
| Revelation | Evidence changes a live Thread's meaning. State what it grants, endangers or demands, and who else knows. | Establish the truth on the DM side. Let the Party choose whether to expose, exploit, contest or ignore it. Preserve Player ownership of PC history. |
| Murder | An established death or a preventable attack draws the Party into a question. State the victim, means, culprit and independent leads. Explain any already-established absence of the killer. | Clues are true and usable. A present killer can be caught or confronted. Their later escape is not guaranteed. |
| False Accusation | An accuser presents persuasive false evidence. State their motive, the authorities' actual means, the real culprit and ways to expose the lie. | Contesting the charge, submitting, bargaining and fleeing each have a consequence and a route onward. The Party can prove innocence across several actions, and one failed roll leaves other ways open. |
| Stranger | A person makes an urgent request or offer with terms, evidence and a deadline. | Acceptance, renegotiation, investigation and rejection get concrete World responses. The Party alone chooses whether to take the job. |
