# Influence

The 2024 rules resolve social pressure with the Influence action. Confirm current wording with the `dnd5e-srd-api` skill when a figure matters.

1. **Frame it.** What does the NPC want right now, and what is the Party asking, at what cost or threat to them?
2. **Starting point.** Attitude (Friendly, Indifferent, Hostile) and, separately, how they meet this request (Willing, Hesitant, Unwilling).
3. **Approach.** Conversation, evidence, a favour, a gift, a threat, an appeal or leverage, and why it fits their fear, limit and contradiction.
4. **Roll only when** the outcome is uncertain, the stakes matter and a miss changes the situation. Write it as Ability (Skill) and DC. Otherwise state what happens.
5. **Movement.** Success improves attitude, willingness, access or the terms. A miss spends time, trust, leverage, safety or a chance. Neither rewrites what the NPC believes.
6. **New position.** What they will now do, won't do, or need next.

Information the Session depends on never rides on one roll: plant it as about three Clues (a statement, a trace, a witness, a document, a consequence). A miss can muddy which is true, add danger or cost time, and a way forward stays open.
