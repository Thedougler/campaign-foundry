# Composing a journey

Read during prep-session step 3 when the Session's middle is travel between Locations. The Region page owns the routes, times, costs, navigation checks and powers on the road; this reference turns the route the Party takes into chart rows, one leg at a time. A route the Region page leaves unstated goes to `location-design`'s Region branch before charting.

## Size the journey

Classify the chosen route by its Region travel time: **Close** arrives within a day, **Far** takes several days, **Very Far** takes weeks or leaves the Region. The class sets the journey's total events: Close one, Far two or three, Very Far five at most. Events follow the class rather than a per-day schedule, so the chart states elapsed time as the Region's route time, not a day-by-day count.

Split the route into legs where its terrain, mode or exposure changes. Each leg is a middle row of the Scene Chart, takes its Kind from its strongest event, and counts against step 3's middle-Scene ceiling; a Close journey is one leg. **Done when** the journey has a class, an event total within it, and legs that fit the ceiling.

## Fill each leg

Write each leg's five slots into its row's planning notes, and hand them to the kind skill as the row's card material:

1. **Departure cost** — what leaving this point spends or exposes: supplies, a deadline, an ally left behind, a debt that comes due.
2. **Landmark** — at least one named landmark the Party can approach, avoid or exploit, with a position on the route and a use. A Party that passes it by still finishes the leg.
3. **Events** — the smallest set that serves the leg, at least one. Derive each from a named PC's Thread, goal or bond crossed with a fact of this leg, and state that derivation in one clause. When no live material reaches the leg, ask the DM which pressure to pull.
4. **Toll** — at least one concrete toll with its amount: rations, Hit Dice, an Exhaustion level, a damaged or lost possession, or hours against a named clock.
5. **Arrival** — one line on how the Party arrives changed: what it knows, holds, owes or has lost at the leg's end.

Assign travel roles from the Region's navigation, lookout and supply needs, and name for each role the complication its failed check opens: a situation the Party answers, carried into the next row's entry state rather than subtracted as a flat penalty.

**Done when** every leg names all five slots, every event's derivation clause names a PC and a leg fact, the journey's event total matches its class, and each assigned travel role names the complication its failure opens.
