# Composing travel

Read during prep-session step 3 when the Party spends the Session's middle moving between Locations. The Region page defines the routes, times, costs, navigation checks and powers on the road. This reference turns the route the Party takes into chart rows, one leg at a time. A route the Region page leaves unstated goes to `location-design`'s Region branch before charting.

## Size the trip

Classify the chosen route by its Region travel time. The class sets the trip's total events:

| Class | Region travel time | Events |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Close | within a day | 1 |
| Far | several days | 2 or 3 |
| Very Far | weeks, or leaves the Region | up to 5 |

The event count comes from the class and ignores the number of days. The chart states elapsed time as the Region's route time in one figure.

Split the route into legs where its terrain, mode or exposure changes. Each leg is a middle row of the Scene Chart and takes its Kind from its strongest event. Every leg counts against step 3's middle-Scene ceiling. A Close route is one leg. **Done when** the trip has a class, an event total within it, and legs that fit the ceiling.

## Fill each leg

Write each leg's five slots into its row's planning notes, and hand them to the kind skill as the row's card material:

1. **Departure cost.** What leaving this point spends or exposes, such as supplies, a deadline, an ally left behind or a debt that comes due.
2. **Landmark.** At least one named landmark the Party can approach, avoid or exploit, with a position on the route and a use. A Party that passes it by still finishes the leg.
3. **Events.** The smallest set that serves the leg, at least one. Derive each from a named PC's Thread, goal or bond crossed with a fact of this leg, and state that derivation in one clause. When no live Thread, goal or bond fits this leg, ask the DM which pressure to pull.
4. **Toll.** At least one concrete toll with its amount, such as rations, Hit Dice, an Exhaustion level, a damaged or lost possession, or hours against a named clock.
5. **Arrival.** One line on how the Party arrives changed: what it knows, holds, owes or has lost at the leg's end.

Assign travel roles from the Region's navigation, lookout and supply needs, and state for each role the complication its failed check opens. That complication is a situation the Party answers, carried into the next row's entry state in place of a flat penalty.

**Done when** each leg's planning notes fill all five slots, every event's derivation clause states a PC and a leg fact, the trip's event total matches its class, and each assigned travel role states the complication its failure opens.
