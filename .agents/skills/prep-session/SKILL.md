# Prep a Session

Prep produces everything the DM needs to run the upcoming Session from the Wiki alone. The Scene Chart orders pressure and opportunities, and the Party's choices and dice determine outcomes. The moments only the room can time, such as riffing on a Player's joke, dropping a patrol on a stalled plan or calling a break, rely on the Angles, bench lines, Cues and planted details Prep leaves within reach. Prep supplies that material and leaves its timing to the DM.

[The shared Scene procedure](../../../docs/agents/scene-pages.md) owns Scripting the Game order, Scene page contracts, Encounter balance, Narration and conditional hand-offs. This skill owns Session composition and the final hot/index/gate/log/Push cycle. Every path below is vault-relative (from `wiki/`). The Session folder is `<campaign-folder>/Sessions/Session <N>/`, and each page in it is titled `Session <N> - <Scene title or kind>` and filed as that title's slug, `session-<N>-<scene-slug>.md`.

## Steps

1. **Orient.** Read `user-config.md`, the Campaign's `campaign-config.md`, its `hot.md` (orientation, not evidence), its `story-so-far.md` when it exists (the arc's pressures, open promises and PC trajectories, which feed the Threads and Spotlights of step 5), its Campaign folder's `index.md` and the last ten entries of its `log.md`, in that order. Then read `docs/agents/scene-pages.md` and `docs/wiki-layout.md` whole, and these templates in `wiki/templates/`: `Prep.md`, `previously-on.md`, `hot.md` and `scene-hook.md`, `scene-development.md`, `scene-cliffhanger.md`, `scene-climax.md`, `scene-resolution.md`. Read each kind's `SKILL.md` (`hook-scene`, `development-scene`, `cliffhanger-scene`, `climax-scene`, `resolution-scene`) now, because charting picks cards from them.

2. **Fix the target and the intent.** The Session to prep is the one the DM names; otherwise it is the Session after the last one with a Recap. Canon runs in this order: what the DM says, then what the Wiki says. The entry state is the end of the last Session the DM counts as played, so read that Session's Recap and final Scene. When the Wiki contains a Recap, a later Previously On or hot claims for a Session the DM calls unplayed, leave those pages unchanged and ground the entry on the earlier Session. Record the conflict for your report.

   When the Session folder already holds a Prep, read it and every Scene page its chart links. This is the existing plan. Rewrite each page at its current path when its row is retained, and give a new row a new path. Every page of a played Session stays as it is. A played Session has a Recap the DM counts.

   Take the settled `plan-session` intent: the DM's fixed points, opening, Threads, Climax, Resolution, Spotlights and decisions left to Prep. An already-settled brief is sufficient. When no intent exists, settle it with the DM through `plan-session`'s checks.

3. **Ground the Party.** Search the Wiki (`.omp/AGENTS.md` § Wiki access) and read each page behind a hit in full. A snippet only says where to read. Look for what the entry depends on. Read the entry Session's Recap and final Scene, plus active Threads and open Quests. Read Factions and House Rules in play, and every participating PC's Sheet, Goals and bonds, Plans and journey Thread. Which further pages bear on the intent is your judgement. Follow the links these pages make until the Hook's entry has no unsourced dependency, and decide any truly missing one consistently with Canon. Record the Party's actual Location, in-world date, knowledge, possessions, conditions, resources and live clocks that affect the opening. Distinguish played events from unplayed Prep alternatives. A Creature's or NPC's statistics are the ones under its own page's `## Statblock`.
4. **Budget and chart.** Read `session_length_hours` from the Campaign overview `<campaign-folder>/<campaign-folder>.md`; when it is blank, use `wiki/dm-settings.md`. Convert it to table minutes. Normally reserve about ninety minutes for Hook, Climax and Resolution together. The middle-Scene ceiling is `max(0, floor((total minutes - 90) / 30))`, not a quota. A four-hour Session permits five middle Scenes. For a shorter Session, keep the three framing Scenes and shorten their allocations to fit. Assign explicit Minutes, leaving room for breaks and rules lookups. Distinguish table time from in-world clock units.

   Order the rows by the shared procedure's **Scripting the Game** section, and choose each row's card from its kind's skill. Use `hook-scene` for Hook and `development-scene` for Development. Use `cliffhanger-scene` for Cliffhanger, `climax-scene` for Climax and `resolution-scene` for Resolution. When the Party spends the middle of the Session moving between Locations, compose those rows with [the travel reference](references/journey.md). The Region page supplies the routes and their costs.

   Format: the Prep template's Scene Chart table with its columns `#`, `Scene` (a link to the row's page), `Kind` (the exact kind), `Minutes` and `Threads` (links). Under the table, one planning note per row, so every row has its kind, purpose, trigger, card, planted detail, Minutes and destination, in the template's `[!tiles]` storyboard, giving its situation, its independent trigger, the selected card, the Spotlight, incoming alternatives, the conditional destination, the detail it plants for a later callback with where that could return (`docs/agents/narrative-devices.md` § Callbacks) and, where relevant, escalation. A situation comes to the Party through an arrival, a clock, an opposition move or a preceding consequence. The Party chooses how to answer. A Player-dependent detour is a labelled optional branch with a trigger and rejoin state. Make the first physical contest forgiving of sound choices and reserve the opposition's strongest established challenge and the highest stakes for the Climax. Shape the peaks with these notes:

   - **Break hinge.** Mark the row nearest the Session's midpoint whose end changes state, an irreversible turn or a warm close, as the place a break fits. The DM calls the break.
   - **Planning handle.** Where a row invites the Party to plan, choose one outside stimulus, such as a patrol on its round, and enter it in step 5's Opposition timeline. The DM drops it when the plan stalls.
   - **One place, rising peaks.** When consecutive rows share a Location, each row's solve opens the next row's harder question.
   - **Ending.** The Resolution row states the ending the next Session needs, a stinger or a coda (`docs/agents/narrative-devices.md` § Closing stinger), from the swing in `story-so-far.md` § Pacing when it exists.

5. **Distribute Threads, pressure and Clues.** Each part below is a section of the Prep template.

   - `## Threads`: three to five live Threads consistent with the intent. List urgent ones first and have every Scene advance one. A Thread that could wait three Sessions is important rather than urgent. Keep faction-driven Threads to two per Session and let at most one major revelation land. One line per Thread, linked, giving where it is planted, tested and moved or conditionally resolved, and the Angle it gives the Party. Include a Thread the Climax touches. Then fill `### Spotlights` per the Prep template's comment, opening on the `- **Lead PC.**` line for the Lead PC the intent names. Place the Lead PC's possibilities in Scenes as situations with several exits, normally the Climax or the Development feeding it. Give each other PC one item mapping them to a linked Scene, with the goal, bond or fear their Player may act on. Prep doesn't record or assume a stage change, because only `story-arc` judges stages, after play. Two PCs share a Scene only when each has a distinct opportunity with its own stakes.
   - `## Opposition`: link the opposition and state its Session goal, means and why pursuing that goal brings it into the Party's path. Then its unopposed timeline, one entry per move: in-world time or event trigger, action, observable sign, consequence and what interference changes. Derive the timeline by running `simulate-npcs` with purpose offscreen, over the opposition's leaders and across the Session's in-world span. Keep each move whose sign the Party can perceive. Each sign states what becomes visible and leaves its cause for the Party to infer. The agenda advances without requiring the Party to accept a job or follow a lead. Give each linked speaking cast member a **bench line**: the moment they grab the Scene and how to play them instantly, ending on their line as a Cue in their voice (`npc-design` `references/craft.md` § Voice).
   - `## Clues`: fill the template's `| Clue | Found in |` table with about ten distinct true, concrete facts. Each fact is revealable in at least two linked Scenes. State the source or interaction that reveals it at each route. Each conclusion or access essential to progress gets three independent discovery routes across the Session, not three attempts at the same roll. List these in a `###` subsection under Clues. These facts and routes go to each Scene's author to build into its page. Apply `docs/agents/narrative-devices.md` § Planted details and connection, § Clue ladders and § Red herrings.

6. **Cast before creating.** Inventory the chart's NPCs, Locations, Creatures, Items and rewards, plus needed Factions, Vehicles, Spells, Lore and Handouts. Follow the shared procedure's **Cast** section. Search both of the Wiki's homes for a fitting page first, the Campaign folder and the vault-root kind folders. An unrevealed page (`CONTEXT.md` **Revealed**) that misfits only in its draft details is reflavoured to the role in place of a new page, and a revealed page fits only through additive change. Next search published or homebrew material through web search, then the 2024 SRD through `dnd5e-srd-api` as the last fallback, before novel design. For each missing owner, use its skill: `npc-design`, `location-design`, `creature-design`, `item-design`, `faction-design`, `vehicle-design`, `spell-design` or `lore-design`. Use `statblock-design` for a fought Creature or NPC with no statblock, and `dungeon-design` when a Site needs area-by-area exploration. You may delegate independent owner pages to concurrent subagents. Author each required Handout from `wiki/templates/handout.md`, with `theatre-of-the-mind` for its spoken or shown text. Give each owner its purpose, Canon, Party facts, target path and this Prep as the enclosing operation, and keep the final index, gate, log and Push with this Prep. Each page has one writer. Every fought Creature and NPC has a statblock on its own page, and every reward has complete rules and conditional availability rather than a name-only promise.

7. **File the Prep.** Fill `wiki/templates/Prep.md` at `<campaign-folder>/Sessions/Session <N>/session-<N>-prep.md`, titled `Session <N> - Prep`. Keep every property and the section order, and remove the `%% %%` guidance comments. Properties: `type: Prep`, a one-line `summary`, `sources` holding repo-relative archived Raw paths (`archive/<file>.md`) used by the Prep or `[]`, and `date` set to the Session's in-world date. `## Session question` gives the one question the Climax settles, then where the Party starts, with the Location linked. File the chart with its planning notes and Spotlights, and each `Optional.` section whose condition the Session meets (Threads, Opposition with its timeline and bench lines, Clues), as steps 4 and 5 set out. Give each Scene a path in the same folder, `session-<N>-<scene-slug>.md` titled `Session <N> - <Scene title>`, and file each new one there as a copy of its kind's template with `title`, `type` and `kind` set, so the chart's links and the Scenes' links to each other resolve whatever order the Scenes are written in. Run `bun run cfcli -- check "<Prep path>"` and repair each finding it reports.

8. **Fill every Scene and reconcile.** For each row, author the Scene through the kind's skill with this Prep as the caller, yourself in chart order or through subagents, one per Scene, in one parallel batch. Every spoken line, callout and Cue alike, meets `theatre-of-the-mind` (the exemplar-DM bar of ADR 0029). Each Scene works from its row and card, Minutes, Party, relevant owner pages, opposition state, Clues and discovery routes, Spotlight, every reachable predecessor outcome and exact destination paths. A parallel Scene takes its predecessors' outcomes from their planning notes. Take each finished Scene's touched paths, design decisions and outgoing states before writing the Scenes that receive them. After a parallel batch, run the page check over every page the batch and its supporting runs touched, since a page finished early can echo a sibling or owner page written after it, and send each page's findings back to its writer.

   Carry alternative knowledge, positions, ownership, conditions, resources and clock states forward as alternatives. A later opening supports success, partial result, failure, delay, refusal or withdrawal whenever those results are reachable. Its Narration and rulings select the corresponding branch. Proposed deaths, captures, rewards and Thread changes stay conditional in Prep and Scenes rather than changing current World pages or `hot.md`.

   After each Scene, update affected rows, routes and receiving entries. If a branch prevents a later premise, replace that premise with its actual consequence and recompute the remaining order and budget; keep the intent's purpose, not a guaranteed victory. If an earlier result can settle the central question, give it a direct Resolution hand-off instead of requiring another showdown. Each reachable outcome gets a coherent receiving entry or Session-ending carry-forward.

9. **Locate Previously On.** The Previously On for Session N lives at `Sessions/Session <N>/session-<N>-previously-on.md` and recounts Session N-1. It is read at the start of Session N. Ingesting Session N-1's Transcript writes it. When it exists, link it from the Prep's opening material and keep its played Narration as it stands. When it is missing, read Session N-1's Recap and have `theatre-of-the-mind` fill `wiki/templates/previously-on.md` at that path, ending on the actual last played moment. An available Transcript in `archive/` supplies grounding. Ingesting unprocessed Raw is `ingest` work. It recounts only played events. A first Session skips this step.

10. **Supply Visuals.** Hand `visuals` every cast page, Scene and Handout the Session uses, with this Prep as the completion owner. It reuses fitting images and supplies each battle map an Encounter needs, each portrait, banner or scene-setter the Session surfaces, and each Handout image, or leaves its fallback where generation is unavailable. Report missing map data, a battle map's Universal VTT file, separately from a missing image. Report each fallback that stands with its page and exact prerequisite.

11. **Cold-read the full Session.** Follow each entry and conditional exit across the actual pages and resolving embeds, as a DM with no chat or sourcebooks. Check the chart's shared order and Minutes against the chosen length, each row's trigger and live Thread, the opposition's independent advances, all PC Spotlights, Clue discovery routes and critical-route redundancy. Check that each Lead PC possibility works whichever way the Player answers it, refusal and ignoring it included. Trace every Climax dependency to an earlier Scene or established Canon, and every Climax result to the matching Resolution payoff, reward, Thread state and closing Narration. Check branch transitions preserve losses and live choices without teleporting the Party or assuming acceptance. Fix local defects and recompute affected rows and entries. A capability gap stays a named requirement, never a fictional success.

12. **Refresh and file.** Rewrite `<campaign-folder>/hot.md` whole from `wiki/templates/hot.md`, about 500 words and at most 550. Set `date` to the Party's in-world date. Fill `## Active Threads` when a Thread is active (one line per Thread, linked, where it stands and its next move), `## Last Session` once a Session has been played (the entry Session's changes, each linked to the page it changed) and `## Next Session` (where the Party stands, with the Location linked, links to this Prep and its Hook, and the open questions it is set to run). Future outcomes remain conditional. Then run the **Completion commands** below and record one `prep` operation containing every touched content page, including dependent cast, Handouts, Previously On and hot.

13. **Push and report.** Follow `push-session` with the World, Campaign, Session, Wiki root and complete touched paths, using the Push commands below and `bun run cfcli -- push --help` for the current flags. Preview, fix Wiki-side warnings and rerun the gate/log cycle for any additional content edits, then run the actual build without `--dry-run`. A running Foundry or MCP bridge is not required for the offline Adventure build; installation uses only an explicitly supplied modules folder, and the DM performs Adventure import. Live touches remain the Push skill's conditional branch.

    If packing tools, permissions or another real prerequisite are unavailable, attempt the permitted path, retain the observed error and report exactly what remains unavailable. Count only the actual build as a Push. A built module does not prove live import. Report the chart briefly, design decisions left to Prep, any Canon conflict from step 2, unresolved requirements and remaining DM actions.

## Completion commands

Check each page as you finish it and repair its findings before opening the next page, per `skill://lint` § Commands (**Page check**). After content is ready, close per `skill://lint` § Commands with every touched content page, hot included, logging one `prep` operation with `--title "Session <N> Prep"`. The CLI owns generated index and log files, and an assigned filesystem root takes **Another root** there.

Push through `push-session` (see `bun run cfcli -- push --help` for the current flags):

```bash
bun run cfcli -- push --campaign "<Campaign>" --session <N> --dry-run --json
bun run cfcli -- push --campaign "<Campaign>" --session <N> --json
```

Actual Push writes its own `push` log entry.
