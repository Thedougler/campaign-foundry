# Climax Scene

The Climax is where the Session's Threads meet at their highest stakes. It pays off what the earlier Scenes planted: the Party arrives equipped by what they learned and marked by what they lost. Follow `docs/agents/scene-pages.md`, with this craft; its Encounters section sets the balance when there's a fight.

## Craft

- **Earned.** List what the Scenes before planted (Clues, allies, weaknesses, wounds), and give each a use here. Anything the Climax leans on that nothing planted goes back into an earlier Scene.
- **Threads converge.** Name each Thread that meets here (`## Threads`) and what the Climax decides for it.
- **The whole situation at the brink.** The Opening shows the opposition doing what the Party came to stop, what is at stake and where, the features the fight can use, and the pressure rising. Everything a player needs to plan a first move is in it.
- **More than hit points.** Stakes the Party can win or lose apart from the fight: the ritual, the hostage, the document, the door.
- **Balanced High.** A fight here is a High encounter by the 2024 budget unless the chart says otherwise, and uses the opposition's best tactics, phases or reinforcements.
- **Several endings.** At least three outcomes play can reach (defeat, escape, compromise, sacrifice, exposure), each handing to the Resolution with what changed.

## Cards

| Card | The Climax |
| --- | --- |
| Final Battle | the opposition fights with everything it has, over something that matters |
| Final Revelation | the truth behind the Session lands, and the Party must act on it at once |
| Sacrifice | winning costs something the Party must choose to give up |
| Desperate Gambit | the Party's plan relies on one risky move under pressure |
| Reckoning | the consequences of earlier choices arrive together |
| Siege | the Party holds a place against a larger force |
| Negotiation Under Duress | talk decides it, with violence one wrong word away |
| Catastrophe | the disaster happens, and the Party saves what it can |
| Trial | the Party makes its case before a judge who holds the power |
