# Settlement

A Settlement is a table interface, not an encyclopedia: an arrival image, districts the locals recognise, the things a Party seeks on purpose, local rules that change a choice, and what is unsettling it right now.

## Page

- **At a glance:** size, who rules it visibly, its mood, what is unsettling it now (a live pressure, never closed history), and what it's known for.
- **Districts:** one line each on what the Party sees walking in, what draws people there, and the trouble there now; the landmarks that orient a newcomer; getting across town, after dark, and where movement is restricted.
- **Services:** how to arrive and leave, stay, buy, sell and commission, and heal, with prices only where the page sets them. Link the ones that recur; incidental ones stay unlinked until play makes them matter.
- **Factions here:** each Faction's public position, local goal, leverage and current move in this Settlement. Their full agendas live on their own pages.
- **Local rules:** only law, custom and constraint that change a choice (weapons, magic, violence, curfew, status, trade).
- **Rumors:** what the streets say, each with its truth on the DM's side.
- **Depth:** history that still bites, hidden places and people each with the Clue that reveals them, and each live conflict: who wants what change, who opposes it, the signs visible before anyone digs, at least two ways in, and what happens next, and when, if nobody intervenes.

## Done

- The Party can arrive, orient and seek at least one place, service or route on purpose.
- The pressure is live now and grows from something already on a page.
- Every conflict has visible signs and at least two approaches or a costly bypass.
