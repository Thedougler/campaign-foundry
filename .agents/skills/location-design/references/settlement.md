# Settlement: finding a foothold

Use this branch for a Settlement. A village may have a landing, market and shared walk; a city needs distinct districts and movement between them. The current Settlement template determines the page shape.

## Design

1. **Explain why people live here.** State its scale, economic engine, necessities brought from outside and who controls the work or trade. Use that engine to place authority, scarcity and competing claims. Give newcomers visible evidence of what is unsettled now, rather than reopening a closed historical crisis.
2. **Let visitors orient.** Describe the approach, arrival point and landmark used to find the next destination. Give each useful district or small-village equivalent a position, reason to visit, visible character and current opportunity or trouble. State crossing times, transport, restricted routes and changes after dark.
3. **Make services seekable.** Cover arrival/departure, lodging, supplies, buying/selling and any specialist help relevant to the visit. Give each available service a location, hours or access conditions, concrete cost and keeper. Link recurring service Sites and the NPCs the Party will deal with, creating only the needed complete pages through the common authoring process. If a service is unavailable, state the nearest linked alternative and how to reach it. Preserve established prices.
4. **Name local power.** Link each involved Faction, give its local objective and leverage, and link the NPC who represents its current move here. For every law or custom that changes a choice, name the enforcer, enforcement method and penalty. Keep ordinary background laws out unless they affect the request.
5. **Give competing sides approaches.** For each live situation, name the opposed wants, the service/district affected, signs a visitor can notice, and the next consequence with its time. Provide at least two approaches or a costly way around the dispute, explaining what each risks or buys. Let the Party bargain, investigate, aid, expose or leave; outcomes remain undecided. Rumours pair the street claim with the DM's truth and a person, record or Site that can reveal it.

## Table-use criterion

The Party can arrive, orient, obtain the relevant services at known costs and decide whom to approach from this page alone. Authority has faces and enforceable consequences; opposed situations advance at stated times and offer more than one response. The number of districts and service pages matches the Settlement and requested visit, not a city-sized checklist imposed on a hamlet.

Each is stated on the page: every offered service with its keeper, access terms and cost, or the linked nearest alternative; every live dispute with opposed wants, noticeable signs, at least two approaches or a costly bypass, and its timed next consequence. Services listed without keepers or prices are directory labels, not table use.
