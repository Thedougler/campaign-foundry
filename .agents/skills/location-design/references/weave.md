# Weaving the Canon into a place

For every inventory entry and every invention, decide five things, then write them onto the page:

| Where | Tell | Truth | Use | Find |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| the spot it sits in, and why there | the lasting sign anyone can perceive | the DM's answer, by name | the verb it invites and what that changes | how a closer look, a check or an action turns the tell into the truth |

## Tells

A tell is the only part of a secret, Item, hazard or presence that reaches the Narration. A strong one is concrete and lasting (an object, mark, sound or smell with a source), specific to its truth, plain enough to sit in a sentence about something else at the same weight as the details around it, and answerable: the page says what a closer look finds.

| Truth | Vague | Strong |
| --- | --- | --- |
| a smuggler's cache under the third berth | something seems hidden near the berths | the third berth's planks are newer than the rest and nailed with brass |
| a creature nests in the chimney | you sense a presence | the soot on the hearth is scored with three-toed prints leading up the flue |
| a sea god claims the village | an old power lingers | every net hung to dry has one knot tied backward |

Each truth gets its own tell; the Narration carries one per truth and the rest of the Clues live on the DM's side.

## Secrets

State the truth in plain sentences: who, what, why and what's at stake. A secret a choice depends on gets three Clues, each in a different spot: one showing how it works (a mechanism, a routine, a trace of use), one showing what happened (a witness, a document, damage), and one pointing where it leads (a map, a symbol, a trail). One is the Narration's tell, one answers a declared action (Search, Study, Influence, Utilize), and one comes through a person, document or consequence. A failed check costs time, position or trust and leaves the other Clues in place.

## Hazards

Sign (the tell), trigger, effect, counterplay, bypass and leverage. A hazard that can't be carried belongs to its Site; one that can is an Item.

## Items

Where it sits and why (who put it there, who wants it), how it's reached (locked, guarded, buried, for sale, worn), and the trace visible before anyone reaches it: the case, the wear on the shelf, a buyer's name chalked on the lid. Its rules stay on its Item page.

## People and Creatures

Present: where they are, what they're doing, what they want, and the sign of their habits that shows even when they're out of sight. Absent: the lasting sign of who was here.

## History and Factions

History shows in structure and wear: repairs, additions, defacements, things built for one purpose used for another. A Faction's claim shows as sigils, tolls, patrol wear and who is welcome. Link the Lore or Faction page for each.

## Evidence of the past

For one or two past events the Canon supports, leave the evidence and keep the explanation on the DM's side: a clean rectangle on the wall where a painting hung, cage bars bent outward, three coats of paint on a sign with three owners' names, a place set for someone who never sat down.
