# Location design

Produce a page the DM can run during play, where each fact bears on a choice at the table. The Kind's table-use facts go on the page itself, and the reply only says where they are. Keep the DM's requested purpose and existing Canon, and choose exactly one Location Kind. Containment is a `parent` link, including a Site inside a Site.

## Workflow

1. **Establish the brief and evidence.** Read sections 1 and 2 of [references/authoring.md](references/authoring.md) before design for orientation, Canon, the current template and sourcing. A composed request may reuse the caller's completed setup and fill only the missing evidence. **Done when** the requested use, Kind, containing Location, preserved facts and sources are known.
2. **Make the situation spatial.** Name who wants a concrete change here, who opposes it, and what changes next at a stated time if nobody acts. Derive the pressure from retrieved Canon or the DM's intent. Show how geography, construction, work or a local custom makes that struggle playable: what visitors can notice, reach, use or change. Keep a quiet shop or refuge at its own scale. **Done when** the DM has named opposed wants, a timed consequence, and physical details that change a choice rather than merely decorate it.
3. **Design the selected Kind.** Follow the matching section below and its conditional reference. Scope a partial request to the affected content without changing the caller's objective. **Done when** the design has produced every fact that branch's table-use criterion names, ready to file on the page, and any required child procedure has returned its actual artifacts.
4. **Expose truths through evidence.** When placing hidden truths, hazards, Items, inhabitants or historical traces, read [references/weave.md](references/weave.md). Put the observable tell where the Party can encounter it and the explicit answer where the DM will look it up. **Done when** every important element has a location and a usable reveal or interaction, and every hazard can be noticed and answered.
5. **Author and finish.** Follow sections 3 and 4 of [references/authoring.md](references/authoring.md) when writing, linking, composing Narration, checking and logging. **Done when** the pages meet the template and branch criteria with every table-use fact filed on the page, the standalone gate/log are complete, or the caller has the full composed return for its single gate/log.

## Region

For map-scale travel, read [references/region.md](references/region.md) before arranging borders, routes and reachable Locations. It owns travel choices, navigation, rest and supply, active powers, encounters and rumours at the Region's scale. Preserve known routes and their costs. Expand the map while the Party keeps choosing its own route.

## Settlement

For a populated Location, read [references/settlement.md](references/settlement.md) before arranging arrival, districts, services, local rules and opposed situations. It covers the Settlement's economic engine and the people the Party can approach. Scale the interface to a village or city, and leave room-by-room keys to Sites that need them.

## Site

For a bounded visit or exploration, read [references/site.md](references/site.md) before choosing the useful key and local interactions. It owns ordinary Sites: shops, landmarks, buildings and compact visits.

When the Site needs room-by-room exploration with interconnected areas and expedition pressure, invoke `dungeon-design` **once for stocking**, after this workflow's evidence and situation are established. Supply the Site path or intended page, actual Site template, Canon inventory, containing Location, Party facts relevant to danger, requested exploration scope and caller operation. Receive the keyed stock, supporting owner pages and their paths. Resume here at evidence placement and authoring. The dungeon-design handoff covers stocking only, and this skill runs the rest of the Location workflow. A simple shop or landmark stays a simple Site.

## Return

Give a concise reply that states the Location and any dependent pages, the important Canon decisions and their grounding pages, and the observed gate/log result. Before returning, check every fact the reply offers against the pages: a route, cost, DC, keeper or key that lives only in the reply is undelivered. For composition, return those artifacts and touched paths under the caller's operation instead of appending a child log. State any real blocker precisely.

On a DM request for an evaluation, read [the eval workflow](../../../evals/README.md) and run the cases in [evals/cases.yaml](evals/cases.yaml) with `run-evals`.
