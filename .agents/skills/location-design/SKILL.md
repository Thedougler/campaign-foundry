# Location design

Produce a page the DM can run during play, where each fact bears on a choice at the table. The Kind's table-use facts go on the page itself, and the reply only says where they are. Keep the DM's requested purpose and existing Canon, and choose exactly one Location Kind. Containment is a `parent` link, including a Site inside a Site.

## Workflow

1. **Establish the brief and evidence.** Read sections 1 and 2 of [references/authoring.md](references/authoring.md) for Canon, the current template and sourcing. A composed request may reuse the caller's completed setup and fill only the missing evidence.
2. **Make the situation spatial.** Name who wants a concrete change here, who opposes it, and what changes next at a stated time if nobody acts. Derive the pressure from retrieved Canon or the DM's intent. Show how geography, construction, work or a local custom makes that struggle playable: what visitors can notice, reach, use or change, each physical detail changing a choice. Keep a quiet shop or refuge at its own scale. A filed Location whose structure is sound but whose presence reads generic gets a `flesh-out` pass in place of a redesign.
3. **Design the selected Kind.** Follow the matching section below and its reference. Scope a partial request to the affected content without changing the caller's objective.
4. **Expose truths through evidence.** When placing hidden truths, hazards, Items, inhabitants or historical traces, read [references/weave.md](references/weave.md). Put the observable tell where the Party can encounter it and the explicit answer where the DM will look it up.
5. **Author and finish.** Follow sections 3 and 4 of [references/authoring.md](references/authoring.md) when writing, linking, composing Narration and closing, and hand the filed pages to `visuals` before section 4's close.

## Region

For map-scale travel, read [references/region.md](references/region.md) before arranging borders, routes and reachable Locations. It owns travel choices, navigation, rest and supply, active powers, encounters and rumours at the Region's scale. Preserve known routes and their costs. Expand the map while the Party keeps choosing its own route.

## Settlement

For a populated Location, read [references/settlement.md](references/settlement.md) before arranging arrival, districts, services, local rules and opposed situations. It covers the Settlement's economic engine and the people the Party can approach. Scale the interface to a village or city, and leave room-by-room keys to Sites that need them.

## Site

For a bounded visit or exploration, read [references/site.md](references/site.md) before choosing the useful key and local interactions. It owns ordinary Sites: shops, landmarks, buildings and compact visits.

When the Site needs room-by-room exploration with interconnected areas and expedition pressure, invoke `dungeon-design` for stocking, after this workflow's evidence and situation are established. Supply the Site path or intended page, actual Site template, Canon inventory, containing Location, Party facts relevant to danger, requested exploration scope and caller operation. Receive the keyed stock, supporting owner pages and their paths. Resume here at evidence placement and authoring. The dungeon-design hand-off covers stocking only, and this skill runs the rest of the Location workflow. A simple shop or landmark stays a simple Site.

## Return

Reply with the Location and any dependent pages, and the important Canon decisions with their grounding pages. For composition, return those artifacts and touched paths under the caller's operation.

On a DM request for an evaluation, run the cases in [evals/cases.yaml](evals/cases.yaml) through `skill://dogfood`.
