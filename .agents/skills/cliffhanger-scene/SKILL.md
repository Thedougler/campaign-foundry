# Cliffhanger Scene

A Cliffhanger is a contest whose outcome stays in doubt to the end, putting bodies, a Vehicle or a place at physical risk. It tests what the last Development revealed, and its result is the changed situation the next Development deals with. Follow `docs/agents/scene-pages.md`, with this craft; its Encounters section sets the balance.

## Craft

- **Short and sharp.** Three to five rounds of combat, or twenty to thirty minutes for a chase or hazard. The page states how it ends, and the pressure ends it if nobody else does.
- **Threat up front.** The danger is visible in the Opening and real by the first round.
- **Objectives beyond killing.** Each side wants something concrete (seize the ledger, cross the causeway, drag the prisoner to the boat). Reaching it, breaking off, surrendering or escaping each end the contest.
- **Opposition that plays to win.** Each opposing side has its Creatures embedded in the Encounter and one tactics line: opening move, how it adapts when countered, when it breaks, and how it leaves.
- **Terrain that acts.** Two or three features with rulings (cover, a climb DC, difficult ground, something that falls or burns), and one change that lands mid-contest on a named trigger or round.
- **Escalation each round.** A pressure tick moves the objective, narrows an exit or adds a threat, so the contest sharpens rather than grinds.
- **Losing is interesting.** A loss changes the situation (captured, separated, the Item taken, the route cut) and hands the next Development a problem. Death is possible when the fiction and the table's tone allow it, and the page says when.
- **One at a time.** A Cliffhanger hands to a Development, never to another Cliffhanger.

## Cards

| Card | The contest |
| --- | --- |
| Ambush | the opposition strikes first from hiding |
| Confrontation | a standoff that turns violent |
| Chase | the Party pursues, or is pursued, through changing ground |
| Race | two sides against a clock or to the same goal |
| Duel | one champion against one, with others watching or interfering |
| Battle | a large fight where objectives matter more than the kill count |
| Monster | one terrible Creature and the place it hunts |
| Obstacles | the land itself is the opponent: collapse, flood, fire, storm |
| Dogfight | Vehicles or mounts fighting in motion |
| Skirmish | a quick clash with a small force that reveals the larger one |
