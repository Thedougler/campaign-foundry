## What happened

The bell rang before dawn and the water under [[Reedholt]] dropped a foot. [[Odalys Ferro]] calmed the crowd on the plank walk while [[Nib Ashwater]] pulled the Party into the tally house. Nib showed them the tally-sticks of the drowned, and Odalys read the Vessen knots. Nib told them the ledger lay in the bell loft. [[Pell Rushlight]] brought a note from [[Hobb Tarrow]] warning that [[Ilse Corran]]'s boats were on the water.

The Party crossed the Under-Boards along a shell ridge. Two [[Mire Drowner]] creatures attacked from the mud. [[Brannoch Vell]] held the ridge while [[Tamsin Wick]] and Odalys dragged a grappled ally clear, and the Party used one charge of the [[Ebb Lantern]] to cross the last stretch dry.

In the chapel tower the Party met [[Sable]]. Three goblin hands of [[The Reedrunners]] climbed the stair behind them. Odalys spoke the names of the drowned aloud, and Sable gave her the ledger. The goblins fled when Brannoch held the stair. The tide returned as the Party left the chapel.

Back in the tally house the Party read the marked page. The ledger records the Compact's order to open Crookback Sluice and is signed Corvin Tarrow. At dusk a groan rolled in from the east.
